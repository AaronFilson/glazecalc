What is a unity molecular formula?

A glaze recipe says how much of each material to weigh out. The unity molecular formula, also called the Seger formula, says what those materials become in the kiln: how many molecules of each oxide there are, scaled so that the fluxes add up to one.

Fluxes make the glaze melt, alumina stiffens the melt, and silica forms the glass. Comparing those three groups explains why one glaze is glossy and another matte, or why one runs while another stays put, and it shows how to replace a material that is no longer sold.
