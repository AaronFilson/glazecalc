## Banyere ngwugwu a {#about}

Ngwugwu a bụ maka onye ọ bụla na-akuzi mgbakọ glaze: ụlọ akwụkwọ, klas n'ụlọ ọrụ ite, otu ọmụmụ nke ndị ọkpụ ite na-arụ ọrụ, ma ọ bụ onye ọkpụ ite na-amụ naanị ya. Ọ nwere nkuzi isii, nke ọ bụla nwere ebumnuche ya, ihe a ga-ahazi, ihe atụ a rụrụ na ihe n'ezie, otu e si eme ya na Glazecalc, mpempe ọrụ na ajụjụ nlele.

Usoro nkuzi ndị a sochiri otu ọmụmụ e bipụtara: 陶磁器釉薬技術研究会 (otu ọmụmụ teknụzụ glaze seramiiki) nke Ibaraki Prefectural Industrial Technology Center na Japan, nke nwere nkuzi isii maka ndị ọkpụ ite ntorobịa 12 n'afọ 2015 ma kọọ ha n'akụkọ nyocha ya. Nkuzi ya nke sochiri ya na kọleji nka seramiiki Kasama nyere ọrụ tile atọ nke glaze 42, 90 na 78, nke ọ bụla a gbara ọkụ na oxidation na reduction. **Glazecalc enweghị njikọ na ebe ahụ ma ọ bụ kọleji ahụ.** Anyị na-esochi usoro nke atụmatụ ha e bipụtara, ma na-akọwa ya n'okpuru; ederede, ihe atụ na mpempe ọrụ ebe a bụ nke anyị. Nkuzi n'obodo ndị ọzọ na-akụzi otu nzọụkwụ ndị a n'usoro fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ otu: SCT305 nke Bilecik Şeyh Edebali University na Turkey, ART 195 nke Glendale Community College na US, usoro nkuzi Rhineland-Palatinate maka ụlọ akwụkwọ seramiiki na Germany, na akwụkwọ 李堅萍 nke 2006 maka ndị nkuzi na Taiwan.

### Otu esi eji ya

- Nkuzi ọ bụla na-ewe ihe dị ka awa 2. Nkuzi 4 na 5 chọrọ igwe ngụkọ, na kọmputa ma ọ bụ ekwentị nwere Glazecalc maka ụzọ abụọ ọ bụla.
- Ihe atụ a rụrụ na-eji ihe a na-ere na Japan na ọnụọgụ dị n'ọbá akwụkwọ ọkọlọtọ Glazecalc: 福島長石 (feldspar Fukushima), whiting (石灰石, calcium carbonate), otu kaolin na silica (珪石). Jiri nyocha onye na-ere ihe gị ma ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ya: tinye ya na [ibe Ihe eji arụ ọrụ](/material), ọnụọgụ ndị ahụ ga-agbanwe, mana usoro agaghị agbanwe.
- Ịgba ọkụ bụ SK 8, {{2282 °F; 1250 °C}} dị ka e kwuru. Jiri cone klas gị na-agba ọkụ ruo; [Ịgba ọkụ ruo SK cone](/guides/firing#sk-cones) na-akọwa nọmba SK.
- Iji bipụta mpempe ọrụ ma ọ bụ maapụ tile, bipụta ibe a site na brawsa. Ịchekwa usoro, ngwakọta na ndetu chọrọ akaụntụ n'efu.

> [!WARNING]
> Nkuzi 1, 3 na 6 bụ ọrụ n'ụlọ nyocha: ịtụ ihe kpọrọ nkụ na ịgba ọkụ. Tupu onye ọ bụla atụọ ihe ọ bụla, gụọ [Omume kacha mkpa](/guides/safe-mixing#habits). Uzuzu glaze kpọrọ nkụ bụ ihe ize ndụ kacha ukwuu.

## Nkuzi 1: Ihe na tile triaxial {#materials}

> [!WARNING]
> Tụọ ihe kpọrọ nkụ nwayọọ, were mmiri hichaa, ma yie respirator kwesịrị ahụ gị ka ntụ ndị ahụ na-akpọ nkụ. Buru ụzọ gụọ [Ihe ize ndụ kacha ukwuu: uzuzu](/guides/safe-mixing#the-main-hazard) na [Respirator](/guides/safe-mixing#respirators).

### Ebumnuche

- Kpọọ aha ọrụ atọ dị na glaze: onye na-ewu iko {{en: glass former}}, flux na onye na-eme ka ọ kwụsie ike {{en: stabilizer}}.
- Kewaa ihe 4 a na-ahụkarị site na oxide ha na-eweta.
- Guzobe ngwakọta triaxial, ma gụọ tile ya dịka maapụ.

### Ihe a ga-ahazi

- Akpa otu nke 福島長石, whiting, otu kaolin na silica, ma ọ bụ ihe klas gị nwere.
- [Maapụ tile triaxial](#tile-maps) dị n'okpuru, a bipụtara, na test tile 21 maka nwa akwụkwọ ọ bụla maka triangle ọ bụla.
- Tile a gbara ọkụ site na triaxial gara aga, ma ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ha, ka a na-eziga ha n'aka n'aka.

### Ihe atụ a rụrụ

Nyocha nke ihe ọ bụla, site n'ọbá akwụkwọ Glazecalc, na-egosi oxide ọ na-ahapụ mgbe a gbachara ọkụ. Loss on ignition (LOI) bụ ihe na-efu n'ọkụ.

## Nkuzi 2: Kemistri ị chọrọ {#chemistry}

### Ebumnuche

- Gụọ chemical formula: SiO₂, CaO, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃.
- Gbakọọ molar mass site na atomic weight.
- Tụgharịa gram ka ọ bụrụ mole, ma laghachi.

### Ihe a ga-ahazi

- Periodic table maka nwa akwụkwọ ọ bụla, na igwe ngụkọ.
- Atomic weight ndị dị na tebụl dị n'okpuru (ọnụọgụ ọkọlọtọ IUPAC, dịka Glazecalc si eji ha).

### Ihe atụ a rụrụ

Table: Atomic weight e ji ebe a (g/mol)
Label: Atomic weight

| Element   | Akara | Atomic weight |
| --------- | ----- | ------------: |
| Oxygen    | O     |        15.999 |
| Carbon    | C     |        12.011 |
| Sodium    | Na    |         22.99 |
| Aluminium | Al    |        26.982 |
| Silicon   | Si    |        28.085 |
| Potassium | K     |        39.098 |
| Calcium   | Ca    |        40.078 |

Formula na-agụ atom. SiO₂ bụ otu silicon na oxygen abụọ, ya mere otu mole ya na-arịa 28.085 + 2 × 15.999 = 60.083 g. Al₂O₃ bụ 2 × 26.982 + 3 × 15.999 = 101.961 g. CaO bụ 56.077 g, whiting, CaCO₃, bụkwa 100.086 g; ọbá akwụkwọ Glazecalc na-ekpuchi ya ruo 100.08.

**Mole** bụ ọnụọgụ, dịka otu dozin: otu ọnụ ọgụgụ molecule n'agbanyeghị ihe ha bụ. Mole bụ gram ÷ molar mass:

- 25 g silica bụ 25 ÷ 60.083 = 0.416 mol nke SiO₂.
- 20 g whiting bụ 20 ÷ 100.08 = 0.200 mol. Na kiln (ebe a na-agba ite ọkụ) ọ na-efu carbon dioxide ya (CO₂, 44.009 g/mol) ma hapụ 0.200 mol nke CaO, nke na-arịa 0.200 × 56.077 = 11.2 g. Ọnụ ọgụgụ efu ahụ bụ LOI ya: 43.97%.

2. Lelee otu molar mass n'aka megide ibe ahụ.

## Nkuzi 3: Ịgụ UMF {#reading-the-formula}

> [!WARNING]
> Ịgbakwunye agba na glaze pụtara ịtụ ihe na-enye agba, ụfọdụ n'ime ha chọrọ nlezi anya karịa ihe ntọala. Buru ụzọ gụọ [Ihe ndị chọrọ nlezi anya kacha](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Ebumnuche

- Gụọ kọlụm atọ nke UMF (unity molecular formula, ゼーゲル式, usoro Seger): flux, onye na-eme ka ọ kwụsie ike, onye na-ewu iko.
- Chọta glaze na chaatị silica–alumina, ma mara ihe chaatị ahụ nwere ike na ihe ọ nweghị ike ịgwa gị.
- Tinye agba na glaze e mere kwa n'usoro nzọụkwụ, ma dekọọ ya.

### Ihe a ga-ahazi

- UMF dị n'okpuru, e dere ya n'ụzọ buru ibu.
- Glaze doro anya e mere kwa, na ihe na-enye agba dịka red iron oxide (弁柄).

### Ihe atụ a rụrụ

Glaze nke nkuzi 4, 福島長石 45, whiting 20, kaolin 10 na silica 25, nwere UMF a (ọnụọgụ Glazecalc, a kpuchiri):

Table: UMF n'kọlụm ya atọ
Label: UMF

| Flux (RO, R₂O) | Onye na-eme ka ọ kwụsie ike (R₂O₃) | Onye na-ewu iko (RO₂) |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| K₂O 0.18       | Al₂O₃ 0.43                         | SiO₂ 3.45             |
| Na₂O 0.10      |                                    |                       |
| CaO 0.72       |                                    |                       |

- Flux na-ejikọta ruo 1: nke ahụ bụ iwu nke formula ahụ. Nọmba ọzọ ọ bụla bụ mole maka otu mole nke flux.
- Silica na alumina bụ 3.45 ÷ 0.43, ihe dị ka 8: glaze na-egbuke egbuke a na-ahụkarị.
- Calcium bụ ọtụtụ nke flux, dịka n'ọtụtụ glaze lime nke Japan (石灰釉).

Chaatị nwere silica n'akụkụ na alumina n'elu na-edebe glaze ọ bụla dịka otu isi. Nnwale Seger na chaatị Stull nke 1912 bụ ụdị a, 釉性状図 na-edekwa tile a gbara ọkụ nke klas n'onwe ya na ya. Ibe usoro Glazecalc na-adọta chaatị Stull, glaze a na-anọkwa na mpaghara ya na-egbuke egbuke. Mpaghara Stull sitere na flux na ịgba ọkụ nke ya: ntụzịaka, ọ bụghị amụma. [Grid tile](#tile-maps) dị n'okpuru na-enye klas ohere ịdọta chaatị nke ya n'ịgba ọkụ nke ya.

::stull-chart

Iji tinye agba na glaze doro anya e mere kwa nke i na-amaghị usoro ya, Glazecalc enweghị ike ịgbakọ formula ya. Kama nke ahụ, tinye ihe na-enye agba n'elu 100 ahụ: 100 g glaze kpọrọ nkụ na iron oxide 1, 2 na 3 g, otu tile maka nke ọ bụla, a gbara ọkụ na oxidation na reduction.

3. Maka ntọala nke ị maara usoro ya, mee ka ihe na-enye agba bụrụ mgbakwunye ma jiri line blend na [Ngwakọta](/blends) mee ya n'usoro nzọụkwụ.

## Nkuzi 5: Site na UMF ruo usoro {#formula-to-recipe}

### Ebumnuche

- Họrọ ihe maka formula ebumnuche, ma gbakọọ ole nke ọ bụla.
- Jiri Chọta usoro maka ebumnuche, ma kwuo otu ọ si bịaru nso.

### Ihe a ga-ahazi

- Ebumnuche dị n'okpuru, mpempe ọrụ 5, na igwe ngụkọ; Glazecalc na akaụntụ.

### Ihe atụ a rụrụ

Ebumnuche: 0.2 K₂O, 0.1 Na₂O, 0.7 CaO, 0.4 Al₂O₃ na 4.0 SiO₂. Were ihe ndị ahụ n'usoro a, nke ọ bụla maka oxide naanị ya nwere ike iweta:

1. **Feldspar, maka potash na soda.** Otu unit nke 福島長石 (536.8 g, ịdị arọ nwere otu mole nke flux) na-eweta 0.94 mol nke K₂O na Na₂O. Ebumnuche chọrọ 0.3, ya mere 0.3 ÷ 0.94 = 0.319 unit: 171.4 g. Ọ na-eweta kwa CaO 0.015, Al₂O₃ 0.328 na SiO₂ 1.865.
2. **Whiting, maka calcium fọdụrụ.** 0.7 − 0.015 = 0.685 mol × 100.08 = 68.6 g.
3. **Kaolin, maka alumina fọdụrụ.** 0.4 − 0.328 = 0.072 mol. EPK kaolin nwere otu mole nke Al₂O₃ n'ime 272.6 g, ya mere 19.6 g, nke na-eweta kwa SiO₂ 0.149.
4. **Silica, maka ihe fọdụrụ.** 4.0 − 1.865 − 0.149 = 1.986 mol × 60.083 = 119.3 g.

Batch bụ 379.0 g. Dịka pasentị: 福島長石 45.2, whiting 18.1, kaolin 5.2, silica 31.5. Feldspar na-edozi nha potash na soda, ya mere usoro ahụ na-enye K₂O 0.195 na Na₂O 0.104: dị nso n'ebumnuche, ọ bụghị n'ime ya kpọmkwem.

## Nkuzi 6: Ụrọ ahụ na ntụpọ glaze {#body-and-defects}

> [!WARNING]
> Glaze a gbara ọkụ nwere ike ịhapụ metal n'ime nri na ihe ọṅụṅụ, ọ nwekwara ike ịdị mma anya mgbe ọ na-eme nke ahụ. Glaze nwere barium dị elu ma ọ bụ nwere copper, na glaze ọ bụla nwere lead (Pb), nwere ike ịhapụ metal (leach). Naanị nnwale metal na-apụta {{en: leach test}} a na-eme n'ụlọ nyocha n'ite a gbara ọkụ na-egosi ihe ọ na-ahapụ; ọ nweghị ngụkọ nwere ike ime nke ahụ. Gụọ [Nchekwa nri](/guides/glazing-basics#food-safety) na [Ihe ndị chọrọ nlezi anya kacha](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Ebumnuche

- Tụọ shrinkage (mbelata nha) na water absorption (ịmị mmiri) nke ụrọ.
- Kpọọ aha ntụpọ ndị a na-ahụkarị, ihe ndị na-akpatakarị ha na nzọụkwụ mbụ nke idozi.
- Mara ntụpọ ndị na-eme ka ite bụrụ nke na-adịghị mma maka nchekwa.

### Ihe a ga-ahazi

- Mpempe ụrọ ịnwale nke ụrọ gị, e mere akara 100 mm n'agbata ha mgbe ha ka mmiri, a gbara ọkụ na nke a na-agbaghị.
- Tile nwere ntụpọ, ma ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ha: crazing, crawling, pinholes, ịsọ.

### Ihe atụ a rụrụ

- **Shrinkage**: (ogologo e mere akara − ogologo a gbara ọkụ) ÷ ogologo e mere akara × 100. Akara 100 mm n'agbata ha nke na-atụ 88 mm mgbe ịgba ọkụ gasịrị: shrinkage 12%.
- **Water absorption**: (ịdị arọ mgbe e wesịrị ya na mmiri − ịdị arọ kpọrọ nkụ) ÷ ịdị arọ kpọrọ nkụ × 100. Mpempe a gbara ọkụ nke 50.0 g nke na-arịa 50.6 g mgbe e wesịrị ya na mmiri: 1.2%.
