## Game da wannan kunshi {#about}

An yi wannan kunshi ne don duk wanda yake koyar da lissafin glaze: makaranta, ajin studio, ƙungiyar karatu ta maginan tukwane masu aiki, ko magini da yake koyo shi kaɗai. Yana da zama shida, kuma kowanne yana da manufofinsa, abin da za a shirya, misali da aka yi aiki da shi da kayan aiki na gaske, yadda ake yi a Glazecalc, takardar aiki da tambayoyin tabbatarwa.

Tsarin zaman yana bin ƙungiyar karatu da aka buga: 陶磁器釉薬技術研究会 (ƙungiyar karatu kan glaze na yumɓu) ta Cibiyar Fasahar Masana'antu ta Lardin Ibaraki a Japan, wadda ta gudanar da zama shida ga matasa maginan tukwane 12 a 2015, ta kuma ba da rahoton su a rahoton bincikenta. Kwasonta na gaba a kwalejin zane-zanen yumɓu ta Kasama ya ba da ayyukan tayil uku na glaze 42, 90 da 78, kowanne an gasa shi a oxidation da kuma a reduction. **Glazecalc ba shi da alaƙa da cibiyar ko kwalejin.** Muna bin tsarin bayaninsu da aka buga, kuma mun ambace shi a ƙasa; rubutun, misalan da takardun aiki na nan namu ne. Kwasa-kwasai a wasu ƙasashe suna koyar da matakai iri ɗaya kusan a tsari ɗaya: SCT305 na Jami'ar Bilecik Şeyh Edebali a Turkiyya, ART 195 na Glendale Community College a Amurka, tsarin karatun makarantun yumɓu na Rhineland-Palatinate a Jamus, da takardar 李堅萍 ta 2006 ga malamai a Taiwan.

### Yadda ake amfani da shi

- Kowanne zama yana ɗaukar kusan awa 2. Zama na 4 da na 5 suna buƙatar na'urorin lissafi, da kwamfuta ko waya mai Glazecalc ga kowane ma'aurata biyu.
- Misalan da aka yi aiki da su suna amfani da kayan da ake sayarwa a Japan da lambobin da ke cikin laburaren Glazecalc na yau da kullum: 福島長石 (feldspar na Fukushima), whiting (石灰石, calcium carbonate), kaolin da silica (珪石). Ku yi amfani da analysis na mai sayar da kayanku idan kuna da shi: ku ƙara shi a [shafin Kayan aiki](/material), lambobin za su canja, amma hanyar ba za ta canja ba.
- Gasar tana a SK 8, {{2282 °F; 1250 °C}} a kimanta. Ku yi amfani da cone da ajinku yake gasawa da shi; [Gasa zuwa cone na SK](/guides/firing#sk-cones) yana bayyana lambobin SK.
- Don buga takardar aiki ko taswirar tayil, ku buga wannan shafi daga browser. Ajiye girke-girke, haɗe-haɗe da rubuce-rubuce yana buƙatar asusu na kyauta.

> [!WARNING]
> Zama na 1, 3 da 6 aikin lab ne: auna busassun kaya da gasa. Kafin kowa ya auna wani kaya, ku karanta [Ɗabi'un da suka fi muhimmanci](/guides/safe-mixing#habits). Ƙurar glaze busasshiya ita ce babban haɗari.

## Zama na 1: Kayan aiki da tayil ɗin triaxial {#materials}

> [!WARNING]
> Ku auna busassun kaya a hankali, ku share a jike, kuma ku sa respirator da ya dace da fuska muddin foda tana bushe. Ku fara karanta [Babban haɗari: ƙura](/guides/safe-mixing#the-main-hazard) da [Respirator](/guides/safe-mixing#respirators).

### Manufofi

- Ku ambaci ayyuka uku a cikin glaze: mai gina gilashi, flux (mai narkarwa) da mai daidaitawa.
- Ku rarraba kayan da aka saba amfani da su guda 4 bisa oxide da suke kawowa.
- Ku kafa haɗin triaxial, kuma ku karanta tayil ɗinsa kamar taswira.

### Shiri

- Buhu ɗaya kowanne na 福島長石, whiting, kaolin da silica, ko kayan da ajinku yake da su.
- [Taswirar tayil ɗin triaxial](#tile-maps) da ke ƙasa, a buga ta, da tayil ɗin gwaji 21 ga kowane ɗalibi ga kowane triangle.
- Tayil ɗin da aka gasa daga triaxial na baya, idan kuna da su, don a wuce da su a hannu.

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

Analysis na kowane kaya, daga laburaren Glazecalc, yana ba da oxide da yake bari bayan gasa. Raguwar nauyi a wuta (LOI) ita ce abin da yake konewa ya tafi.

| 福島長石 (feldspar) | 65.4 | 19.53 | 10.76 | 3.77 |  0.49 |  0.18 | Flux, da alumina da silica nasa       |

Feldspar shi kaɗai yana narkewa a SK 8 ya zama gilashi mai tauri; whiting shi kaɗai ba ya narkewa; silica shi kaɗai ba ya narkewa. Idan aka haɗa su, suna narkewa fiye da kowanne ɗaya daga cikinsu. Haɗin triaxial yana nuna inda: taswirar tayil da ke ƙasa tana canja uku ɗin da kashi ~20% (18 cikin 90) a kowane mataki, da kaolin kashi 10% a kowane tayil.

## Zama na 2: Sinadaran da kuke buƙata {#chemistry}

### Manufofi

- Ku karanta dabarar sinadari: SiO₂, CaO, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃.
- Ku lissafa molar mass daga nauyin atom.
- Ku mayar da gram zuwa mole, da kuma akasin haka.

### Shiri

- Teburin abubuwa na periodic ga kowane ɗalibi, da na'urorin lissafi.
- Nauyin atom a teburin da ke ƙasa (ƙa'idodin IUPAC na yau da kullum, kamar yadda Glazecalc yake amfani da su).

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

Table: Nauyin atom da ake amfani da su a nan (g/mol)
Label: Nauyin atom

| Abu       | Alama | Nauyin atom |
| --------- | ----- | ----------: |
| Oxygen    | O     |      15.999 |
| Carbon    | C     |      12.011 |
| Sodium    | Na    |       22.99 |
| Aluminium | Al    |      26.982 |
| Silicon   | Si    |      28.085 |
| Potassium | K     |      39.098 |
| Calcium   | Ca    |      40.078 |

Dabara tana ƙirga atom. SiO₂ silicon ɗaya ne da oxygen biyu, don haka mole ɗaya nasa yana nauyin 28.085 + 2 × 15.999 = 60.083 g. Al₂O₃ shi ne 2 × 26.982 + 3 × 15.999 = 101.961 g. CaO shi ne 56.077 g, kuma whiting, CaCO₃, shi ne 100.086 g; laburaren Glazecalc yana gyara shi zuwa 100.08.

**Mole** adadi ne, kamar dozin: adadin ƙwayoyin halitta iri ɗaya ko mene ne su. Mole shi ne gram ÷ molar mass:

- 25 g na silica shi ne 25 ÷ 60.083 = 0.416 mol na SiO₂.
- 20 g na whiting shi ne 20 ÷ 100.08 = 0.200 mol. A cikin kiln yana rasa carbon dioxide ɗinsa (CO₂, 44.009 g/mol) ya bar 0.200 mol na CaO, wanda nauyinsa 0.200 × 56.077 = 11.2 g. Wannan asara ita ce LOI nasa: 43.97%.

### A Glazecalc

1. Ku buɗe [Whiting](/materials/whiting) da [Silica](/materials/silica): kowane shafi yana ba da dabarar da nauyinta.
2. Ku duba molar mass ɗaya da hannu, ku kwatanta da shafin.

## Zama na 3: Karanta UMF {#reading-the-formula}

> [!WARNING]
> Canza launin glaze yana nufin auna masu launi, wasu daga cikinsu suna buƙatar kulawa fiye da kayan tushe. Ku fara karanta [Kayan da suka fi buƙatar kulawa](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Manufofi

- Ku karanta ginshiƙai uku na UMF (unity molecular formula) (ゼーゲル式, dabarar Seger): flux, mai daidaitawa, mai gina gilashi.
- Ku sami glaze a kan jadawalin silica da alumina, kuma ku san abin da jadawalin zai iya faɗa da wanda ba zai iya faɗa ba.
- Ku canza launin glaze da aka riga aka yi a matakai, kuma ku rubuta shi.

### Shiri

- UMF da ke ƙasa, a rubuta shi babba.
- Glaze mai haske da aka riga aka yi, da mai launi kamar red iron oxide (弁柄).

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

Glaze na zama na 4, 福島長石 45, whiting 20, kaolin 10 da silica 25, yana da wannan UMF (lambobin Glazecalc, an gyara su):

Table: UMF a ginshiƙansa uku
Label: UMF

| Flux (RO, R₂O) | Mai daidaitawa (R₂O₃) | Mai gina gilashi (RO₂) |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| K₂O 0.18       | Al₂O₃ 0.43            | SiO₂ 3.45              |
| Na₂O 0.10      |                       |                        |
| CaO 0.72       |                       |                        |

- Flux suna kai 1 gaba ɗaya: wannan ita ce ƙa'idar dabarar. Kowace lamba ta daban mole ce ga kowane mole na flux.
- Silica zuwa alumina shi ne 3.45 ÷ 0.43, kusan 8: glaze mai sheƙi na yau da kullum.
- Calcium shi ne mafi yawan flux, kamar yadda yake a yawancin glaze na lime na Japan (石灰釉).

Jadawali mai silica a kwance da alumina a tsaye yana sanya kowane glaze a matsayin digo. Gwajin Seger da jadawalin Stull na 1912 irin wannan ne, kuma 釉性状図 yana sanya tayil ɗin da aji ya gasa a kansa. Shafin girke-girke na Glazecalc yana zana taswirar Stull, kuma wannan glaze yana cikin yankinta mai haske. Yankunan Stull sun fito ne daga flux da gasar da ya yi amfani da su: jagora ce, ba hasashe ba. [Grid ɗin tayil](#tile-maps) da ke ƙasa yana bar aji ya zana jadawalinsa a gasarsa.

::stull-chart

Don canza launin glaze da aka riga aka yi wanda ba ku san girke-girkensa ba, Glazecalc ba zai iya lissafa dabararsa ba. Ku ƙara mai launin a kan 100 maimakon haka: 100 g na busasshen glaze da 1, 2 da 3 g na iron oxide, tayil ɗaya kowanne, a gasa a oxidation da reduction.

### A Glazecalc

1. A [shafin girke-girke](/recipe), ku shigar da girke-girken da ke sama kuma ku karanta **UMF** da **Taswirar Stull** ɗinsa.
2. Ku canja silica daga 25 zuwa 35, ku kalli digon yana matsawa zuwa dama.
3. Ga tushen da kuke da girke-girkensa, ku mai da mai launin ƙari, kuma ku yi amfani da line blend a [Haɗe-haɗe](/blends) don ku haɓaka shi mataki-mataki.

## Zama na 4: Daga girke-girke zuwa UMF {#recipe-to-formula}

### Manufofi

- Ku lissafa UMF daga girke-girke da hannu.
- Ku duba shi da lissafin Glazecalc da kansa.

### Shiri

- Takardar aiki ta 4, analysis huɗu daga zama na 1, da na'urorin lissafi.

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

Girke-girken: 福島長石 45, whiting 20, EPK kaolin 10, silica 25. Ku ɗauki kowane adadi a matsayin gram. Ga kowane kaya, gram × kashi na oxide ÷ 100 ÷ molar mass nasa yana ba da mole nasa. Teburin yana ba da manyan oxide; Glazecalc yana ƙirga ƙananan ma (iron da titanium na kaolin, magnesium na feldspar).

| -------- | ---: | -----: | -----: | -----: | -----: | -----: |
| 福島長石 |   45 | 0.0514 | 0.0274 | 0.0039 | 0.0862 | 0.4898 |
| Whiting  |   20 |      – |      – | 0.1998 |      – |      – |
| Kaolin   |   10 | 0.0004 | 0.0001 | 0.0003 | 0.0367 | 0.0761 |

## Zama na 5: Daga UMF zuwa girke-girke {#formula-to-recipe}

### Manufofi

- Ku zaɓi kaya don manufar dabara, kuma ku lissafa nawa na kowanne.
- Ku yi amfani da Nemo girke-girke don manufa, kuma ku faɗi yadda ya kusanto.

### Shiri

- Manufar da ke ƙasa, takardar aiki ta 5, da na'urorin lissafi; Glazecalc da asusu.

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

Manufar: 0.2 K₂O, 0.1 Na₂O, 0.7 CaO, 0.4 Al₂O₃ da 4.0 SiO₂. Ku ɗauki kayan bisa wannan tsari, kowanne don oxide da shi kaɗai ne zai iya kawowa:

1. **Feldspar, don potash da soda.** Raka'a ɗaya ta 福島長石 (536.8 g, nauyin da yake ɗauke da mole ɗaya na flux) tana kawo 0.94 mol na K₂O da Na₂O. Manufar tana buƙatar 0.3, don haka 0.3 ÷ 0.94 = raka'a 0.319: 171.4 g. Tana kuma kawo 0.015 CaO, 0.328 Al₂O₃ da 1.865 SiO₂.
2. **Whiting, don sauran calcium.** 0.7 − 0.015 = 0.685 mol × 100.08 = 68.6 g.
3. **Kaolin, don sauran alumina.** 0.4 − 0.328 = 0.072 mol. EPK kaolin yana ɗauke da mole ɗaya na Al₂O₃ a cikin 272.6 g, don haka 19.6 g, wanda kuma yake kawo 0.149 SiO₂.
4. **Silica, don sauran.** 4.0 − 1.865 − 0.149 = 1.986 mol × 60.083 = 119.3 g.

Batch ɗin shi ne 379.0 g. A kashi: 福島長石 45.2, whiting 18.1, kaolin 5.2, silica 31.5. Feldspar shi ne yake kayyade rabon potash zuwa soda, don haka girke-girken yana ba da 0.195 K₂O da 0.104 Na₂O: kusa da manufar, ba daidai da ita ba.

### A Glazecalc

1. Ku buɗe [Nemo girke-girke don manufa](/solve), ku zaɓi **UMF (mole, flux suna kai 1 gaba ɗaya)**, kuma ku rubuta manufar.
2. A ƙarƙashin **Kayan aiki**, ku ba shi 福島長石, whiting, EPK kaolin da silica, kuma ku zaɓi **Nemo**.
3. Yana ba da 福島長石 44.9, whiting 18.2, kaolin 5.6 da silica 31.3, da tebur na yadda kowane oxide ya kusanto. Ku kwatanta shi da lissafinku na hannu: mai nemo yana ƙirga kowane ƙaramin abu, kuma yana samun mafi dacewa da kayan huɗun suka bari.
4. **Buɗe a matsayin sabon girke-girke**, kuma ku duba shi a shafin girke-girke.

## Zama na 6: Yumɓun jiki da lahanin glaze {#body-and-defects}

> [!WARNING]
> Glaze da aka gasa zai iya sakin ƙarfe cikin abinci da abin sha, kuma yana iya yin kyau sosai yayin da yake yin hakan. Glaze mai yawan barium ko mai copper, da duk glaze mai darma (lead, Pb), na iya zubar da ƙarfe. Gwajin zubar ƙarfe na lab na tukunyar da aka gasa ne kawai yake nuna abin da take saki; babu lissafin da zai iya. Ku karanta [Lafiyar abinci](/guides/glazing-basics#food-safety) da [Kayan da suka fi buƙatar kulawa](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Manufofi

- Ku auna ƙanƙancewar jiki da shan ruwansa.
- Ku ambaci lahani na yau da kullum, dalilansu na yau da kullum da gyara na farko.
- Ku san waɗanne lahani ne suke sa kaya su zama marasa aminci.

### Shiri

- Sandunan gwaji na yumɓun jikinku, an yi musu alama a nisan 100 mm tsakani yayin da suke jike, an gasa su da waɗanda ba a gasa ba.
- Tayil masu lahani, idan kuna da su: crazing, crawling, pinholes, running.

### Misali da aka yi aiki da shi

- **Ƙanƙancewa**: (tsawon da aka yi wa alama − tsawon bayan gasa) ÷ tsawon da aka yi wa alama × 100. Alamomi a nisan 100 mm tsakani da suke auna 88 mm bayan gasa: ƙanƙancewa 12%.
- **Shan ruwa**: (nauyi bayan jiƙawa − nauyin busasshe) ÷ nauyin busasshe × 100. Sandar da aka gasa mai nauyin 50.0 g da take 50.6 g bayan jiƙawa: 1.2%.
