## Xidhmadan ku saabsan {#about}

Xidhmadan waxay u tahay qof kasta oo barta xisaabinta glaze-ka: iskuul, fasal istuudiyo, koox waxbarasho oo dhoobo-sameeyayaal shaqeynaya ah, ama dhoobo-sameeye keligiis wax barta. Waxay leedahay lix casho, mid kastana wuxuu leeyahay ujeeddooyinkiisa, waxa la diyaarinayo, tusaale la xalliyay oo walxo dhab ah leh, sida loogu sameeyo Glazecalc, warqad shaqo iyo su'aalo hubin ah.

Habka cashooyinka waxa uu raacayaa koox waxbarasho la daabacay: 陶磁器釉薬技術研究会 (koox waxbarasho glaze-ka dhoobada) ee Xarunta Farsamada Warshadaha ee Gobolka Ibaraki ee Japan, taas oo 2015 qabatay lix casho oo loogu talagalay 12 dhoobo-sameeye yar, kuna soo bandhigtay warbixinteeda cilmi-baadhiseed. Koorsadeeda xigtay ee kuliyadda farshaxanka dhoobada ee Kasama waxay dejisay saddex hawlood tile ah oo kala ah 42, 90 iyo 78 glaze, mid walbana lagu gubay oxidation iyo reduction. **Glazecalc xarunta iyo kuliyadda kala xidhiidh ma laha.** Waxaan raacnaa habka qorshahooda la daabacay, waana soo xigannaa hoos; qoraalka, tusaalooyinka iyo warqadaha shaqada ee halkan ku jira waa kuwayaga. Koorsooyinka dalal kale waxay baraan isla tallaabooyinkan oo qiyaastii isla habkaas ah: SCT305 ee Jaamacadda Bilecik Şeyh Edebali ee Turkiga, ART 195 ee Glendale Community College ee Mareykanka, manhajka Rhineland-Palatinate ee dugsiyada dhoobada ee Jarmalka, iyo warqadda 李堅萍 ee 2006 ee macallimiinta Taiwan.

### Sida loo isticmaalo

- Casho kastaa wuxuu qaadanayaa qiyaastii 2 saacadood. Cashooyinka 4 iyo 5 waxay u baahan yihiin xisaabiyayaal, iyo kombuyuutar ama telefoon leh Glazecalc labo-labo.
- Tusaalooyinka la xalliyay waxay isticmaalaan walxo laga iibiyo Japan iyo tirooyinka maktabadda caadiga ah ee Glazecalc: 福島長石 (feldspar-ka Fukushima), whiting (石灰石, calcium carbonate), kaolin iyo silica (珪石). Haddii aad haysato falanqaynta alaab-qeybiyahaaga, ku dar [bogga Walxaha](/material), tirooyinkuna way isbeddelayaan, laakiin habka ma isbeddelo.
- Gubiddu waa SK 8, {{2282 °F; 1250 °C}} magac ahaan. Isticmaal cone-ka fasalkaagu ku gubo; [Ku gubidda cone-yada SK](/guides/firing#sk-cones) wuxuu sharaxayaa lambarrada SK.
- Si aad u daabacdo warqad shaqo ama khariidad tile, daabac bogga browser-ka. Kaydinta habab, isku-darro iyo qoraallo waxay u baahan tahay akoon bilaash ah.

> [!WARNING]
> Cashooyinka 1, 3 iyo 6 waa shaqo shaybaar: miisaanidda walxo qalalan iyo gubid. Intaan qofna walax miisaamin, akhri [Caadooyinka ugu muhiimsan](/guides/safe-mixing#habits). Boodhka glaze-ka qalalan waa khatarta ugu weyn.

## Casho 1: Walxaha iyo tile-yada triaxial {#materials}

> [!WARNING]
> Si tartiib ah u miisaan walxaha qalalan, ku nadiifi qoyaan, oo xidh respirator ku habboon inta budada qallalan tahay. Marka hore akhri [Khatarta ugu weyn: boodhka](/guides/safe-mixing#the-main-hazard) iyo [Respirator-ada](/guides/safe-mixing#respirators).

### Ujeeddooyin

- Magacaaw saddexda shaqo ee glaze-ka ku jira: dhisaha muraayadda, flux iyo xasiliye.
- U kala sooc 4 walax oo caadi ah oksaydhyada ay keenaan.
- Dejiso isku-darka triaxial, oo tile-yadiisa u akhri sida khariidad.

### Diyaarso

- Hal bac oo mid kasta ah oo ka mid ah 福島長石, whiting, kaolin iyo silica, ama walxaha fasalkaagu haysto.
- [Khariidadda tile-ka triaxial](#tile-maps) ee hoose, oo la daabacay, iyo 21 test tile arday kasta, saddexagal kasta.
- Tile-yo la gubay oo triaxial hore, haddii aad haysato, si loo wareejiyo.

### Tusaale la xalliyay

Falanqaynta walax kasta, ee maktabadda Glazecalc, waxay siisaa oksaydhyada ay ka dhigto gubidda kadib. Luminta gubidda (LOI) waa waxa gubma oo baxa.

### Gudaha Glazecalc

1. Fur walax kasta [bogga Walxaha](/material), ama bogga u furan dadweynaha, sida [Fukushima Feldspar](/materials/fukushima-feldspar). Qor oksaydhyada ay keento.
2. Samee saddex hab: 福島長石 90 oo kaolin 10 leh, whiting 90 oo kaolin 10 leh, iyo silica 90 oo kaolin 10 leh. [Isku-darrada](/blends) ka dooro **Triaxial (3 habab)** iyo 6 saf: 21 tile, sida khariidadda hoose.
3. Dooro batch-ka tile kasta oo **Daabac** waxa la miisaamayo. Gubidda kadib, tile-yada aad jeceshahay ka kaydi habab, adigoo ka bilaabaya sawirka tile-yada.

## Casho 2: Kimistariga aad u baahan tahay {#chemistry}

### Ujeeddooyin

- Akhri qaabka kimikaad: SiO₂, CaO, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃.
- Ka soo saar miisaanka mol-ka miisaanka atamka.
- U beddel garaam mol, kana beddel mol garaam.

### Diyaarso

- Shaxda xilliyeedka (periodic table) arday kasta, iyo xisaabiyayaal.
- Miisaannada atamka ee shaxda hoose (qiimayaasha caadiga ah ee IUPAC, sida Glazecalc u isticmaalo).

### Tusaale la xalliyay

Table: Miisaannada atamka ee halkan lagu isticmaalay (g/mol)
Label: Miisaannada atamka

| Curiye    | Astaan | Miisaanka atamka |
| --------- | ------ | ---------------: |
| Oxygen    | O      |           15.999 |
| Carbon    | C      |           12.011 |
| Sodium    | Na     |            22.99 |
| Aluminium | Al     |           26.982 |
| Silicon   | Si     |           28.085 |
| Potassium | K      |           39.098 |
| Calcium   | Ca     |           40.078 |

Qaabka kimikaad wuxuu tiriyaa atamyada. SiO₂ waa hal silicon iyo laba oxygen, sidaa darteed hal mol oo ka mid ah wuxuu miisaamaa 28.085 + 2 × 15.999 = 60.083 g. Al₂O₃ waa 2 × 26.982 + 3 × 15.999 = 101.961 g. CaO waa 56.077 g, whiting-ka, CaCO₃, waa 100.086 g; maktabadda Glazecalc waxay u dhawaysaa 100.08.

**Mol** waa tiro, sida tiro-darsin: isla tirada molecule-yada wax kasta ha ahaadeen. Mol waa garaam ÷ miisaanka mol-ka:

- 25 g oo silica ah waa 25 ÷ 60.083 = 0.416 mol oo SiO₂ ah.
- 20 g oo whiting ah waa 20 ÷ 100.08 = 0.200 mol. Kiln-ka gudahiisa waxay luntaa carbon dioxide (CO₂, 44.009 g/mol), waxayna ka dhigtaa 0.200 mol oo CaO ah, oo miisaankiisu yahay 0.200 × 56.077 = 11.2 g. Lumintaas waa LOI-giisa: 43.97%.

### Gudaha Glazecalc

1. Fur [Whiting](/materials/whiting) iyo [Silica](/materials/silica): bog kasta wuxuu bixiyaa qaabka kimikaad iyo miisaannadiisa.
2. Hubi hal miisaan mol gacanta, adigoo ku barbardhigaya bogga.

## Casho 3: Akhrinta UMF {#reading-the-formula}

> [!WARNING]
> Midabaynta glaze waxay ku lug leedahay miisaanidda midabeeyayaal, qaarkood oo u baahan taxaddar ka badan walxaha aasaasiga ah. Marka hore akhri [Walxaha u baahan taxaddarka ugu badan](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Ujeeddooyin

- Akhri saddexda tiir ee UMF (ゼーゲル式, qaabka Seger): flux-yada, xasiliyaha, dhisaha muraayadda.
- Ka hel glaze jaantuska silica–alumina, oo ogow waxa jaantuska sheegi karo iyo waxa uusan sheegi karin.
- Midabee glaze diyaarsan tallaabo tallaabo, oo diiwaangeli.

### Diyaarso

- UMF-ta hoose, oo si weyn loo qoray.
- Glaze hufan oo diyaarsan, iyo midabeeye sida red iron oxide (弁柄).

### Tusaale la xalliyay

Glaze-ka casho 4, 福島長石 45, whiting 20, kaolin 10 iyo silica 25, wuxuu leeyahay UMF kan (tirooyinka Glazecalc, la dhaweeyay):

Table: UMF oo saddexda tiir ku qoran
Label: UMF

| Flux-yada (RO, R₂O) | Xasiliyaha (R₂O₃) | Dhisaha muraayadda (RO₂) |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| K₂O 0.18            | Al₂O₃ 0.43        | SiO₂ 3.45                |
| Na₂O 0.10           |                   |                          |
| CaO 0.72            |                   |                          |

- Flux-yadu waxay isku noqdaan 1: taasi waa xeerka qaabka. Tiro kasta oo kale waa mol mol kasta oo flux ah.
- Silica ilaa alumina waa 3.45 ÷ 0.43, qiyaastii 8: glaze ifaya oo caadi ah.
- Calcium waa inta badan flux-ga, sida glaze-yada lime ee Japan intooda badan (石灰釉).

Jaantus silica ee dhinac marayo iyo alumina ee kor u socota wuxuu glaze kasta dhigaa dhibic ahaan. Tijaabooyinka Seger iyo jaantuska Stull ee 1912 waa nooca noocaas ah, 釉性状図 wuxuuna ku khariidadeeyaa tile-yada fasal laftiisu gubay. Bogga habka ee Glazecalc wuxuu sawiraa shaxda Stull, glaze-kanna wuxuu ku jiraa gobolkeeda dhalaalaya. Gobollada Stull waxay ka yimaadeen flux-yadiisa iyo gubiddiisa: hage, mana aha saadaal. [Shabakadda tile-ka](#tile-maps) ee hoose waxay fasalka u ogolaataa inuu jaantuskiisa u sawiro gubiddiisa.

::stull-chart

Si aad u midabayso glaze diyaarsan oo aadan aqoon habkiisa, Glazecalc ma xisaabin karo qaabkiisa. Ku dar midabeeyaha 100-ka kor: 100 g oo glaze qalalan ah oo leh 1, 2 iyo 3 g oo iron oxide ah, hal tile mid kasta, lagu gubay oxidation iyo reduction.

### Gudaha Glazecalc

1. [Bogga habka](/recipe) geli habka kor ku qoran, oo akhri **UMF (unity formula)** iyo **Shaxda Stull**.
2. Silica ka beddel 25 una beddel 35, oo daawo dhibicda oo midigta ku dhaqaaqaysa.
3. Aasaas aad habkiisa haysato, ka dhig midabeeyaha lagu darayo oo isticmaal isku-darka xariiqda [Isku-darrada](/blends) si aad u tallaabeyso.

## Casho 4: Laga bilaabo hab ilaa UMF {#recipe-to-formula}

### Ujeeddooyin

- Gacanta ka soo saar UMF hab.
- Ku hubi shaqada Glazecalc laftiisa.

### Diyaarso

- Warqad shaqo 4, afarta falanqaynood ee casho 1, iyo xisaabiyayaal.

### Tusaale la xalliyay

Habka: 福島長石 45, whiting 20, EPK kaolin 10, silica 25. Qaado qadar kasta inay tahay garaam. Walax kasta, garaam × boqolkiiba oksaydhka ÷ 100 ÷ miisaankiisa mol-ka waxay bixisaa mol-kiisa. Shaxdu waxay bixisaa oksaydhyada waaweyn; Glazecalc wuxuu tiriyaa kuwa yaryar sidoo kale (birta iyo titanium-ka kaolin-ka, magnesium-ka feldspar-ka).

| -------- | -----: | -----: | -----: | -----: | -----: | -----: |

- K₂O 0.0518 ÷ 0.2847 = 0.18; Na₂O 0.10; CaO 0.72.

- SiO₂ 0.9820 ÷ 0.2847 = 3.45.

3. Sadar kasta ku barbardhig warqaddaada shaqo. Kadibna beddel hal qadar, oo xisaabi tiirka ay beddesho oo kaliya.

## Casho 5: Laga bilaabo UMF ilaa hab {#formula-to-recipe}

### Ujeeddooyin

- Dooro walxo bartilmaameed qaab ah, oo xisaabi inta mid kasta.
- Isticmaal U xalli bartilmaameed, oo sheeg inta uu u dhowaado.

### Diyaarso

- Bartilmaameedka hoose, warqad shaqo 5, iyo xisaabiyayaal; Glazecalc oo akoon leh.

### Tusaale la xalliyay

Bartilmaameedka: 0.2 K₂O, 0.1 Na₂O, 0.7 CaO, 0.4 Al₂O₃ iyo 4.0 SiO₂. Qaado walxaha sida tartiibkan ah, mid kasta oksaydhka uu keeni karo oo keliya awgeed:

1. **Feldspar-ka, potash-ka iyo soda-ta awgood.** Hal cutub oo 福島長石 ah (536.8 g, miisaanka hayaa hal mol oo flux ah) wuxuu keenaa 0.94 mol oo K₂O iyo Na₂O ah. Bartilmaameedku wuxuu u baahan yahay 0.3, sidaa darteed 0.3 ÷ 0.94 = 0.319 cutub: 171.4 g. Wuxuu sidoo kale keenaa 0.015 CaO, 0.328 Al₂O₃ iyo 1.865 SiO₂.
2. **Whiting, calcium-ka haray awgiis.** 0.7 − 0.015 = 0.685 mol × 100.08 = 68.6 g.
3. **Kaolin, alumina-ta harta awgeed.** 0.4 − 0.328 = 0.072 mol. EPK kaolin wuxuu hayaa hal mol oo Al₂O₃ ah 272.6 g gudahood, sidaa darteed 19.6 g, taas oo sidoo kale keenta 0.149 SiO₂.
4. **Silica, inta hartay awgeed.** 4.0 − 1.865 − 0.149 = 1.986 mol × 60.083 = 119.3 g.

Batch-ku waa 379.0 g. Boqolkiiba: 福島長石 45.2, whiting 18.1, kaolin 5.2, silica 31.5. Feldspar-ku wuxuu go'aamiyaa saamiga potash-ka iyo soda-ta, sidaa darteed habku wuxuu bixiyaa 0.195 K₂O iyo 0.104 Na₂O: bartilmaameedka u dhow, laakiin ma aha isagii.

### Gudaha Glazecalc

1. Fur [U xalli bartilmaameed](/solve), dooro **UMF (moles, flux-yadu isku noqdaan 1)**, oo ku qor bartilmaameedka.
2. Hoostiisa **Walxaha**, ka dhig 福島長石, whiting, EPK kaolin iyo silica, oo dooro **Xalli**.
3. Wuxuu bixiyaa 福島長石 44.9, whiting 18.2, kaolin 5.6 iyo silica 31.3, iyo shax muujisa inta oksaydh kasta u dhow yahay. Ku barbardhig shaqadaada gacanta: xalliyuhu wuxuu tiriyaa dhammaan walxaha yaryar, wuxuuna helaa isku-habboonaanta ugu fiican ee afarta walax ogolaadaan.
4. **U fur hab cusub**, oo ku hubi bogga habka.

## Casho 6: Dhoobada jidhka iyo cilladaha glaze-ka {#body-and-defects}

> [!WARNING]
> Glaze la gubay wuxuu biraha ka sii deyn karaa cuntada iyo cabitaanka, wuxuuna u ekaan karaa mid qumman inta uu taas sameynayo. Glaze-yada barium badan leh ama copper leh, iyo glaze kasta oo rasaas (lead, Pb) leh, biraha way ka soo bixin karaan. Tijaabo leach ah oo shaybaar oo weelka la gubay oo keliya ayaa muujisa waxa uu sii daayo; xisaab midna ma muujin karto. Akhri [Badbaadada cuntada](/guides/glazing-basics#food-safety) iyo [Walxaha u baahan taxaddarka ugu badan](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Ujeeddooyin

- Cabbir ku-yaraanta iyo nuugista biyaha ee dhoobada jidhka.
- Magacaaw cilladaha caadiga ah, sababahooda caadiga ah iyo xal koowaad.
- Ogow cilladaha ka dhiga weelasha kuwo aan ammaan ahayn.

### Diyaarso

- Bar tijaabo ah oo dhoobada jidhkaaga ah, oo la calaamadeeyay 100 mm oo kala fog inta ay qoyan yihiin, la gubay iyo aan la gubin.
- Tile-yo cillado leh, haddii aad haysato: crazing, crawling, pinholing, qulqul.

### Tusaale la xalliyay

- **Ku-yaraanta**: (dhererka la calaamadeeyay − dhererka la gubay) ÷ dhererka la calaamadeeyay × 100. Calaamado 100 mm oo kala fog oo gubidda kadib cabbira 88 mm: 12% ku-yaraan.
- **Nuugista biyaha**: (miisaanka kadib qooynta − miisaanka qalalan) ÷ miisaanka qalalan × 100. Bar la gubay oo 50.0 g ah oo qooynta kadib miisaamaya 50.6 g: 1.2%.
