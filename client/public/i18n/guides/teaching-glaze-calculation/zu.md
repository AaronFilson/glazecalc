## Mayelana nale phakheji {#about}

Le phakheji ingeyabo bonke abafundisa ukubala i-glaze{{en: glaze}}: isikole, iklasi yesitudiyo, iqembu lokufunda lababumbi abasebenzayo, noma umbumbi ozifundela yedwa. Inezifundo eziyisithupha, futhi isifundo ngasinye sinezinhloso zaso, okufanele ukulungiselele, isibonelo esixazululiwe esinokokwakha kwangempela, indlela yokusebenza ku-Glazecalc, iphepha lokusebenza nemibuzo yokuhlola.

Ukulandelana kwezifundo kulandela iqembu lokufunda elishicilelwe: i-陶磁器釉薬技術研究会 (iqembu lokufunda i-glaze yobumba) le-Ibaraki Prefectural Industrial Technology Center eJapan, elaqhuba izifundo eziyisithupha ababumbi abasha abangu-12 ngo-2015 labika ngazo embikweni walo wocwaningo. Isifundo salo esilandelayo ekolishi lobuciko bobumba laseKasama sabeka imisebenzi emithathu yama-tile yama-glaze angu-42, angu-90 nangu-78, ngalinye elishiswe ku-oxidation nange-reduction. **I-Glazecalc ayixhumene nalesi sikhungo noma nekolishi.** Silandela ukulandelana kohlaka lwabo olushicilelwe, futhi silucaphuna ngezansi; umbhalo, izibonelo namaphepha okusebenza lapha yizethu. Izifundo ezisemazweni amanye zifundisa izinyathelo ezifanayo ngokulandelana okucishe kufane: i-SCT305 ye-Bilecik Şeyh Edebali University eTurkey, i-ART 195 ye-Glendale Community College e-US, uhlelo lwezifundo lwezikole zobumba zaseRhineland-Palatinate eJalimane, nephepha lika-李堅萍 lango-2006 loothisha eTaiwan.

### Indlela yokuyisebenzisa

- Isifundo ngasinye sithatha cishe amahora angu-2. Izifundo 4 no-5 zidinga amakhalkhuletha, nekhompyutha noma ifoni ene-Glazecalc kubabili ngamunye.
- Izibonelo ezixazululiwe zisebenzisa okokwakha okuthengiswa eJapan nezibalo zomtapo ojwayelekile we-Glazecalc: 福島長石 (i-Fukushima feldspar), i-whiting{{en: whiting}} (石灰石, i-calcium carbonate), i-kaolin ne-silica (珪石). Sebenzisa ukuhlaziywa komphakeli wakho lapho unakho: yengeze ekhasini [Lokokwakha](/material), izinombolo ziyashintsha, kodwa indlela ayishintshi.
- Ukushisa kungu-SK 8, {{2282 °F; 1250 °C}} ngokwegama. Sebenzisa i-cone iklasi yakho eshisa kuyo; [Ukushisa ngama-cone e-SK](/guides/firing#sk-cones) kuchaza izinombolo ze-SK.
- Ukuphrinta iphepha lokusebenza noma imephu yama-tile, phrinta leli khasi kusuka kusiphequluli. Ukulondoloza amaresiphi, ukuxuba namanothi kudinga i-akhawunti yamahhala.

> [!WARNING]
> Izifundo 1, 3 no-6 ngumsebenzi welabhorethri: ukukala okokwakha okomile nokushisa. Ngaphambi kokuba noma ubani akale okokwakha, funda [Imikhuba ebaluleke kakhulu](/guides/safe-mixing#habits). Uthuli lwe-glaze eyomile yiyo ingozi enkulu.

## Isifundo 1: Okokwakha nama-tile e-triaxial {#materials}

> [!WARNING]
> Kala okokwakha okomile kancane, uhlanze ngamanzi, futhi ugqoke i-respirator elingana kahle kuyilapho izimpuphu zomile. Funda kuqala [Ingozi enkulu: uthuli](/guides/safe-mixing#the-main-hazard) no-[Ama-respirator](/guides/safe-mixing#respirators).

### Izinhloso

- Qamba imisebenzi emithathu ku-glaze: umakhi wengilazi, i-flux{{en: flux}} nesiqinisi.
- Hlukanisa okokwakha okujwayelekile okungu-4 ngama-oxide akuletha.
- Hlela i-triaxial blend, ufunde ama-tile ayo njengemephu.

### Lungiselela

- Isikhwama ngasinye se-福島長石, i-whiting, i-kaolin ne-silica, noma okokwakha okunakho iklasi yakho.
- [Imephu ye-triaxial](#tile-maps) engezansi, eshicilelwe, nama-test tile angu-21 umfundi ngamunye kunxantathu ngamunye.
- Ama-tile ashisiwe e-triaxial yangaphambilini, uma unawo, ukuze adluliselwe.

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

Ukuhlaziywa kokokwakha ngakunye, okuvela emtatsheni we-Glazecalc, kunikeza ama-oxide ashiya ngemva kokushisa. I-loss on ignition (i-LOI) yilokho okusha kuphele.

Label: Okokwakha okune

| Okokwakha             | SiO₂ | Al₂O₃ |   K₂O | Na₂O |   CaO |   LOI | Umsebenzi wawo                         |
| --------------------- | ---: | ----: | ----: | ---: | ----: | ----: | -------------------------------------- |
| 福島長石 (i-feldspar) | 65.4 | 19.53 | 10.76 | 3.77 |  0.49 |  0.18 | I-flux, ene-alumina ne-silica yayo     |
| I-whiting (石灰石)    |    – |     – |     – |    – | 56.03 | 43.97 | I-flux (i-calcium)                     |
| EPK Kaolin            | 45.7 |  37.4 |  0.33 | 0.06 |  0.18 |  13.9 | Isiqinisi: umthombo omkhulu we-alumina |
| I-silica (珪石)       |  100 |     – |     – |    – |     – |     – | Umakhi wengilazi                       |

I-feldspar yodwa incibilika ku-SK 8 ibe yingilazi eqinile; i-whiting yodwa ayincibiliki; i-silica yodwa ayincibiliki. Uma zixutshiwe, zincibilika kangcono kakhulu kunanoma yiyiphi eyodwa yazo. I-triaxial blend ikhombisa ukuthi kuphi: imephu yama-tile engezansi ihlukanisa ezintathu ngezinyathelo ze-~20% (18 kwama-90), nge-kaolin engu-10% ku-tile ngayinye.

::shops

### Ku-Glazecalc

1. Vula okokwakha ngakunye ekhasini [Lokokwakha](/material), noma ikhasi lawo eliphubliki, njenge-[Fukushima Feldspar](/materials/fukushima-feldspar). Qaphela ukuthi iletha ama-oxide ani.
2. Yenza amaresiphi amathathu: 福島長石 90 ne-kaolin 10, i-whiting 90 ne-kaolin 10, ne-silica 90 ne-kaolin 10. Ekhasini [Lokuxuba kwama-test tile](/blends), khetha **I-triaxial (amaresiphi angu-3)** nemigqa engu-6: ama-tile angu-21, afana nemephu engezansi.
3. Khetha i-batch ye-tile ngayinye bese **Phrinta** lokho okufanele ukukale. Ngemva kokushisa, londoloza ama-tile owathandayo njengamaresiphi kusukela emidwebweni yama-tile.

## Isifundo 2: Ikhemistri okudingayo {#chemistry}

### Izinhloso

- Funda ifomula yamakhemikhali: SiO₂, CaO, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃.
- Bala isisindo se-mole kusukela ezisindweni ze-athomu.
- Guqula amagremu abe ama-mole, nama-mole abe amagremu.

### Lungiselela

- Ithebula leziqephu (periodic table) lomfundi ngamunye, namakhalkhuletha.
- Izisindo ze-athomu ezitafuleni elingezansi (amanani avamile e-IUPAC, njengoba i-Glazecalc iwasebenzisa).

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

Table: Izisindo ze-athomu ezisetshenziswe lapha (g/mol)
Label: Izisindo ze-athomu

| I-element   | Uphawu | Isisindo se-athomu |
| ----------- | ------ | -----------------: |
| I-oxygen    | O      |             15.999 |
| I-carbon    | C      |             12.011 |
| I-sodium    | Na     |              22.99 |
| I-aluminium | Al     |             26.982 |
| I-silicon   | Si     |             28.085 |
| I-potassium | K      |             39.098 |
| I-calcium   | Ca     |             40.078 |

Ifomula ibala ama-athomu. I-SiO₂ iyi-silicon eyodwa nama-oxygen amabili, ngakho i-mole eyodwa yayo ikala 28.085 + 2 × 15.999 = 60.083 g. I-Al₂O₃ ingu-2 × 26.982 + 3 × 15.999 = 101.961 g. I-CaO ingu-56.077 g, futhi i-whiting, i-CaCO₃, ingu-100.086 g; umtapo we-Glazecalc uyisondeza ku-100.08.

**I-mole** iwukubala, njengedazini: inani elifanayo lama-molecule noma ngabe yiwaphi. Ama-mole angamagremu ÷ isisindo se-mole:

- U-25 g we-silica angu-25 ÷ 60.083 = 0.416 mol ye-SiO₂.
- U-20 g we-whiting angu-20 ÷ 100.08 = 0.200 mol. Ku-kiln ilahlekelwa i-carbon dioxide yayo (CO₂, 44.009 g/mol) futhi ishiya 0.200 mol ye-CaO, ekala 0.200 × 56.077 = 11.2 g. Lokho kulahlekile yi-LOI yayo: 43.97%.

### Ku-Glazecalc

1. Vula [i-Whiting](/materials/whiting) ne-[Silica](/materials/silica): ikhasi ngalinye linikeza ifomula nezisindo zayo.
2. Hlola isisindo se-mole esisodwa ngesandla uqhathanise nekhasi.

## Isifundo 3: Ukufunda i-UMF {#reading-the-formula}

> [!WARNING]
> Ukufaka umbala ku-glaze kusho ukukala okokufaka umbala, okunye kudinga ukunakekelwa okungaphezu kokokwakha kwesisekelo. Funda kuqala [Okokwakha okudinga ukunakekelwa kakhulu](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Izinhloso

- Funda amakholomu amathathu e-UMF{{en: unity formula}} (ゼーゲル式, ifomula ka-Seger): ama-flux, isiqinisi, umakhi wengilazi.
- Thola i-glaze eshadini le-silica–alumina, uyazi ukuthi ishadi lingakutshela ini nokuthi alikwazi ukukutshela.
- Faka umbala ku-glaze eselungisiwe ngezinyathelo, bese uyarekhoda.

### Lungiselela

- I-UMF engezansi, ebhalwe inkulu.
- I-glaze ecwebile eselungisiwe, nokokufaka umbala okufana ne-red iron oxide (弁柄).

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

I-glaze yesifundo 4, 福島長石 45, i-whiting 20, i-kaolin 10 ne-silica 25, ine-UMF elandelayo (izibalo ze-Glazecalc, ezisondeziwe):

Table: I-UMF emakholomu ayo amathathu
Label: I-UMF

| Ama-flux (RO, R₂O) | Isiqinisi (R₂O₃) | Umakhi wengilazi (RO₂) |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| K₂O 0.18           | Al₂O₃ 0.43       | SiO₂ 3.45              |
| Na₂O 0.10          |                  |                        |
| CaO 0.72           |                  |                        |

- Ama-flux ahlangana abe ngu-1: lowo ngumthetho wefomula. Inombolo ngayinye enye ingama-mole kuma-mole ngamunye ama-flux.
- I-silica ku-alumina ingu-3.45 ÷ 0.43, cishe u-8: i-glaze ejwayelekile ecwebezelayo.
- I-calcium iyingxenye enkulu ye-flux, njengama-glaze amaningi ekalishi aseJapan (石灰釉).

Ishadi elinesilica ngokuvundlile ne-alumina ngokuphakeme libeka i-glaze ngayinye njengephoyinti. Izivivinyo zika-Seger neshadi lika-Stull lango-1912 zinjalo, futhi i-釉性状図 ibeka kulo ama-tile ashiswe yiklasi uqobo. Ikhasi leresiphi le-Glazecalc lidweba ishadi le-Stull, futhi le glaze ihlala endaweni yalo ecwebezelayo. Izindawo zika-Stull zivela kuma-flux akhe nokushisa kwakhe: umhlahlandlela, hhayi isibikezelo. [Igridi yama-tile](#tile-maps) engezansi ivumela iklasi idwebe ishadi layo ekushiseni kwayo.

::stull-chart

Ukufaka umbala ku-glaze eselungisiwe ongayazi iresiphi yayo, i-Glazecalc ayikwazi ukubala ifomula yayo. Esikhundleni salokho, engeza okokufaka umbala ngaphezu kwe-100: u-100 g we-glaze eyomile ngo-1, 2 nango-3 g we-iron oxide, i-tile eyodwa ngayinye, eshiswe ku-oxidation nange-reduction.

### Ku-Glazecalc

1. Ekhasini [leresiphi](/recipe), faka iresiphi engenhla, ufunde **I-UMF** ne-**Ishadi le-Stull**.
2. Shintsha i-silica kusuka ku-25 kuya ku-35, ubuke iphoyinti lihambela ngakwesokudla.
3. Kusisekelo onayo iresiphi yaso, yenza okokufaka umbala kube isithasiselo bese usebenzisa i-line blend ekhasini [Lokuxuba kwama-test tile](/blends) ukuyenyusa ngezinyathelo.

## Isifundo 4: Kusuka eresiphini kuya ku-UMF {#recipe-to-formula}

### Izinhloso

- Bala i-UMF kusukela eresiphini ngesandla.
- Yihlole ngokumelene nokubala kwe-Glazecalc uqobo.

### Lungiselela

- Iphepha lokusebenza 4, ukuhlaziywa okune kwesifundo 1, namakhalkhuletha.

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

Iresiphi: 福島長石 45, i-whiting 20, i-kaolin ye-EPK 10, i-silica 25. Thatha inani ngalinye njengamagremu. Kokokwakha ngakunye, amagremu × iphesenti le-oxide ÷ 100 ÷ isisindo sayo se-mole kunikeza ama-mole ayo. Ithebula linikeza ama-oxide aphambili; i-Glazecalc ibala namanye amancane (i-iron ne-titanium ye-kaolin, i-magnesium ye-feldspar).

| 福島長石  |       45 | 0.0514 | 0.0274 | 0.0039 | 0.0862 | 0.4898 |

Ama-flux ahlangana abe ngu-0.0518 + 0.0275 + 0.2041 + 0.0014 (i-magnesia) = 0.2847 mol. Hlukanisa isamba ngasinye ngayo:

- I-K₂O 0.0518 ÷ 0.2847 = 0.18; i-Na₂O 0.10; i-CaO 0.72.
- I-Al₂O₃ 0.1229 ÷ 0.2847 = 0.43.
- I-SiO₂ 0.9820 ÷ 0.2847 = 3.45.

Leyo yifomula yesifundo 3.

3. Qhathanisa umugqa ngamunye nephepha lakho lokusebenza. Bese ushintsha inani elilodwa, ubale kuphela ikholomu elishintshayo.

## Isifundo 5: Kusuka ku-UMF kuya eresiphini {#formula-to-recipe}

### Izinhloso

- Khetha okokwakha kwefomula ehlosiwe, ubale ukuthi ungakanani ngakunye.
- Sebenzisa i-Xazulula uye kokuhlosiwe, uthi isondela kangakanani.

### Lungiselela

- Okuhlosiwe okungezansi, iphepha lokusebenza 5, namakhalkhuletha; i-Glazecalc nge-akhawunti.

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

Okuhlosiwe: 0.2 K₂O, 0.1 Na₂O, 0.7 CaO, 0.4 Al₂O₃ no-4.0 SiO₂. Thatha okokwakha ngalolu hlelo, ngakunye ku-oxide okungayiletha kuphela:

1. **I-feldspar, ye-potash ne-soda.** Iyunithi eyodwa ye-福島長石 (536.8 g, isisindo esiphethe i-mole eyodwa yama-flux) iletha ama-mole angu-0.94 e-K₂O ne-Na₂O. Okuhlosiwe kudinga u-0.3, ngakho 0.3 ÷ 0.94 = 0.319 weyunithi: 171.4 g. Iphinde ilethe 0.015 CaO, 0.328 Al₂O₃ no-1.865 SiO₂.
2. **I-whiting, ye-calcium esele.** 0.7 − 0.015 = 0.685 mol × 100.08 = 68.6 g.
3. **I-kaolin, ye-alumina esele.** 0.4 − 0.328 = 0.072 mol. I-kaolin ye-EPK iphethe i-mole eyodwa ye-Al₂O₃ ku-272.6 g, ngakho 19.6 g, futhi iphinde ilethe 0.149 SiO₂.
4. **I-silica, yokusele.** 4.0 − 1.865 − 0.149 = 1.986 mol × 60.083 = 119.3 g.

I-batch ingu-379.0 g. Njengamaphesenti: 福島長石 45.2, i-whiting 18.1, i-kaolin 5.2, i-silica 31.5. I-feldspar inquma isilinganiso se-potash ku-soda, ngakho iresiphi inikeza 0.195 K₂O no-0.104 Na₂O: eduze nokuhlosiwe, hhayi ngqo kukho.

### Ku-Glazecalc

1. Vula [Xazulula uye kokuhlosiwe](/solve), khetha **I-UMF (ama-mole, ama-flux ahlangana abe ngu-1)**, bese uthayipha okuhlosiwe.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-**Okokwakha**, yinike 福島長石, i-whiting, i-kaolin ye-EPK ne-silica, bese ukhetha **Xazulula**.
3. Inikeza 福島長石 44.9, i-whiting 18.2, i-kaolin 5.6 ne-silica 31.3, nethebula lokuthi i-oxide ngayinye isondela kangakanani. Qhathanisa nokubala kwakho ngesandla: i-solver ibala wonke amancane, futhi ithola ukulingana okuhle kakhulu okuvunyelwa yilokhu okokwakha okune.
4. **Vula njengeresiphi entsha**, bese uyihlola ekhasini leresiphi.

## Isifundo 6: Ubumba namaphutha e-glaze {#body-and-defects}

> [!WARNING]
> I-glaze eshisiwe ingakhipha izinsimbi ekudleni nasezintweni zokuphuza, futhi ingabukeka iphelele ngenkathi yenza kanjalo. Ama-glaze aphethe i-barium eningi noma ane-copper, nanoma yiyiphi i-glaze enomthofu (lead, Pb), angakhipha izinsimbi. Kuphela isivivinyo selabhorethri sokuphuma kwezinsimbi{{en: leach test}} sesitsha esishisiwe esibonisa ukuthi sikhipha ini; akukho kubala okungakwenza lokho. Funda [Ukuphepha kokudla](/guides/glazing-basics#food-safety) no-[Okokwakha okudinga ukunakekelwa kakhulu](/guides/safe-mixing#materials).

### Izinhloso

- Linganisa ukuncipha nokumuncwa kwamanzi kobumba.
- Qamba amaphutha avamile, izimbangela zawo ezivamile nesixazululo sokuqala.
- Yazi ukuthi yimaphi amaphutha enza izitsha zingaphephi.

### Lungiselela

- Imivimbo yokuhlola yobumba lwakho, ephawulwe ibe ngu-100 mm ukusuka komunye nomunye kusamanzi, eshisiwe nengashiswanga.
- Ama-tile anamaphutha, uma unawo: i-crazing, i-crawling, i-pinholing, ukugeleza.

### Isibonelo esixazululiwe

- **Ukuncipha**: (ubude obuphawuliwe − ubude obushisiwe) ÷ ubude obuphawuliwe × 100. Amaphawu angu-100 mm ukusuka komunye nomunye alinganisa u-88 mm ngemva kokushisa: ukuncipha okungu-12%.
- **Ukumuncwa kwamanzi**: (isisindo ngemva kokucwilisa − isisindo esomile) ÷ isisindo esomile × 100. Umvimbo oshisiwe wa-50.0 g osinda u-50.6 g ngemva kokucwiliswa: 1.2%.
