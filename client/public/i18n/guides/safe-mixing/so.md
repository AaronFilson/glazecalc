## Khatarta ugu weyn: boodhka {#the-main-hazard}

Khatarta muddada dheer ee ugu weyn ee shaqada glaze-ka waa boodhka, gaar ahaan **boodhka silica ee la neefsado** (respirable crystalline silica): qaybo quartz ama cristobalite ah (qaababka kiristaalka ee silica) oo aad u yar oo gaari kara gudaha qoto dheer ee sambabada. Silica kiristaalka ahi waxay noqon kartaa qiyaastii 10 ilaa 60% miisaanka engegan ee glaze, dhoobooyinka badankoodna (marka laga reebo kaolin) iyo feldspar badan ayaa sidoo kale wata quartz yar-yar oo jilicsan. Hay'adda Caafimaadka iyo Badbaadada ee UK (Health and Safety Executive, HSE) waxay sheegtaa in boodhkani "aad u yar yahay oo aan lagu arki karin iftiinka caadiga ah", sidaas darteed qol u eg mid nadiif ah caddayn uma aha in hawadiisu nadiif tahay.

Haddii la neefsado muddo dheer, wuxuu keeni karaa **silicosis** (cudur sambabada), cudur sambabada ah oo HSE ku tilmaanto "khatar ah oo aan dib loo celin karin", kana dhalin kara "naafo joogto ah iyo dhimasho hore". Wuxuu kale oo keeni karaa **COPD** (cudurka sambabada ee xannibaadda joogtada ah: bronchitis muddo dheer ah iyo emphysema), oo sigaar cabiddu ka sii dartu. IARC, hay'adda kansarka ee Ururka Caafimaadka Adduunka, waxay silica kiristaalka ah ee la neefsado gelisaa Kooxda 1, "kansar ku keena dadka".

Silica isuma beddesho qiiq kiln-ka gudihiisa, glaze qoyanna khatar neefsasho ma aha haddii aan la buufin mooyee: ciirta buufintu waxay wadataa silica waxayna u engegtaa boodh (eeg Buufinta, hoos). Waqtiyada laga taxaddaro waa marka walaxdu engegan tahay: furitaanka bacaha, miisaamidda, budada biyaha lagu walaaqayo, iyo nadiifinta dhibcaha, buufinta dhinac u baxday iyo qashinka qoyan marka ay dib ugu engegaan boodh.

### Xadka soo-gaadhista

Xeerarka goobta shaqadu waxay dejiyaan **xad soo-gaadhis** (exposure limit): inta ugu badan ee silica ee shaqaaluhu neefsan karo, celcelis ahaan maalin 8 saacadood ah, milligram (mg) ama microgram (µg, kun-meelood meel milligram) mitir kasta oo kuul ah oo hawo ah.

## Caadooyinka ugu muhiimsan {#habits}

Ilaalinta badankeedu waxay ka timaadaa caadooyin, ma aha qalab. Talada HSE ee warshadaha dhoobada waxay ku bilaabantaa ka fogaanshaha budada, kadibna hoos ayay uga sii socotaa.

### Ka fogow budada meesha aad kari karto

- **Iibso glaze diyaar ahaan loo qasay** (qoyan) marka ay shaqadaada ku habboon tahay. Talada ugu horreysa ee HSE waa "Iibso glaze diyaar u ah isticmaalka, marka ay suurtagal tahay", ama qaab xabbad-xabbad ah ama dareere qaro weyn (slurry) ah.
- **Dooro habab ammaan badan.** HSE waxay soo jeedisaa "glaze-yo ka kooban silica kiristaal ah oo ka yar". Digitalfire waxay soo jeedisaa **frit** (dhalo warshad lagu sameeyay oo budo loo shiiday, oo aad uga milma wax yar marka loo eego walxaha ceeriin) halkii barium carbonate ceeriin ah, iyo **stain** (midab dhoobo oo warshad lagu sameeyay) halkii manganese, cobalt ama nickel badan oo madow loogu talagalay.

### Marka aad budo isticmaalayso

- **Si tartiib ah u gaf.** HSE waxay leedahay "Si tartiib ah uga shub/uga gaf bacaha" oo ka fogow weelal waaweyn oo qoto dheer, si boodhku u yaro meesha uu ku dhacayo.
- **Daboolka ku hay** mar kasta oo weel aan la isticmaalayn, oo ay ku jirto inta u dhaxaysa miisaanka iyo baaldiga.
- **Ka shaqee meel aan dabayl lahayn.** Ka miisaan meel ka fog albaabbada, daaqadaha iyo waddooyinka lagu socdo, dadka kalena qolka ka saar.
- **Budada ku dar biyaha.** Waxaan soo jeedinaynaa in biyaha baaldiga marka hore lagu shubo oo budada si tartiib ah loogu daro, halkii biyo lagu shubi lahaa raso engegan. Tani waa dhaqan caadi ah, ma aha xeer ka yimid ilaha.
- **Ku walaaq walaaqe,** ha ku walaaqin gacmahaaga, oo xiro gacmo-gashi.
- **Qoyaan ku hay.** Nadiifi daadashada qoyan "si aysan u engegin", jajabka qoyanna ku hay weelal hawo-xidhan.
- **Ka taxaddar bacaha.** Kiishashku dibadda ayay boodh ku sitaan. Kuwa madhan si tartiib ah u duub oo bac geli ka hor intaadan tuurin.
- **Budooyinka ku kaydi** iyagoo dabooban, dhulka ka sarreeya oo ka fog meesha dadku maraan.

### Kala horreyn macquul ah

1. Iibso glaze qoyan oo diyaar ahaan loo qasay, oo iska dhaaf budada.
2. Budada ku qas hoos dabool hawo-wareejin leh (hood), ama bannaanka hawo deggan oo ka fog dadka kale; labada xaaladoodba xiro respirator.
3. Gudaha oo aan dabool lahayn: respirator, gafid tartiib ah, daboollo xiran, iyo nadiifin qoyan isla markiiba.

Kala horreyntan waa soo jeedin naga timid, oo ku dhisan mabda'a HSE ee ah in budada marka hore laga fogaado. Miisaamidda iyo isku-darka tallaabo-tallaabo, eeg [Sida loo sameeyo glaze](/guides/making-a-glaze).

## Respirator-ada {#respirators}

**Respirator** (maaskarada neefsashada) waa maaskaro loo ogolaaday inay ka shaandhayso qaybaha yaryar hawada aad neefsanayso. Waxay shaqeysaa oo keliya marka ay wejigaaga si fiican ugu xidhanto, waxayna taageertaa caadooyinka wanaagsan, ma beddesho.

### Heerarka gobol ahaan

Table: Heerarka respirator-ada qaybaha gobol ahaan (u-dhigmid qiyaas ah ee boodhka glaze-ka)
Label: Heerarka respirator-ada

| Gobol                     | Heer maalinle | Heer sare | Raadi                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Maraykanka                | N95           | P100      | Oggolaanshaha NIOSH ee maaskarada ama baakadeeda                                     |
| UK iyo Midowga Yurub      | FFP2          | FFP3      | EN 149 maaskarada la tuuro; nus-maaskaro EN 140 oo leh shaandhooyin EN 143 P2 ama P3 |
| Australia iyo New Zealand | P2            | P3        | AS/NZS 1716                                                                          |

Heerka sare wuxuu shaandheeyaa qayb ka weyn oo qaybaha yaryar ah. Iluhu way ku kala duwan yihiin waxa P3 Australia uu shaandheeyo (ugu yaraan 99%, ama ugu yaraan 99.95%), sidaas darteed iibso heerka la oggolaaday ee laga iibiyo gobolkaaga. **Nus-maaskaro** (half mask) dib loo isticmaali karo (oo dabooshaa sanka iyo afka, shaandhooyin la beddeli karo leh) oo lagu rakibay shaandhooyin P100 (P3 meel ka baxsan Maraykanka) waa doorasho caadi ah oo istuudiyaha.

### Ku habboonaanta iyo xidhitaanka

Maaskaro hawo ka soo dusisaa waxay boodh ka soo gelisaa geesaheeda, heer kasta oo ay tahayba. HSE waxay ka digtaa in timo-gadhka gaaban iyo gadhku "ka dhigaan wax aan suurtagal ahayn in si wanaagsan loo xidho".

- **Xiir wax yar ka hor intaadan shaqo bilaabin.** Tilmaamuhu way ku kala duwan yihiin inta ay tahay inuu "xiiran" dhowaa. Warshad UK ah, oo soo xiganaysa HSE, waxay leedahay in la xiiray 8 saacadood gudahood ka hor bilaabidda shaqada. Heerka imtixaanka ku-habboonaanta ee Australia iyo New Zealand wuxuu weydiiyaa xiirid ka hor imtixaanka ku-habboonaanta, "doorbid ahaan 12 saacadood gudahood". Xiiridda wax yar ka hor intaadan shaqeyn waxay buuxisaa labadaba.
- **Haddii aad gadh haysato,** **respirator koronto ku shaqeeya** (PAPR: koofiyad ama helmet leh marawaxad keenta hawo la shaandheeyay) oo aan wejiga ku dhegin uma baahna in wejiga lagu xidho.
- **Cabbirka sax.** Ku-habboonaan xumo waa sababta ugu weyn ee hawo-dusidda. Goobaha shaqadu waxay isticmaalaan **imtixaan ku-habboonaan** (fit test), hubin in maaskaro gaar ah ay ku xidhanto weji gaar ah. Guriga, waxaan soo jeedinaynaa inaad raacdo tilmaamaha warshadda ee hubinta xidhitaanka mar kasta oo aad xidhato.

> [!NOTE]
> Fikradayada, ku-habboonaantu way ka muhiimsan tahay heerka: N95, FFP2 ama P2 si fiican ugu habboon inta badan way ka fiicnaan doontaa P100 dabacsan.

### Goorta la xidho

HSE waxay leedahay in respirator "caadi ahaan aan loo baahnayn" meesha miisaamidda iyo isku-darku ka dhacaan god hawo-wareejin leh, laakiin "loo baahan karo dayactirka iyo nadiifinta". Istuudiyaha guriga badankoodu ma laha god, sidaas darteed waxaan soo jeedinaynaa inaad xidhato mid (ugu yaraan N95, FFP2 ama P2; P100, FFP3 ama P3 ayaa ka fiican) mar kasta oo aad:

- furayso bacaha ama budo shubayso;
- miisaamayso budooyin oo biyo ku walaaqayso;
- xoqayso, sandpaper ku xoqayso ama shiidayso glaze engegan ama bisque;
- samaynayso nadiifin engegan kasta, ama madhinayso vacuum;
- buufinayso glaze (god buufin gudihiis: eeg hoos).

### Waxa aan respirator ahayn

- Maaskarada qalliinka, maaskarada maradda ah iyo maaskarada "boodhka dhibta" ee aan lahayn calaamad NIOSH, EN 149 ama AS/NZS 1716. Kuma tiirsanaan lahayn boodhka glaze-ka.
- Respirator boodh "kaama ilaalin doono gaasaska, uumiga" (OSHA), sidaas darteed waxba kama tarto qiiqa kiln-ka.
- Daryeel kaaga: OSHA waxay xustaa in respirator wasakh ah ama si khaldan loo isticmaalay "noqon karo khatar". Ku kaydi isagoo nadiif ah, kana fog dharka shaqada ee boodhka leh.

## Nadiifinta, dharka iyo gacmaha {#cleaning}

Boodhka dhulka, miisaska iyo dharka ku degaa wuxuu dib ugu noqdaa hawada mar kasta oo la qaso. Ka dib ka-shaqeynta tartiibka ah, nadiifinta qoyani waa caadada ugu faa'iidada badan ee aad yeelan karto.

### Ku nadiifi qoyaan

- **Ku masax oo ku tir biyo.** Jaamacadda Utah waxay ku talisaa in dhulka iyo meelaha shaqada maalin kasta lagu masaxo masaxaad qoyan, HSE-na waxay goobaha shaqada ka codsataa inay maalin kasta dhammaadkeeda dhaqaan. Guriga, waxaan soo jeedinaynaa in la tiro oo la masaxo kadib kulan kasta oo isku-dar ah.
- **Weligaa ha ku xaaqin engeg, ha ku burushin engeg, hana isticmaalin hawo cadaadis leh.** OSHA way mamnuucdaa kuwan meesha ay soo-gaadhista ku kordhiyaan, HSE-na waxay leedahay weligaa ha ka burushin ama ha ka afuufin boodhka maqaarka ama dharka.
- **Vacuum ku isticmaal oo keliya mishiin loo dhisay boodhka yar ee khatarta ah:** **HEPA** Maraykanka (shaandho qabata ugu yaraan 99.97% qaybaha 0.3 µm ballaadhkoodu yahay), ama ugu yaraan **boodh Class M** UK iyo Midowga Yurub. Meelaha kale, raadi qiimeyn u dhiganta. Vacuum-yada caadiga ah ee bakhaarka iyo guriga sidan looma qiimeeyo, mana isticmaali lahayn, maadaama boodhka yari ka gudbi karo oo ka bixi karo hawo-saarka. Xiro respirator-kaaga marka aad mid madhinayso.

### Dharka, cuntada iyo gacmaha

- **Xiro dhar istuudiyaha oo keliya ah** oo ka samaysan maro si dhow loo tolay oo aan boodh xajin, oo si joogto ah u dhaq (Utah waxay soo jeedisaa toddobaad kasta), dhar kale la'aantood. HSE waxay xustaa in dharka boodhka lihi ku sii soo-gaadhsiiyo kadib marka shaqada la dhammeeyo, sidaas darteed waxaan soo jeedinaynaa inaad beddesho ka hor intaadan gelin qaybta kale ee guriga.
- **Istuudiyaha laguma cuno, laguma cabbo, sigaarna laguma cabbo.** Sida Digitalfire u dhigto, "qofku waa inuusan barium carbonate ku khaldin sonkorta miiska marka uu kafee diyaarinayo". Weel kasta calaamadee.
- **Gacmahaaga dhaq** ka hor cunidda, cabbidda, sigaar cabidda ama isticmaalka musqusha, oo nadiifi ciddiyahaaga hoostooda. Isticmaal biyo diirran iyo saabuun jilicsan halkii nadiifiye xoqa.

Ilaalinta boodhka iyo walxaha ka fog carruurta iyo xayawaanka guriga, eeg [Ha sumayn qoyskaaga](/guides/home-safety).

## Hawo-saarka goobta iyo buufinta {#extraction-and-spraying}

### Hawo-saarka goobta

**Hawo-saarka goobta** (local exhaust ventilation, LEV) wuxuu boodhka ku qabtaa meesha lagu sameeyo: dabool ama god shaqada dushiisa ah, oo leh marawaxad hawada ka jiidda wejigaaga oo dhismaha ka saarta. Miisaamiddu waa meesha boodhka badankiisu ka dhasho, sidaas darteed HSE waxay sharraxdaa goob miisaamid oo xidhan oo hore yar oo furan leh, Digitalfire-na dabool dalool-daloolan oo miisaanka ka sarreeya. Haddii aad mid leedahay, talada goobta shaqada ee HSE ayaa khuseysa:

- ka fogee albaabbada, daaqadaha iyo waddooyinka lagu socdo, sababtoo ah dabayshu way jebisaa;
- soo geli **hawo beddel ah** (make-up air) oo nadiif ah (hawo cusub oo beddesha waxa marawaxaddu saarto, iyada oo loo marinayo daloolka albaabka, daaqad ama god hawo-gelin);
- hawo-saarka u dir bannaanka, kana fogee albaabbada, daaqadaha iyo meelaha hawadu ka gasho;
- hubi inuu shidan yahay oo shaqeynayo ka hor intaadan bilaabin.

Ma jiro il bixisa tirooyinka socodka hawada ee dabool hiwaayadeed. Dabool la'aan, ku tiirso ka-shaqeyn tartiib ah, respirator iyo nadiifin qoyan.

### Buufinta

HSE waxay ka digtaa in buufintu "soo saari karto ciiro ka kooban RCS" (boodhka silica ee la neefsado), iyo in "ciirada glaze-ku si dhakhso ah u engegto oo boodh isu beddesho". Il kasta oo aan helnay waxay leedahay ku buufi oo keliya god buufin oo hawo-wareejin leh gudihiisa.

- HSE waxay rabtaa god hawo laga saaro iyo, "meesha ay suurtagal tahay, god buufin oo biyo gadaal ka leh", kaas oo buufinta dhinac u baxda biyo ku qabta.
- Isticmaal miis wareega god-ka gudihiisa, xiro gacmo-gashi nitrile iyo respirator, oo nadiifi god-ka iyo shaandhadiisa ka hor inta buufinta dhinac u baxday aysan engegin.
- Ka hor glaze-ka, burushka ku tirtiridda boodhka bisque waxay sidoo kale sameysaa boodh; waxaan soo jeedinaynaa isbuunyo qoyan halkeeda.

> [!WARNING]
> Haddii aadan haysan god buufin oo hawo laga saaro, ha buufin. Burush ku mari ama dhex geli halkeeda: HSE waxay burush-marinta iyo dhex-gelinta u magacawdaa siyaabo hawada silica uga yareeya.

## Hawo-saarka kiln-ka {#kiln}

### Waxa gubiddu soo deyso

Gubid kasta waxay soo deysaa qiiq, xitaa dhoobo caadi ah. Biyo iyo carbon dioxide ayaa ka baxa, iyo "carbon monoxide yar" oo ka yimaada gubashada walxaha noolaha ah, Dave Finkelnburg, oo wax ku qoraya Ceramics Monthly, wuxuu suurtagalnimada carbon monoxide ugu yeeraa "khatarta ugu degdegsan". **Carbon monoxide** (CO) waa gaas jidhka ka gaajeeya oksijiinta, kuna keena daal, madax-xanuun iyo dawakhaad. Sulfur ku jira dhoobada ball iyo stoneware ayaa gubidda bisque siisa urta ukunta qudhunta, qiiqa sulfur-kuna wuxuu gubaa indhaha, sanka iyo sambabada.

Walxaha glaze-ku wax kale ayay ku daraan. Oksaydh bireed qaarkood ayaa sameeya qiiq, "gaar ahaan copper, zinc, manganese", kaas oo keeni kara **qandhada qiiqa birta** (metal fume fever), xanuun leh qarqaryo iyo qandho. Rasaas (lead, Pb) waa "gaar ahaan khatar", sida ay Jaamacadda Utah sheegto, sababtoo ah waxay "uumi isu beddeshaa heer kul oo si qaraabo ah u hooseeya": marka la gubo heerkulka stoneware, "rasaastu uumi ayay isu beddeli doontaa". Fluorspar, cryolite iyo lepidolite waxay soo daayaan iskudhisyo fluorine. Qofna ma daabicin cabbirro laga qaaday kiln-yada hiwaayadda, sidaas darteed waxaan soo jeedinaynaa in gubid kasta hawada laga saaro.

### Hawo-saarka hoos-u-jiidka (downdraft vent)

**Downdraft vent** waa qalab marawaxad yar oo kiln-ka lagu rakibo. Wuxuu hawo yar ka soo jiidaa qolka gubidda, wuxuu ku qasaa hawada qolka wuxuuna tuubo ku mariyaa bannaanka, isagoo qiiqa qabta ka hor inta uusan qolka gaadhin. Warshadda kiln-ka Skutt waxay leedahay dhammaan warshadaha kiln-ku waxay ku taliyaan mid.

- **Wuxuu u baahan yahay hawo beddel ah.** EnviroVent 2 ee Skutt wuxuu bannaanka u diraa 140 cubic feet (qiyaastii 4 m³) hawo ah daqiiqad kasta, intaas oo kale waa inay dib u soo gashaa.
- **Wuxuu saaraa "qiiqa khatarta ah oo keliya, ma aha kulaylka"** (Skutt), waana inuu u shaqeeyaa si ka madax-bannaan kululaynta iyo qaboojinta dhismaha.
- **Tuubo ku mari bannaanka,** kana fogee daaqadaha, albaabbada iyo meelaha hawadu ka gasho. Finkelnburg wuxuu ku taliyaa marawaxad squirrel-cage (centrifugal) ah, ma aha marawaxad baal leh (propeller).
- Xeerarka deegaanka qaarkood waxay u baahan yihiin hawo-saare kiln, sidaas darteed hubi kuwaaga.

**Hood vent** (dabool kiln-ka ka sarreeya) waa beddelka. Wuxuu soo jiidaa qiiqa ka baxsada iyo hawo badan oo qolka ah, laakiin waxba kama soo jiido kiln-ka gudihiisa, wuxuuna sidoo kale u baahan yahay hawo beddel ah.

### Meesha kiln-ka la dhigo

- Waxaan soo jeedinaynaa garaash, aqal yar ama qol loo gooni yeelay kiln-ka halkii meel lagu noolyahay, oo ah mid aad ka xidhi karto carruurta iyo xayawaanka guriga, sida Skutt ku taliso.
- **Masaafada bannaan.** Iluhu way kala duwan yihiin: Skutt waxay qeexdaa ugu yaraan 18 in (46 cm) gidaar kasta ama walax **gubanaysa** (combustible: wax kasta oo guban kara), halka CIRMA, shirkad caymis oo Connecticut ah, ay soo jeediso 3 ft (0.9 m) wax kasta oo guban kara. Raac buugga tilmaamaha kiln-kaaga; haddii aadan haysan, ka ilaali 18 in gidaarada iyo 3 ft wax kasta oo guban kara.
- **Dhulka.** Isticmaal dhul aan guban karin sida shub (concrete) ama tile dhoobo ah, weligaa ha isticmaalin alwaax, roog ama vinyl. Alwaaxda, warqadda iyo dareerayaasha milmay (solvents) ka fogee kiln-ka.
- **Korontada.** CIRMA waxay ku talisaa wareeg koronto oo gaar ah, ha isticmaalin fidiyeyaal koronto ama ilaaliyeyaal korontada kediska ah (surge protectors), ka fur fiilada inta u dhaxaysa gubidyada, iyo dab-demiye 25 ft (7.6 m) gudahood ah.
- **Kulaylka qolka.** Skutt waxay leedahay qolka ka hooseysii {{38 °C; 100 °F}}, haddii kale kontaroolahu wuxuu dami karaa kiln-ka. Maadaama hawo-saaruhu aanu kulayl saarin, qol yar wuxuu u baahan karaa marawaxad sidoo kale. Ha u oggolaan madaxyada biyo-buufiyeyaasha (sprinkler) iyo dareemayaasha kulaylka inay si toos ah uga sarreeyaan kiln-ka.
- **Digniinta carbon monoxide.** Ma aanan helin xeer u baahan mid kiln koronto ah, laakiin walxaha noolaha ah ee gubanayaa waxay sameeyaan CO, sidaas darteed digniin ku taal qolka kiln-ka iyo qolalka ku xiga waa tallaabo caqli leh oo jaban (eeg [Ha sumayn qoyskaaga](/guides/home-safety)).

Raku wuxuu sameeyaa qiiq iyo CO badan; Utah waxay ku talisaa in bannaanka lagu sameeyo, meel ka fog meelaha hawadu ka gasho iyo daaqadaha. Gubidda lafteeda, eeg [Gubidda kiln fudud](/guides/firing).

## Walxaha u baahan taxaddarka ugu badan {#materials}

Digitalfire waxay ku dartaa in stain-yadu guud ahaan aad uga ammaan badan yihiin oksaydhyada ceeriinka ah, iyo in "glaze-yada aan isku dheelitirnayn ee sii daaya biraha sunta ah laga samayn karo walax kasta": ammaanka cuntadu wuxuu ku xiran yahay habka oo dhan, ma aha hal walax.

## Gacmo-gashiga, maqaarka iyo indhaha {#gloves-and-eyes}

### Maqaarka

- **Xiro gacmo-gashi** glaze qoyan ama engegan; Utah waxay leedahay "waa in la xirtaa". HSE waxay nitrile u magacawdaa buufinta. Ma jirto il isbarbardhigtay walxaha gacmo-gashiga, sidaas darteed waxaan soo jeedinaynaa nitrile dhammaan shaqada glaze-ka.
- **Kareemada difaacu ma aha gacmo-gashi.** HSE: "'Kareemada difaacu' ma aha 'gacmo-gashi dareere ah'".
- Boodhka silica iyo shaqada qoyani labaduba waxay keenaan **dermatitis taabasho** (contact dermatitis: maqaar casaan ah, xanuun leh, dillaacsan), maqaarka dillaacsanina wuxuu u oggolaadaa walxaha inay galaan. Ku dhaq biyo diirran iyo saabuun jilicsan, oo isticmaal kareem shaqada kadib.
- **Dabool nabarrada.** Iskudhisyada barium, tusaale ahaan, looma malaynayo inay dhex maraan maqaar caafimaad qaba, laakiin way mari karaan maqaar jeexan.

### Indhaha

- Xiro muraayadaha badbaadada marka aad shiidayso alaab glaze leh. Waxaan sidoo kale soo jeedinaynaa marka aad budooyin shubayso.
- **Fiirinta kiln kulul gudihiisa.** Kiln-yadu waxay soo deyaan shucaac **infrared** ah (kulayl aadan arki karin) kaas oo "keeni kara cataract (biyo-indhood)". Heerarka mugdiga ee lagu taliyay ee muraayadaha IR loo qiimeeyay way kala duwan yihiin: 1.7 ilaa 3.0 (Jaamacadda Utah) iyo 2.0 ilaa 5.0 (Jaamacadda Calgary). Heerka mugdiga 3 wuxuu ku dhex jiraa labadaba; door mid ka mugdi badan haddii dhibco ka muuqdaan aragtidaada.

## Akhrinta warqadda xogta badbaadada {#safety-data-sheets}

**Warqadda xogta badbaadada** (safety data sheet, SDS; kuwa duugga ahi waxay leeyihiin MSDS) waa dukumentiga alaab-qeybiyaha ee ku saabsan waxa alaabtu ka kooban tahay, khataraheeda iyo sida loola dhaqmo. Walax ceeriin kasta iyo glaze ganacsi kasta waa inay leeyihiin mid; ka raadi bogga alaabta ama weydii alaab-qeybiyaha. Waxay leedahay 16 qaybood, labana ayaa ugu muhiimsan:

- **Qaybta 2, Aqoonsiga khatarta:** kala-saaridda, **eray calaamad** sida "Danger" (Khatar), **sawir-calaamado** (pictograms: astaamo digniin oo yaryar) iyo bayaannada khatarta.
- **Qaybta 8, Xakamaynta soo-gaadhista iyo ilaalinta shakhsiga:** xadka soo-gaadhista, **xakamaynta injineerinka** (engineering controls) ee loo baahan yahay (hawo-wareejin iyo hawo-saar) iyo qalabka ilaalinta ee la xidho.

Sawir-calaamadaha iyo bayaannada khatarta waxay ka yimaadaan **GHS** (Globally Harmonized System), nidaamka caalamiga ah ee calaamadeynta kiimikooyinka. Borax, tusaale ahaan, wuxuu wataa sawir-calaamadda khatarta caafimaadka GHS08, erayga calaamadda "Danger" iyo bayaanka khatarta H360FD, "waxyeeleyn kara bacriminta; waxyeeleyn kara ilmaha aan dhalan". Inta aan ognahay, SDS-yada UK, Midowga Yurub, Australia iyo New Zealand waxay isticmaalaan isla 16-ka qaybood.

- **Hubi qaabka silica.** HSE waxay ka digtaa in silica kiristaalka ah "si khaldan loogu calaamadeyn karo 'amorphous silica'".
- **"Not classified" (aan la kala saarin) macnaheedu ma aha "waxyeello la'aan".** Glaze dhammaystiran oo aan sawir-calaamado lahayn wali wuxuu sameeyaa boodh silica marka uu engegan yahay.

> [!NOTE]
> Hagahan wuxuu isku soo ururiyaa talooyin la daabacay, laakiin ma beddeli karo warqadda xogta badbaadada ee walaxda, buugga tilmaamaha kiln-kaaga ama xeerarka deegaankaaga. Meesha ay ka duwan yihiin waxa aad halkan ku akhrinayso, raac iyaga.

## Tilmaamaha rasmiga ah gobol ahaan {#official-guidance}

Kuwani waa ilaha asalka ah ee ugu mudan in la akhriyo. Badankood waxaa loo qoray goobaha shaqada, laakiin taladoodu si fiican ayay ugu gudubtaa istuudiyaha guriga.

- **Maraykanka:** [heerka silica](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1053) ee OSHA (29 CFR 1910.1053) iyo [taladeeda dadka doorta inay xidhaan respirator](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.134AppD); [digniinta silicosis](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/92-107) ee NIOSH.
- **Boqortooyada Midowday:** warqadaha COSHH essentials ee HSE ee dhoobada (COSHH waa xeerarka Control of Substances Hazardous to Health, xakamaynta walxaha khatarta ku ah caafimaadka), gaar ahaan [CR1 ee diyaarinta glaze-yada iyo midabada](https://www.hse.gov.uk/PUBNS/guidance/cr1.pdf) iyo [CR5 ee buufintooda](https://www.hse.gov.uk/PUBNS/guidance/cr5.pdf), iyo [aasaaska imtixaanka ku-habboonaanta](https://www.hse.gov.uk/respiratory-protective-equipment/fit-testing-basics.htm) ee HSE.
- **Midowga Yurub:** Directive (EU) 2017/2398 ayaa dejisa xadka silica ee waajibka ah; xadka dal kasta oo xubin ah, iyo hay'addiisa qaranka ee badbaadada shaqada, waxay ku jiraan Xadka soo-gaadhista, kor. ECHA, Hay'adda Kiimikooyinka Yurub, waxay daabacdaa kala-saaridyo sida kuwa borates.
- **Australia:** [boggaga silica kiristaalka ah iyo silicosis](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/crystalline-silica-and-silicosis/research-lower-workplace-exposure-standard-respirable-crystalline-silica-0) ee Safe Work Australia.
- **New Zealand:** WorkSafe New Zealand ayaa dejisa heerarka soo-gaadhista goobta shaqada; [taariikhda heerka silica](https://mbie.govt.nz/building-and-energy/building/building-and-construction-consultations/work-with-engineered-stone-and-materials-containing-crystalline-silica/annex-iii) ee MBIE ayaa bixisa taariikhaha.
- **Kiln-yada:** [hagaha qolka kiln-ka](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0889/3726/7497/files/Designing-A-Kiln-Room-1.pdf) ee Skutt, [warqadda Orton iyo CCSA ee qiiqa kiln-ka](https://assets.noviams.com/novi-file-uploads/ccsa/Product_Safety/Fumes.pdf), iyo ka sarreeya dhammaan buugga tilmaamaha kiln-kaaga.
