## Ingozi enkulu: uthuli {#the-main-hazard}

Ingozi enkulu yesikhathi eside emsebenzini we-glaze{{en: glaze}} wuthuli, ikakhulukazi **uthuli lwe-silica eyikristalu oluhogelwayo** (respirable crystalline silica): izinhlayiya ze-quartz noma ze-cristobalite (izinhlobo ze-silica eyikristalu) ezincane ngokwanele ukuthi zingene ekujuleni kwamaphaphu. I-silica eyikristalu ingaba cishe u-10 kuya ku-60% wesisindo esomile se-glaze, futhi iningi lobumba (ngaphandle kwe-kaolin) nama-feldspar amaningi nawo aphethe i-quartz ecolekileyo. I-Health and Safety Executive (HSE) yase-UK ithi lolu thuli "lucoleke kakhulu futhi alubonakali ekukhanyeni okujwayelekile", ngakho igumbi elibukeka lihlanzekile aliyona ubufakazi bokuthi umoya uhlanzekile.

Uma luhogelwa isikhathi eside, lungabangela **i-silicosis**, isifo samaphaphu i-HSE esichaza ngokuthi "sibi futhi asilapheki" futhi singa "bangela ukukhubazeka unomphela nokufa ngaphambi kwesikhathi". Lungabangela futhi **i-COPD** (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: i-bronchitis yesikhathi eside ne-emphysema), ebhebhetheka ngokubhema. I-IARC, inhlangano yomdlavuza ye-World Health Organization, ibeka i-silica eyikristalu ehogelwayo ku-Group 1, "ibangela umdlavuza ebantwini".

I-silica ayiphenduki intuthu ku-kiln, futhi i-glaze emanzi ayiyona ingozi yokuhogela ngaphandle uma ifuthwa: inkungu yokufutha iphethe i-silica futhi yome ibe uthuli (bheka Ukufutha, ngezansi). Izikhathi zokuqaphela yilapho okokwakha komile: uma uvula izikhwama, ukala, uxuba impuphu emanzini, nalapho uhlanza amaconsi, i-glaze efuthwe yeqa, nodaka sekuphinde komile kwaba uthuli.

### Imingcele yokuchayeka

Imithetho yasemsebenzini ibeka **umngcele wokuchayeka**: i-silica eningi kakhulu isisebenzi esingayihogela, ilinganiselwe phezu kosuku lwamahora angu-8, ngama-milligram (mg) noma ama-microgram (µg, inkulungwane yengxenye ye-milligram) ngemitha ngayinye eyi-cubic yomoya.

## Imikhuba ebaluleke kakhulu {#habits}

Ukuvikeleka okuningi kuvela emikhubeni, hhayi ezintweni zokusebenza. Iseluleko se-HSE sezindawo zokubumba siqala ngokugwema impuphu bese siya phansi kusukela lapho.

### Gwema impuphu lapho ungakwazi

- **Thenga i-glaze{{en: glaze}} esixutshiwe kakade** uma kufanelana nomsebenzi wakho. Isiphakamiso sokuqala se-HSE sithi "Thenga i-glaze esilungele ukusetshenziswa, uma kungenzeka", noma esesimweni sezinhlamvu noma sodaka (slurry).
- **Khetha amaresiphi aphephile.** I-HSE iphakamisa "ama-glaze anokuncane kwe-silica eyikristalu". I-Digitalfire iphakamisa **i-frit** (ingilazi eyenziwe efektri yagaywa yaba impuphu, encibilika kancane kakhulu kunokokwakha okungaphekiwe) esikhundleni se-barium carbonate engaphekiwe, ne-**stain** (umbala wobumba owenziwe efektri) esikhundleni senani elikhulu le-manganese, i-cobalt noma i-nickel kwemibala emnyama.

### Uma usebenzisa impuphu

- **Kha kancane.** I-HSE ithi "Thela/kha kancane ezikhwameni" futhi ugweme iziqukathi ezinkulu nezijulile, ukuze uthuli lungawi kude kangako.
- **Gcina izivalo zivaliwe** noma nini lapho isiqukathi singasetshenziswa, kuhlanganise naphakathi kwesikali nebhakede.
- **Sebenza kude nomoya ovunguzayo.** Kala kude neminyango, amafasitela nezindlela zokuhamba, futhi ugcine abanye abantu bengekho egumbini.
- **Faka impuphu emanzini.** Siphakamisa ukuthi uqale ufake amanzi ebhakedeni bese wengeza impuphu kancane, kunokuthela amanzi enqwabeni eyomile. Lokhu kungumkhuba ojwayelekile, hhayi umthetho ovela emithonjeni.
- **Xuba ngento yokuxuba,** hhayi ngezandla zakho, futhi ugqoke amagilavu.
- **Kugcine kumanzi.** Hlanza okuchitheke kumanzi "ukuze kungomi", futhi ugcine izinsalela ezimanzi eziqukathini ezivalwe ngokuqinile umoya ungangeni.
- **Qaphela izikhwama.** Amasaka aphethe uthuli ngaphandle. Songa ezingenalutho kancane bese uzifaka esikhwameni ngaphambi kokuzilahla.
- **Gcina izimpuphu** zimbozwe, zingekho phansi, futhi kude nalapho abantu behamba khona.

### Uhlelo olunengqondo lokukhetha

1. Thenga i-glaze emanzi esixutshiwe kakade, ungasebenzisi impuphu.
2. Xuba usebenzisa impuphu ngaphansi kwe-hood enomoya, noma ngaphandle emoyeni ozolile kude nabanye abantu; gqoka i-respirator kunoma yisiphi isimo.
3. Ngaphakathi ngaphandle kwe-hood: i-respirator, ukukha kancane, izivalo zivaliwe, nokuhlanza okumanzi masinyane.

Lolu hlelo luyisiphakamiso sethu, olwakhelwe phezu kwesimiso se-HSE sokuqala ngokugwema impuphu. Ukuze ukale futhi uxube isinyathelo ngesinyathelo, bheka [Indlela yokwenza i-glaze](/guides/making-a-glaze).

## Ama-respirator {#respirators}

**I-respirator** iyimaski eqinisekisiwe ukuthi ihlunga izinhlayiya ezincane emoyeni owuphefumulayo. Isebenza kuphela uma ivala ngokuqinile ebusweni bakho, futhi isekela imikhuba emihle kunokuba ithathe indawo yayo.

### Amazinga ngokwesifunda

| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Izinga eliphezulu lihlunga ingxenye enkulu yezinhlayiya ezincane. Imithombo ayivumelani ngokuthi i-P3 yase-Australia ihlunga malini (okungenani u-99%, noma okungenani u-99.95%), ngakho thenga izinga eliqinisekisiwe elithengiswa esifundeni sakho. **Imaski uhhafu** esebenza kaningi (evala ikhala nomlomo, enezihlungi ezishintshwayo) efakwe izihlungi ze-P100 (P3 ngaphandle kwe-US) iwukukhetha okujwayelekile ezitudiyo.

### Ukulingana nokuvala

Imaski evuzayo ingenisa uthuli emaphethelweni ayo, noma ngabe iyiliphi izinga layo. I-HSE iyaxwayisa ukuthi isiqhumane nentshebe "kwenza kungenzeki ukuthola ukuvala okuhle".

- **Shefa ngaphambi nje kokuthi usebenze.** Iziqondiso ziyehluka ngokuthi "ukushefa kahle" kumele kube kwakamuva kangakanani. Umkhiqizi wase-UK, ecaphuna i-HSE, uthi kushefwe phakathi kwamahora angu-8 ngaphambi kokuqala umsebenzi. Izinga lokuhlola ukulingana lase-Australia ne-New Zealand licela ukushefa ngaphambi kokuhlolwa kokulingana, "okungcono phakathi kwamahora angu-12". Ukushefa ngaphambi nje kokusebenza kufeza kokubili.
- **Uma ugcina intshebe,** **i-respirator enamandla kagesi** exegayo (PAPR: i-hood noma isigqoko esinefeni elethe umoya ohlungiwe) ayidingi ukuvala ebusweni.
- **Thola usayizi ofanele.** Ukungalingani kahle kuyimbangela enkulu yokuvuza. Ezindaweni zokusebenza basebenzisa **ukuhlolwa kokulingana** (fit test), ukuhlola ukuthi imaski ethile ivala kahle ebusweni obuthile. Ekhaya, siphakamisa ukuthi ulandele imiyalelo yomkhiqizi yokuhlola ukuvala njalo uma uyigqoka.

> [!NOTE]
> Ngokombono wethu, ukulingana kubaluleke ngaphezu kwezinga: i-N95, i-FFP2 noma i-P2 elingana kahle ivamise ukwenza kangcono kune-P100 exegayo.

### Nini ukuyigqoka

I-HSE ithi i-respirator "ngokuvamile ayidingeki" lapho ukukala nokuxuba kwenzeka endaweni evalekile enomoya, kodwa "ingadingeka ekunakekeleni imishini nasekuhlanzeni". Iningi lezitudiyo zasekhaya alinayo indawo evalekile, ngakho siphakamisa ukuthi uyigqoke (okungenani i-N95, i-FFP2 noma i-P2; i-P100, i-FFP3 noma i-P3 ingcono) noma nini lapho:

- uvula izikhwama noma uthela impuphu;
- ukala izimpuphu futhi uzixuba emanzini;
- ukhuhla, ugudla noma ugaya i-glaze eyomile noma i-bisque;
- wenza noma yikuphi ukuhlanza okomile, noma uthulula i-vacuum;
- ufutha i-glaze (ebhokisini lokufutha: bheka ngezansi).

### Okungeyona i-respirator

- Amaski okuhlinza, amaski endwangu namaski "othuli oluyinhlupheko" (nuisance dust) angenalo uphawu lwe-NIOSH, i-EN 149 noma i-AS/NZS 1716. Thina besingeke sithembele kuwo othulini lwe-glaze.
- I-respirator yothuli "ngeke ikuvikele kumagesi, nomhwamuko" (OSHA), ngakho ayenzi lutho entuthwini ye-kiln.
- Nakekela eyakho: i-OSHA iphawula ukuthi i-respirator engcolile noma esetshenziswe kabi "ingaba yingozi". Yigcine ihlanzekile, kude nezingubo zokusebenza ezinothuli.

## Ukuhlanza, izingubo nezandla {#cleaning}

Uthuli oluhlala phansi, emabhentshini nasezingutsheni lubuyela emoyeni njalo uma luphazanyiswa. Ngemva kokuphatha ngobumnene, ukuhlanza ngamanzi kungumkhuba owusizo kakhulu ongaba nawo.

### Hlanza ngamanzi

- **Mopha futhi usule ngamanzi.** I-University of Utah incoma ukumopha phansi nezindawo zokusebenza ngamanzi nsuku zonke, futhi i-HSE icela izindawo zokusebenza ukuthi zigeze ekupheleni kosuku ngalunye. Ekhaya, siphakamisa ukusula nokumopha ngemva kwesikhathi ngasinye sokuxuba.
- **Ungalokothi ushanele ngokomile, uxubhe ngokomile noma usebenzise umoya ocindezelwe.** I-OSHA iyakwenqabela lokhu lapho kwandisa ukuchayeka, futhi i-HSE ithi ungalokothi uxubhe noma uphephethe uthuli esikhumbeni noma ezingutsheni.
- **Sebenzisa i-vacuum kuphela uma kungumshini owakhelwe uthuli olucolekile oluyingozi:** **i-HEPA** e-US (isihlungi esibamba okungenani u-99.97% wezinhlayiya ezingu-0.3 µm ububanzi), noma okungenani **i-dust Class M** e-UK nase-EU. Kwezinye izindawo, bheka izinga elilinganayo. Ama-vacuum ajwayelekile asemsebenzini nasekhaya awalinganiswanga ngale ndlela, futhi thina besingeke siwasebenzise, ngoba uthuli olucolekile lungadlula luphume ngomoya awukhiphayo. Gqoka i-respirator yakho uma uthulula eyodwa.

### Izingubo, ukudla nezandla

- **Gqoka izingubo zesitudiyo kuphela** zendwangu elukwe ngokuqinile engabambi uthuli, futhi uzigeze kaningi (i-Utah iphakamisa masonto onke), zihlukaniswe nezinye izingubo zokuwasha. I-HSE iphawula ukuthi izingubo ezinothuli ziqhubeka zikuchaya ngemva kokuba umsebenzi usuphelile, ngakho siphakamisa ukuthi ushintshe ngaphambi kokungena kuyo yonke enye indlu.
- **Akukho ukudla, ukuphuza noma ukubhema esitudiyo.** Njengoba i-Digitalfire isho, "umuntu akufanele adide i-barium carbonate noshukela wetafula lapho enza ikhofi". Faka ilebula kuso sonke isiqukathi.
- **Geza izandla zakho** ngaphambi kokudla, ukuphuza, ukubhema noma ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese, futhi uhlanze ngaphansi kwezinzipho zakho. Sebenzisa amanzi afudumele nensipho emnene kunokusebenzisa isihlanzi esihuzukisayo.

Ukuze ugcine uthuli nokokwakha kude nezingane nezilwane ezifuywayo, bheka [Ungawadliseli ushevu umndeni wakho](/guides/home-safety).

## Ukukhipha umoya endaweni nokufutha {#extraction-and-spraying}

### Ukukhipha umoya endaweni

**Ukukhipha umoya endaweni** (local exhaust ventilation, LEV) kubamba uthuli lapho lwenziwa khona: i-hood noma indawo evalekile phezu komsebenzi, enefeni edonsa umoya kude nobuso bakho iwukhiphele ngaphandle kwesakhiwo. Ukukala yilapho kwenziwa khona uthuli oluningi, ngakho i-HSE ichaza isiteshi sokukala esivalekile esinengxenye encane evulekile ngaphambili, ne-Digitalfire i-hood enemigodi engenhla kwesikali. Uma unayo, iseluleko se-HSE sasemsebenzini siyasebenza:

- yigcine kude neminyango, amafasitela nezindlela zokuhamba, ngoba umoya ovunguzayo uyayehlula;
- ngenisa **umoya obuyiselayo** ohlanzekile (umoya omusha othatha indawo yalowo ofeni iwukhiphayo, ngesikhala somnyango, ifasitela noma imbobo yokungenisa);
- thumela umoya okhishwayo ngaphandle, kude neminyango, amafasitela nezimbobo zokungenisa umoya;
- hlola ukuthi ivuliwe futhi iyasebenza ngaphambi kokuqala.

Awukho umthombo onikeza izibalo zokugeleza komoya kwe-hood yabazilibazisayo. Ngaphandle kwe-hood, thembela ekuphatheni ngobumnene, ku-respirator nasekuhlanzeni okumanzi.

### Ukufutha

I-HSE iyaxwayisa ukuthi ukufutha "kungaveza inkungu equkethe i-RCS" (uthuli lwe-silica eyikristalu oluhogelwayo), nokuthi "inkungu ye-glaze{{en: glaze}} yoma ngokushesha iphenduke uthuli". Yonke imithombo esiyitholile ithi fusa kuphela ngaphakathi kwebhokisi lokufutha elinomoya.

- I-HSE ifuna indawo evalekile ekhipha umoya futhi, "lapho kungenzeka khona, ibhokisi elinamanzi ngemuva", elibamba i-glaze efuthwe yeqa emanzini.
- Sebenzisa itafula elijikayo ebhokisini, gqoka amagilavu e-nitrile ne-respirator, futhi uhlanze ibhokisi nesihlungi salo ngaphambi kokuba i-glaze efuthwe yeqa yome.
- Ukususa uthuli ku-bisque ngebhulashi ngaphambi kokufaka i-glaze nakho kwenza uthuli; siphakamisa isipontshi esimanzi esikhundleni salokho.

> [!WARNING]
> Uma ungenalo ibhokisi lokufutha elikhipha umoya, ungafuthi. Faka ngebhulashi noma ngokucwilisa esikhundleni salokho: i-HSE ibala ukufaka ngebhulashi nokucwilisa njengezindlela zokwenza i-silica encane esemoyeni.

## Ukukhipha umoya ku-kiln {#kiln}

### Lokho ukushisa okukukhiphayo

Konke ukushisa kukhipha intuthu, ngisho nobumba obungenalutho. Kuphuma amanzi ne-carbon dioxide, kanye "ne-carbon monoxide encane" evela ezintweni eziphilayo ezivuthayo, futhi uDave Finkelnburg, ebhalela i-Ceramics Monthly, ubiza ingozi engaba khona ye-carbon monoxide ngokuthi "ingozi esheshayo kakhulu". **I-carbon monoxide** (CO) igesi elambisa umzimba umoya-mpilo (oxygen), ibangele ukukhathala, ikhanda elibuhlungu nesiyezi. Isibabule (sulfur) esobumbeni be-ball nobe-stoneware sinika ukushisa kwe-bisque iphunga laso leqanda elibolile, futhi intuthu yesibabule ishisa amehlo, ikhala namaphaphu.

Okokwakha kwe-glaze{{en: glaze}} kwengeza okunye. Amanye ama-oxide ensimbi akha intuthu, "ikakhulukazi i-copper, i-zinc, i-manganese", engabangela **imfiva yentuthu yensimbi** (metal fume fever), isifo esinamakhaza nemfiva. Umthofu (lead, Pb) "uyingozi ikakhulukazi", kusho i-University of Utah, ngoba "uba umhwamuko ekushiseni okuphansi uma kuqhathaniswa": uma ushiswa emazingeni okushisa e-stoneware, "umthofu uzoba umhwamuko". I-fluorspar, i-cryolite ne-lepidolite zikhipha izinhlanganisela ze-fluorine. Akekho oshicilele izilinganiso ezivela kuma-kiln abazilibazisayo, ngakho siphakamisa ukukhipha umoya kukho konke ukushisa.

### Ama-vent adonsela phansi

**I-downdraft vent** iyunithi encane enefeni efakwe ku-kiln. Idonsa umoya omncane egumbini lokushisa, iwuhlambulule ngomoya wegumbi bese iwudlulisela ngaphandle ngepayipi, ibamba intuthu ngaphambi kokuthi ifike egumbini. Umkhiqizi wama-kiln u-Skutt uthi bonke abakhiqizi bama-kiln bancoma eyodwa.

- **Idinga umoya obuyiselayo.** I-EnviroVent 2 ka-Skutt ithumela amafidi ayi-cubic angu-140 (cishe u-4 m³) omoya ngaphandle umzuzu ngamunye, futhi kumele kubuye ngaphakathi okulingana nalokho.
- **Isusa "intuthu eyingozi kuphela, hhayi ukushisa"** (Skutt), futhi kufanele isebenze ngokuzimela ohlelweni lwesakhiwo lokufudumeza nokupholisa.
- **Yidlulisele ngaphandle ngepayipi,** kude namafasitela, iminyango nezimbobo zokungenisa umoya. UFinkelnburg uncoma ifeni ye-squirrel-cage (centrifugal), hhayi ifeni ye-propeller.
- Eminye imithetho yendawo idinga i-vent ye-kiln, ngakho hlola eyakho.

**I-hood vent** (isembozo esingenhla kwe-kiln) iyenye indlela. Idonsa intuthu ephunyukayo kanye nomoya omningi wegumbi, kodwa ayidonsi lutho ngaphakathi kwe-kiln, futhi nayo idinga umoya obuyiselayo.

### Lapho ungabeka khona i-kiln

- Siphakamisa igaraji, ishede noma igumbi elibekelwe i-kiln kunendawo yokuhlala, futhi ongalivalela izingane nezilwane ezifuywayo, njengoba u-Skutt eluleka.
- **Izikhala.** Imithombo iyehluka: u-Skutt ucacisa okungenani amayintshi angu-18 (46 cm) kunoma yiluphi udonga noma okokwakha **okungasha** (combustible: noma yini engasha), kanti i-CIRMA, umshuwalense wase-Connecticut, iphakamisa amafidi angu-3 (0.9 m) kunoma yini engasha. Landela incwadi yemiyalelo ye-kiln yakho; uma ungenayo, gcina amayintshi angu-18 ezindongeni namafidi angu-3 kunoma yini engasha.
- **Phansi.** Sebenzisa phansi okungashi njengokhonkolo noma ithayela lobumba, hhayi neze ukhuni, ukhaphethi noma i-vinyl. Gcina amapulangwe, iphepha nama-solvent kude ne-kiln.
- **Ugesi.** I-CIRMA incoma isekhethi eyodwa ebekelwe i-kiln kuphela, kungabikho izintambo zokwelula noma izivikeli zokugxuma kukagesi (surge protectors), ukukhipha ipulaki phakathi kokushisa okukodwa nokulandelayo, nesicishamlilo esingaphakathi kwamafidi angu-25 (7.6 m).
- **Ukushisa kwegumbi.** U-Skutt uthi gcina igumbi lingaphansi kuka-{{38 °C; 100 °F}}, kungenjalo isilawuli singacisha i-kiln. Njengoba i-vent ingasusi ukushisa, igumbi elincane lingadinga nefeni. Gwema ukuthi amakhanda okufafaza amanzi (sprinklers) nezinzwa zokushisa zibe ngqo ngenhla kwe-kiln.
- **I-alamu ye-carbon monoxide.** Asitholanga mthetho odinga eyodwa ku-kiln kagesi, kodwa izinto eziphilayo ezivuthayo zenza i-CO, ngakho i-alamu egumbini le-kiln nasemagumbini aseduze nalo iyisinyathelo esinengqondo nesishibhile (bheka [Ungawadliseli ushevu umndeni wakho](/guides/home-safety)).

I-raku yenza intuthu eningi kakhulu ne-CO; i-Utah iyeluleka ukuthi yenziwe ngaphandle, kude nezimbobo zokungenisa umoya namafasitela. Ngokushisa uqobo, bheka [Ukushisa ku-kiln elula](/guides/firing).

## Okokwakha okudinga ukunakekelwa kakhulu {#materials}

Iningi lokokwakha kwe-glaze{{en: glaze}}, kuhlanganise i-silica, ubumba, ama-feldspar nama-frit, ikakhulukazi kuyingozi yothuli, imikhuba engenhla eyilawulayo. Uhlu olufushane lunoshevu ngokwalo, ngokuhogela, ngokugwinya noma ngokuthinta isikhumba. I-Digitalfire yengeza ukuthi ama-stain ngokuvamile aphephe kakhulu kunama-oxide angaphekiwe, nokuthi "ama-glaze angalinganiseli akhipha izinsimbi ezinoshevu angenziwa nganoma yikuphi okokwakha": ukuphepha ekudleni kuncike kuyo yonke iresiphi, hhayi esithakweni esisodwa.

## Amagilavu, isikhumba namehlo {#gloves-and-eyes}

### Isikhumba

- **Gqoka amagilavu** nge-glaze{{en: glaze}} emanzi noma eyomile; i-Utah ithi "kumele agqokwe". I-HSE ibala i-nitrile ekufutheni. Awukho umthombo oqhathanise izinto amagilavu enziwe ngazo, ngakho siphakamisa i-nitrile kuwo wonke umsebenzi we-glaze.
- **Ama-barrier cream awawona amagilavu.** I-HSE: "'Ama-barrier cream' awawona 'amagilavu awuketshezi'".
- Uthuli lwe-silica nomsebenzi omanzi kokubili kubangela **i-contact dermatitis** (isikhumba esibomvu, esibuhlungu, esiqhekekile), futhi isikhumba esiqhekekile singenisa okokwakha. Geza ngamanzi afudumele nensipho emnene, futhi usebenzise ukhilimu wangemva komsebenzi.
- **Vala amanxeba.** Izinhlanganisela ze-barium, isibonelo, azicatshangwa ukuthi zidlula esikhumbeni esingalimele, kodwa zingadlula esikhumbeni esilimele.

### Amehlo

- Gqoka izibuko zokuphepha lapho ugaya izingcezu ezine-glaze. Siphakamisa futhi ukuzigqoka lapho uthela izimpuphu.
- **Ukubheka ngaphakathi kwe-kiln eshisayo.** Ama-kiln akhipha imisebe ye-**infrared** (ukushisa ongakuboni) "engabangela i-cataract". Amazinga ombala (shade) anconyiwe ezibuko ezilinganiselwe i-IR ayehluka: 1.7 kuya ku-3.0 (University of Utah) no-2.0 kuya ku-5.0 (University of Calgary). I-shade 3 ingena kukho kokubili; khetha emnyama kakhulu uma kuvela amabala ekuboneni kwakho.

## Ukufunda i-safety data sheet {#safety-data-sheets}

**I-safety data sheet** (SDS; ezindala zithi MSDS) iyincwadi yomhlinzeki ngokuqukethwe ngumkhiqizo, izingozi zawo nokuwuphatha. Konke okokwakha okungaphekiwe nayo yonke i-glaze{{en: glaze}} yokuthengisa kufanele kube nayo; bheka ekhasini lomkhiqizo noma ubuze umhlinzeki. Inezigaba ezingu-16, futhi ezimbili zibaluleke kakhulu:

- **Isigaba 2, Ukuhlonza izingozi:** ukuhlukaniswa, **igama lesixwayiso** njengo-"Danger", **ama-pictogram** (izimpawu ezincane zesixwayiso) nezitatimende zengozi.
- **Isigaba 8, Ukulawulwa kokuchayeka nokuzivikela:** imingcele yokuchayeka, **izilawuli zobunjiniyela** ezidingekayo (umoya nokukhipha umoya) nezinto zokuzivikela okufanele uzigqoke.

Ama-pictogram nezitatimende zengozi zivela ku-**GHS** (Globally Harmonized System), uhlelo lwamazwe ngamazwe lokufaka amalebula kumakhemikhali. I-borax, isibonelo, iphethe i-pictogram yengozi yempilo i-GHS08, igama lesixwayiso elithi "Danger" nesitatimende sengozi H360FD, "kungalimaza ukuzala; kungalimaza ingane engakazalwa". Ngokwazi kwethu, ama-SDS ase-UK, e-EU, e-Australia nase-New Zealand asebenzisa izigaba ezifanayo ezingu-16.

- **Hlola uhlobo lwe-silica.** I-HSE iyaxwayisa ukuthi i-silica eyikristalu "ingafakwa ilebula ngephutha ngokuthi 'amorphous silica'".
- **"Not classified" akusho "ayinangozi".** I-glaze eseqediwe engenawo ama-pictogram isenza uthuli lwe-silica uma yomile.

> [!NOTE]
> Lo mhlahlandlela uqoqa iseluleko esishicilelwe, kodwa awuthathi indawo ye-safety data sheet yokokwakha, incwadi yemiyalelo ye-kiln yakho noma imithetho yendawo yakini. Lapho zehluka kulokho okufunda lapha, zilandele.
