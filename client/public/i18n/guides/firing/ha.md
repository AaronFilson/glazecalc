## Cone: abin da suke aunawa {#cones}

> [!NOTE]
> Ku fara bin littafin jagora na kiln ɗinku. Wannan jagora ya tattara abin da masu yin cone da masu yin kiln suke wallafawa game da kiln na lantarki masu sauƙi; ba ya maye gurbin umarnin samfurin kiln ɗinku, kuma inda biyun suka saɓa, littafin jagora na kiln shi ne ake bi.

**Pyrometric cone** (mazugi) wani ɗan ƙaramin mazugi ne siriri mai siffar dala, wanda aka yi da garin yumɓu na ma'auni ɗaya. Idan ya sami isasshen zafi, yakan yi laushi ya lanƙwasa ta hanyar da take maimaituwa iri ɗaya. Kuna ajiye cone a cikin kiln (murhun gasa tukwane) kusa da tukwane, sannan ku karanta yadda suka lanƙwasa.

Cone suna auna **heatwork** (aikin zafi), wato tasirin zafi da lokaci a haɗe. A kalaman Orton, babban mai yin cone a Amurka, "Cone ba zafi kaɗai suke aunawa ba." Cone da aka zafafa a hankali yakan lanƙwasa a zafin da ya fi ƙasa da na wanda aka zafafa da sauri, kuma glaze{{en: glaze}} ma haka yake. Saboda haka Orton yana bayar da zafin kowane cone a wani gudun hawan zafi da aka faɗa, a cikin {{180 °F; 100 °C}} na ƙarshe na gasar. Da zarar cone ya fara motsi, yakan lanƙwasa cikin ɗan ƙaramin tazara, "galibi ƙasa da 40° F" (22 °C).

### Lambobin cone

Lambobin cone suna farawa daga 022, mafi ƙarancin zafi, har zuwa 42. Sifiri a gaba yana nuna jerin masu ƙarancin zafi, kuma ana karanta shi "oh": cone 06 ("oh-six") bai kai cone 04 zafi ba, kuma cone 01 yana buƙatar zafi ƙasa da cone 1. Orton ya yi gargaɗin cewa yana da muhimmanci kada a haɗa jerin biyun. Tazarar tana da girma: cone 06 yana lanƙwasa a zafin da ya fi na cone 6 sanyi da sama da {{400 °F; 220 °C}}.

### Ma'anar "cone 6" a aikace

Jadawalin yana bayar da cone 6 mai tsayawa da kansa a matsayin {{2232 °F; 1222 °C}}, amma sai idan {{180 °F; 100 °C}} na ƙarshe sun hau a {{108 °F/h; 60 °C/h}}. Idan zafin ya hau a {{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}, cone ɗin dai yana lanƙwasa a {{2269 °F; 1243 °C}}. Kiln na gaske ma sun bambanta: Digitalfire ya ruwaito cewa "Yawancin kiln suna kaiwa cone 6 a 2200°F, ba 2232°F ba". Don haka "cone 6" yana nufin tukwane sun sami aikin zafin da yake lanƙwasa cone 6, ko me ma'aunin zafi ya karanta a lokacin.

## Gasa bisa zafi {#by-temperature}

Kiln mai **controller** (na'urar sarrafa gasa) yana gasawa zuwa zafin da kuka saita, wanda wata sanda mai auna zafi kusa da bango take karantawa. Masu sayar da kaya a Birtaniya, Turai, Australia da New Zealand sukan bayar da zafin gasa maimakon cone.

Zafi kaɗai ba ya faɗin yawan gasar da tukwane suka samu. Glaze, kamar cone, suna amsa **heatwork** (aikin zafi): ƙoli ɗaya yana bayar da sakamako mai zafi fiye bayan hawa a hankali ko tsayawa, kuma mai ƙarancin zafi bayan hawa da sauri. Jadawalin Orton yana nuna girmansa: cone 6 yana lanƙwasa a {{2165 °F; 1185 °C}} idan zafi yana hawa a {{27 °F/h; 15 °C/h}}, kuma a {{2269 °F; 1243 °C}} idan yana hawa a {{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}. Don haka zafin gasa yana nufin an kai wannan zafin a wani gudu da aka faɗa, tare da duk wani tsayawa da mai yin glaze ya bayar.

Idan girke-girke ya bayar da cone a maimakon haka, ku neme shi a jadawalin da ke sama, a layin gudun da kiln ɗinku yake hawa a ƙarshen gasar. Tsayawa ma yana ƙara aikin zafi: duba Tsayawa yana ƙara aikin zafi, a sama.

Sandar auna zafin tana karanta iskar da ke kusa da bango, ba tukwane ba. Idan gasa tana fitowa da zafi fiye ko ƙasa da yadda ya kamata, littafin jagora na controller ɗinku yana bayanin **offset** ɗinsa, wanda yake gyara abin da yake karantawa.

## Kafa rukunin cone {#cone-pack}

**Cone mai shaida** (witness cone) cone ne da aka kafa a kan shiryayye kusa da tukwane, inda yake samun zafi ɗaya da su. Orton ya faɗi aikinsa a fili: "Ma'aunin aikin zafi na gaskiya kaɗai shi ne daga babban cone ko mai tsayawa da kansa da aka ajiye a kan shiryayye kusa da tukwane." Kiln sitter yana kusa da bango, kuma sandar auna zafi ta controller ko ta pyrometer tana karanta zafin kusa da bango; cone mai shaida yana karanta inda tukwane suke.

### Cone masu tsayawa da kansu ko plaque

L&L, mai yin kiln a Amurka, yana ba da shawarar cone masu tsayawa da kansu ga rukunin "saboda su ne mafi sauƙin amfani kuma suna bayar da sakamako mafi daidaito": tsayinsu da karkatarsu suna cikin jikinsu.

Manyan cone suna buƙatar **plaque** (mariƙin da aka riga aka yi) ko **dunƙulen** yumɓu mai laushi, mai ramuka, wanda aka huda da fensir. Ku kafa kowanne inda daidai 2 in (51 mm) suke bayyane, yana karkata 8° daga tsaye zuwa **fuskar lanƙwasarsa**. Don gano wannan fuska, ku tsayar da cone a kan gindinsa a wuri mai lebur, ku riƙe shi ta ƙarshensa, sannan ku sake shi: zai faɗi zuwa fuskar lanƙwasarsa.

### Inda za a ajiye rukunan

1. Ku ajiye rukunin "can cikin kiln, amma kuma... ana iya ganinsa ta ramin leƙe" (Orton), kusa da tsakiyar kayan da aka loda kuma nesa da iska mai shigowa (L&L). Ba daidai a bakin ramin leƙe ba: ya kamata cone su ji zafin da tukwane suke ji.
2. Kafin ku rufe murfi, ku leƙa ta ramin leƙe ku tabbatar cewa kuna ganin cone ukun duka.
3. A kiln mai sitter, ku ajiye shiryayyen rukunin aƙalla 1 in (25 mm) sama ko ƙasa da bututun sitter, don kada shiryayye mai karkata ya toshe sitter.
4. A gasarku ta farko-farko, ku ajiye rukuni a shiryayyen sama, na tsakiya da na ƙasa. Gwajin daidaitawa na L&L ya fi haka: rukunai biyu na cone 07, 06 da 05 a kowane shiryayye, ɗaya a gefe ɗaya kuma a tsakiya.

Ku sa ran bambanci: Orton ya ce "Galibi, za ku ga aƙalla bambancin cone ɗaya daga sama zuwa ƙasan kiln", kuma wuraren da aka cika sosai suna gasuwa da ƙarancin zafi. Rukunan da ba ku iya gani yayin gasa suna bayar da bayani bayan gasar.

## Kiln sitter {#kiln-sitter}

**Kiln sitter** makunni ne na inji, a wajen kiln masu sauƙi da tsofaffi da yawa, wanda yake kashe kiln idan ƙaramin cone ya lanƙwasa. Wanda aka fi sani shi ne Dawson Kiln-Sitter. Yana sauƙaƙa gasa, amma abin sauƙi ne kawai, ba na'urar tsaro ba.

### Yadda yake aiki

Wani bututun porcelain yana shiga cikin kiln. A ƙarshensa, ƙaramin cone ko sanda yana kwance a kan mariƙan ƙarfe biyu, ƙarƙashin **sensing rod** (sandar ji) wadda take juyawa kamar allon wasan sama-da-ƙasa. A wajen kiln, ɗayan ƙarshen sandar yana riƙe da wani nauyi ta ƙaramar ƙugiya. Idan cone ya yi laushi ya lanƙwasa zuwa kusan 90°, sandar tana saukowa har ta saki nauyin, nauyin ya faɗi, kuma wutar da ke zuwa elements (wayoyin zafi) ta yanke.

Ƙananan cone da sanduna "suna bayar da gasa daidai da juna". Orton ya lura cewa masu farawa da yawa suna zaɓar sanduna, domin siffarsu mai kauri ɗaya tana sauƙaƙa ajiye su ta hanya ɗaya kowane lokaci. Ƙaramin cone yana siriri zuwa ƙarshe, don haka inda yake zaune yana da muhimmanci: idan siririn ɓangarensa yana ƙarƙashin sandar, kiln yana kashewa da ƙarancin zafi, idan kuma ɓangarensa mai kauri ne, da zafi fiye, bambanci "har ya kai 1/2 cone".

### Wane cone ake sakawa a sitter

Majiyoyi sun saɓa. Dawson, wanda ya yi sitter, ya ce a yi amfani da lamba ɗaya da cone na gasa da ke kan shiryayye, kuma a koma lamba ɗaya mai zafi fiye kawai idan cone na shiryayye bai lanƙwasa ba. L&L ya ce a yi amfani da lamba ɗaya mai zafi fiye da cone na shiryayye, domin sitter yana kusa da bango da elements, yana samun zafi fiye, kuma yakan kashe da wuri. Orton ya warware ƙa'idar: "Cone na shiryayye shi ne alƙalin ƙarshe na gasar, ba cone ko sandar Kiln-Sitter ba."

> [!NOTE]
> Inda muke ba da shawarar farawa: ku saka ƙaramin cone mai lamba ɗaya da burin zafinku a sitter, tare da rukunin cone a kan shiryayye. Idan cone na gasa na shiryayye bai lanƙwasa sosai ba lokacin da kiln ya kashe, a gasa ta gaba ku koma lamba ɗaya mai zafi fiye a sitter, ko ku sa ɓangaren cone mai kauri a ƙarƙashin sandar. Idan ya lanƙwasa fiye da kima, ku yi akasin haka.

### Kafa shi, da bincike kafin kowace gasa

Hanyar Dawson, wadda kuma take aiki a matsayin bincike kafin kowace gasa:

1. Ku kashe dukkan suwis. Ku tabbatar cewa sandar ji tana motsi ba tare da tsaiko ba, tana zaune a tsakiyar bututun, kuma ba ta lanƙwashe ko ta yi tsatsa ba. Idan tana da nauyin motsi, Dawson ya ce a sauya bututun gaba ɗaya.
2. Ku shafa siririn **kiln wash** na zafi mai yawa (shafen da yake hana abu da ya narke mannewa) a kan mariƙan cone da kuma sandar inda take taɓa cone. Kada ku taɓa shafa wash a kan cone ko bututun porcelain.
3. Ku loda shiryayyu da tukwane aƙalla 1 in (25 mm) sama ko ƙasa da bututun, don kada wani abu ya taɓa shi, ya faɗa masa ko ya toshe shi.
4. Ku ɗaga nauyin, ku danna ƙugiyar ƙasa a kan maɓallin sakin, sannan ku kwantar da cone ko sandar a kan mariƙan, jingine da matakin ƙarfe, kamar yadda littafin jagora na sitter ɗinku ya nuna, ta hanya ɗaya kowane lokaci. Ku sake dubawa kafin ku rufe murfi: "CONE KO SANDAR DA BA A AJIYE DAIDAI BA ZAI IYA JAWO GASA FIYE DA KIMA."
5. Ku saita limit timer ku tura plunger ciki har ya kulle. Ku riƙe kiln a daidaice, kuma ku bar wurin da ke ƙarƙashin nauyin a buɗe, don nauyin ya iya faɗuwa.

## Karanta cone {#reading-cones}

Ana karanta cone sau biyu: ta ramin leƙe yayin da kiln yake gasawa, da kuma a hannunku bayan ya huce.

### Yayin gasa

**Ramin leƙe** (peephole) ɗan ƙaramin rami ne ta bangon kiln, wanda ake rufewa da toshe mai cirewa. Kada ku taɓa leƙawa ba tare da kariyar ido ba. Orton ya ce "Ya kamata a yi amfani da gilashin walda don guje wa yiwuwar raunin ido. Ba a ba da shawarar gilashin rana ba." Gilashin walda na shade 3, masu kariya daga infrared, su ne aka fi ba da shawara; ku yi amfani da masu duhu fiye idan kuna ganin tabo bayan haka.

- Idan **cone mai jagora** ya fara lanƙwasawa, ƙarshe ya kusa: ku kalla sosai daga nan.
- Da zarar cone ya fara, yana ɗaukar minti 15 zuwa 25 kafin ya lanƙwasa (sabuwar ƙasidar Orton ta ce 15 zuwa 22), a hankali da farko, sannan da sauri da zarar ya wuce rabi, a ƙarfe 3.
- **Cone na gasa** ya gama idan ƙarshensa ya yi daidai da gindinsa.

A kiln na hannu, ramin leƙe ne kaɗai alamarku a lokacin gasa, shi ya sa dole rukunin ya zauna inda kuke iya ganinsa.

### Bayan gasa

Tsakanin ƙarfe 4 da taɓa shiryayye bambancin ƙarami ne, "galibi digiri 1 ko 2".

## Gasa zuwa cone na SK {#sk-cones}

Cone na SK su ne pyrometric cone da aka yi bisa ma'aunin Japan, JIS R 8101, kuma dillalai kamar Yuko Trading (有恒商事) suke sayarwa a Japan. Kamar kowane cone, cone na SK yana auna aikin zafi, ba zafi kaɗai ba: yana yin laushi ya lanƙwasa idan tukwane sun sami isasshen zafi, kuma kuna karanta yadda ya lanƙwasa. Cone masu shaida, karanta yadda cone ya lanƙwasa, da tsayawa suna aiki a gare shi kamar kowane cone. Wannan sashe yana faɗin tsarin kowane cone, kuma ba ya taɓa juya lambar SK zuwa lambar wani tsari: lambobin jerin cone daban-daban ba sa musanyuwa.

> [!NOTE]
> Koyaushe ku rubuta "SK" kafin lambar. Tsofaffin girke-girken Japan da yawa suna cewa "SK 8" ko "SK 9" kawai ba tare da faɗin cone na wa ba. Cone na Kyoto da cone na SK bisa JIS suna da lambobi iri ɗaya amma suna lanƙwasa a zafi daban-daban (duba Cone na Kyoto, a ƙasa).

### Manyan cone

Ma'aunin yana da girma biyu. Manyan cone (大型コーン) "galibi ana amfani da su ne don auna matakin gasa" (焼度測定), wanda shi ne abin da maginiya ko magini yake so. Ƙananan cone (SK 26 zuwa SK 42, a saman jerin) "galibi ana amfani da su ne don gwajin jure zafi" kuma ba a tattauna su a nan ba. Teburin da ke ƙasa yana bayar da manyan cone daga SK 022 zuwa SK 20.

Lambobin suna farawa daga SK 022, mafi ƙarancin zafi, har zuwa SK 20 a nan. Tsakanin SK 015 da SK 6, lambobin da aka buga suna ɗauke da "a" (SK 015a, SK 6a); ku rubuta shi kamar yadda aka buga.

### Kafa manyan cone

Ku kafa kowane babban cone a plaque ko dunƙulen yumɓu, yana karkata zuwa fuskar lanƙwasarsa. Kamar kowane cone, ku riƙe tsayi da karkata iri ɗaya daga gasa zuwa gasa, domin bambanci a ɗayansu yana canza yadda yake lanƙwasa. Ku kafa cone ukun na rukunin (a ƙasa) tare, can cikin kiln kusa da tsakiyar kayan da aka loda, inda suke samun zafin da tukwane suke samu, amma kuma inda kuke iya ganinsu ta ramin leƙe; kafin ku rufe kiln, ku tabbatar cewa kuna ganin ukun duka.

Ba mu sami umarnin wani mai yi ko mai sayarwa ba game da zurfin ko kusurwar da za a kafa cone na SK. Mafi kusa shi ne wani ma'auni na ɗakin gwaje-gwaje, JIS R 2204, gwajin Japan na yawan zafin da kayan jure wuta suke iya jurewa: yana kafa kowane cone na SK da fuskarsa mai alama a 80° zuwa wani tsayayye mai lebur, kuma ƙafarsa kusan 4 mm a cikinsa. Wannan gwaji ne na kayan jure wuta, ba ƙa'idar gasa tukwane ba, kuma mun karanta shi a cikin kwafin ma'aunin, ba a ma'aunin kansa ba.

### Cone mai jagora, na gasa da mai tsaro

Ana sayar da cone ana kuma kafa su uku-uku: cone mai jagora, cone na gasa a burin zafi, da cone mai tsaro. Ku ɗauki cone na gasa a matsayin lambar SK da girke-girkenku ko yumɓunku ya ambata, cone mai jagora a matsayin cone na gaba mai ƙarancin zafi (layi ɗaya sama a teburin), cone mai tsaro kuma a matsayin na gaba mai zafi fiye. Idan cone mai jagora ya fara lanƙwasawa, ƙarshe ya kusa; idan cone mai tsaro ya lanƙwasa, gasar ta wuce gona da iri.

### Cone na Kyoto

Cone na Kyoto (京都ゼーゲル), waɗanda cibiyar birnin Kyoto take yi, ba a yin su yanzu: rahotannin labarai sun ce cibiyar ta daina yin su a ƙarshen shekarar kuɗi ta 2009. A teburin dai, Kyoto SK 8 (1198 °C) ya lanƙwasa kusa da Orton 5 (1207 °C), kuma Kyoto SK 10 (1232 °C) inda Orton 7 (1232 °C) ya lanƙwasa. ## Gasa bisque {#bisque}

Gasar **bisque** (gasa ta farko; biscuit a Birtaniya) ita ce gasa ta farko, mai ƙarancin zafi, wadda take mayar da busasshen yumɓu tukwane masu ƙarfi, masu ramuka, shirye don shafa glaze{{en: glaze}}. Galibi ana gasa ta zuwa cone 06–04, {{1828–1945 °F; 998–1063 °C}} a jadawalin Orton; maginan Birtaniya sukan gasa zuwa kusan {{1000 °C; 1832 °F}}, kusa da cone 06. Ya kamata bisque ya kasance mai ramuka, "galibi fiye da 15%" (Digitalfire), domin ya tsotse ruwa daga glaze. Bisque mai laushi da yawa yakan ɗauki glaze da kauri sosai, wanda daga baya yake tsagewa ya yi **crawling** (ya janye ya zama tsibirai, ya bar yumɓu a fili). Bisque da ba a gasa shi isasshe ba kuma "yana ci gaba da raguwa yayin gasa glaze", abin da yake ɓata dacewar glaze. Loda yana da sauƙi: tukwane za su iya taɓa juna, ana iya gasa murfi a kan tukwanensu, kuma kwanuka suna haɗuwa baki da baki maimakon a saka ɗaya cikin ɗaya.

### Dalilin da ya sa farkon gasa yake tafiya a hankali

Ruwa yana faɗaɗa fiye da sau dubu idan ya zama tururi, don haka dole a busar da tukwane masu danshi a hankali. L&L da Orton suna ba da shawara cewa "Ya kamata a yi taka-tsantsan na musamman ƙasa da 1500°F (815°C) don a zafafa yumɓun daidai", kuma dole a ƙone carbon kafin kusan {{1200 °F; 650 °C}}. Sama da {{1500 °F; 816 °C}} kiln zai iya hawa da sauri.

Rage gudu ba ya tsada sosai, don haka shawararmu ita ce a riƙe shi ga tukwane masu kauri.

### Candling: busarwa a cikin kiln

**Candling** yana nufin riƙe kiln a ƙaramin zafi, da murfi a ɗan buɗe ko vent (mafitar hayaƙi) yana aiki, har sai danshin ƙarshe ya fita daga tukwane. Majiyoyi sun saɓa kan zafin:

### Jadawalan controller

**Controller** (na'urar sarrafa gasa) na'ura ce ta lantarki da take gasa kiln ta hanyar **schedule** (jadawalin gasa): jerin **segments** (matakai), kowanne gudun hawan zafi (**ramp**), zafin buri da tsayawa idan an so. ## Gasa glaze {#glaze}

Gasa glaze{{en: glaze}} tana narkar da glaze ya zama gilashi a kan bisque. Yawancin masu farawa suna gasa glaze na zafi ƙasa a cone 06–04 ko glaze na zafi tsakiya a cone 5–6. Ku gasa zuwa cone ɗin da ke kan alamomin yumɓunku da glaze ɗinku, tare da rukunin cone a kewayen wannan lamba. Don abin da yake faruwa a cikin glaze yayin da yake narkewa, duba [Tushen glaze](/guides/glazing-basics).

### Loda don gasa glaze

- Kada tukwanen da aka shafa wa glaze su taɓa juna ko bango: ku bar kusan ½ in (13 mm).
- Ku hana glaze hawa ƙafar tukunya (**dry-footing**), ko ku ajiye tukwane na zafi ƙasa a kan **stilts**, ƙananan mariƙai masu tsini. Bai kamata stoneware da porcelain su tsaya a kan stilts ba, domin suna nutsewa cikinsu a waɗannan zafin; ku yi amfani da kiln wash na zafi mai yawa ko yashin silica a maimakon haka.
- Ku bar aƙalla 1 in (25 mm) tsakanin gefen shiryayyu da bango ko sitter.

## Kiln na hannu dalla-dalla {#manual-kilns}

### Yadda suwis suke aiki

Tsofaffin kiln (L&L ya ce galibi waɗanda aka yi kafin 1975) suna da suwis masu matsayi huɗu: Off (a kashe), Low (ƙasa), Medium (tsakiya) da High (sama), babu komai a tsakani. Kiln na baya suna amfani da **infinite switches** (suwis marasa iyaka), masu alamar Off, sannan 1 zuwa 11, sannan High a kiln na L&L. Infinite switch ba ya aika ƙarancin wuta. Yana kunna elements gaba ɗaya ya kashe su a zagaye mai maimaituwa: a Low, L&L ya ce, "za su iya kasancewa a kunne kamar daƙiƙa 7, a kashe daƙiƙa 23 a zagaye na daƙiƙa 30". Yayin da saitin yake ƙaruwa, suna daɗewa a kunne, kuma a High suna kunne kodayaushe. Lambobin ba kaso daidai ba ne, don haka ku koyi kiln ɗinku da cone da rubuce-rubuce.

## Fitar da hayaƙi, leƙawa da tsaro {#venting}

### Fitar da hayaƙi

A farkon gasa, yumɓu da glaze suna fitar da tururin ruwa da hayaƙi, ciki har da carbon monoxide, kuma glaze za su iya fitar da hayaƙi mai guba a ƙarshen gasa ma. L&L ya kira fitar da su "dole". Idan babu tsarin vent, wannan yana nufin murfi a ɗan buɗe da ramukan leƙe a buɗe a matakan farko, kamar a hanyoyin da ke sama; rage gudun zafafawa a wannan lokaci kuma yana taimakon carbon ya ƙone ya fita. A kiln mara vent, Dawson, wanda ya yi sitter, ya kuma ce a bar ramin leƙe na sama a buɗe tsawon gasar gaba ɗaya: fitar da hayaƙin "zai rage lalacewar sassan ƙarfe na kiln kuma ya ƙara tsawon rayuwar bututun". **Downdraft vent** (vent mai amfani da wuta da aka haɗa da kiln) yana canza wannan. Shi yake fitar da hayaƙin, don haka Orton ya ce murfi da ramukan leƙe suna zama a rufe tsawon gasar gaba ɗaya sai don duba cone, kuma L&L ya ce a bar vent a kunne a toshe ramukan leƙe.

Don fitar da hayaƙi daga ɗaki, da ƙararrawar carbon monoxide, duba [Haɗawa cikin aminci da iska mai kyau](/guides/safe-mixing) da [Kada ka sa wa iyalinka guba](/guides/home-safety).

### Yayin da kiln yake gasawa

- Ku leƙa ta ramin leƙe sai da gilashin walda na shade 3 masu kariya daga IR kaɗai; gilashin rana ba su isa ba. Skutt yana ba da shawarar a murɗa toshen a wurinsu don kada su faɗi.
- Ku zauna kusa ku duba kiln yayin da yake gasawa, musamman kusa da lokacin da ake tsammanin zai gama; sitter ba na'urar tsaro mai tabbaci ba ne. Ku gasa ne kawai idan wani babba zai kasance a gida kuma a farke tsawon gasar gaba ɗaya.
- Kada ku taɓa gasa a kan ko kusa da wani abu mai ci da wuta, kamar ƙasan katako ko kafet.
- Ku kiyaye tazarar da ke cikin littafin jagorarku. Alƙaluman da aka wallafa suna daga 12 in (30 cm) zuwa 3 ft (91 cm); inda littafin bai faɗi ba, 18 in (46 cm) daga bango da 3 ft (91 cm) daga duk wani abu mai ci da wuta shi ne zaɓi mafi aminci.
- Ku bar wurin da ke kewayen nauyin sitter a buɗe don ya iya faɗuwa.
- Idan gasa ta ƙare, ku kashe suwis ku kuma kashe breaker.

## Hucewa da sauke kaya {#cooling}

Hucewa ɓangare ne na gasa. L&L da Orton suna ba da shawara: "Ku bar kiln ya huce da kansa da murfi a rufe", da "Kada ku taɓa shigar da iska mai sanyi cikin kiln mai zafi." Buɗewa da wuri "zai iya jawo tsagewa", kuma silica da ke cikin tukwane yana raguwa farat ɗaya yayin da yake hucewa ya wuce {{1063 °F; 573 °C}}. Idan babu controller, ku riƙe kiln a rufe "har sai zafi ya sauka ƙasa sosai da jan wuta (900°F / 482°C)", kuma aƙalla awa 8. Kada ku taɓa tilasta wa kiln da yake har yanzu yana haske ya huce. Tsagewa masu kaifi a tukwanen da aka gasa alama ce ta huce da sauri sosai.

> Shawararmu: ku buɗe murfi sai ƙasa da kusan {{200 °F; 93 °C}} kaɗai, kuma ku sauke kaya ƙasa da kusan {{125 °F; 52 °C}}, ko lokacin da kuke iya riƙe tukwane cikin sauƙi da hannu ba tare da kariya ba. Kayan glaze, masu tauri da kama da gilashi, suna buƙatar haƙuri fiye da bisque. Idan babu pyrometer, ku jira har sai kiln ya huce gaba ɗaya.

Ku karanta cone masu shaida da zarar kun buɗe kiln, kafin a motsa komai, kuma ku rubuta abin da kuka gani tun yana sabo a zuciyarku.

## Bayan gasa {#after}

### Ku rubuta shi

Ku rubuta kowace gasa: kwanan wata, kiln, kayan da aka loda, jadawalin ko lokutan suwis, karatun agogo, da yadda kowane cone mai shaida da cone na sitter suka lanƙwasa, shiryayye bayan shiryayye. Ku ajiye cone ɗin da aka gasa ma. Orton ya lura cewa "Kwatanta sakamakon gasa 6 zuwa 10 na ƙarshe zai nuna sauye-sauye a hankali da suke faruwa a kiln, kamar tsufar elements." [Bayanan gasa](/firing) na Glazecalc za su iya riƙe waɗannan rubuce-rubuce, da ginshiƙan da kuka zaɓa kamar lokaci, zafi, cone, ramp da tsayawa; sakamakon glaze zai iya shiga [bayananku](/notes).

:::orton

### Kula da kiln sitter

- Ku goge mariƙan cone "bayan kowace gasa da ƙaramin burushin waya". Idan wash ko narkakken cone ba ya fita, ku juya mariƙan ko ku sauya su; Dawson yana ba da shawarar a tanadi mariƙai nau'i biyu.
- "KADA KU TAƁA AMFANI DA MAI MAI SANYA SULUƁI KOWANE IRI A KAN KILN-SITTER."
- Ku duba wurin juyawa bayan kowane wata 6–12, kuma ku sauya sandar da ta lanƙwashe ko ta zaizaye.
- Ku duba daidaiton da firing gauge bayan kowace gasa 30.

:::

### Shiryayyu da kiln wash

Kiln wash (shafen kare shiryayye) yana kare shiryayyu daga glaze da yake ɗiga kuma yana barin tukwanen da aka gasa su tashi cikin sauƙi. Ku shafa shi a saman kowane shiryayye da ƙasan kiln kaɗai, "kusan kaurin katin waya". Mafi kyawun hanyar L&L ita ce shafi sirara uku, a gasa tsakanin shafi da shafi idan zai yiwu. Maginiya da magina da yawa kuma suna ajiye tukwane masu glaze mai gudana a kan **cookie**, faifan bisque da yake tare ɗigon. Wannan al'ada ce ta gama gari, ko da yake ba mu sami shawarar wani mai yin kiln a kanta ba.

### Elements

Elements suna zaizayewa: wani shafin oxide yana taruwa, wutar da take gudana tana raguwa, kuma gasa tana yin jinkiri. Dogon tsayawa a zafi mai yawa yana zaizaye elements da thermocouple da sauri. Skutt yana ba da shawarar gasar gwaji ga sabon kiln ko wanda aka gyara, don elements su samar da shafin oxide mai kariya. Ku bi umarnin mai yin kiln ɗinku wajen gwadawa da sauya elements.

## Zobba da sanduna {#rings}

Masana'antar Birtaniya ta kuma yi amfani da wasu alamomin aikin zafi biyu. **Bullers rings** faifai ne masu lebur da suke raguwa daidai da aikin zafi, kuma ana auna su da ma'auni bayan gasa. **Holdcroft bars**, tun 1898, suna sunkuyawa a tsakiya a kan mariƙai biyu, tunanin da ke bayan sandar kiln sitter. Ba mu sami teburin da yake juya ɗayansu zuwa lambobin cone na Orton ba, don haka wannan jagora yana amfani da cone na Orton; muna shirin tattauna zobba da sanduna a wani sabon bugu na gaba.
