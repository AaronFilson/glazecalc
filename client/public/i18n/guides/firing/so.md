## Cone-yada: waxa ay cabbiraan {#cones}

> [!NOTE]
> Marka hore raac buugga tilmaamaha kiln-kaaga. Hagahan wuxuu isku soo ururiyaa waxa ay sameeyayaasha cone-yada iyo kiln-yada ka daabacaan kiln-yada koronto ee fudud; ma beddelo tilmaamaha moodelkaaga, meeshii ay labadu ku kala duwan yihiinna buugga tilmaamaha ayaa la raacayaa.

**Cone-ka pyrometric** (pyrometric cone) waa ahraam yar oo dhuuban oo laga sameeyay budo dhoobo ah oo heer go'an leh. Markii uu kulayl ku filan helo, wuu jilcaa oo u foorarsadaa si mar walba isku mid ah. Cone-yada waxaad kiln-ka (foornada dhoobada lagu dubo) dhigtaa alaabta agteeda, kadibna akhri inta ay foorarsadeen.

Cone-yadu waxay cabbiraan **shaqada kulaylka** (heatwork), saamaynta isku darka ah ee heerkulka iyo waqtiga. Erayada Orton, sameeyaha cone-yada ugu weyn ee Maraykanka: "Cone-yadu ma cabbiraan heerkulka oo keliya." Cone si tartiib ah loo kululeeyay wuxuu ku foorarsadaa heerkul ka hooseeya kan si dhakhso ah loo kululeeyay, glaze-kuna (glaze) sidaas oo kale ayuu yeelaa. Sidaas darteed Orton wuxuu cone kasta heerkulkiisa ku bixiyaa xawaare kululayn oo la sheegay, {{180 °F; 100 °C}} ee ugu dambeeya gubidda. Marka cone uu bilaabo dhaqaaqa, wuxuu ku foorarsadaa xad cidhiidhi ah, "badanaa wax ka yar 40° F" (22 °C).

### Lambarrada cone-yada

Lambarrada cone-yadu waxay ka bilaabmaan 022, kan ugu qabow, ilaa 42. Eber hore ku jira wuxuu calaamadeeyaa taxanaha qabow, waxaana loo akhriyaa "oh": cone 06 ("oh-six") wuu ka qabow yahay cone 04, cone 01-na wuxuu u baahan yahay kulayl ka yar cone 1. Orton wuxuu ka digayaa in ay muhiim tahay in labada taxane aan la isku qaldin. Farqigu waa weyn yahay: cone 06 wuxuu ku foorarsadaa in ka badan {{400 °F; 220 °C}} ka qabow cone 6.

### Joogsigu wuxuu kordhiyaa shaqada kulaylka

**Joogsi** (hold, sidoo kale loo yaqaan **soak**) wuxuu kiln-ka ku hayaa hal heerkul muddo. Maadaama cone-yadu cabbiraan shaqada kulaylka, joogsi xagga sare ah wuxuu meel mari karaa heerkul ugu sarreeya oo ka kulul. Orton wuxuu bixiyaa qiyaasahan:

Label: Joogsiyada iyo shaqada kulaylka

| Joogsi                | Qiyaas ahaan shaqo kulayl la mid ah                |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Qiyaastii 20 daqiiqo  | Heerkul ugu sarreeya oo {{18 °F; 10 °C}} ka kulul  |
| 40–60 daqiiqo         | Heerkul ugu sarreeya oo {{36 °F; 20 °C}} ka kulul  |
| Qiyaastii 2 saacadood | Heerkul ugu sarreeya oo {{54 °F; 30 °C}} ka kulul  |

Orton wuxuu ku daraa in joogsi 1–2 saacadood ah "uu ku filnaan karo in uu qalloociyo lambarka cone ee xiga".

Sidaas darteed joogsi weligiis bilaash ma aha: haddii aad mid ku darto, filo in cone-yadu sii foorarsadaan, oo hoos u dhig heerkulka ugu sarreeya si ay isugu dheelitirmaan.

### Waxa "cone 6" ula jeedo marka la dhaqan galiyo

Jaantusku wuxuu cone 6 is-taaga u bixiyaa {{2232 °F; 1222 °C}}, laakiin keliya marka {{180 °F; 100 °C}} ee ugu dambeeya ay ku kacaan {{108 °F/h; 60 °C/h}}. Haddii uu ku kaco {{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}, isla cone-kaasi wuxuu ku foorarsadaa {{2269 °F; 1243 °C}}. Kiln-yada dhabta ahi mar kale way kala duwan yihiin: Digitalfire wuxuu sheegaa in "Kiln-yada intooda badan ay cone 6 gaadhaan 2200°F, ma aha 2232°F". Sidaas darteed "cone 6" waxay ka dhigan tahay in alaabtu heshay shaqada kulaylka ee foorarisa cone 6, wax kasta oo uu heerkul-cabbiruhu akhriyay waqtigaas.

## Ku gubidda heerkul {#by-temperature}

Kiln leh **controller** wuxuu ku gubaa heerkul aad dejiso, oo uu akhriyo dareeme (probe) u dhow derbiga. Alaab-qeybiyeyaasha UK, Yurub, Australia iyo New Zealand badanaa waxay bixiyaan heerkul gubid halkii cone.

Heerkul keligiis ma sheego inta alaabtu gubatay. Glaze-yadu, sida cone-yada, waxay ka jawaabaan **shaqada kulaylka**: isla heerkulka ugu sarreeya wuxuu bixiyaa natiijo ka kulul kicitaan tartiib ah ama joogsi kadib, mid ka qabowna kicitaan dhakhso ah kadib. Jaantuska Orton wuxuu muujiyaa baaxadda: cone 6 wuxuu ku foorarsadaa {{2165 °F; 1185 °C}} marka uu ku kaco {{27 °F/h; 15 °C/h}}, iyo {{2269 °F; 1243 °C}} marka uu ku kaco {{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}. Sidaas darteed heerkul gubid wuxuu ka dhigan yahay heerkulkaas oo lagu gaadhay xawaare la sheegay, iyo joogsi kasta oo sameeyuhu bixiyo.

Marka hab uu cone bixiyo, ka hel jaantuska kore, tiirka xawaaraha uu kiln-kaagu ku kaco dhammaadka gubidda. Joogsiguna wuxuu kordhiyaa shaqada kulaylka: eeg Joogsigu wuxuu kordhiyaa shaqada kulaylka, kor.

Dareemuhu wuxuu akhriyaa hawada u dhow derbiga, ma aha alaabta. Haddii gubidyadu ka soo baxaan kuwo ka kulul ama ka qabow intii la rabay, buugga tilmaamaha controller-kaaga ayaa sharxaya **offset**-kiisa, kaas oo saxa akhriska.

## Diyaarinta xidhmada cone-yada {#cone-pack}

**Cone markhaati ah** (witness cone) waa cone la dhigo raf alaabta agteeda, halkaas oo uu ka helo isla kulaylka. Orton si cad ayuu u sheegaa doorkiisa: "Cabbirka dhabta ah ee keliya ee shaqada kulaylka waa cone Weyn ama Is-taaga oo rafka la dhigay alaabta agteeda." Kiln sitter wuxuu fadhiyaa meel u dhow derbiga, dareemaha controller ama pyrometer-na wuxuu akhriyaa heerkulka u dhow derbiga; cone markhaati ahi wuxuu akhriyaa meesha dheryaha yaalaan.

**Xidhmada cone-yada** (cone pack) waa saddex cone markhaati ah oo la wada dhigo, lambar mid ah ayay kala fog yihiin:

| Gubid    | Bartilmaameedka                     | 04             | 6            | Gubiddu waxay joogtaa meeshii saxda ahayd |

### Cone-yo is-taaga ama plaque

L&L, sameeye kiln oo Maraykan ah, wuxuu xidhmada ugu talinayaa cone-yo is-taaga "sababtoo ah waa kuwa ugu fudud isticmaalka, waxayna bixiyaan natiijooyinka ugu joogtada badan": dhererkooda iyo janjeerkoodu way ku dhisan yihiin.

Cone-yada waaweyni waxay u baahan yihiin **plaque** (hay diyaarsan) ama **xabbad** (pat) dhoobo jilicsan oo daloolo leh, oo qalin lagu duleeliyay. Mid walba ku dhig si sax ah 2 inch (51 mm) oo muuqda, 8° ka janjeera toosnaanta xagga **wejigiisa foorarsiga**. Si aad wejigaas u hesho, cone-ka ku taag saldhigiisa meel siman, ka qabo caarada, kadibna sii daa: wuxuu u dhacaa xagga wejiga foorarsiga.

### Meesha xidhmooyinka la dhigo

1. Xidhmada dhig "qoto dheer gudaha kiln-ka, haddana... laga arki karo peephole" (Orton), u dhow bartamaha rarka oo ka fog hawo socota (L&L). Ha dhigin peephole-ka agtiisa: cone-yadu waa inay dareemaan kulaylka ay alaabtu dareemayso.
2. Intaadan daboolka xidhin, ka eeg peephole-ka oo hubi inaad arki karto saddexda cone.
3. Kiln leh sitter, rafka xidhmada ka dhig ugu yaraan 1 inch (25 mm) ka sarreeya ama ka hooseeya tuubada sitter-ka, si raf janjeera uusan u xannibin sitter-ka.
4. Gubidyadaada ugu horreeya, xidhmo dhig rafka sare, kan dhexe iyo kan hoose. Tijaabada hagaajinta ee L&L way ka sii fogaataa: laba xidhmo oo cone-yo 07, 06 iyo 05 ah raf kasta, mid cidhifka iyo mid bartamaha.

Filo farqiyo: Orton wuxuu leeyahay "Caadi ahaan, waxaad arkaysaa ugu yaraan farqi hal cone ah inta u dhaxaysa sare iyo hoose ee kiln-ka", meelaha si cufan loo raray waxay gubtaan si ka qabow. Xidhmooyinka aadan arki karin inta gubiddu socoto weli waxay bixiyaan diiwaan dabadeed.

## Kiln sitter-ka {#kiln-sitter}

**Kiln sitter** waa furaha farsamada, oo ku yaal dibadda kiln-yo badan oo fudud iyo kuwo hore, kaas oo kiln-ka damiya marka cone yar uu foorarsado. Kan ugu caansan waa Dawson Kiln-Sitter. Wuxuu gubidda ka dhigaa mid fudud, laakiin waa sahlanaan, ma aha qalab badbaado.

### Sida uu u shaqeeyo

Tuubo porcelain ah ayaa gudaha kiln-ka gasha. Dhammaadkeeda, cone yar ama bar ayaa dul jiifa laba tiir oo bir ah, oo ka hooseeya **ulka dareemaha** (sensing rod) oo u wareega sida lulid. Dibadda kiln-ka, dhinaca kale ee ulku wuxuu ku hayaa culays ciddi yar. Marka cone-ku jilco oo foorarsado ilaa qiyaastii 90°, ulku wuxuu hoos ugu dhacaa inta ku filan in uu culayska sii daayo, culayska ayaa dhaca, korontada element-yada kululeeyana waa la gooyaa.

Cone-yada yaryar iyo bar-yadu "waxay bixiyaan daaweyn kulayl oo u dhiganta". Orton wuxuu xusaa in bilowyaal badan ay doortaan bar-yada, sababtoo ah qaabkooda simani wuxuu fududeeyaa in mar walba si isku mid ah loo dhigo. Cone yar wuu sii dhuubaa, sidaas darteed meesha uu fadhiyo ayaa muhiim ah: qaybtiisa dhuuban oo ulka hoostiisa ah kiln-ku wuxuu damaa isagoo ka qabow, qaybta qarada weynna isagoo ka kulul, isbeddel "ilaa 1/2 cone" ah.

### Cone-kee ayaa sitter-ka la geliyaa

Iluhu way isku khilaafaan. Dawson, oo sameeyay sitter-ka, wuxuu leeyahay isticmaal isla lambarka cone-ka gubidda ee rafka, oo hal lambar ka kulul u gudub keliya haddii cone-ka rafku aan foorarsan. L&L wuxuu leeyahay isticmaal hal lambar ka kulul cone-yada rafka, sababtoo ah sitter-ku wuxuu fadhiyaa meel u dhow derbiga iyo element-yada, kulayl badan ayuu helaa, wuxuuna u janjeeraa in uu goor hore damiyo. Orton ayaa mabda'a dejiya: "Cone-ka rafka ayaa ah garsooraha ugu dambeeya ee habka gubidda, ma aha cone-ka ama bar-ka Kiln-Sitter-ka."

> [!NOTE]
> Barta aan ka bilowno: sitter-ka geli cone yar oo la lambar ah bartilmaameedkaaga, rafkana xidhmo cone ah. Haddii cone-ka gubidda ee rafku aan ku filnayn foorarsigiisa marka kiln-ku damo, mar dambe sitter-ka hal lambar ka kulul geli, ama qaybta qarada weyn ee cone-ka ulka hoostiisa dhig. Haddii uu xad-dhaaf u foorarsado, samee lidkeeda.

### Diyaarinta, iyo hubinta gubid kasta ka hor

Habka Dawson, oo sidoo kale ah hubin gubid kasta ka hor:

1. Dami dhammaan furayaasha. Hubi in ulka dareemuhu si xor ah u dhaqaaqo, uu ku fadhiyo bartamaha tuubada, aanu qalloocsanayn ama daxalaysnayn. Haddii uu gaabis yahay, Dawson wuxuu leeyahay beddel qaybta tuubada oo dhan.
2. Tiirarka cone-ka iyo ulka meesha uu cone-ka taabto ku mari lakab khafiif ah oo **kiln wash** gubid sare ah (dahaar ilaaliya oo ka hortaga in walax dhalaashay ay ku dhegto). Weligaa ha marin wash cone-ka ama tuubada porcelain-ka.
3. Rafaha iyo alaabta ku rar ugu yaraan 1 inch (25 mm) ka sarreeya ama ka hooseeya tuubada, si aanay waxba u taaban, ugu dhicin ama u xannibin.
4. Kor u qaad culayska, ciddiyaha hoos ugu riix kiciyaha (trigger), cone-ka ama bar-ka si siman ugu jiifi tiirarka, oo ku tiirsan tallaabada birta ah, sida buugga tilmaamaha sitter-kaagu muujiyo, mar walba si isku mid ah. Hubi mar kale intaadan daboolka xidhin: "CONE AMA BAR SI KHALAD AH LOO DHIGAY WUXUU KEENI KARAA XAD-DHAAF GUBID."
5. Deji limit timer-ka oo riix badhanka (plunger) ilaa uu xidhmo. Kiln-ka ku hay meel siman, meesha culayska hoostiisana bannaan ka dhig, si culaysku u dhici karo.

### Limit timer-ka

## Akhrinta cone-yada {#reading-cones}

Cone-yada laba jeer ayaa la akhriyaa: peephole-ka laga eego inta kiln-ku gubayo, iyo gacantaada marka uu qaboobo.

### Inta gubiddu socoto

**Peephole** waa dalool yar oo dhex mara derbiga kiln-ka, oo lagu xidho fur la saari karo. Weligaa ha ka eegin adigoon xidhan ilaalinta indhaha. Orton wuxuu leeyahay "Muraayadaha alxanka (welding) waa in la isticmaalo si looga fogaado dhaawac indhaha oo suurtagal ah. Muraayadaha qorraxda laguma talinayo." Muraayadaha alxanka ee shade 3, oo loo qiimeeyay infrared, ayaa inta badan lagu taliyaa; isticmaal kuwo ka madow haddii aad dhibco aragto dabadeed.

- **Cone-ka hagaha** (guide cone) oo bilaaba foorarsiga wuxuu ka dhigan yahay in dhammaadku dhow yahay: halkan wixii ka dambeeya si dhow u daawo.
- Marka cone uu bilaabo, wuxuu qaataa 15 ilaa 25 daqiiqo si uu u foorarsado (buug-yaraha cusub ee Orton wuxuu leeyahay 15 ilaa 22), marka hore si tartiib ah, kadibna si dhakhso ah marka uu dhaafo kalabarka, saacadda 3.
- **Cone-ka gubiddu** (firing cone) wuu dhammaaday marka caaradiisu la siman tahay saldhigiisa.

Kiln gacmeed, peephole-ku waa calaamadda keliya ee dhabta ah ee waqtiga dhabta ah, taas ayaana sababta xidhmadu ay u fadhiyo meel aad arki karto.

## Ku gubidda cone-yada SK {#sk-cones}

Cone-yada SK waa cone-yada pyrometric ee lagu sameeyay heerka Japan, JIS R 8101, waxaana Japan ku iibiya ganacsato sida Yuko Trading (有恒商事). Sida cone kasta, cone SK wuxuu cabbiraa shaqada kulaylka, ma aha heerkulka oo keliya: wuu jilcaa oo foorarsadaa marka alaabtu hesho kulayl ku filan, adiguna waxaad akhrisaa inta uu foorarsaday. Cone-yada markhaatiga, akhrinta inta cone foorarsaday, iyo joogsiyadu u shaqeeyaan sida cone kasta. Qaybtani waxay magacawdaa nidaamka cone kasta, weligeedna ma beddesho lambar SK oo ay u beddesho lambarka nidaam kale: lambarrada taxanaha cone-yada kala duwan lama kala beddeli karo.

> [!NOTE]
> Mar walba "SK" ku qor lambarka hortiisa. Habab badan oo Japan ah oo hore waxay yidhaahdaan keliya "SK 8" ama "SK 9" iyagoon sheegin cone-kee. Cone-yada Kyoto iyo cone-yada SK ee JIS waxay sitaan lambarro isku mid ah laakiin waxay ku foorarsadaan heerkullo kala duwan (eeg Cone-yada Kyoto, hoos).

### Cone-yada waaweyn

Heerku wuxuu leeyahay laba cabbir. Cone-yada waaweyn (大型コーン) "waxaa inta badan loo isticmaalaa cabbirka heerka gubidda" (焼度測定), taas oo ah waxa dhoobo-sameeyuhu rabo. Cone-yada yaryar (SK 26 ilaa SK 42, xagga sare ee xadka) "waxaa inta badan loo isticmaalaa tijaabooyinka u adkaysiga kulaylka" halkanna laguma daboolin. Jadwalka hoose wuxuu bixiyaa cone-yada waaweyn SK 022 ilaa SK 20.

Lambarradu waxay ka bilaabmaan SK 022, kan ugu qabow, ilaa SK 20 halkan. Inta u dhaxaysa SK 015 iyo SK 6 lambarrada la daabacay waxay sitaan "a" (SK 015a, SK 6a); u qor sida loo daabacay.

### Dhigidda cone-yada waaweyn

Cone weyn kasta ku dhig plaque ama xabbad dhoobo ah, adigoo u janjeerinaya xagga wejigiisa foorarsiga. Sida cone kasta, dhererka iyo janjeerka ka dhig isku mid gubid ilaa gubid, sababtoo ah farqi midkood ah wuxuu beddelaa inta uu foorarsado. Saddexda cone ee xidhmada (hoos) wada dhig, qoto dheer gudaha kiln-ka oo u dhow bartamaha rarka, halkaas oo ay ka helaan kulaylka ay alaabtu hesho, haddana meel aad ka arki karto peephole; intaadan kiln-ka xidhin, hubi inaad arki karto saddexdaba.

Ma helin tilmaamo sameeye ama iibiye oo sheegaya inta qoto dheer ama xagasha cone-yada SK la dhigayo. Waxa ugu dhow waa heer shaybaadh, JIS R 2204, tijaabada Japan ee inta kulayl ah ee walax u adkaysata kulaylka (refractory) ay u adkaysato: waxay cone SK kasta dhigtaa iyadoo wejigiisa calaamadeysan uu 80° ku yahay saldhig siman, cagtiisuna qiyaastii 4 mm ay gudaha u gasho. Taasi waa tijaabo walxaha u adkaysta kulaylka, ma aha xeer gubidda dheryaha, waxaana ku akhrinay qoraal laga soo guuriyay heerka, ma aha heerka laftiisa.

### Cone-yada hagaha, gubidda iyo ilaaliyaha

Cone-yada waxaa loo iibiyaa oo loo dhigaa saddex: cone hage ah, cone gubid oo bartilmaameedka ah, iyo cone ilaaliye ah. Cone-ka gubidda u qaado lambarka SK ee habkaaga ama dhoobadaadu magacawdo, cone-ka hagaha cone-ka xiga ee ka qabow (hal saf kor jadwalka) iyo cone-ka ilaaliyaha kan xiga ee ka kulul. Marka cone-ka hagahu bilaabo foorarsiga, dhammaadku waa dhow yahay; marka cone-ka ilaaliyuhu foorarsado, gubiddu way dhaaftay.

### Cone-yada Kyoto

## Gubidda bisque {#bisque}

Gubidda **bisque** (gubidda koowaad; biscuit UK-da) waa gubidda koowaad ee hoose ee dhoobada qalalan u beddesha alaab adag oo daloolo leh oo diyaar u ah glaze. Badanaa waxaa lagu gubaa cone 06–04, {{1828–1945 °F; 998–1063 °C}} jaantuska Orton; dhoobo-sameeyayaasha UK badanaa waxay ku gubaan qiyaastii {{1000 °C; 1832 °F}}, u dhow cone 06. Bisque-ku waa inuu ahaado mid daloolo leh, "guud ahaan in ka badan 15%" (Digitalfire), si uu biyaha uga nuugo glaze-ka. Bisque aad u jilicsan wuxuu qaataa glaze aad u qaro weyn, kaas oo markaas dillaaca oo **crawl** gala (dib ugu soo ururo jasiirado, isagoo dhoobo qaawan ka taga). Bisque si yar loo gubay sidoo kale "wuxuu sii wadaa isku-soo-ururka inta lagu jiro gubidda glaze-ka", taas oo xumaysa ku-habboonaanta glaze-ka. Raridda waa la cafiyaa: xabbaduhu way isa taaban karaan, daboolada waxaa lagu gubi karaa dheryahooda dushooda, baaquliyadana af-ka-af ayaa la isugu dhigaa halkii la isku dhex geliyo.

## Gubidda glaze-ka {#glaze}

Gubidda glaze-ku waxay glaze-ka u dhalaalisaa muraayad bisque-ka dul saaran. Bilowyaasha intooda badan waxay glaze-yada gubidda hoose ku gubaan cone 06–04 ama glaze-yada gubidda dhexe cone 5–6. ### Raridda gubidda glaze-ka

- Xabbadaha glaze-ka leh waa inaanay isa taaban ama derbiyada taaban: u daa qiyaastii ½ inch (13 mm).
- Glaze-ka ka ilaali cagta (**dry-footing**), ama alaabta gubidda hoose dul dhig **stilts**, taageerooyin yaryar oo caarooyin leh. Stoneware iyo porcelain waa inaanay stilts dul istaagin, kuwaas oo heerkulkaas ku dhex gala; isticmaal kiln wash gubid sare ah ama ciid silica ah.
- U daa ugu yaraan 1 inch (25 mm) cidhifyada rafaha iyo derbiga ama sitter-ka dhexdooda.

## Hawo-saarka, ka eegidda iyo badbaadada {#venting}

### Hawo-saarka

Bilowga gubidda, dhoobada iyo glaze-ku waxay soo daayaan uumi biyood iyo qiiqyo, oo ay ku jiraan carbon monoxide, glaze-yaduna waxay soo deyn karaan qiiqyo sun ah xitaa goor dambe oo gubidda ah. L&L wuxuu hawo-saarkooda ugu yeedhaa "lagama maarmaan". Nidaam hawo-saar la'aan, taasi waxay ka dhigan tahay daboolka oo kor loo qabtay iyo peephole-yada oo furan marxaladaha hore, sida hababka kor ku xusan; gaabinta kululaynta waqtigan sidoo kale waxay caawisaa gubashada kaarboonka. Kiln aan hawo-saar lahayn, Dawson, oo sameeyay sitter-ka, sidoo kale wuxuu leeyahay peephole-ka sare u daa furan gubidda oo dhan: hawo-saarku "wuxuu yarayn doonaa xumaanshaha qaybaha birta ah ee kiln-ka wuxuuna kordhin doonaa cimriga qaybta tuubada". **Hawo-saar downdraft** (downdraft vent: hawo-saar koronto ku shaqeeya oo kiln-ka lagu xidhay) ayaa tan beddela. Isaga ayaa hawo-saarka sameeya, sidaas darteed Orton wuxuu leeyahay daboolka iyo peephole-yadu way xidhnaanayaan gubidda oo dhan marka laga reebo hubinta cone-yada, L&L-na wuxuu leeyahay hawo-saarka shid oo xidh peephole-yada.

Hawo-wareejinta qolka, iyo digniinta carbon monoxide, eeg [Isku-darka ammaanka ah iyo hawo-wareejinta](/guides/safe-mixing) iyo [Ha sumayn qoyskaaga](/guides/home-safety).

### Inta kiln-ku gubayo

- Peephole ka eeg keliya adigoo xidhan muraayadaha alxanka shade 3 ee loo qiimeeyay IR; muraayadaha qorraxdu kuma filna. Skutt wuxuu ku taliyaa in furka la maroojiyo meeshooda si aanay u dhicin.
- Agtiisa joog oo hubi kiln-ka inta uu gubayo, gaar ahaan marka uu ku dhow yahay waqtiga dhammaadka la filayo; sitter ma aha qalab aan fashilmin. Gub keliya marka qof weyn uu guriga joogo oo soo jeedo gubidda oo dhan.
- Weligaa ha ku gubin wax guban kara korkiisa ama agtiisa, sida sagxad alwaax ah ama roog.
- Ilaali masaafada bannaan ee buuggaaga tilmaamaha. Tirooyinka la daabacay waxay u dhexeeyaan 12 inch (30 cm) ilaa 3 feet (91 cm); meesha buugga tilmaamuhu aanu waxba ka sheegin, 18 inch (46 cm) derbiyada iyo 3 feet (91 cm) wax kasta oo guban kara waa doorashada ugu ammaanka badan.
- Meesha ku wareegsan culayska sitter-ka bannaan ka dhig si uu u dhici karo.
- Marka gubiddu dhammaato, dami furayaasha, sidoo kale dami breaker-ka.

## Qaboojinta iyo ka soo saarista {#cooling}

Qaboojintu waa qayb ka mid ah gubidda. L&L iyo Orton waxay ku taliyaan: "Kiln-ka u daa inuu si dabiici ah u qaboobo isagoo daboolku xidhan yahay", iyo "Weligaa hawo qabow ha gelin kiln kulul." Furitaan goor hore ah "wuxuu keeni karaa dillaac", silica-da alaabta ku jirtana si kedis ah ayay isu soo ururtaa marka ay dhaafto {{1063 °F; 573 °C}} inta ay qaboobayso. Controller la'aan, kiln-ka xidhnaan ku hay "ilaa heerkulku si fiican uga hooseeyo kulaylka casaanka (900°F / 482°C)", ugu yaraanna 8 saacadood. Weligaa si khasab ah ha u qaboojin kiln weli iftiimaya. Dillaacyo cidhifyo af leh oo alaabta la gubay ku jira waa calaamad qaboojin aad u dhakhso badan.

Raryada glaze-ka, oo cufan oo muraayad u eg, waxay u baahan yihiin samir ka badan bisque. Pyrometer la'aan, sug ilaa kiln-ku si buuxda u qaboobo.

Akhri cone-yada markhaatiga isla marka aad kiln-ka furto, ka hor inta aan waxba la dhaqaajin, oo qor waxa aad aragto inta ay weli cusub tahay.

## Gubidda kadib {#after}

### Diiwaangeli

Qor gubid kasta: taariikhda, kiln-ka, rarka, jadwalka ama waqtiyada furayaasha, akhriska timer-ka, iyo sida cone markhaati kasta iyo cone-ka sitter-ku u foorarsadeen, raf raf. Sidoo kale hay cone-yada la gubay. Orton wuxuu xusaa in "Isbarbardhigga natiijooyinka 6 ilaa 10-kii gubid ee ugu dambeeyay uu muujin doono isbeddellada tartiibka ah ee kiln-ka ka dhacaya, sida gabowga element-yada kululeeya." [Diiwaannada gubidda](/firing) ee Glazecalc ayaa hayn kara diiwaannadan, iyadoo leh tiirar aad doorato sida waqtiga, heerkulka, cone-ka, ramp-ka iyo joogsiga; natiijooyinka glaze-kuna waxay geli karaan [qoraaladaada](/notes).

:::orton

### Daryeelka kiln sitter-ka

- Nadiifi tiirarka cone-ka "gubid kasta kadib burush yar oo silig ah". Haddii wash ama cone dhalaalay aanu ka bixin, rog tiirarka ama beddel; Dawson wuxuu soo jeediyaa in la haysto laba lammaane.
- "WELIGAA HA ISTICMAALIN SAYID (LUBRICANTS) NOOC KASTA AH KILN-SITTER-KA."
- Baadh meesha uu ku wareego (pivot) 6–12 biloodba mar, oo beddel ul qalloocsan ama duugoobay.
- Ku hubi hagaajinta firing gauge-ka 30-kii gubidba mar.

:::

### Rafaha iyo kiln wash-ka

Kiln wash (dahaarka ilaaliya rafaha kiln-ka) wuxuu rafaha ka ilaaliyaa glaze dhibcaya, wuxuuna u oggolaadaa dheryaha la gubay in kor looga qaado. Ku mari keliya dusha raf kasta iyo sagxadda kiln-ka, "qiyaastii qaradda kaadh boosta". Habka ugu wanaagsan ee L&L waa saddex lakab oo khafiif ah, oo la gubo lakabyada dhexdooda haddii ay suurtagal tahay. Dhoobo-sameeyayaal badan sidoo kale xabbadaha leh glaze-yo socda waxay dul dhigaan **cookie**, saxan bisque ah oo qabta dhibcaha. Tani waa dhaqan caadi ah, inkastoo aanaan helin talo sameeye kiln ah oo ku saabsan.

### Element-yada

Element-yadu way duugoobaan: lakab oksaydh ah ayaa ku sameysma, koronto ka yar ayaa socota, gubidyaduna way gaabiyaan. Joogsiyo dheer oo heerkul sare ah ayaa si dhakhso ah u duugeeya element-yada iyo thermocouple-ka. Skutt wuxuu ku taliyaa gubid tijaabo ah kiln cusub ama la dayactiray, si element-yadu u sameeyaan lakab oksaydh ah oo ilaaliya. Raac tilmaamaha sameeyahaaga ee tijaabinta iyo beddelidda element-yada.

## Giraanta iyo bar-yada {#rings}

Warshadaha Ingiriisku waxay sidoo kale isticmaaleen laba tilmaame shaqo kulayl oo kale. **Bullers rings** waa saxanno siman oo u yaraada si u dhiganta shaqada kulaylka, waxaana lagu cabbiraa cabbir (gauge) gubidda kadib. **Holdcroft bars**, laga soo bilaabo 1898, waxay bartamaha ka laabtaan iyagoo dul saaran laba tiir, fikradda ka dambaysa bar-ka kiln sitter-ka. Ma helin jadwal midkood u beddela lambarrada cone-ka Orton, sidaas darteed hagahan wuxuu isticmaalaa cone-yada Orton; waxaan qorsheynaynaa inaan giraanta iyo bar-yada ku daboolno nooc dambe.
