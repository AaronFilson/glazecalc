## Ama-cone: akalani {#cones}

> [!NOTE]
> Landela kuqala incwadi yemiyalelo ye-kiln yakho. Lo mhlahlandlela uqoqa lokho okushicilelwa abenzi bama-cone nabenzi bama-kiln mayelana nama-kiln kagesi alula; awuthathi indawo yemiyalelo yemodeli yakho, futhi lapho kokubili kungavumelani khona, kuhamba incwadi yemiyalelo.

I-**pyrometric cone** iyiphiramidi encane, ezacile, eyenziwe ngempuphu ye-ceramic emisiwe. Uma isithole ukushisa okwanele, iyathamba bese igobela phansi ngendlela ephindekayo. Ubeka ama-cone ku-kiln (isithando sokushisa izitsha zobumba) eceleni kwezitsha, bese ufunda ukuthi agobe kangakanani.

Ama-cone akala i-**heatwork** (umsebenzi wokushisa), umphumela ohlangene wezinga lokushisa nesikhathi. Ngamazwi ka-Orton, umenzi omkhulu wama-cone e-US, "Ama-cone awakali izinga lokushisa lodwa." I-cone eshiswa kancane igoba ezingeni lokushisa eliphansi kunaleyo eshiswa ngokushesha, futhi i-glaze yenza okufanayo. Ngakho u-Orton unikeza izinga lokushisa le-cone ngayinye ngesivinini sokushisa esishiwo kuma-{{180 °F; 100 °C}} okugcina okushisa. Uma i-cone iqala ukunyakaza, igoba ebangeni elincane, "ngokuvamile ngaphansi kuka-40° F" (22 °C).

### "I-cone 6" isho ukuthini empeleni

Ishadi linikeza i-cone 6 ezimele ngokwayo njengo-{{2232 °F; 1222 °C}}, kodwa kuphela uma ama-{{180 °F; 100 °C}} okugcina ekhuphuka ngo-{{108 °F/h; 60 °C/h}}. Uma ikhuphuka ngo-{{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}, i-cone efanayo igoba ku-{{2269 °F; 1243 °C}}. Ama-kiln angempela ahluka futhi: i-Digitalfire ibika ukuthi "Ama-kiln amaningi afinyelela ku-cone 6 ku-2200°F, hhayi ku-2232°F". Ngakho "i-cone 6" isho ukuthi izitsha zithole i-heatwork egoba i-cone 6, noma ngabe i-thermometer ibifundani ngaleso sikhathi.

## Ukushisa ngezinga lokushisa {#by-temperature}

I-kiln ene-**controller** ishisa ize ifike ezingeni lokushisa olisethayo, elifundwa yi-probe eseduze nodonga. Abahlinzeki base-UK, eYurophu, e-Australia nase-New Zealand bavame ukunikeza izinga lokushisa esikhundleni se-cone.

Izinga lokushisa lodwa alisho ukuthi izitsha zishiswe kangakanani. Ama-glaze, njengama-cone, aphendula ku-**heatwork**: isiqongo esifanayo sinikeza umphumela oshisa kakhulu ngemuva kokukhuphuka kancane noma ukubamba, kanye nomphumela opholile ngemuva kokukhuphuka ngokushesha. Ishadi lika-Orton likhombisa ubukhulu balokho: i-cone 6 igoba ku-{{2165 °F; 1185 °C}} uma ikhuphuka ngo-{{27 °F/h; 15 °C/h}} naku-{{2269 °F; 1243 °C}} uma ikhuphuka ngo-{{270 °F/h; 150 °C/h}}. Ngakho izinga lokushisa lisho lelo zinga lokushisa elifinyelelwe ngesivinini esishiwo, nanoma yikuphi ukubamba umenzi akunikezayo.

Uma iresiphi inikeza i-cone esikhundleni salokho, yithole eshadini elingenhla, ekholomu yesivinini i-kiln yakho ekhuphuka ngaso ekupheleni kokushisa. Ukubamba nakho kwengeza i-heatwork: bona Ukubamba kwengeza i-heatwork, ngenhla.

I-probe ifunda umoya oseduze nodonga, hhayi izitsha. Uma ukushisa kuphuma kushisa kakhulu noma kupholile kunalokho okufanele, incwadi yemiyalelo ye-controller yakho ichaza i-**offset** yayo, elungisa lokho ekufundayo.

## Ukusetha i-cone pack {#cone-pack}

I-**witness cone** (i-cone yobufakazi) yi-cone ebekwe eshalofini eceleni kwezitsha, lapho ithola khona ukushisa okufanayo. U-Orton ucacile ngendima yayo: "Okuwukuphela kwesilinganiso seqiniso se-heatwork kuvela ku-cone Enkulu noma Ezimele ngokwayo ebekwe eshalofini eduze kwezitsha." I-kiln sitter ihlala eduze nodonga, futhi i-probe ye-controller noma ye-pyrometer ifunda izinga lokushisa eduze nodonga; i-witness cone ifunda lapho izimbiza zikhona.

### Ama-cone azimele ngokwawo noma i-plaque

I-L&L, umenzi wama-kiln wase-US, incoma ama-cone azimele ngokwawo e-pack "ngoba alula kakhulu ukuwasebenzisa futhi anikeza imiphumela engaguquki kakhulu": ubude bawo nokuncika kwawo kwakhelwe kuwo.

Ama-cone amakhulu adinga i-**plaque** (isibambi esenziwe ngomumo) noma i-**pat** (isigaxa) sobumba oluthambile, olunezimbotshana, oluhlatshwe izimbobo ngepensela. Beka ngayinye kubonakala amayintshi angu-2 (51 mm) ncamashi, incike ngo-8° ukusuka ekumeni mpo ibheke **ebusweni bayo bokugoba**. Ukuze uthole lobo buso, misa i-cone ngesisekelo sayo endaweni eyisicaba, uyibambe ngesihloko bese uyiyeka: iwela ngasebusweni bokugoba.

### Lapho ungabeka khona ama-pack

1. Beka i-pack "ekujuleni kwe-kiln, kodwa... ibonakala ngembobo yokulunguza" (Orton), eduze nenkaba yomthwalo futhi kude nomoya ovunguzayo (L&L). Hhayi khona ngasembotsheni yokulunguza: ama-cone kufanele azwe ukushisa izitsha ezikuzwayo.
2. Ngaphambi kokuvala isivalo, lunguza ngembobo yokulunguza bese uhlola ukuthi uyawabona yini wonke ama-cone amathathu.
3. Ku-kiln ene-sitter, gcina ishalofu le-pack okungenani iyintshi engu-1 (25 mm) ngaphezulu noma ngaphansi kweshubhu ye-sitter, ukuze ishalofu elitsheleka lingakwazi ukuminyanisa i-sitter.
4. Ekushiseni kwakho kokuqala, beka i-pack emashalofini aphezulu, aphakathi naphansi. Ukuhlolwa kokulinganisa kwe-L&L kuya phambili: ama-pack amabili ama-cone 07, 06 no-05 kuwo wonke amashalofu, eyodwa onqenqemeni neyodwa enkabeni.

Lindela umehluko: u-Orton uthi "Ngokuvamile, uzobona okungenani umehluko we-cone eyodwa kusukela phezulu kuya phansi ku-kiln", futhi izindawo eziminyene zishisa zipholile. Ama-pack ongakwazi ukuwabona ngesikhathi sokushisa asanikeza irekhodi ngemuva kwalokho.

## I-kiln sitter {#kiln-sitter}

I-**kiln sitter** iyiswishi yomshini, ngaphandle kwama-kiln amaningi alula nawakudala, evala i-kiln uma i-cone encane igoba. Eyaziwa kakhulu yi-Dawson Kiln-Sitter. Yenza ukushisa kube lula, kodwa iyinto yokwenza kube lula, hhayi into yokuphepha.

### Isebenza kanjani

Ishubhu ye-porcelain ingena ku-kiln. Ekugcineni kwayo, i-cone encane noma i-bar ilala inqamula izisekelo ezimbili zensimbi, ngaphansi kwe-**sensing rod** (induku yokuzwa) ejikajika njengesi-seesaw. Ngaphandle kwe-kiln, elinye icala lenduku libambe isisindo ngenzipho encane. Uma i-cone ithamba futhi igoba cishe ku-90°, induku yehla ngokwanele ukuze idedele isisindo, isisindo siyawa, futhi ugesi oya kuma-element uyanqamuka.

Ama-cone amancane nama-bar "anikeza ukushisa okulinganayo". U-Orton uphawula ukuthi abaqalayo abaningi bakhetha ama-bar, ngoba isimo sawo esilinganayo senza kube lula ukuwabeka ngendlela efanayo njalo. I-cone encane iyancipha, ngakho lapho ihlala khona kubalulekile: uma ingxenye yayo encane ingaphansi kwenduku i-kiln ivala ipholile, futhi uma ingxenye yayo ewugqinsi ishisa kakhulu, umehluko "ongafika engxenyeni ye-cone (1/2 cone)".

### Iyiphi i-cone engena ku-sitter

Imithombo ayivumelani. U-Dawson, owenza i-sitter, uthi sebenzisa inombolo efanayo ne-cone yokushisa eshalofini, futhi uye enombolweni eyodwa eshisa kakhulu kuphela uma i-cone yeshalofu ihlala ingagobile. I-L&L ithi sebenzisa inombolo eyodwa eshisa kakhulu kunama-cone eshalofu, ngoba i-sitter ihlala eduze nodonga nama-element, ithola ukushisa okwengeziwe, futhi ivame ukuvala kusesekuseni. U-Orton uqinisa umgomo: "I-cone yeshalofu ingumahluleli wokugcina wenqubo yokushisa, hhayi i-cone noma i-bar ye-Kiln-Sitter."

> [!NOTE]
> Indawo yethu yokuqala: faka i-cone encane yenombolo efanayo nenhloso yakho ku-sitter, ne-cone pack eshalofini. Uma i-cone yokushisa yeshalofu ingagobanga ngokwanele lapho i-kiln ivala, ngokuzayo yiya enombolweni eyodwa eshisa kakhulu ku-sitter, noma ubeke ingxenye ewugqinsi ye-cone ngaphansi kwenduku. Uma igobe ngokweqile, yenza okuphambene.

### Ukuyisetha, nokuhlola ngaphambi kokushisa ngakunye

Inqubo ka-Dawson, ephinde isebenze njengokuhlola ngaphambi kokushisa ngakunye:

1. Cisha wonke amaswishi. Hlola ukuthi i-sensing rod inyakaza ngokukhululekile, ihlala phakathi nendawo eshubhwini futhi ayigobile noma ayigqwalile. Uma ivila, u-Dawson uthi shintsha yonke ingxenye yeshubhu.
2. Gcoba ungqimba oluncane lwe-**kiln wash** yokushisa okuphezulu (ungqimba oluvikelayo oluvimbela izinto ezincibilikile ukuthi zinamathele) ezisekelweni ze-cone nasenduku lapho ithinta khona i-cone. Ungalokothi ugcobe i-wash ku-cone noma eshubhwini ye-porcelain.
3. Layisha amashalofu nezitsha okungenani iyintshi engu-1 (25 mm) ngaphezulu noma ngaphansi kweshubhu, ukuze kungabikho lutho olungayithinta, oluwela kuyo noma oluyivimba.
4. Phakamisa isisindo, cindezela inzipho phansi ku-trigger, bese ubeka i-cone noma i-bar ilale phansi ezisekelweni ibhekene nesinyathelo sensimbi, njengoba incwadi yemiyalelo ye-sitter yakho ikhombisa, ngendlela efanayo njalo. Phinda uyihlole ngaphambi kokuvala isivalo: "I-CONE NOMA I-BAR EBEKWE KABI INGADALA UKUSHISA NGOKWEQILE."
5. Setha i-limit timer bese uphusha i-plunger ize ikhiye. Gcina i-kiln ilinganile nendawo engaphansi kwesisindo ingenalutho, ukuze isisindo sikwazi ukuwa.

### I-limit timer

## Ukufunda ama-cone {#reading-cones}

Ama-cone afundwa kabili: ngembobo yokulunguza ngesikhathi i-kiln ishisa, nasesandleni sakho uma isipholile.

### Ngesikhathi sokushisa

I-**peephole** (imbobo yokulunguza) yimbobo encane edongeni lwe-kiln, evalwa ngesivimbo esikhipheka. Ungalokothi ulunguze ngaphandle kokuvikela amehlo. U-Orton uthi "Kufanele kusetshenziswe izibuko zabashiseli ukugwema ukulimala kwamehlo okungenzeka. Izibuko zelanga azinconywa." Izibuko zokushisela ze-shade 3, ezenzelwe i-infrared, yizo ezinconywa kakhulu; khetha ezimnyama kakhulu uma ubona amachashazi ngemuva kwalokho.

- **I-guide cone** iqala ukugoba kusho ukuthi ukuphela sekuseduze: buka ngokucophelela kusukela lapha.
- Uma i-cone isiqalile, ithatha imizuzu engu-15 kuya ku-25 ukugoba (incwajana entsha ka-Orton ithi engu-15 kuya ku-22), kancane ekuqaleni, bese ngokushesha uma isidlule phakathi, lapho iwashi likhomba u-3.
- **I-firing cone** iqedile uma isihloko sayo silingana nesisekelo sayo.

Ku-kiln yesandla, imbobo yokulunguza iwukuphela kwesibonakaliso sakho sesikhathi sangempela, yingakho i-pack kufanele ihlale lapho ungayibona khona.

## Ukushisa ngama-cone e-SK {#sk-cones}

Ama-cone e-SK angama-pyrometric cone enziwe ngokwezinga laseJapane, i-JIS R 8101, futhi athengiswa eJapane ngabathengisi abafana ne-Yuko Trading (有恒商事). Njenganoma iyiphi i-cone, i-cone ye-SK ikala i-heatwork, hhayi izinga lokushisa lodwa: iyathamba bese igobela phansi uma izitsha zithole ukushisa okwanele, bese ufunda ukuthi igobe kangakanani. Ama-witness cone, ukufunda ukuthi i-cone igobe kangakanani, nokubamba kusebenza kuyo njengakunoma iyiphi i-cone. Le ngxenye ibiza uhlelo lwe-cone ngayinye, futhi ayilokothi iguqule inombolo ye-SK ibe yinombolo yolunye uhlelo: izinombolo zochungechunge oluhlukene lwama-cone azishintshaniswa.

> [!NOTE]
> Njalo bhala "SK" ngaphambi kwenombolo. Amaresiphi amaningi amadala aseJapane athi kuphela "SK 8" noma "SK 9" engasho ukuthi i-cone kabani. Ama-cone ase-Kyoto nama-cone e-SK e-JIS anezinombolo ezifanayo kodwa agoba emazingeni okushisa ahlukene (bona Ama-cone ase-Kyoto, ngezansi).

### Ama-cone amakhulu

Izinga linosayizi ababili. Ama-cone amakhulu (大型コーン) "asetshenziselwa ikakhulukazi ukukala izinga lokushisa" (焼度測定), okuyikho umbumbi akufunayo. Ama-cone amancane (SK 26 kuya ku-SK 42, phezulu ebangeni) "asetshenziselwa ikakhulukazi ukuhlola ukumelana nokushisa" futhi awaxoxwa lapha. Ithebula elingezansi linikeza ama-cone amakhulu kusukela ku-SK 022 kuya ku-SK 20.

Izinombolo zisuka ku-SK 022, epholile kakhulu, zize zifike ku-SK 20 lapha. Phakathi kuka-SK 015 no-SK 6 izinombolo eziphrintiwe zinokuthi "a" (SK 015a, SK 6a); kubhale njengoba kuphrintiwe.

### Ukubeka ama-cone amakhulu

Beka i-cone enkulu ngayinye ku-plaque noma esigaxeni sobumba, incike ibheke ebusweni bayo bokugoba. Njenganoma iyiphi i-cone, gcina ubude nokuncika kufana ukusuka ekushiseni kuya ekushiseni, ngoba umehluko kokubili kushintsha ukuthi igoba kangakanani. Beka ama-cone amathathu e-pack (ngezansi) ndawonye, ekujuleni kwe-kiln eduze nenkaba yomthwalo, lapho ethola ukushisa izitsha ezikutholayo, kodwa lapho ungawabona khona ngembobo yokulunguza; ngaphambi kokuvala i-kiln, hlola ukuthi uyawabona yini omathathu.

Asitholanga miyalelo yomenzi noma yomthengisi yokuthi ama-cone e-SK abekwa ajule kangakanani noma ku-engeli enjani. Okuseduze kakhulu yizinga lelabhorethri, i-JIS R 2204, ukuhlolwa kwaseJapane kokuthi into engashi (refractory) imelana nokushisa okungakanani: ibeka i-cone ye-SK ngayinye ubuso bayo obumakiwe bungu-80° esitendini esiyisicaba nonyawo lwayo lungene cishe ngo-4 mm kuso. Lokho kungukuhlolwa kwezinto ezingashi, hhayi umthetho wokushisa izimbiza, futhi sakufunda embhalweni okopishwe walelo zinga, hhayi ezingeni ngokwalo.

### Ama-cone e-guide, e-firing ne-guard

Ama-cone athengiswa futhi abekwa ngamathathu: i-guide cone, i-firing cone enhlosweni, ne-guard cone. Thatha i-firing cone njengenombolo ye-SK iresiphi yakho noma ubumba bakho obusho yona, i-guide cone njenge-cone elandelayo epholile (umugqa owodwa ngenhla ethebuleni) ne-guard cone njengelandelayo eshisa kakhulu. Uma i-guide cone iqala ukugoba, ukuphela sekuseduze; uma i-guard cone igoba, ukushisa sekudlulele kakhulu.

## Ukushisa i-bisque {#bisque}

Ukushisa kwe-**bisque** (i-biscuit e-UK) ukushisa kokuqala, okuphansi, okuguqula ubumba olomile lube izitsha eziqinile, ezinezimbotshana, ezilungele i-glaze. Ngokuvamile kushiswa ku-cone 06–04, {{1828–1945 °F; 998–1063 °C}} eshadini lika-Orton; ababumbi base-UK bavame ukushisa cishe ku-{{1000 °C; 1832 °F}}, eduze ne-cone 06. I-bisque kufanele ihlale inezimbotshana, "ngokuvamile ngaphezu kuka-15%" (Digitalfire), ukuze imunce amanzi ku-glaze. I-bisque ethambe kakhulu ithatha i-glaze ibe wugqinsi kakhulu, bese iqhekeka futhi i-**crawl** (ihoxe yenze iziqhingi, ishiye ubumba olungembozwe). I-bisque engashiswanga ngokwanele nayo "iqhubeka nokuncipha ngesikhathi sokushisa i-glaze", okonakalisa ukufaneleka kwe-glaze. Ukulayisha kuyaxolela: izingcezu zingathintana, iziciko zingashiswa phezu kwezimbiza zazo, futhi izitsha zibekwa umlomo kuya emlonyeni esikhundleni sokufakwa enye phakathi kwenye.

## Ama-kiln esandla ngokuningiliziwe {#manual-kilns}

### Amaswishi asebenza kanjani

I-**pyrometer** yisilinganiso sokushisa esifundwa ku-**thermocouple**, i-probe yokushisa edlula odongeni lwe-kiln. Ikhombisa ukuthi i-kiln ikhuphuka ngokushesha kangakanani, ukuze ukhethe ikholomu efanele yeshadi lama-cone. Kodwa ifunda eduze nodonga, hhayi ezitsheni, futhi iyaphambuka. I-L&L iphawula ukuthi i-thermocouple yohlobo K ingaphambuka ngaphezu kuka-{{25 °F; 14 °C}} ngemuva kokushisa okuphindaphindiwe kwe-cone 6, "umehluko omkhulu kune-cone ephelele", kanti i-cone ezimele ngokwayo yehluka okungeqile ku-{{4 °F; 2 °C}}. U-Orton uthi ama-pyrometer adinga ukulinganiswa kabusha ngezikhathi ezithile, futhi ama-cone yiwo ondlela yokuwahlola.

## Ukuphuma komoya, ukulunguza nokuphepha {#venting}

### Ukuphuma komoya

Ekuqaleni kokushisa, ubumba ne-glaze kukhipha umhwamuko wamanzi nentuthu, kuhlanganise ne-carbon monoxide, futhi ama-glaze angakhipha intuthu enoshevu nangemuva ekushiseni. I-L&L ithi ukuyikhipha "kubalulekile kakhulu". Uma ingekho i-vent, lokho kusho ukuthi isivalo sivulwe kancane nezimbobo zokulunguza zivuliwe ezigabeni zokuqala, njengasezinqubweni ezingenhla; ukwehlisa ukushisa ngalesi sikhathi nakho kusiza i-carbon ukuthi ishe iphele. Ku-kiln engenayo i-vent, u-Dawson, owenza i-sitter, uthi futhi shiya imbobo yokulunguza ephezulu ivuliwe ukushisa konke: ukuphuma komoya "kuzonciphisa ukonakala kwezingxenye zensimbi ze-kiln futhi kwandise impilo yengxenye yeshubhu". I-**downdraft vent** (i-vent esebenza ngogesi efakwe ku-kiln) iyakushintsha lokhu. Yiyo ekhipha umoya, ngakho u-Orton uthi isivalo nezimbobo zokulunguza zihlala zivaliwe ukushisa konke ngaphandle kokuhlola ama-cone, futhi i-L&L ithi shiya i-vent ivuliwe bese uvimba izimbobo zokulunguza.

Ngokukhipha umoya egumbini, nangama-alamu e-carbon monoxide, bona [Ukuxuba ngokuphepha nomoya ohlanzekile](/guides/safe-mixing) kanye [Ungawadliseli ushevu umndeni wakho](/guides/home-safety).

### Ngesikhathi i-kiln ishisa

- Lunguza ngembobo yokulunguza kuphela ngezibuko zokushisela ze-shade 3 ezenzelwe i-IR; izibuko zelanga azanele. I-Skutt yeluleka ukusonta izivimbo endaweni yazo ukuze zingawi.
- Hlala eduze futhi uhlole i-kiln ngesikhathi ishisa, ikakhulukazi eduze nesikhathi sayo esilindelekile sokuqeda; i-sitter ayiyona into engehluleki. Shisa kuphela uma umuntu omdala ezoba sekhaya futhi ephapheme ukushisa konke.
- Ungalokothi ushise phezu noma eduze kwanoma yini evuthayo, njengephansi lokhuni noma ukhaphethi.
- Gcina izikhala ezisencwadini yakho yemiyalelo. Izibalo ezishicilelwe zisuka kumayintshi angu-12 (30 cm) kuya kumafidi angu-3 (91 cm); lapho incwadi yemiyalelo ingasho lutho, amayintshi angu-18 (46 cm) ukusuka ezindongeni namafidi angu-3 (91 cm) ukusuka kunoma yini evuthayo kuyinketho ephephe kakhulu.
- Gcina indawo ezungeze isisindo se-sitter ingenalutho ukuze sikwazi ukuwa.
- Uma ukushisa kuphela, cisha amaswishi futhi ucishe ne-breaker.

## Ukupholisa nokukhipha {#cooling}

Ukupholisa kuyingxenye yokushisa. I-L&L no-Orton beluleka: "Vumela i-kiln iphole ngokwemvelo isivalo sivaliwe", futhi "Ungalokothi ufake umoya opholile ku-kiln eshisayo." Ukuvula kusesekuseni kakhulu "kungadala ukuqhekeka", futhi i-silica esezitsheni iyancipha ngokuzumayo njengoba ipholela ngale kuka-{{1063 °F; 573 °C}}. Uma ingekho i-controller, gcina i-kiln ivaliwe "kuze kube yilapho izinga lokushisa lehla kakhulu ngaphansi kokushisa okubomvu (900°F / 482°C)", futhi okungenani amahora angu-8. Ungalokothi uphoqe ukupholisa i-kiln esakhanya. Imifantu enemiphetho ebukhali ezitsheni ezishisiwe iwuphawu lokupholisa ngokushesha kakhulu.

### Uvula nini futhi ukhiphe

Label: Ukuvula nokukhipha

| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| L&L, ama-kiln esandla                                          | "Linda iphole ngokuphelele ngaphambi kokuyivula."                                 |

> Isiphakamiso sethu: vula isivalo kuphela ngaphansi kuka-{{200 °F; 93 °C}} cishe, futhi ukhiphe ngaphansi kuka-{{125 °F; 52 °C}} cishe, noma uma ungabamba izitsha kahle ngezandla ezingenalutho. Imithwalo ye-glaze, eminyene futhi efana nengilazi, idinga ukubekezela okwengeziwe kune-bisque. Uma ungenayo i-pyrometer, linda kuze kube yilapho i-kiln iphole ngokuphelele.

Funda ama-witness cone ngokushesha nje uma uvula i-kiln, ngaphambi kokuba kuhanjiswe lutho, futhi ubhale lokho okubonayo kusesekusha.

## Ngemuva kokushisa {#after}

### Kubhale

Bhala ukushisa ngakunye: usuku, i-kiln, umthwalo, uhlelo noma izikhathi zamaswishi, lokho i-timer ekufundayo, nokuthi i-witness cone ngayinye ne-cone ye-sitter kugobe kanjani, ishalofu ngeshalofu. Gcina nama-cone ashisiwe. U-Orton uphawula ukuthi "Ukuqhathanisa imiphumela yokushisa okungu-6 kuya ku-10 kokugcina kuzokhombisa izinguquko ezenzeka kancane ku-kiln, njengokuguga kwama-element okushisa." Ama-[rekhodi okushisa](/firing) e-Glazecalc angagcina lawa marekhodi, anamakholomu owakhethayo njengesikhathi, izinga lokushisa, i-cone, i-ramp nokubamba; imiphumela ye-glaze ingaya [emanothini](/notes) akho.

:::orton

### Ukunakekela i-kiln sitter

- Hlanza izisekelo ze-cone "ngemuva kokushisa ngakunye ngebhulashi elincane lensimbi". Uma i-wash noma i-cone encibilikile ingaphumi, jika izisekelo noma uzishintshe; u-Dawson uphakamisa ukugcina amapheya amabili.
- "UNGALOKOTHI USEBENZISE IZINTO ZOKUGCOBA (LUBRICANTS) ZANOMA YILUPHI UHLOBO KU-KILN-SITTER."
- Hlola i-pivot njalo ezinyangeni ezingu-6–12, futhi ushintshe induku egobile noma eguguleyo.
- Hlola ukulungiswa nge-firing gauge njalo ngemuva kokushisa okungu-30.

:::

### Amashalofu ne-kiln wash

I-kiln wash (ungqimba oluvikelayo olugcotshwa emashalofini) ivikela amashalofu ku-glaze econsayo futhi ivumela izimbiza ezishisiwe ziphakamiseke. Yigcobe phezulu eshalofini ngalinye naphansi kwe-kiln kuphela, "cishe ubukhulu bekhadi leposi". Umkhuba ongcono kakhulu we-L&L yizingqimba ezintathu ezincane, kushiswe phakathi kwezingqimba uma kungenzeka. Ababumbi abaningi baphinde babeke izingcezu ezinama-glaze agobhozayo phezu kwe-**cookie**, idiski ye-bisque ebamba amaconsi. Lokhu kungumkhuba ovamile, nakuba singatholanga seluleko somenzi we-kiln ngakho.

### Ama-element

Ama-element ayaguga: ungqimba lwe-oxide luyakhula, kugeleza ugesi omncane, futhi ukushisa kuhamba kancane. Ukubamba isikhathi eside ezingeni lokushisa eliphezulu kuguga ama-element ne-thermocouple ngokushesha. I-Skutt yeluleka ukushisa kokuhlola kwe-kiln entsha noma elungisiwe, ukuze ama-element akhe ungqimba lwe-oxide oluvikelayo. Landela imiyalelo yomenzi wakho yokuhlola nokushintsha ama-element.

## Izindandatho nama-bar {#rings}

Imboni yaseBrithani isebenzise nezinye izinkomba ezimbili ze-heatwork. Ama-**Bullers ring** angamadiski ayisicaba ancipha ngokulingana ne-heatwork futhi alinganiswa nge-gauge ngemuva kokushisa. Ama-**Holdcroft bar**, kusukela ngo-1898, ayatshekela phakathi phezu kwezisekelo ezimbili, umqondo ongemuva kwe-bar ye-kiln sitter. Asitholanga thebula eliguqula noma yiluphi lwazo lube yizinombolo zama-cone ka-Orton, ngakho lo mhlahlandlela usebenzisa ama-cone ka-Orton; sihlela ukuxoxa ngezindandatho nama-bar enguqulweni ezayo.
