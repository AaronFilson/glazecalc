## Ngaphambi kokuqala {#before-you-start}

Ukwenza i-glaze kusho ukukala **okokwakha** okuyimpuphu (amaminerali agayiwe, ubumba nama-**frit**, okuyingilazi eyenziwe efektri yagaywa yaba yimpuphu) kusuka kuresiphi, nokukuxuba namanzi kube yi-**slurry**, uketshezi olujiyile lwempuphu ephethwe ngamanzi. Bese uyihlunga, ulungise ukujiya kwayo, futhi uyihlole ebumbeni lwakho ngaphambi kokuyethemba embizeni. Zonke izinombolo kulo mhlahlandlela ziyisiqalo: amanzi, ukujiya nesihlungo esifanele i-glaze yakho yilokho okuboniswa ukuhlola kwakho, ngakho bhala phansi okwenzayo nokukutholayo.

### Okudingayo

- **Isikali** esifunda kuze kufike ku-0.01 g sokuhlola nokokufaka umbala, nesikhulu sama-batch angamakhilogremu.
- **Iziqukathi ezinezivalo**: eziphansi zokokwakha okomile, namabhakede e-glaze. UDerek Au ugcina ama-glaze okuhlola eziqukathini zesobho ezinezivalo.
- **Isikopi esihlanzekile sento ngayinye yokwakha**, ukuze impuphu eyodwa ingangeni kwenye.
- **Ithuluzi lokuxuba**: i-drill ene-paddle yokuxuba, noma ukuhlola okuncane i-blender egcinelwe i-glaze kuphela. I-whisk izokwenza uma ungenalutho olunye.
- **Izihlungo**, ezithengiswa e-UK njengama-sieve nama-**lawn**: i-mesh 80, kanye ne-mesh 100 noma 120 yama-glaze anokokufaka umbala.
- **I-graduated cylinder noma isirinji ka-100 mL**, sokukala i-specific gravity.
- **Amalebula, ipeni elingangenwa amanzi nencwajana yokubhala**, noma [amanothi](/notes) ne-[amarekhodi okushisa](/firing) e-Glazecalc.
- **Ama-test tile** enziwe ngobumba ozolufaka i-glaze.
- **I-respirator, amagilavu enjoloba, isipontshi ne-mop.**

> [!WARNING]
> **Impuphu ye-glaze eyomile iyingozi yothuli.** I-silica eyikristalu ingaba ngu-10 kuya ku-60% we-glaze eyomile, futhi i-Health and Safety Executive (HSE) yase-UK ixwayisa ngokuthi uthuli lwayo alubonakali ekukhanyeni okujwayelekile. Kopisha kancane usendaweni ephansi, gcina iziqukathi zivaliwe, manzisa impuphu ngokushesha okukhulu, futhi uhlanze kumanzi, ungalokothi usebenzise umshanelo. Gqoka i-respirator ekulingene kahle ngesikhathi izimpuphu zisomile (i-N95 noma i-P100 e-US, i-FFP2 noma i-FFP3 e-UK nase-EU, i-P2 noma i-P3 e-Australia nase-New Zealand). [Umhlahlandlela wokuxuba ngokuphepha](/guides/safe-mixing) uchaza ngokugcwele uthuli, ama-respirator nokuhlanza.

## Ukuthenga okokwakha {#buying-materials}

### Lapho ungathenga khona

Ababumbi bathenga kubahlinzeki be-ceramic, abathengisa ubumba, okokwakha kwe-glaze namathuluzi. Ithebula likhombisa abanye bezwe lakho, ngamunye uhlolwe kusayithi lesitolo uqobo, futhi ungakhetha elinye izwe noma isifunda ukuze ubone izitolo zakhona. Isitoko siyashintsha, ngakho hlola ngaphambi kokuoda. Abakhiqizi abakhulu abafana ne-Sibelco ne-Imerys bathengisela izimboni; ababumbi bathenga okokwakha kwabo ezitolo ezinjengalezi. Isayithi lokubhekisela le-ceramics i-Digitalfire ligcina uhlu lwezitolo zabahlinzeki zezinye izindawo.

::shops

### Ungathenga okungakanani

Isaka elijwayelekile lingu-**50 lb (22.7 kg)** e-US kanye no-**25 kg (55 lb)** kwezinye izindawo, ngakho womabili acishe alingane. Abahlinzeki abaningi baphinde bathengise amaphakethe angu-1, 2.5 noma 5 kg (cishe u-2.2, 5.5 noma 11 lb) okuhlola. I-Glazy, isizindalwazi samaresiphi esivulekile, yeluleka ukuthi uthenge okokwakha okusebenzisa njalo (i-feldspar, i-silica, i-kaolin, i-whiting, ne-frit ye-mid-fire) ngamasaka agcwele, avame ukushibha ngo-20 kuya ku-30%. Thenga **okokufaka umbala**, ama-oxide ensimbi nama-stain anika i-glaze umbala, ngamanani amancane: iningi lakho lisetshenziswa ngaphansi kuka-5%. Uhlu lwesibonelo lokuthenga lwe-Glazy lwafika cishe ku-$515 we-mid-fire no-$397 we-high-fire, ngamanani ase-US.

### Isethi yokuqala

Uhlu oluyisisekelo lwe-Glazy lwanoma yiliphi izinga lokushisa yi-silica, i-kaolin, i-feldspar, i-whiting, i-zinc oxide, ne-dolomite noma i-talc. Iningi lama-glaze e-mid-fire (i-cone 5 kuya ku-6) liphinde lidinge i-boron, ngokuvamile evela ku-frit, i-Glazy ethi "iyafana njalo futhi incibilika kancane kunama-borate aluhlaza". Isethi engezansi iyisiphakamiso sethu, esithathwe ohlwini lwe-Glazy nohlwini lwenkundla yokuxoxa. [Umhlahlandlela we-Izisekelo ze-glaze](/guides/glazing-basics) uchaza ukuthi into ngayinye yenzani.

### Amashidi edatha namashidi edatha yokuphepha

Cela imibhalo emibili yento ngayinye yokwakha. **Ishidi ledatha lobuchwepheshe** (TDS) linika **ukuhlaziywa kwamakhemikhali** ayo: isabelo se-oxide ngayinye empuphuni, ngesisindo. I-Glazecalc isebenza ngalokhu, futhi ungangeza okokwakha kusuka ekuhlaziyweni komhlinzeki [ekhasini Lokokwakha](/material). **Ishidi ledatha yokuphepha** (SDS) lisho ukuthi iphathwa kanjani impuphu eluhlaza. Gcina i-SDS ngayinye, futhi uyifunde ngaphambi kokuxuba.

Ukuhlaziywa kuyizilinganiso, hhayi izithembiso. I-Valentine Clays iphrinta ethi "MEAN VALUES. THESE DO NOT REPRESENT A SPECIFICATION" eshidini layo ledatha le-flint, futhi ukuhlaziywa kwe-Glazy kwe-Custer feldspar yeminyaka ehlukene kusukela cishe ku-68.5% kuya ku-72.4% we-silica. Siphakamisa ukuthi ubhale **inombolo ye-lot** yesaka ngalinye (ikhodi ye-batch yalo) emarekhodini akho, ukuze ukwazi ukubona ukuthi ushintsho ku-glaze owethembayo luvela yini kokokwakha.

I-HSE ixwayisa ngokuthi i-silica eyikristalu "ingabhalwa kabi njenge-'amorphous silica'". Futhi i-SDS imayelana nempuphu, hhayi imbiza: ayisho lutho ngokuthi i-glaze eshisiwe iphephile yini ekudleni. Ukuhlolwa kwaselabhorethri kwezinsimbi eziphuma embizeni eshisiwe (leach test) kuphela okungakubonisa lokho.

### Amagama ayashintsha, ngakho thenga ngamakhemikhali

Amagama ezinkampani ayeza ahambe njengoba izimayini zivalwa. I-Custer feldspar, eyayimbiwa e-South Dakota iminyaka engaphezu kwengu-75, yaphela ngasekupheleni kuka-2023. Lapho i-Clay Art Center ishintshela ku-G-200 AU naku-Mahavir, cishe ingxenye yama-glaze ayo yadinga ukulungiswa. I-G-200 ne-G-200 HP nazo azisekho, futhi i-Laguna manje ingenisa i-Mahavir, amakhemikhali ayo asondele kuwe-G-200.

**I-Gerstley Borate**, iminerali ye-boron yemvelo esemaresiphini amaningi amadala, ibilokhu iyivelakancane selokhu imayini yayo yavalwa ngo-2000, nakuba i-New Mexico Clay yabika ngoNovemba 2025 ukuthi i-Laguna yayihlinzeka nge-"Gerstley Borate Original". Imithombo ayivumelani nge-**Gillespie Borate** njengengena esikhundleni sayo. Umenzi wayo uyithengisa njengelingana iphawundi ngephawundi. I-Berkeley Potters Studio ithi "ayiyona into engena esikhundleni ngqo", futhi i-Digitalfire yathola ukuthi yashintsha umbala we-glaze. Indlela ephephile ukuziqhathanisa zombili ngama-oxide azo, bese uyahlola.

Amagama ezifunda nawo ayehluka. E-North America, i-**flint**, i-quartz ne-silica kusho i-silica egayiwe efanayo, futhi i-Digitalfire ithi cishe ngeke uyithole impuphu ye-flint yangempela kubahlinzeki. E-UK, i-flint iwumkhiqizo wayo: eye-Valentine iyi-silica e-calcined (eshisiwe). Zombili cishe ziyi-silica ehlanzekile, ngakho silindele ukuthi zisondelane, kodwa hlola ukuhlaziywa. Nge-kaolin, i-Glazy ithi ukushintshana okukodwa ngokukodwa, njenge-EPK esikhundleni se-china clay, "kuvame ukusebenza kahle, ikakhulukazi uma amanani \<10%".

Amaresiphi amaningi abala okokwakha okuthengiswa kwelinye izwe hhayi kwelinye. Ithebula elingezansi lithatha into ngayinye yokwakha esemtapweni we-Glazecalc engathengiswa lapho uthenga khona, futhi lithole eseduze kakhulu yohlobo lwayo ethengiswayo, ngokuhlaziywa kwazo. Inani likhombisa ukuthi zihlukene kangakanani: amagremu ama-oxide azo ashisiwe ahluka ngawo, ku-100 g ngamunye wokokwakha, ahlanganiswe kuwo wonke ama-oxide. Uma kungaphakathi kuka-15 g cishe, eyodwa ingavamise ukungena esikhundleni senye ekuhloleni kokuqala. Amagama aphethe amakhodi abenzi nezitolo uqobo, ukuze ukwazi ukuwacela ngegama.

::local-equivalents

> [!NOTE]
> I-Glazecalc iqhathanisa okokwakha ngalokho okulethayo ku-glaze eshisiwe, hhayi ngegama. [Iresiphi](/recipe) enento yokwakha engasenziwa isho ukuthi yini esetshenziswayo manje. **Phakamisa amanani futhi uqhathanise** ibala kabusha amanani ento engena esikhundleni, futhi **Fanisa nalokho enginakho** yenza iresiphi kabusha ngokokwakha okusesheshini lakho. Kokubili kufanisa ama-oxide ashisiwe kuphela, ngakho hlola i-batch encane kuqala.

## Ukugcina okokwakha nama-glaze {#storing}

### Okokwakha okomile

Gcina izimpuphu zomile, eziqukathini ezinezivalo ezihlala zivaliwe uma ungakopishi; i-Glazy yeluleka ezingangeni moya. Khetha iziqukathi eziphansi kunemigqomo ejulile: i-HSE iphawula ukuthi umgqomo ojulile ukwenza ukhothame ungene, futhi uthuli lunebanga elide lokuwa. Zigcine eshalofini, ukuze ukwazi ukuhlanza nge-mop ngaphansi. I-soda ash ne-Cornish stone kumunca umswakama emoyeni. Omunye umbumbi wenkundla yokuxoxa uzomisa ethreyini yokubhaka cishe ku-{{50 °C; 122 °F}}, futhi uxwayisa ngokuthi amabhakede apende amancane angaqhekeka egaraji elibanda kuze kuqandeke. Siphakamisa ukuthi ugcine okokwakha okuncibilikayo (i-soda ash, i-borax, i-lithium carbonate, nama-borate aluhlaza afana ne-Gerstley Borate) kungangeni moya futhi kungaphakathi endlini.

### Ama-glaze axutshiwe

I-glaze esebhakedeni ihlala ishintsha. Okokwakha okuncibilika kancane (i-nepheline syenite, i-lithium carbonate, i-Gerstley Borate, i-colemanite, i-strontium carbonate, nama-frit ngokujwayelekile) kushintsha ukugeleza kwayo ngokuhamba kwesikhathi futhi kungamilisa amakristalu amancane, ikakhulukazi egumbini elishisayo. I-Gerstley Borate ijiyisa i-slurry ibe yi-gel, "ngisho nasemaphesentini amancane". Amanzi ansundu phezu kwe-glaze ezinzile ayinsimbi encibilikile: wathele, bese uwafaka esikhundleni ngamanzi ahlanzekile. Lindela futhi:

- **Ukuqina phansi**: i-glaze exutshwe yajiya ngokwanele ukuze ingadingi okokwengeza ivamise ukuzinza ibe yisendlalelo esiqinile. Ukuyihlambulula nokuyenza i-gel kuyasiza (bheka i-Specific gravity).
- **Amaphunga**, avela kumagciwane adla ama-gum afana ne-CMC. I-glaze enuka isengasetshenziswa uma igcobeka kahle.
- **Ukuqandeka**: vikela amabhakede kukho. Ncibilikisa eliqandekile ekushiseni kwegumbi, futhi ulihlole ukuthi alinayo yini imifantu.

Ungabuyiseli i-glaze esetshenzisiwe ebhakedeni elikhulu, noma ucwilise amabhulashi kulo. Ngaphambi kokusebenzisa i-glaze egciniwe, yigovuze, ukale i-specific gravity yayo futhi, uyihlunge uma inezigaxa noma amakristalu.

### Amalebula

Faka ilebula kuwo wonke amabhakede ngepeni elingangenwa amanzi. UZakin, ebhalela i-Ceramic Arts Network, unika igama le-glaze, iresiphi yayo ephelele kuhlanganise nokokufaka umbala, i-cone, usuku futhi, esitudiyo esabelwanayo, izinhlamvu zokuqala zegama lakho. Siphakamisa ukuthi wengeze i-specific gravity oyihlosile nanoma yimaphi ama-Epsom salts noma i-CMC. I-UT Austin iphinde isebenzise imibala: amabhakede awolintshi e-low-fire, amhlophe e-mid-fire. Phatha i-glaze engenalebula njengengase iqukethe umthofu (lead, Pb), futhi ungayisebenzisi ezimbizeni zokudla.

## Ukukala {#weighing}

### Amaphesenti nama-batch

Iresiphi inika into ngayinye yokwakha njengephesenti lesisindo esomile. Okokwakha okuyinhloko kuvame ukuhlangana kube ngu-100, nokokufaka umbala kuvame ukungezwa ngaphezulu. Ukwenza i-**batch**, inani olixuba ngesikhathi esisodwa, phindaphinda yonke inombolo ngobukhulu be-batch buhlukaniswe ngo-100. Ku-5000 g, i-UT Austin iphindaphinda ngo-50, ngakho into engu-4% iba ngu-200 g.

> [!NOTE]
> [Ekhasini leresiphi](/recipe), **Linganisa ne-batch** yenza ukuphindaphinda: faka u-500 g, ngokwesibonelo, futhi wonke amanani aba ngamagremu okukala, noma amaphawundi nama-ounce uma uwakhetha ngaphansi kwe-Izilungiselelo. **Phrinta** inganika uhlu lwe-batch kuphela, olunesamba esiqhubekayo sokukala ebhakedeni elilodwa nebhokisi lokumaka eceleni kwento ngayinye.

### Khetha isikali senani lakho elincane kakhulu

**I-resolution** yesikali, isinyathelo esincane kunazo zonke esisibonisayo, inquma ukuthi ungakala kahle inani elincane kangakanani. Ekuhloleni kuka-100 g, okokufaka umbala okungu-0.5% kungu-0.5 g. Isikali esifunda kuze kufike ku-0.1 g singaphambuka ngo-0.05 g, cishe u-10% walokho; ku-0.15 g, iphutha licishe libe yingxenye eyodwa kwezintathu. Lokho kungukubala kwethu, kodwa kubonisa ukuthi kungani okokufaka umbala kudinga isikali esicolekile.

Iseluleko siyehluka ngokuthi udinga isikali esicolekile kangakanani. UDerek Au wathola ukuthi isikali sedijithali esishibhile sika-200 g sasingalungile njalo. Uncoma isikali sika-200 g esifunda kuze kufike ku-0.01 g uma uthenga esisodwa kuphela, futhi uthi i-triple-beam balance enhle "ithembeke kakhulu kunesikali sedijithali esishibhile". Ukukhetha okuphephile, ngokubona kwethu, ngu-0.01 g wokuhlola (50 kuya ku-200 g) nokokufaka umbala, nesikali sebhentshi esifunda kuze kufike ku-0.1 g noma 1 g sama-batch ka-1 kg (cishe u-2 lb) nangaphezulu. Hlola zombili ngezikhathi ezithile nge-calibration weight.

### Kala ngokulandelana, futhi umake ngayinye

1. Hlola ukuthi unokwanele kwayo yonke into yokwakha.
2. Gqoka i-respirator yakho. Hlola ukuthi isikali sihlanzekile futhi silinganile.
3. Beka isiqukathi esingenalutho esikalini bese ucindezela **tare**, okusetha ukufunda kube ngu-zero ukuze ukale kuphela lokho okwengezayo.
4. Kala into ngayinye ngokulandelana ngesikopi sayo esihlanzekile, vala isiqukathi ngasinye ngaphambi kokuvula esilandelayo, futhi umake into ngayinye ngokushesha nje uma isingenile.

UDerek Au ukala endishini eyodwa ngezinqwaba ezihlukene, ukuze iphutha likwazi ukukopishwa liphume. Uma ukala ebhakedeni elilodwa ngaphandle kokwenza i-tare phakathi kokokwakha, isamba esiqhubekayo se-Glazecalc sikutshela ukuthi isikali kufanele sifunde malini ngemva kwento ngayinye.

## Ukuxuba nokuhlunga {#mixing}

### Amanzi amangakanani

Imithombo ayivumelani. U-Derek Au uqala kujiyile, ngo-50 mL wamanzi ku-100 g wempushana, bese engeza amaningi kamuva. I-Potterycrafts inikeza u-65 mL ku-100 g we-glaze eyodwa eyimpushana. I-UT Austin isebenzisa izisindo ezilinganayo zamanzi nempushana, futhi lapho i-Digitalfire ifaka u-20 kg wempushana ku-20 kg wamanzi, yakala i-specific gravity (i-SG) engu-1.46.

Amanzi ashesha ukwengezwa futhi ahamba kancane ukukhishwa, ngakho qala ngamancane. Isilinganiso sethu: qala ngo-**cishe u-70 kuya ku-80 mL ku-100 g** (u-700 kuya ku-800 mL ngekhilogremu, noma cishe u-11 kuya ku-12 US fl oz ngephawundi). Cwilisa, xuba futhi uhlunge kulokho kujiya, bese wengeza amanzi kancane kancane kuze kube yilapho i-SG ilungile. U-1 mL wamanzi unesisindo esingu-1 g, ngakho ungawakala. Uma weqa, yeka i-glaze izinze, bese ususa amanzi acwebile angaphezulu ngesipontshi noma ngokuwathela.

### Amathuluzi namanzi

- **Umxubi one-propeller.** I-Digitalfire ithi omuhle "ubalulekile". Isibhoro sikagesi esinentambo, esinomxubi, senza umsebenzi ebhakedeni. I-UT Austin iwusebenzisa okungenani imizuzu engu-4 kuya ku-5 ngemuva kokuthi i-glaze ibukeke ixubekile, futhi igcina insingo kude nebhakede, engakhuhla ipulasitiki kulo.
- **I-blender.** "Isebenza kahle kakhulu kuma-batch amancane" (Digitalfire). Yigcinele i-glaze kuphela: akumele ibuyele ekhishini.
- **I-whisk noma induku.** Ukukhetha kuka-Zakin lapho kungekho mxubi.

Amanzi nawo ayahluka, futhi i-Digitalfire iphawula ukuthi ashintsha indlela i-slurry eziphatha ngayo. U-Derek Au usebenzisa amanzi e-reverse osmosis ngoba amanzi akhe empompini alukhuni. Noma yini oyisebenzisayo, yibhale phansi.

### Isinyathelo ngesinyathelo

1. Gqoka i-respirator yakho, namagilavu enjoloba uma izandla zakho zizongena ngaphakathi. U-Zakin ugqoka imaski kusukela ekuqaleni kokukala kuze kube yilapho i-glaze isimanzi.
2. Kala cishe u-70 kuya ku-80 mL wamanzi ku-100 g ngamunye wempushana ebhakedeni.
3. Faka impushana ekaliwe kancane, usendaweni ephansi. I-UT Austin ifaka impushana emanzini afudumele; u-Zakin no-Au bafaka amanzi empushaneni. Siphakamisa impushana emanzini, ephakamisa uthuli oluncane, ngaphandle kweresiphi ene-bentonite eningi: xuba izimpushana zayo ezomile ndawonye kuqala.
4. **Yicwilise**: yeka impushana icwile ngaphandle kokuxova. I-Digitalfire ilinda cishe imizuzu engu-20. Izimpushana ezine-gum noma i-binder zidinga ukuhlala ubusuku bonke.
5. Xuba kuze kungabi khona izindawo ezomile noma izigaxa.
6. Kuhlunge kabili (bheka ngezansi).
7. Kumboze futhi ukuyeke kuphumule ubusuku bonke, bese uxuba futhi uhlole phansi ukuthi akukho yini ungqimba oluqinile.
8. Kala i-SG, futhi wengeze amanzi kancane kancane ukufinyelela okuhlosiwe. Ukuze wazi i-SG eyomile ye-glaze yakho, kala ne-slurry esiqediwe (bheka ngezansi).
9. Faka ilebula ebhakedeni, futhi ubhale phansi lokho okwenzile.
10. Hlanza ngamanzi: sula phansi ngomopho futhi usule yonke indawo, kuhlanganise nangaphandle kwezitsha zokokwakha.

### Ukuhlunga

**Isihlungo** siyinetha elisohlakeni. Ukuphusha i-glaze ngaso ngebhulashi kwephula izigaxa ezishiywa ukuxuba futhi kubambe izinhlayiya ezingashisa zibe amachashazi. Lapho inombolo ye-**mesh** iphakeme, isihlungo sicolekile: i-mesh engu-80 inezimbobo ezicishe zibe ngu-0.18 mm, ne-mesh engu-100 cishe u-0.15 mm.

Imithombo incoma ama-mesh ahlukene. U-Zakin uhlunga kabili nge-mesh engu-50 noma engu-80. U-Derek Au usebenzisa i-mesh engu-120 kuma-glaze amaningi, ikakhulukazi lawo ane-cobalt noma i-iron: ukudlula kathathu nge-mesh engu-80 kwasashiya amachashazi e-iron ku-celadon eyodwa, futhi ukudlula kanye nge-mesh engu-120 kwakulungisa. ## I-specific gravity {#specific-gravity}

**I-specific gravity** (i-SG) ikutshela ukuthi ingakanani impushana ku-slurry yakho. Yisisindo sevolumu ye-glaze sihlukaniswe ngesisindo sevolumu efanayo yamanzi. Amanzi angu-1.00, futhi i-glaze engu-{{SG 1.45}} inesisindo esingu-145 g ku-100 mL. Ukuxuba kuze kufike ku-SG ekaliwe kuyindlela yokuthola ukujiya okufanayo, nongqimba olufanayo, njalo. Ayinamayunithi, ngakho iyafana ngamagremu noma ama-ounce.

### Yikale ngokukala isisindo sika-100 mL

1. Xova i-glaze ukuze kungabi khona okuzinzile phansi.
2. Beka i-**graduated cylinder** ka-100 mL (ishubhu ende yokukala enamamaki) noma isirinji ka-100 mL esikalini, bese usetha isikali ku-zero (tare).
3. Yigcwalise nge-glaze kuze kube ngu-100 mL ngqo, ufunde imaki ezingeni lamehlo.
4. Yikale bese uhlukanisa amagremu ngo-100: u-145 g yi-SG 1.45.

I-Digitalfire nayo ifaka imaki enkomishini ezingeni lika-500 g wamanzi, iyigcwalise nge-glaze kuze kufike emakini, bese ihlukanisa isisindo ngo-500; isilinganiso esikhulu, esigcwele kakhulu sinembe kakhulu. I-**hydrometer**, into entantayo efundwa lapho ihlala khona ku-glaze, iyashesha kodwa ingafunda kabi ama-glaze ajiyile noma aqinile. Ama-hydrometer ase-UK, njengawe-Potclays, afunda ngamadigri e-Baumé (°Bé). Ngokubala kwethu, i-SG = 145 ÷ (145 − °Bé), ngakho u-46 °Bé ucishe ube ngu-1.46. Izincwadi ezindala zase-UK zinikeza i-**pint weight**, ama-ounce ku-pint eyodwa ye-imperial ye-glaze: yihlukanise ngo-20, ngakho u-29 oz ucishe ube yi-SG 1.45 (nakho ukubala kwethu).

### Thola i-SG eyomile ye-glaze yakho ngenkathi uxuba

U-2.6 ongenhla ukuqagela. Isibalo se-glaze yakho, ukuminyana kwempushana yayo eyomile, singabalwa ngezinombolo ezintathu ongazithatha ngenkathi uxuba. Ngaso, ungabala kamuva nge-SG ukuthi ingakanani impushana kunoma yiliphi ibhakede laleyo glaze, namanzi e-batch elandelayo. Ukubala kwethu, hhayi okomthombo:

1. Bhala phansi isisindo sempushana eyomile ku-batch: isamba seresiphi.
2. Kala ibhakede elingenalutho ngaphambi kokuqala, noma ubhale isisindo salo kulo.
3. Xuba, hlunga futhi ulethe i-glaze ku-SG yayo njengokujwayelekile. Phusha konke ngesihlungo futhi ukuhlambululele ebhakedeni, ukuze kungasali mpushana ngaphandle.
4. Kala ibhakede eligcwele bese ukhipha isisindo sebhakede. Leso yisisindo se-slurry.
5. Kala i-SG.

Bese:

- **Amanzi** = isisindo se-slurry − isisindo sempushana.
- **Ivolumu ye-slurry** ngo-mL = isisindo se-slurry ngamagremu ÷ i-SG.
- **Ivolumu yempushana** = ivolumu ye-slurry − amanzi (u-1 g wamanzi ungu-1 mL).
- **I-SG eyomile** = isisindo sempushana ÷ ivolumu yempushana.

Isibonelo: u-1000 g wempushana wenza u-1980 g we-slurry ku-{{SG 1.45}}. Amanzi angu-980 g, i-slurry ingu-1980 ÷ 1.45 = cishe u-1366 mL, impushana ithatha u-1366 − 980 = 386 mL, futhi i-SG eyomile ingu-1000 ÷ 386 = cishe u-2.59.

Kala yonke i-slurry ekugcineni, hhayi amanzi ngenkathi uwathela, ukuze amanzi ahwamukayo noma achaphazekayo angabalwa. Amaphutha amancane ayakhula lapha: ukufunda kwe-SG okuphambuke ngo-0.01 kuhambisa impendulo cishe ngo-2 kuya ku-4%, ngaphezulu ku-glaze emncane. Sebenzisa i-batch ka-1 kg noma ngaphezulu, isikali esinembile, nendlela yenkomishi ka-500 g engenhla ye-SG. Iningi lokokwakha okuvamile liphakathi kuka-2.5 no-2.9, ngakho isibalo esikude kakhulu ngaphandle kwalokho, ku-glaze engenayo i-zircon eningi noma umthofu, kungenzeka kusho iphutha lokukala. Bhala isibalo kuresiphi.

### Usawoti we-Epsom, i-CMC gum ne-bentonite

**Usawoti we-Epsom** wenza i-glaze iqine kancane lapho imile futhi igeleze futhi lapho ixovwa. Lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi **ukuyi-flocculate**, futhi i-glaze eziphatha kanje iyi-**thixotropic**: isakazeka ngokulinganayo kakhulu, iconsa kancane futhi izinza kancane. I-Digitalfire iphakamisa ukuqala cishe ngo-2 g nge-gallon uma i-SG isilungile (cishe u-0.5 g ngelitha, ukuguqula kwethu) nokulungisa inani njalo uma uxuba, ngoba ayikho iresiphi okufanele ibeke umthamo. Siphakamisa ukuwuncibilikisa emanzini ashisayo amancane kuqala. Esibonelweni se-Digitalfire, i-glaze engu-{{SG 1.45}} yaconsa, ngakho amanzi ayehlisela ku-{{SG 1.43}} futhi usawoti we-Epsom wayiqinisa futhi yaba njengokhilimu, futhi yasakazeka ngokulinganayo. Ukuhlola okusheshayo: xova, yima bese ubuka. I-glaze eqinile iqhubeka inyakaza cishe imizuzwana engu-2 noma engu-3, bese iyama futhi ibuyele emuva kancane.

**I-CMC gum** (carboxymethyl cellulose) yenza i-slurry inamathele kakhulu futhi yome kancane, futhi ishiya ungqimba oluqinile olubambelela esitsheni. Ibaluleke kakhulu kuma-glaze ebhulashi nakuma-glaze ane-frit eningi. Isibonelo se-Digitalfire sixuba u-3.75 g ku-1000 g we-slurry, futhi kufanele ingene nge-blender. I-glaze ene-CMC ngeke iqine ngosawoti we-Epsom, futhi njengoba amagciwane edla i-gum, ingashintsha ebhakedeni futhi iqale ukunuka.

**I-bentonite**, ubumba olucolekile kakhulu, isiza ukugcina ezinye izinhlayiya zintanta. Ama-glaze okucwilisa ngokuvamile athembele kubumba oluluhlaza olungu-15 kuya ku-25% kulokhu. Kuresiphi enobumba obuncane, cishe u-1 kuya ku-3% we-bentonite uyasiza; i-Digitalfire ithi i-glaze enobumba obungaphezu kuka-15% akufanele idinge i-bentonite engaphezu kuka-1% wesisindo se-glaze eyomile.

## Ama-test tile nama-line blend {#testing}

### Qala kancane

U-Derek Au ukhuphuka ngezinyathelo ezintathu: ukuhlola kuka-50 noma ku-100 g, isivivinyo sika-1 kuya ku-2 kg uma kusebenza, bese kuba yi-batch ye-studio ka-5 kuya ku-10 kg. Ngokwesilinganiso sethu, ukuhlola kuka-100 g ku-{{SG 1.45}} kwenza cishe u-135 mL (cishe u-4½ US fl oz) we-slurry, okwanele ukucwilisa ama-tile amancane ambalwa enkomishini emincane.

### Hlola kubumba lwakho

I-glaze ingabukeka ihluke kakhulu kobunye ubumba noma kwenye i-kiln: i-Clay Art Center yahlola i-glaze yayo ngayinye eyenziwe kabusha ezinhlotsheni ezine zobumba, ku-cone 6 naku-cone 10. Umhlahlandlela we-Potclays wabaqalayo wokuhlola i-glaze uphakamisa:

- ama-**test tile** angu-6 kuya ku-10 (izingcezu ezincane zokuzama i-glaze) obumba ozolusebenzisa ngempela, uphawula ukuthi ashiswe nge-bisque noma omile du
- ushintsho olulodwa ngesikhathi
- ukucwilisa kanye nokucwilisa kabili, ukuze ubone i-glaze emncane neshubile
- yonke i-tile emthwalweni ofanayo we-kiln
- imaki ku-tile ngayinye ngaphambi kokushisa, ngesitembu, ngokubaza noma ngepensela ye-underglaze. Ama-tile ashisiwe ayafana, futhi imaki eyenziwe ngaphambi kokushisa iyahlala ku-kiln, ngakho ungahlala wazi ukuthi i-tile ngayinye ingeyaluphi uhlobo lweresiphi.

Siphakamisa ama-tile ama mpo, ngoba i-tile eyisicaba ifihla ukuthi i-glaze igeleza ibangakanani, anomugqa onokwakheka okuveza ukuthi i-glaze iphuka kanjani emaphethelweni, nonyawo olungenalutho. Beka ngayinye esitsheni esincane noma esicucwini se-bisque, noma eshalofini elinokufakwe i-kiln wash, ukubamba amaconsi. Beka i-**witness cone**, i-cone encane yobumba egoba lapho isithole ukushisa okwanele, eduze kwama-tile ukukhombisa lokho akutholile; [umhlahlandlela wokushisa](/guides/firing) uchaza ama-cone. Nika i-tile ngayinye ikhodi emfushane, futhi uyibhale eceleni kweresiphi emarekhodini akho. U-Willers, ebhalela i-Ceramic Arts Network, usebenzisa izinombolo nezinhlamvu, futhi ubhala uhlelo ngokwalo ekuqaleni kwencwadi.

### Ama-line blend

I-**line blend** ingumugqa wezivivinyo ezihamba ngezinyathelo ezilinganayo zisuka kokuxutshwa okukodwa ziye kokunye: zisuka ku-glaze eyodwa ziye kwesibili, noma zisuka ku-glaze eyisisekelo ziye ku-glaze efanayo enokokufaka umbala okuningi. U-Derek Au, ngokwesibonelo, unyusa i-iron oxide nge-glaze eyisisekelo ecwebile ngezinyathelo zika-1%. Indlela yevolumu ehlotshaniswa no-Ian Currie yonga isikhathi, ngoba uxuba izingxenye ezimbili zokugcina kuphela:

1. Xuba ama-glaze amabili okugcina, u-A no-B, njengama-batch osayizi ofanayo, futhi uhlunge womabili.
2. Engeza amanzi kulowo onevolumu encane kuze kube yilapho womabili enevolumu emanzi efanayo ngqo.
3. Xova womabili, bese udonsa ukuxutshwa ngakunye ngesirinji ukufaka enkomishini yakho enenombolo.
4. Xova inkomishi ngayinye bese ucwilisa i-tile.

Esibonelweni sethu esixazululiwe, u-A ungu-100 g wesisekelo esicwebile, futhi u-B uyafana kodwa kwengezwe u-4 g we-red iron oxide (ukwengeza okungu-4%). I-millilitre ngayinye ka-B iqukethe ingxenye efanayo yesisekelo njenge-millilitre ka-A, kanye ne-iron yakho, ngakho izinkomishi zihamba zisuka ekungabini ne-iron ziye ku-4% ngezinyathelo zika-1%. Ingxenye ngayinye yokugcina inikeza u-100 mL ku-blend, okuhlanganiswa yi-batch ka-100 g ngokwesilinganiso sethu.
