## Idan an haɗiye wani abu {#if-something-is-swallowed}

> [!NOTE]
> Idan akwai yiwuwar yaro ko dabbar gida ya haɗiye, ya shaƙa ko ya zubar da wani kayan glaze, ku kira layin guba na yankinku yanzu, ko da ya yi kama da lafiya ƙalau. Kada ku jira alamun ciwo. Idan wani ya suma, ba ya numfashi, yana fama da numfashi ko yana farfaɗiya, ku fara kiran lambar gaggawa.

Wannan jagora ba shawarar likita ba ce: cibiyar guba, likita ko likitan dabbobi ne ke yanke abin da za a yi. Wannan shafi yana taimaka muku ku kai gare su da sauri, kuma, mafi kyau ma, ku guji buƙatar hakan.

::poison-lines

Muna ba da shawarar ku ajiye lambobin yankinku a wayarku yau, da kuma a kan kati a cikin ƙofar wurin aikinku.

### Abin da za ku shirya kafin ku kira

- Sunan kayan daidai yadda aka rubuta shi a kan buhu ko bokiti, da takardar bayaninsa ko **takardar bayanan aminci** (SDS: takardar mai sayarwa da ke bayyana haɗarin kaya da taimakon farko). Hoton buhun ma ya isa.
- Ga glaze da aka haɗa, tsarin glaze ɗinsa. Tsarin glaze ɗinku na Glazecalc shi ne jerin abubuwan da ke cikin glaze ɗin.
- Kusan nawa, kuma yaushe.
- Ta wace hanya: an haɗiye, an shaƙa, a kan fata ko a cikin ido.
- Duk wata alamar ciwo zuwa yanzu.
- Kimanin nauyin yaron ko dabbar, domin adadi ɗaya ya fi muhimmanci ga ƙaramin jiki. Ga dabbar gida, kuma irinta, shekarunta da duk wata matsalar lafiya.

### Abin da ba za ku yi ba

> [!WARNING]
> NHS na ba da shawara: kada ku yi ƙoƙarin sa mutumin ya yi amai, kuma kada ku ba shi komai ya ci ko ya sha. Ga dabbar gida, kada ku yi komai har sai likitan dabbobi ko layin guba na dabbobi ya gaya muku abin da za ku yi.

Idan za ku je asibiti, NHS ta ce ku ɗauki kwalin kayan ko samfurinsa idan hakan ba shi da haɗari. Ga kayan glaze, wannan na nufin takardar bayanin buhun ko takardar bayanan aminci.

## Abin da ya fi haɗari idan an haɗiye {#most-dangerous}

Kayan glaze na iya cutar da iyali ta hanyoyi biyu daban-daban:

- Haɗari **na gaggawa** (cutarwa daga adadi ɗaya, cikin 'yan sa'o'i) shi ne haɗiye kaya **mai narkewa**, wanda ke narkewa a cikin ruwa ko acid na ciki. Ajiya a kulle da lambobin da ke sama ne ke magance shi.
- Haɗari **na dogon lokaci** (cutarwa daga ƙananan adadi masu yawa) ƙura ce, musamman ta silica da darma (lead, Pb), wadda ba ta nuna alamun ciwo da farko kuma tana bin tufafi zuwa gida. Halayen tsafta ne ke magance shi.

Yawancin glaze silica ne, kaolin da feldspar, waɗanda da ƙyar suke narkewa. Muna ɗaukar su a matsayin haɗarin numfashi na dogon lokaci, ba gaggawar haɗiye ba. Kayan masu narkewa da ke ƙasa su ne za ku fara kullewa.

### Masu launi da darma

**Masu launi** su ne oxide na ƙarfe da stain da ke ba glaze launinsa: copper, cobalt, chrome, manganese, nickel, stain na cadmium-selenium da sauransu. Wani shafin aminci na ginin tukwane na jami'a ya ambaci darma (lead, Pb), cadmium, cobalt da chromium cikin abubuwan glaze masu haɗari musamman. Ba mu sami adadi masu inganci ga kowane mai launi ba, don haka ku kira ko da wane adadi ne. Bokitin glaze da aka haɗa yana ɗauke da kayan da ke cikin buhunan da aka samo shi daga ciki, sai dai a jike.

Babban haɗarin darma daban ne: ba cokali ɗaya ba, sai dai ƙananan adadin ƙura da ake haɗiye cikin makonni ko watanni, ba tare da alamun gargaɗi ba (duba Darma da yara, a ƙasa).

## Storing materials at home {#storing-materials lang=en}

No official body sets rules for storing glaze materials at home, but health and safety pages agree: keep the work in its own space, away from children. Pennsylvania's health department, writing about hobbies that use lead, says to "Utilize a designated workspace or garage for your hobby and keep children away from this area."

### A storage checklist

These are general poison-prevention rules applied to glaze. No ceramics standard sets them, but we suggest all of them:

- Keep dry materials locked away: a cabinet, or a room, garage or shed that locks. A high shelf helps, but children climb. If you can lock up only a few things at first, start with the soluble materials and colorants.
- Keep materials in their original bags or tubs. If you move one into another container, label it with the name exactly as written on the bag.
- Never use food or drink containers (yogurt tubs, jars, bottles) for glaze materials, even labeled. A child knows what a yogurt tub holds.
- Keep studio tools (scoops, spoons, whisks, sieves, scales) in the studio. Never use kitchen tools for glaze, or glaze tools for food.
- Keep wet glazes in labeled buckets with the lids on.
- Keep each material's safety data sheet with it, on paper or on your phone.
- Keep nothing from the studio in the kitchen: no bags, buckets, tools or test tiles.

For buying and storing materials in more depth, see [How to make a glaze](/guides/making-a-glaze).

### Glaze waste and rinse water

Never pour glaze, or water you have rinsed glaze tools in, down the sink or onto the garden. The UK Crafts Council suggests a separate bucket of water for washing hands, brushes, sieves and tools. Let it settle overnight, pour off the clear water, then dry the settled glaze and put it in the trash (it goes to landfill), never down the drain.

We suggest a lid on that bucket: the sludge holds the same metals as the glaze, and pets drink standing water. Disposal rules vary from place to place, so check each product's safety data sheet, and ask your local council or water company if you are unsure.

Some studios fire the dried sludge before they throw it away. They put it in an old bowl that will be used up: after the firing the bowl cannot be used again. Fill the bowl only a quarter full, because the sludge foams and bubbles as it melts, and cover it with a plate. Set it on a kiln-washed shelf or a spare slab, in case it runs over. The firing turns the sludge into glass, which holds the metals much more tightly than loose powder, so it is safer in a landfill. Handle the dry sludge as you would dry glaze, with a respirator and without making dust.

## Ajiye ƙura a wurin aiki {#keeping-dust-in-the-studio}

Ma'aikatar lafiya ta Pennsylvania ta faɗi matsalar a fili: "Ƙura mai ɗauke da darma daga sha'awarku na iya manne wa tufafinku, takalmanku, gashinku, fatarku da kayanku, kuma cikin sauƙi ta koma motarku da saman abubuwa a gidanku." CDC ta ƙara da cewa manya "na iya kawo darma gida tare da su kuma su sa iyalansu ga darma ba tare da sun sani ba." Jagorar Pennsylvania ta ba da maganin:

- Ku wanke hannuwanku kafin ku ci abinci.
- Ku cire tufafi da takalman wurin aiki kafin ku shiga gida.
- Ku wanke tufafin wurin aiki daban da wankin iyali.
- Ku share gida da mota akai-akai da injin tsotsar ƙura mai **HEPA** (wanda ke da matatar mai inganci sosai da ke kama ƙura mai laushi).

### Dokokin gida da muke ba da shawara

- Kada a yi aikin yumɓu ko glaze a kan teburin kicin ko na cin abinci, ko da na ɗan lokaci ne.
- Ku tsabtace wurin aiki da ruwa. Wani shafin aminci na ginin tukwane na jami'a ya ce ku guji share ƙasa a bushe, ku yi amfani da injin tsotsar ƙura mai matatar HEPA kawai, kuma ku goge ƙasa da ruwa kowace rana. Ku kuma goge saman abubuwa da ruwa bayan kowane zaman aiki.
- Ku ɗauki duk ƙurar glaze kamar ƙurar darma, ko da glaze ɗinku babu darma a cikinsu. Ƙura mai ɗauke da barium, chrome, cobalt ko manganese tana bin hanya ɗaya zuwa gida.
- Ku goge hannuwanku da farcenku kafin ku ɗauki yaro, ku dafa abinci ko ku shafa dabbar gida.
- Ku hana yara wasa a wurin aiki, kuma kada ku bari ya zama hanyar gajarta zuwa farfajiya ko gona.

### Ciki da shayarwa

Muna ba da shawarar ƙarin kulawa idan wani a gida yana da ciki ko yana shayarwa. Ku hana su shiga wurin aiki yayin da kuke sarrafa busassun kaya, kuma idan kun yi amfani da darma, ku tambayi likitansu ko ungozomarsu ko gwajin darma a jini yana da ma'ana ga iyalin gidan.

Don sarrafa ƙura yayin da kuke aiki (respirator, hanyoyin amfani da ruwa da iska), duba [Haɗawa cikin aminci da iska mai kyau](/guides/safe-mixing).

## Darma da yara {#lead-and-children}

Darma (lead, Pb) haɗari ne mai shiru. CDC ta lissafa "Haɗawa ko shafa glaze ko launuka masu ɗauke da darma" cikin sha'awoyin da ka iya sa mutane ga darma, kuma ta yi gargaɗi cewa "Yawancin yara da manya da suka sadu da darma ba su da alamun ciwo." A kalmominta, "Hanya ɗaya tilo ta sanin ko kai ko yaronka ka sadu da darma ita ce gwajin darma a jini."

### Dalilin da yara ke ɗaukar ƙari

Yara suna ɗaukar ƙurar gida fiye da manya. Suna shaƙar iska fiye da nauyin jikinsu, kuma EPA ta US ta lura cewa ƙananan yara "galibi suna da yawan haɗiye ƙasa da ƙura saboda halayensu na musamman kamar rarrafe da kai hannu ko abu baki." Ga darma, ƙurar da yaro ke haɗiye daga ƙasa, hannu da kayan wasa ta fi muhimmanci fiye da wadda yake shaƙa.

### Gwajin jini da lambar 3.5

Ana auna darma a jini da **µg/dL** (microgram na darma a kowane deciliter, wato kashi ɗaya cikin goma na lita, na jini). **Ma'aunin darma a jini na nuni** na CDC shi ne 3.5 µg/dL, wanda aka saukar daga 5 µg/dL a 2021. Shi ne matakin da kashi 97.5% na yaran US masu shekaru 1 zuwa 5 ke ƙasa da shi, don haka yaron da ke kansa ko sama da shi yana da darma a jininsa fiye da kusan dukan yara masu shekarunsa. CDC ta ce ma'aunin "ba a kan lafiya aka gina shi ba kuma ba ma'aunin doka ba ne", kuma lokacin da ta saukar da shi ta ce "Saduwa da darma a kowane mataki tana cutar da yara."

> [!NOTE]
> Don haka 3.5 µg/dL alama ce ta ci gaba da bincike, ba iyakar aminci ba. Sakamakon da ke ƙasa da shi ba ya nufin darma (Pb) ba ta yi wata cuta ba. Sakamakon da ke kansa ko sama da shi yana nufin a nemo inda darma ke fitowa; jagorar CDC ita ce a tabbatar da sakamakon huda yatsa da samfurin jini daga jijiya. Likitoci a wajen US suna amfani da jagororinsu, kuma likitanku zai bayyana abin da sakamako ke nufi.

### Abin da muke ba da shawara

- Ku yi amfani da glaze marasa darma inda za ku iya. A gidan da akwai ƙananan yara, muna ba da shawarar kada a yi amfani da darma ko kaɗan.
- Idan kun yi amfani da **frit na darma** (darma da aka riga aka narkar a cikin gilashi aka niƙa ta zama gari), ko tsofaffin glaze da ba ku san abin da ke cikinsu ba, ku tambayi likitan yaronku game da gwajin darma a jini.
- Ku bi dokokin ƙura da ke sama ga kowane glaze, ba waɗanda ke da darma kawai ba.

Glazecalc tana kashe darma sai dai idan kun zaɓe ta a [Saituna](/account). Idan darma a kashe take, ba za ta taɓa ƙara darma a tsarin glaze ko ta ba da shawararta ba.

## Dabbobin gida {#pets}

> [!NOTE]
> Ba mu sami wani bincike ko jagorar likitan dabbobi da aka rubuta don dabbobin gida a gidajen maginan tukwane ba. Shawarar da ke wannan sashe hankali ne kawai, wanda aka gina a kan gaskiya ta gaba ɗaya game da darma (lead, Pb), barium da tsuntsaye. Ba daga majiyar likitan dabbobi ta fito ba.

### Abin da aka sani

Pet Poison Helpline ta ce "Darma guba ce ga dukan nau'in halittu, amma dabbobi ƙanana da/ko masu rashin abinci mai gina jiki ne suka fi haɗarin guba", da alamu kamar amai, gudawa, rashin cin abinci, tangaɗi, farfaɗiya da makanta. Wani rahoton likitan dabbobi ya bayyana wani kare da ya ci wutar wasa (sparklers) mai ɗauke da barium nitrate (gishirin barium mai narkewa, ba kayan glaze ba): ya kamu da rauni kwatsam, yawan yau da bugun zuciya marar tsari, kuma ya warke bayan maganin likitan dabbobi. Tsuntsaye lamari ne na musamman: wani likitan dabbobi na University of Illinois ya bayyana cewa "Tsuntsaye suna da tsarin numfashi mai sarƙaƙiya sosai kuma mai inganci ƙwarai, wanda ke sa su zama masu rauni musamman ga shaƙar hayaƙi da feshi."

### Dokokin hankali

- Ku hana dabbobin gida shiga wurin aiki, da ƙofar a rufe.
- Karnuka suna tauna buhu kuma suna sha daga bokiti. Ku ajiye buhuna a cikin kabad a rufe da murfi a kan kowane bokiti.
- Kyanwoyi suna tafiya cikin ƙura kuma suna lasar ta daga ƙafafunsu da gashinsu. Ku goge ƙasa da ruwa kuma ku hana kyanwoyi shiga wurin aiki, don kada a sami abin da za su lasa.
- Tsuntsaye da ƙananan dabbobi masu shayarwa (zomaye, guinea pig, hamster) suna raba iskar gida. Ku ajiye su a ɗaki mai nisa da ƙofar a rufe yayin da kuke sarrafa busassun kaya da kuma yayin da kiln ke gasa.
- Tankunan kifi suna tara ƙurar da ke sauka. Ku ajiye su a wajen wurin aiki, kuma a rufe.
- Ku ajiye kwanukan abinci da ruwan dabbobi nesa da wurin aiki da kiln, kuma ku yi amfani da kwanukan kasuwa ko waɗanda aka gwada, ba gwaje-gwajen wurin aiki ba.

Idan akwai yiwuwar dabbar gida ta ci ko ta sha wani abu daga wurin aiki, ko tana nuna alamu kamar waɗanda ke sama, ku kira likitan dabbobinku ko layin guba na dabbobi (duba teburin da ke sama), da sunan kayan da nauyin dabbar a hannu.

## Kiln {#the-kiln}

### Wajensa yana yin zafi har ya ƙona

Wani kimanta haɗari na makarantun UK ya ce wajen kiln (murhun gasa tukwane) "na iya kai 160 °C kuma watakila fiye" (kusan 320 °F), kuma wata jagorar kiln ta makaranta ta ruwaito ɗamarar ƙarfe har zuwa 460 °F (kusan 240 °C). Skutt, mai ƙera kiln, yana ba da shawarar ɗakin kiln da ake iya kullewa don hana yara da dabbobin gida. Inda ba za a iya kulle ɗaki ba, jagorar makarantar tana ba da shawarar shinge kamar allon kare kiln.

Ƙananan yara da dabbobin gida ba sa iya fahimtar alamar gargaɗi. Muna ba da shawarar ku ajiye su a bayan ƙofa a kulle ko shinge yayin da kiln ke gasa da kuma yayin da yake hucewa, kuma ku liƙa alamar gargaɗi don sauran mutane.

### Fili a kewayensa

Majiyoyi ba su yarda da juna ba kan fili. Rio Grande ta ce aƙalla inci 12 (30 cm) daga bango da duk abin da ke kamawa da wuta. L&L tana ba da shawara sosai ta inci 18 (46 cm) ga dukan bango, kuma dokar gini da take ambato tana buƙatar ƙafa 3 (0.9 m) daga bangon da ke kamawa da wuta. Ku bi littafin jagorar kiln ɗinku da dokar wuta ta yankinku; idan ba ku da tabbas, ku bar inci 18 daga kowane bango da ƙafa 3 daga duk abin da zai iya ƙonewa. A garaji, ku ajiye motoci, man fetur da fenti nesa sosai.

### Wutar lantarki

Rio Grande tana ba da shawarar **layin wuta na musamman** (wanda ba ya ba wani abu wuta) tare da soket mai ingantaccen ƙasa (ground). Muna ba da shawarar kada a yi amfani da wayar ƙari (extension). Tsakanin gasa, ku kashe breaker ko maɓallin katse wutar kiln, ko ku cire filogi, don kada yaro ya iya kunna shi.

### Ku kasance a wurin lokacin da gasa ke ƙarewa

**Kiln sitter** maɓalli ne na inji a kan kiln da yawa na hannu: ƙaramin cone yana lanƙwasa a ƙarshen gasa kuma yana kashe maɓallin. Littafin jagorarsa ya ce "BA a yi shi don ya zama na'urar kashewa da ba ta kasawa ba." Rio Grande ta ce kada a taɓa barin kiln da ke gasa ba tare da mai lura ba, "musamman kusa da lokacin da ake sa ran zai kashe". Don haka ku zauna kusa ku riƙa duba kiln yayin da yake gasa, musamman kusa da ƙarshe; ku yi gasa ne kawai lokacin da babba zai kasance a gida a farke a duk tsawon gasar, ku tabbatar ya kashe, kuma ku ajiye abin kashe gobara na ABC a ɗakin kiln. Don yadda sitter, controller da jadawali ke aiki, duba [Gasa a cikin kiln mai sauƙi](/guides/firing).

### Hayaƙi da carbon monoxide

Gasa tana fitar da hayaƙi, don haka ku fitar da iskar kiln zuwa waje; [Haɗawa cikin aminci da iska mai kyau](/guides/safe-mixing) ya bayyana yadda. Kiln a cikin garajin da ke manne da gida yana buƙatar hanyar fitar da iska da ke hana hayaƙi kaiwa gida ta ƙofar da ke haɗa su. Ku ajiye tsuntsaye a ɗaki mai nisa duk lokacin da kiln ke gasa.

**Carbon monoxide** (CO) iskar gas ce mai guba da ba za ku iya gani ko jin warinta ba. Wata takardar aminci ta kiln ta jami'a tana buƙatar ƙararrawar CO tare da kiln na gas. Ra'ayoyi sun bambanta game da kiln na lantarki, amma ƙona abubuwa masu rai (a cikin yumɓu, ko abubuwa kamar takarda ko kakin zuma) na iya haifar da CO. Muna ganin ƙararrawar CO a ɗakin kiln da ɗakunan da ke kusa da shi mataki ne mai hankali, mara tsada, ga kowane kiln.

### Ramukan leƙawa da idanu

Hasken infrared, wanda ba za ku iya gani ba, "yana farawa a 752°F (400°C)", kuma Ceramic Arts Network ta ce leƙawa cikin kiln sama da wannan ya kamata a yi shi koyaushe da kariyar ido. Infrared na iya lalata ruwan tabarau na ido kuma ya kai ga yanar ido (cataract), kuma dogon kallo na iya cutar da retina. Tabarau na rana da tabarau na didymium ba sa hana shi.

> [!WARNING]
> Bai kamata yara su leƙa cikin kiln ko kaɗan ba. Ramin leƙawa mai haske yana jan hankalinsu, kuma ba za su ci gaba da sa tabarau na kariya yadda ya kamata ba, don haka ku nisanta su yayin da kiln ke da zafi. Ya kamata manya su leƙa na ɗan lokaci kaɗan kawai, ta tabarau na walda masu ƙimar IR. Majiyoyi suna ba da shawarar shade daga 1.7 zuwa 5; shade 3 zaɓi ne mai hankali, mai duhu fiye da haka idan kun ga ɗigo bayan haka.

## Tukwanen hannu a teburin iyali {#handmade-ware}

Tambayar tukwane a teburin iyali ita ce **zubar ƙarfe (leaching)**: ƙarfe da ke narkewa daga glaze da aka gasa zuwa cikin abinci ko abin sha. FDA ta yi gargaɗi cewa idan tukunya "ba a ƙera ta yadda ya kamata ba, wannan darma na iya zuba cikin abinci da abin sha", kuma "babu yawan wanki, tafasa ko wata hanya da za ta iya cire darma daga tukunya." Hukumomi kamar FDA ta US da EU suna iyakance yawan darma (lead, Pb) da cadmium da tukunyar da aka gama ke fitarwa a gwajin acid na ma'auni, ba abin da glaze ke ɗauke da shi ba. Iyaka mafi tsauri ta US, don kofuna, mug da tulu, ita ce 0.5 µg/mL na darma.

### Babu darma ba ɗaya ba ne da lafiya ga abinci

Glaze da babu darma a cikinsa na iya zubar da ƙarfe har yanzu. Digitalfire, littafin bayani kan sinadaran glaze, ya ambaci glaze masu ɗauke da "masu launi na oxide na ƙarfe masu nauyi, barium, lithium, ko darma" a matsayin abin damuwa, kuma ya yi gargaɗi cewa stain da aka gasa sama da zafin da aka ƙayyade masa na iya fitar da cadmium. Alamar kayan fasaha ta ASTM D-4236 a kan tulu "BA ta tabbatar da cewa glaze da aka gasa lafiya ga abinci ba ne", kuma "Masu ƙera kayan tebur dole su gwada dukan kayan da aka gama don tabbatar da matsayinsu na kayan abinci."

> [!NOTE]
> "Lafiya ga abinci" na nufin wannan glaze, a kan wannan yumɓu, an gasa shi zuwa wannan cone, an shafa shi ta wannan hanya, yana fitar da ƙasa da iyakokin doka. Lissafin tsarin glaze, har da na Glazecalc, na iya nuna tsarin glaze mai haɗari amma ba zai iya tabbatar da cewa tukunya lafiya take ba. Gwajin zubar ƙarfe na tukunyar da aka gasa ne kawai zai iya.

Muna ba da shawarar ku ɗauki alamar "lafiya ga abinci" a kan glaze na kasuwa a matsayin farawa mai kyau, ba tabbaci ba: yumɓu daban, shafi mai kauri, wani cone ko glaze na biyu a sama yana canza sakamakon.

### Gwaji

Ɗakunan gwaje-gwaje suna gwada tukwanen da aka gasa da jiƙawa a acid na ma'auni da ke wakiltar mafi munin yanayi, kamar ASTM C738 a US. Gwajin EU na wani ɗakin gwaji yana buƙatar tukwane huɗu iri ɗaya kuma yana ɗaukar kwanakin aiki bakwai, don haka ku yi ƙarin kowane abu da kuke shirin a gwada.

Ana jayayya kan gwaje-gwajen gida. FDA ta ce kayan gwajin goge-goge na gida na iya nuna darma da ke iya zuba, kuma tukunyar da gwajinta ya nuna darma bai kamata a yi amfani da ita don abinci ba. Ceramics NZ ta yi gargaɗi cewa alƙalaman gwajin darma na masu saye "ba a yi su don wannan amfani ba" a kan tukwane kuma suna iya ba da sakamako na ƙarya cewa akwai darma. Digitalfire tana ba da shawarar barin yankan lemun tsami a kan glaze dare ɗaya a matsayin bincike na kusa-kusa. Fahimta mai aminci: gwajin gida da ya nuna darma yana nufin ku daina amfani da tukunyar don abinci, kuma wanda bai nuna ba ba ya tabbatar da komai. Ɗakin gwaji ne kawai zai iya gaya muku cewa tukunya lafiya take.

### Tukwane masu crazing

**Crazing (ƙananan tsage-tsage)** wani raga ne na tsage-tsage masu laushi a saman glaze, kuma majiyoyi ba su yarda da juna ba game da shi. Wani gwaji na Ceramics Monthly ya gano cewa ana iya tsabtace tayil masu crazing, mafi kyau a injin wanke kwanuka, ko da yake ba su fito da tsabta kamar tayil marasa crazing ba bayan sabulu da ruwa. Digitalfire tana da ra'ayin cewa "Tukwane masu crazing ba su da karɓuwa" kuma crazing "yana raunana tukwane sosai". Hanya mai aminci mai sauƙi ce: kada ku yi amfani da tukwane masu crazing don abinci.

### Glaze na ciki (liner)

**Glaze na ciki (liner glaze)** shi ne glaze a cikin kofi ko kwano, inda abinci ke zama. Digitalfire ta bayyana glaze na ciki da haɗarinsa zai iya zama ƙasa sosai har a yi amfani da shi ba tare da gwajin ɗakin gwaji ba: babu masu launi, barium, darma ko lithium; ya narke sosai; babu crazing; kuma yana jure amfani. Ko a lokacin, gwajin zubar ƙarfe na tukwanenku da kuka gasa ne kawai ke nuna ko lafiya ga abinci suke. Alamun gargaɗi a kowane glaze su ne yawan mai launi, lu'ulu'u a saman da rashin narkewa sosai.

### Dokokin iyali da muke ba da shawara

- Kofuna, kwanuka da faranti na yara: glaze na ciki masu ƙarfi da aka gwada a ɗakin gwaji kawai, ko kayan abinci na kasuwa.
- Kada ku ajiye abinci ko abin sha mai tsami (lemu, tumatir, vinegar, ruwan 'ya'yan itace, giya) a cikin tukwanen da aka shafa wa duk wani abu da ba ku sa an gwada ba.
- Kada ku yi amfani da tukwane masu crazing don abinci.
- Test tile da gwaje-gwaje suna zama a wurin aiki, kada su taɓa shiga kicin, akwatin kayan wasa ko kwanon dabbar gida.
- Ku yi hankali da sauran tukwane ma. FDA tana ba da shawarar taka-tsantsan da tukwanen hannu ko waɗanda ke da kamannin rashin kyau, tsofaffin kayan tarihi, tukwane da suka lalace ko suka goge, kayan da aka samo a kasuwar kayan gwanjo da ado mai haske na lemu, ja ko rawaya.
- Idan yaro ya yi amfani da tukwane da yanzu kuke shakkar su, FDA ta ce ku yi magana da likita game da gwajin darma a jini.

Don ƙarin bayani kan yadda glaze ke narkewa, dalilin da wasu ke zubar da ƙarfe da abin da iyakokin ke nufi, duba [Tushen glaze](/guides/glazing-basics).
