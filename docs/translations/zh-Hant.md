# 繁體中文: 風格指南與詞彙表 (style sheet and glossary)

For translating Glazecalc into Traditional Chinese (`zh-Hant`), in Taiwan's written standard (教育部 forms, 國語), for
potters in Taiwan; Hong Kong reads the same page (docs/asia-plan.md, decision 5). Read [README.md](README.md) first;
[terms.md](terms.md) gives the meaning of every English term below. Every Traditional Chinese message and guide follows
this page: one word for one thing. The page is written from Taiwan's own sources, never by converting Simplified
Chinese: the shops of 鶯歌 that sell glaze materials (the names printed on the bag), the kiln makers, the teaching papers
on 塞格式, the national term bank 樂詞網, Taiwan's law as 全國法規資料庫 publishes it, and the Microsoft Traditional
Chinese (Taiwan) style guide. Where sources disagree or none was read, the Notes say so: those rows are for a Taiwanese
potter to check.

**What the sources could and could not give.** Taiwan's studio vocabulary mixes Japanese words (釜戶長石, 天目, 素燒,
SK cones) with the Chinese of textbooks and of 樂詞網, and the shops write material names as the bag does, often with
the Japanese or American origin in front (日化長石粉, 美國高嶺土). Most material rows rest on two shops' lists (太麟,
釉藥堂) and on 樂詞網; the firing and glaze-structure rows on 李堅萍's two papers and one kiln maker's pages; the law rows
on the law texts read today. Few studio pages could be read in full: search returned mostly summaries, and several
pages refused automated reading (釉藥堂's product pages, the NTUA paper). Rows marked **Usage** give the word Taiwanese
potters use as far as this session could see, from one page or a search extract; **No source** marks a best choice
with no Traditional Chinese source; **Native check** marks the rows a Taiwanese potter should check first.

## 1. Style sheet

### Register

The polite, plain written register of Taiwan's software: **您** for "you" where a pronoun is needed (MS: "Always use the
polite form 您 for 'You' in all of the software products"; Google's and Microsoft's Taiwan help pages do the same), never
你. Apple's Taiwan pages write 你; this page follows MS and Google. Leave the pronoun out wherever the sentence reads
without it, as Chinese prefers: 「請輸入密碼。」 rather than 「請您輸入您的密碼。」.

- **Buttons and menu commands**: a short verb or verb–object, no 請, no punctuation: 「儲存」「刪除」「列印」「比較」「新增原料」.
- **Errors**: a short statement, then what to do, with 請: 「找不到這個配方。」「無法儲存，請再試一次。」 MS's patterns: 「找不到...」,
  「無法...」, "You might want to" → 「建議您...」 (never 「您可能想要...」).
- **Questions and confirmations**: 「要刪除這個配方嗎？」 or, more formal, 「確定要刪除這個配方嗎？」.
- **Guide sentence**: plain statements and 請 for instructions: 「釉藥篩兩次後再使用。」「請戴上防塵口罩。」「釉層太厚時容易流釉。」
- **Prohibitions**: 「請勿…」 for "do not"; 「切勿…」 or 「絕對不要…」 for "never": 「切勿用壓縮空氣吹除粉塵。」 A "never" must not
  weaken to 「最好不要」.
- **should and must** (safety text): "must" → 「必須…」 (or 「一定要…」); "should" → 「應該…」 or 「建議…」; "should not" →
  「不應…」 or 「不建議…」; "must not" → 「不得…」 or 「切勿…」. Never 必須 for "should" or 應該 for "must". "must be tested" →
  「必須送驗」 or 「必須經過溶出試驗」 (the test must be done), never 「必須通過試驗」 (passed). "may" (a hazard) → 「可能…」.
  "some" (some fine quartz) → 「一些」「部分」, never 「少許」「微量」 ("a little").

### Buttons, menus, headings, labels and tabs

- **Buttons, menus, tabs**: short, as Taiwan's software writes them: 「儲存」「刪除」「設定」「列印」「取消」「確定」「另存新檔」 is
  Windows' "Save as"; the app's "Save as a copy" is 「儲存為副本」.
- **Headings and page titles**: a noun phrase, no final 。: 「配方」「設定」「燒窯紀錄」「測溫錐與熱功」. A heading that names an activity
  takes the verb–object form: "Mixing the glaze" → 「調配釉藥」, "Typing amounts" → 「輸入數值」. A question stays one: 「釉為什麼會熔化？」
- **Field labels**: a noun, and the English's colon as a full-width colon with no space: 「原料名稱：」. (MS keeps a
  half-width colon inside software strings and the full-width one in "documentation, Help and web pages"; the app is a
  web page, so full-width throughout.)
- **Confirmations**: "Saved: {name}" → 「已儲存：{name}」.
- **Feature names in running text**: in 「」, as Google's and Apple's Taiwan help pages name buttons and settings (Google:
  輕觸「安全性與登入」; Apple: 前往「設定」): `請按「比較」。` See The app's feature names. MS's half-width `[ ]` is for its own
  documentation and is not used here.

### Capitalisation

Chinese has none. Latin words keep the English's case where they are names, codes or units (Orton, SK, UMF, LOI, Ferro
3134, N95, mg/m³, Google, Apple); the English's Title Case means nothing in Chinese and goes with the English words. A
generic English word kept untranslated drops its plural s (MS 4.2.9: 「選取一或多個 Cookie」), but the app keeps few such words.

### Script and characters

- **Traditional characters as 教育部 writes them** (常用國字標準字體), never converted from Simplified. **臺** in names that
  write it (臺北榮民總醫院, 國立臺灣藝術大學, 臺南藝術大學, 臺北) and in the app's own text (臺灣), as 教育部 and the government write
  it; 台 is accepted everywhere in Taiwan and the browser's own region name is 台灣 (`{region}`), so a page may show both:
  that is correct, and the translator never "fixes" a `{region}` value. **Native check**: the owner may prefer 台 for
  plainness.
- **矽 for silicon, never 硅** (Taiwan's chemistry and 樂詞網: 二氧化矽, 矽石, 矽灰石, 矽酸鋯, 矽肺症). The check rejects 硅 in
  zh-Hant. 釉藥堂 prints 硅石粉 beside 石英粉: never copy it.

  坯體 and 生坯, the textbook form).
- **紀錄 and 記錄**: 教育部 writes 紀錄 for the noun (a record) and 記錄 for the verb (to record): 「燒窯紀錄」「記錄這次燒窯」.
- **Full-width and half-width**: Chinese punctuation full-width (，。、；：！？「」『』（）《》); digits, Latin letters, units and their
  signs half-width (1222 °C, 5%, SK 6a, 3.0/(1.19Q + 1)). Never full-width digits or letters (１２, ＳＫ).

### Spaces

- No space between Chinese characters, and never a full-width space (U+3000).
- **A half-width space between Chinese and a Latin word, a trade name, a code or a figure**, as MS 4.2.10 says and as
  Google's and Apple's Taiwan pages write: 「加入 Ferro 3134」「燒到 SK 8」「Orton 測溫錐」「N95 口罩」「燒到 1222 °C」「最多 7 天」
  「共 10 個」. With a counter after a figure: 「3 種原料」「# 種原料」「30 分鐘」.
- **Placeholders**: a space beside one that holds a figure or Latin text ({count}, {value}, {grams}, {abbr}, {digits}):
  「{count} 種原料」「{abbr} 建議值」; none beside one that holds Chinese ({region}, which the browser gives as 台灣, 美國):
  「在{region}販售」「{region}的限值」. {name} goes in 「」, which needs no space: 「已刪除「{name}」。」 (MS: "No space is needed if the
  placeholder is to be replaced with a localized string").
- **No space next to full-width punctuation** (MS): 「「Gerstley Borate」」 needs none, nor 「（UMF）」, nor before 。，、.
- **Dates and clock times are written solid**, as `Intl` prints them for `zh-Hant-TW` and the app shows its own dates:
  2026年10月14日, 20:00. (MS writes 「2021 年 3 月 14 日」 with spaces; the app's dates come from `Intl` without them, so a
  hand-written date matches the printed ones.)
- **Units**: as the English writes them: 10 g, 1222 °C, 100 °C/h, 80 目 (mesh), 5% (no space before %).
- Inside Latin text the English's spaces stay (Orton cone 6 is written 「Orton 6 號測溫錐」, SK 015a, Gerstley Borate).

### Chinese and Markdown

In the guides, `**…**` and `*…*` do not render when a full-width bracket or stop sits just inside the marks and a Chinese
character just outside them (CommonMark's flanking rule): `**「素燒」**是` shows the asterisks. Keep 「」, （）, 。 and ，
**outside** the emphasis: `「**素燒**」是`, `**素燒**是`. Check every bold term in the preview.

### Punctuation

- **Full-width** ，。、；：！？ in Chinese text. A sentence ends in 。 even after a figure or a Latin word: 「燒到 1222 °C。」
- **、 for lists** (MS: "pause symbol (、) is used in Traditional Chinese"): 「長石、石英、高嶺土」; the English's "A, B, and C" is
  「A、B 及 C」 or 「A、B，以及 C」.
- **Quotations, terms quoted, names of buttons, pages and settings**: 「…」; a quotation inside one: 『…』. The English's “…”
  becomes 「…」.
- **Titles of works** (books, laws, standards, the guides when named as works): 《…》: 《陶藝釉藥學》, 《食品器具容器包裝衛生標準》. The
  five guide titles in running text and links: 《…》 as well (they are documents); as a link's text, the title alone.
- **Brackets**: full-width （） after Chinese text, whatever is inside: 「塞格式（UMF）」「動力型呼吸防護具（PAPR）」「12 英寸（30 cm）」.
  Half-width () only where the word before them is Latin or a figure: 「Ferro 3134 (US)」. (MS and Google use half-width ()
  with spaces in both cases; this page follows the brief's rule, which keeps a Chinese line's punctuation full-width.)
- **Ranges**: the en dash between figures with no spaces, the unit once where the English has it once: "10 to 60%" →
  「10–60%」, "cone 06–04" → 「Orton 06–04 號測溫錐」, "1222–1240 °C" → 「1222–1240 °C」 (MS keeps the en dash in number ranges).
  In a sentence, 至 also works: 「10 至 60%」. Never 「10%～60%」 or 「10%–60%」 where the English has one unit: the check finds a
  figure with a unit the English lacks. Not the full-width tilde ～, and not the em dash, which MS forbids ("it looks the
  same as the Traditional Chinese character for 'one' (一)").
- **Colon**: full-width ：, no space either side.
- No ！ in the app's own voice.

### Numbers and units

- **Half-width Arabic digits** wherever the English has digits. Never full-width digits (１２), Chinese numerals (十二), 萬
  or 億 (12,500 stays 12,500, never 1.25萬), and never a 民國 year.
- **Decimal point and thousands comma**, as English and CLDR's zh-Hant: 1.5, 0.025, 1,000, 12,500. The app prints
  four-digit temperatures ungrouped (1222 °C); write them as the English does.
- **A number the English writes as a word** ("two coats", "three tiles", "once") has no figure in the English, so a digit
  would be a figure the English lacks and the check rejects it. Write the Chinese numeral word: 「兩層」「三片試片」「一次」「兩種
  原料」; "two numbers hotter" → 「溫度高兩號」. Where the English has the digit, the digit stays: "3 coats" → 「3 層」. Use 兩, not 二,
  before a counter (兩層, 兩次).
- **Units**: half-width symbols, as the English writes them: g, kg, mg, µg, mL, L, mm, cm, °C, °F, °Bé, %, mg/m³, µg/cm².
  Not ℃, not 公克, 公分, 毫升 for a symbol the English writes (the check reads ℃ as °C, but this page keeps the English's
  symbol). "per 100 g" → 「每 100 g」. Inches and feet are the words **英寸** and **英尺** (README), with the metric figure in
  brackets as the English gives it: "12 inches (30 cm)" → 「12 英寸（30 cm）」. Mesh → 目: "80 mesh" → 「80 目」 (太麟, 釉藥堂
  write 200目, 270目).
- **Dates**: the Western calendar: 2026年10月14日, 2026年10月. Never the 民國 year (民國115年): it is a figure the English
  lacks. The check reads 10月 as the English October.
- **Clock times**: the 24-hour clock: 20:00 or 20時; "8pm" → 「20:00」, "8am to 5pm" → 「8:00 至 17:00」. Never 上午, 下午, 晚上,
  凌晨 or 中午 (the check reports them). Note for developers: `Intl` in `zh-Hant-TW` prints 「晚上8:00」 unless asked for
  `hourCycle: 'h23'`. "24 hours" → 「24 小時」.
- **Percent additions**: "3% on top of the base" → 「外加 3%」 (李堅萍 2006: 「外加的呈色劑」; see 2.6).

CLDR gives Chinese one category, cardinal and ordinal alike:

### Cones

The word for a pyrometric cone is **測溫錐** (AAT-Taiwan's preferred term; 太麟 sells 「歐頓測溫錐」; 釉藥堂's menu 「測溫錐/測溫環」;
Hong Kong's 藝舍家 writes 測溫錐 too). 樂詞網 gives 示溫熔錐 (材料), the formal term; 三角錐 is a studio word (seen in search
results only, after the Japanese 三角コーン). Use 測溫錐 everywhere; 錐 alone after the first mention in a paragraph.

- **SK cones** are always written **SK**, a half-width space, and the number exactly as printed: 「SK 8」「SK 6a」「SK 015a」. "fire to
  SK 8" → 「燒到 SK 8」. "SK cones (JIS R 8101)" → 「SK 測溫錐（JIS R 8101）」, with the standard only where the English names it.
  Never 「SK8號」, never 「塞格錐 8 號」.
- **Orton cones** keep the name Orton, in Latin letters: "cone 6" → 「Orton 6 號測溫錐」 at the first mention in each section and in
  every table, label and setting; 「6 號錐」 after that within the paragraph. "cone 06" → 「Orton 06 號測溫錐」 (the zero stays: it is
  far cooler than 6). "cone 6 glazes" → 「Orton 6 號的釉」. 太麟 transliterates 歐頓; the app keeps Orton.
- **塞格 only where the English says Seger**: the Seger formula (塞格式, below) and the older European Seger cones of the
  firing guide (「德國的塞格錐」, a series of their own: say nothing that makes them SK or Orton cones). 樂詞網 writes 色格錐; this
  page writes 塞格錐 so that it matches 塞格式.
- **Never** give an SK number for an Orton number or the reverse, never write ≈, 相當於, 等於 or 對應 between them, and never
  add a conversion the English lacks.
- **One number hotter, one number cooler**: 「溫度高一號的測溫錐」「溫度低一號的測溫錐」 (Chinese numerals: the English has no figure).
  Always with 溫度: never 「號數大一號／小一號」, 「上一號／下一號」 or 「高一號」 alone, because in the 0-series (Orton 022 to 01, SK 022 to
  SK 01a) a larger number is cooler. Where the English says "the next number below it in the table", keep the table's
  order: 「表中前一號（溫度較低）的錐」.
- Witness cone 「見證錐」; cone pack 「一組三支的測溫錐」; guide, firing and guard cones 「指示錐」「目標錐」「保護錐」 (2.4).
- Not 圓錐 (the geometric cone).

### The unity formula

**塞格式** (李堅萍 2006: 「塞格式釉方」, 「塞格式釉方表示式 RO．xR2O3．yRO2」; the NTUA paper's title, not reached, also writes
塞格式), with 「（UMF）」 after it at the first mention on each page: 「塞格式（UMF）」. Not 塞格爾式 (Simplified sources write
塞格尔式) and not 釉式 (the empirical formula in general). The app's "unity formula", "Unity formula" and "Unity:" are all

names are in 2.1 (助熔劑, 安定劑, 玻璃形成劑). "in unity" → 「以塞格式表示」; "scaled so the fluxes add up to 1" → 「使助熔劑合計為 1」.

### Gender-neutral wording

Chinese has no grammatical gender, but 他 and 她 differ in writing. Leave the pronoun out, repeat the noun, or use 您 for
the reader (MS: "Rewrite to use the second or third person (您 or 其)… Refer to a person's role… Use 人員 or 個人"). Never
他/她 or 他(她). A potter is 「陶藝家」 or, for a beginner, 「做陶的人」; a worker 「勞工」 (the law's word) or 「工作人員」. Avoid 它 for
things (MS); use 其 or repeat the noun. Children: 「兒童」, babies 「嬰幼兒」 (the food standard's word).

### The reader in the guides

您 where a pronoun is needed, short sentences as the English, and no 我們 for the app unless the English says "we". Instructions
take 請 at the start of a step or none: 「請將釉藥篩兩次。」「釉藥篩兩次後再使用。」 Explanations are plain statements: 「釉層太厚時容易流釉。」
「素燒過的坯體吸水，可以馬上施釉。」 No classical forms (之, 係, 予以) beyond those Taiwanese prose uses every day; no 文言 in the
guides. Quotations from sources are translated into 「…」 and keep their figures as the English writes them.

### Law in Taiwan

As the law writes it, never paraphrased: the region texts must use these words.

- **以下, 未滿, 以上, 超過**: "at most", "no more than", "or less", "up to" → **以下** (the limit itself passes; 衛生標準 writes
  every limit 「…以下」); "less than", "under", "below" → **未滿** (the limit does not pass; 容許暴露標準's own table writes
  「未滿 1」「1 以上，未滿 10」); "at least", "or more", "from" → **以上**; "more than", "over", "above" → **超過**. Never 未滿 or 低於
  for "at most", never 以下 for "less than": a slip moves a legal limit. Check each one in the food-contact, silica and
  dust-mask texts.
- **Food contact**: 食品安全衛生管理法; 《食品器具容器包裝衛生標準》 附表一; the law's heading for ceramics is
  「玻璃、陶瓷器、施琺瑯之器具、容器」; the test is **溶出試驗** with 4%醋酸 at 常溫 (暗處) for 24 小時. The kinds of piece, as the law
  words them: 「深2.5cm以上，且容量1.1L以下」, 「深2.5cm以上，且容量1.1L以上」, 「深2.5cm以下或液體無法充滿者」. (The law's (a) and (b)
  both include 1.1 L, and (a) and (c) both include 2.5 cm: translate the English category the region data gives, and do
  not resolve the overlap in the translation.) The limits: 「鉛：5ppm以下；鎘：0.5ppm以下」, 「鉛：17μg/cm2以下」 (written 17 µg/cm² in the
  app). ppm here is mg/L. There is no separate cooking-ware category.
- **Silica**: 職業安全衛生法; 《勞工作業場所容許暴露標準》 附表二 空氣中粉塵容許濃度. "exposure limit" → **容許濃度**; "8-hour average" →
  **八小時日時量平均容許濃度** (the law's term; 「8 小時平均」 in a caption where the English is plain). "respirable dust" → **可呼吸性粉塵**;
  "total dust" → 總粉塵; "crystalline (free) silica" → **結晶型游離二氧化矽** (職安署指引); quartz 石英, cristobalite 方矽石,
  tridymite 鱗矽石 (樂詞網 礦物; the Chinese 附表二 could not be read, so these two are **Native check**). The limit of 0.1 mg/m³
  applies from 2027年1月1日; until then a content-based formula applies, which this page does not quote (unverified).
  "binding" → 「具法律約束力」.
- **Japan's 管理濃度** (the guides' `silica.kind.control`) is a Japanese legal term: keep 管理濃度 as the English quotes it, and
  translate the explanation; Taiwan has no such term.
- **Silicosis** → **矽肺症** (職安署指引: 「職業性矽肺症」; 樂詞網 also 矽肺病). "pneumoconiosis" → 塵肺症.
- **Dust masks**: CNS 14755「拋棄式防塵口罩」; classes **D2** and **D3** kept as codes (D2 is the class sold as N95-like; the
  percentages are not from a government page: do not add them). The employer's duty is 職業安全衛生設施規則 第277條之1 (not read).
  The OSHA handbook's general word is **呼吸防護具**.
- **GHS**: 《危害性化學品標示及通識規則》 and CNS 15030: 危害圖式 (pictogram), 警示語 **危險** and **警告** (signal word), 危害警告訊息
  (hazard statement), 危害防範措施 (precautionary statement), **安全資料表 (SDS)**. Chinese H-statements as Taiwanese SDSs write them:
  H301 吞食有毒, H302 吞食有害, H350 可能致癌, H360 可能對生育能力或對胎兒造成傷害, H372 長期或重複暴露會對器官造成傷害 (from usage
  on Taiwanese SDSs; CNS 15030 itself was not read: **Native check**). Codes kept.
- **Who to call**: **臺北榮總毒藥物防治諮詢中心**, 02-2871-7121 (24 小時, 免費); **119** for fire and ambulance (消防署: 「火災、急難救助」),
  112 from a mobile without signal; 110 police. As written, never shortened beyond 臺北榮總.
- **Bodies**: 衛生福利部 (衛福部), 衛生福利部食品藥物管理署 (食藥署), 勞動部職業安全衛生署 (職安署), 經濟部標準檢驗局 (標檢局, BSMI). Full name
  at the first mention on a page, short name after.
- **EU law**, where the English names it (Directive 84/500/EEC, Directive (EU) 2017/2398, Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008):
  there is no official Chinese text, and no Taiwanese official page naming them was read. Keep the number and give a plain
  name: 「歐盟指令 84/500/EEC（與食品接觸的陶瓷製品）」, 「歐盟指令 (EU) 2017/2398」, 「歐盟 CLP 規章 (EC) No 1272/2008」. The EU food
  categories must still read so that the 3 L applies only to packaging and storage vessels: "Cooking ware of any size,
  and packaging and storage vessels over 3 L" → 「任何容量的烹調器具，以及容量超過 3 L 的包裝及儲存容器」.
- **US and UK law** names stay in Latin with a Chinese gloss once: OSHA（美國職業安全衛生署）, HSE（英國健康與安全執行署）. Taiwan's own
  職安署 also calls itself OSHA in English: where the English says OSHA, it is always the US agency; write 「美國 OSHA」 at the first
  mention in each section.
- **Hong Kong**: the 衛生署 (Hong Kong writes 衞), the 勞工處, and 999. Hong Kong's poison centre serves health professionals
  only. Names as Hong Kong writes them, when the region data gives them.

### Pitfalls

- **釉, 釉藥, 琺瑯, 亮光漆**: 釉藥 is the glaze, as material and slurry, in every message (shops: 釉藥, 陶瓷釉藥); 釉 in compounds
  and where the fired coat is meant (透明釉, 天目釉, 灰釉, 釉層, 施釉, 「釉熔化」); 釉料 is also common (樂詞網 一般) but not used here.
  Not 琺瑯 (enamel on metal; the food law's 施琺瑯), 搪瓷, or 亮光漆 (varnish).
- **熔塊 and 助熔劑**: a frit is **熔塊** (樂詞網 材料: 熔塊；玻料; 釉藥堂 熔塊/玻璃粉); a flux is **助熔劑** (樂詞網, 李堅萍, 科技大觀園).
  Never 熔劑 alone, which also means a solder flux and a solvent's 溶劑 is a homophone.
- **窯, 爐, 烤箱**: a potter's kiln is **窯** (電窯, 瓦斯窯; 鴻勝); 爐 is industrial or for glass (玻璃爐) and appears in 窯爐 only where the
  English is general; 烤箱 is a kitchen oven, used only where the English says oven.
- **素燒 and 釉燒**: bisque is 素燒 (the firing) and 素燒坯 (the ware); the glaze firing is **釉燒** (studio pages, 原來如此). Taiwanese
  potters also say 本燒 after the Japanese; not used. "biscuit" (UK) is also 素燒.
- **陶器, 炻器, 瓷器**: earthenware is **陶器**, stoneware **炻器** (樂詞網 材料: 陶石器；炻器；缸器), porcelain **瓷器**. Many Taiwanese
  potters call all their high-fired ware 陶 and say 高溫陶; so **show English** helps, and the guides explain the word once.
  "low-fire earthenware" → 「低溫陶器」. Not 粗陶器 (樂詞網 視覺藝術 gives it for stoneware; it reads "coarse earthenware").
- **矽石, 石英, 二氧化矽**: the oxide is **二氧化矽 (SiO₂)**; the material sold is **石英粉** (太麟, 釉藥堂), and in a recipe's
  "Silica" line the material is meant: 石英粉. 矽石 (樂詞網) is not used, to keep one word for the material; 矽 alone is the
  element. Silicone is 矽膠 or 矽利康. Never 硅.
- **碳酸鈣, 石灰石, 石灰**: whiting is **碳酸鈣** (the material: 太麟 碳酸鈣, 石粉(重質碳酸鈣); 釉藥堂 輕質碳酸鈣/石灰石/白堊); 石灰 is
  lime (CaO or Ca(OH)₂), never whiting. CaO is **氧化鈣**, "calcium" 鈣. 白堊 (樂詞網's word for whiting) is chalk: not used.
- **溶出試驗**: the leach test (衛生標準), the test of what metals a fired piece releases. Never narrowed to 鉛、鎘 unless the English
  names them.
- **防塵口罩, 呼吸防護具, 抽風機, 呼吸器**: a respirator is **防塵口罩** (CNS 14755) or, as a class, 呼吸防護具; a hospital ventilator is
  **人工呼吸器** (樂詞網 醫學: respirator → 人工呼吸器, the trap), never meant here; a fan is 抽風機 or 排風扇; local exhaust is 局部排氣.
  "nuisance dust mask" → 「一般口罩（非 CNS 14755 防塵口罩）」.
- **指南 and 使用說明書**: the app's five guides are **指南**; a kiln's or controller's manual is **使用說明書**. Never the same word.
- **棚板, 硼板**: a kiln shelf is **棚板** (太麟, 鴻勝, 科技大觀園). Studios and 釉藥堂 also write 硼板, but 硼 is boron: never write it.
  Hong Kong's shops say 窯板.
- **呈色劑 and 色料**: a colorant (any oxide that colours) is **呈色劑** (李堅萍 2006); a stain (factory-made) is **色料** (太麟 釉下彩色料,
  色料樣版; 樂詞網 材料). "colorants and additives" → 「呈色劑與添加物」.
- **硼, 硼酸, 硼砂, 氧化硼**: B₂O₃ is **氧化硼** (formal) and 硼 for potters' "boron"; 硼酸 is boric acid (H₃BO₃, a reproductive
  toxin in the safety guide), 硼砂 borax. Do not write 硼酸 for B₂O₃.
- **鈉, 碳酸鈉, 鉀**: "soda" (Na₂O) is **鈉 (Na₂O)** / 氧化鈉; soda ash is **碳酸鈉** (太麟); "potash" (K₂O) is **鉀 (K₂O)** / 氧化鉀;
  potassium carbonate (the safety guide's "potash") is 碳酸鉀. 蘇打 alone is not used.
- **開片 and 冷裂**: crazing is 開片 (a defect, or wanted: crackle 冰裂紋); a crack through the pot on cooling is 冷裂 (dunting). See 2.5.
- **Placeholders** cannot take endings, and Chinese needs none: build the sentence around them with a noun or 「」.
  {region} is the country's name from the browser (台灣, 美國, 英國, 中國香港特別行政區), so write each sentence so it reads with
  any country: 「{region}的限值」「在{region}販售的原料」, not 「{region}國的」 or 「{region}政府」. {name} (a recipe or material) goes in
  「」: 「已刪除「{name}」。」 {count} takes its counter: 「{count} 種原料」. Spaces as in Style sheet, Spaces.
