# 简体中文：风格指南与术语表 (style sheet and glossary)

For translating Glazecalc into Simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`), in standard written Chinese as the mainland writes it
(Simplified characters as 《通用规范汉字表》 gives them, mainland words: 软件, 设置, 文件, 登录, 账户, 默认, 信息, 程序, 打印, 网络),
for potters in mainland China; Singapore and Malaysia read the same page. Read [README.md](README.md) first;
[terms.md](terms.md) gives the meaning of every English term below. Every Simplified Chinese message and guide follows
this page: one Chinese word for one thing. The page is written from mainland sources, never by converting Traditional
Chinese: the `zh-Hant` page is written separately, and its words (矽, 軟體, 設定, 釉藥, 塞格式 as Taiwan uses it) are not
this page's. Mainland potters fire mostly by temperature, and those who use cones use imported Orton cones; the
app also has SK cones and Seger cones in places, so the Chinese pages must keep Orton, SK and German Seger cones apart
everywhere, and keep the unity formula (釉式) apart from all three. Where sources disagree or none was read, the Notes
say so: those rows are for a Chinese potter to check.

**What the sources could and could not give.** Chinese has a full ceramics vocabulary, used by the industry, the
textbooks (陶瓷工艺学), the Jun and Jingdezhen workshops and the studio-pottery museums, so the chemistry, material and
defect rows rest on usage that was read today. What was **not** reached is the thing the earlier glossaries leaned on
most: a mainland studio shop's own catalogue. Mainland studio potters buy glaze materials, cones and kilns mostly
through Taobao and Tmall stores, which cannot be read from here, and no Jingdezhen or Foshan studio supplier with its
own catalogue site was found (four searches, 16 October 2026); the industrial suppliers found sell to tile and
sanitaryware works, and their bag names (钾长石粉, 钠长石粉, 石英粉, 高岭土, 水洗球土) agree with the textbooks. The cone and
kiln-sitter rows rest on the catalogue of Orton's Shanghai dealer, 上海麟文仪器, which is good evidence for how Orton's own
words are put into Chinese but not for how studio potters talk. Rows marked **Usage** are the words Chinese potters and
the industry use, read in no single source today; rows marked **No source** are best choices with no Chinese source
at all (mostly the firing-log column labels, which belong to American kilns). The rows a Chinese potter should check
first are marked **Native check**: 釉式 and 塞格 (the unity formula and Seger), 熔剂 (flux), 热功 (heatwork), 证据锥
(witness cone), 导锥 and 后备锥 (guide and guard cone), 热一号/冷一号 (one number hotter/cooler), 保温 (hold), 低温烘烧
(candling), 惊裂 (dunting), 出筋 (breaks), 隔釉剂 (wax resist), 棚板隔离剂 (kiln wash), 外加 (on top of the base) and 两步验证
(two-step sign-in).

## 1. Style sheet

### Register

Plain, polite written Chinese (书面语), no slang, no 网络用语. Sentences short, as the English is.

- **The reader**: leave the pronoun out wherever Chinese allows, which is most places (MS 4.1.12: "omit the pronouns for
  fluent flow of the text"). Where a pronoun is needed, write **您**. This goes against MS, which asks for 你 since its
  2010s voice, but it is the formal register the project asks for in every language, and Google's Chinese help pages
  write 您 ("您可为自己的账号增添一道额外的安全屏障"); Apple and Microsoft write 你. **Owner decision**: 您 (recommended) or 你,
  one of them in every file. Never mix them in one file, never 妳 or 你们 for one reader.
- **Instructions**: the bare verb, with 请 where the English asks politely ("Please …", or a request in an error):
  「将釉浆过筛两次。」「请重新加载页面。」 Not 「你要…」.
- **Prohibitions**: "do not" → 「不要…」; "never" → 「切勿…」 or 「绝不要…」 (a "never" never weakens to 「最好不要」).
- **Statements**: 「这种釉在 1222 °C 熔融。」
- **Errors** (MS 5.5.2): what failed, then what to do: 「无法加载配方。请重新加载页面。」「密码有误，请再试一次。」 "Could not …" is
  「无法…」.
- **Questions and confirmations**: 「要删除这个配方吗？」
- **Examples**: a button 「保存」; an error 「无法保存配方。请稍后再试。」; a guide sentence 「在釉浆中加水之前，先把粉料称好。」 or, where
  "you" must be said, 「如果您的窑没有温控仪，…」.

### Buttons, menus, headings, labels and tabs

- **Buttons and menu commands**: a verb or verb-object phrase, short, no 请, no 。: 「保存」「打印」「删除」「比较」「另存为副本」「添加原料」.
- **Headings, tabs, page titles**: a noun phrase: 「配方」「设置」「烧窑记录」「测温锥与热功」. A heading that names an activity is the
  activity: "Mixing the glaze" → 「调配釉浆」, "Typing amounts" → 「输入数字」. A heading that is a question stays one: 「釉为什么会熔化？」
- **Field labels**: a noun, with the English's colon as a full-width colon ：and nothing after it: 「原料名称：」. (MS uses the
  half-width colon with a space in software; this page uses the full-width one everywhere, in the app and the guides, so
  a translator has one rule.)
- **Confirmations**: "Saved: {name}" → 「已保存：{name}」.
- **Feature names in running text** in “”: 「点击“比较”。」 (MS: 「单击“完成”按钮」).

### Capitalisation

Chinese has none. Latin words keep the English's case where they are names, codes or units (Orton, SK, UMF, LOI, Ferro
3134, KN95, mg/m³); the English's Title Case is dropped with the English words. A Latin word or trade name inside a
Chinese sentence is written as the maker writes it (Gerstley Borate, Zircopax), never in full-width letters (Ｏｒｔｏｎ).

### Script and characters

- **Simplified characters** as 《通用规范汉字表》 gives them: 账户 (not 帐户, which Microsoft still writes), 粘土 and 黏土 both occur
  (this page: **黏土** in running text, as the 规范 form; 粘土 kept inside quotations and names).
- **Silicon is 硅**: 硅, 二氧化硅, 硅酸盐, 硅酸锆, 硅灰石, 硅肺. **Never 矽**: the check rejects it in a Simplified file, and it is
  Taiwan's form. GBZ 2.1—2019 still names its row **矽尘** and its disease **矽肺**, and Chinese Wikipedia titles its article
  矽肺病 while giving 硅肺 as a name; other mainland texts write 硅肺 and 硅沉着病. The app writes **硅尘** (silica dust) and
  **硅肺** (silicosis). Where a region text must quote the law's own row name, 矽尘 cannot pass the check: **owner
  decision** whether to quote it (with a check exception) or to write 「GBZ 2.1 中游离二氧化硅粉尘一栏」. Recommended: the latter.
- **The check's pairs** (`SCRIPT_PAIRS` in `scripts/i18n-check.mjs`): a Simplified message must never contain 矽 鉀 鈉 鈣 鎂 鋅
  鋁 鐵 燒 窯 溫 軟 設 計 數 頁 選 刪 編 顯 錄 碼 語 戶 幫 這 們 個 為 說 時 發. Write 硅 钾 钠 钙 镁 锌 铝 铁 烧 窑 温 软 设 计 数 页 选 删 编 显 录 码
  语 户 帮 这 们 个 为 说 时 发. Watch for Traditional forms that slip in from Taiwanese or Japanese sources: 釉藥 (write 釉料),
  燒成 (烧成), 窯 (窑), 鐵 (铁), 長石 (长石).
- **Half-width** digits, Latin letters, units and their signs (1222 °C, 5%, SK 6a, KN95). Never full-width digits or
  letters (１２, ＳＫ) or ℃ (write °C).

### Spaces

- **No space between Chinese words**, ever, and **never a full-width space** (U+3000).
- **A half-width space between Chinese and a Latin word, a trade name, a code or a figure**, as MS's examples do
  (「发布到 Web 上」「无法使用 Internet」, 「%d 位用户在线」): 「加入 Ferro 3134」「烧到 Orton 测温锥 6 号」「KN95 防尘口罩」「在 1222 °C 保温」
  「# 种原料」「3 次」「14 天」.
- **No space** next to full-width punctuation or brackets (「“Gerstley Borate”」「釉式（UMF）」「1222 °C。」), and none inside a date
  or a clock time written in Chinese: 2026年10月14日, 20时, 15时40分.
- A unit symbol keeps the English's space: 10 g, 1222 °C, 100 °C/h, 80 目 (see Numbers); % takes none: 5%.
- Inside Latin text the English's spaces stay (Orton cone 6 → Orton 测温锥 6 号; SK 015a; Gerstley Borate).
- **Placeholders**: MS says "if uncertain, remove space". This page: no space beside {region} (its value is Chinese: 在{region}),
  and quotation marks around {name} so no space is needed either way: 「“{name}”已保存。」 A number placeholder takes the space:
  「{count} 种原料」.

### Chinese and Markdown

In the guides, `**…**` and `*…*` do not render when a bracket or stop sits just inside the marks and a Chinese
character just outside them (CommonMark's flanking rule): `**“素烧”**是` shows the asterisks. Keep “”, （）, 。, ，and 、
**outside** the emphasis: `“**素烧**”是`, `**素烧**是`, `**釉式**（UMF）`. Check every bold term in the preview.

### Punctuation

- **Full-width** ，。、；：！？ in Chinese text (GB/T 15834). A sentence ends in 。 even after a figure or a Latin word:
  「烧到 1222 °C。」 Never the half-width . or , as sentence punctuation.
- **、** between items of a list in a sentence (MS: 「Word、Excel」); ， between clauses.
- **Quotation marks**: “…” for quotations, terms and the names of buttons and pages; ‘…’ inside them. Never 「」 (Taiwan
  and Japan) and never straight quotes " around Chinese text. The English's “…” around a quotation becomes “…”.
- **《》** for the titles of works and standards: 《食品安全国家标准 陶瓷制品》, 《陶瓷工艺学》. The five guide titles in running text
  are titles too: 《如何调配釉料》. Not around the app's own feature names (those take “”).
- **Brackets**: full-width （） when what is inside contains Chinese: 「釉式（UMF 以熔剂总和为 1）」; half-width () when it is
  Latin or figures only, with a space outside: 「釉式 (UMF)」 is allowed, but this page prefers 「釉式（UMF）」 for the fixed pair.
  The English's metric figure in brackets after inches: 「12 英寸（30 cm）」.
- **Ranges**: the wave dash **～** (U+FF5E, GB/T 15834's 浪纹线) between figures, no spaces: "cone 06–04" → 「Orton 测温锥 06～04 号」,
  "1222–1240 °C" → 「1222～1240 °C」. **The unit once where the English has it once**: "10 to 60%" → **「10～60%」**. GB/T 15835
  5.1.3 asks for 「15%～30%」 (不写为15~30%), but that adds a figure with a unit the English lacks, which the numbers check
  rejects; MS writes 「10 ~ 20%」. So: the unit once where the English has it once, twice where it has it twice.
- **Colon**: full-width ：, nothing before it.
- **No ！** in the app's own voice.
- **Dashes**: the English's em dash and spaced en dash become ，or ——(rarely); a hyphen in a compound disappears.

### Numbers and units

- **Half-width Arabic digits** wherever the English has digits. Never full-width digits, never Chinese numerals (十二)
  for a figure, never **万** or **亿** (12,500 stays 12,500; GB/T 15835 allows 1.25万, but the check would not find the
  figure).
- **Decimal point and thousands comma**, as English and GB/T 15835 5.1.1: **1.5**, 0.025, **1,000**, 12,500. Four-digit
  numbers may stay ungrouped (GB/T 15835: "四位以内的整数可以不分节"), and the app writes four-digit temperatures so: 1222 °C,
  1000 g, as the English does.
- **A number the English writes as a word** ("two coats", "three tiles", "once") has no figure in the English, so a digit
  here would be a figure the English lacks. Write it in Chinese numerals, as Chinese writes such words anyway (MS 4.1.10:
  "If letter is used in source, usually, Chinese character is used"): 「两层」「三片试片」「一次」「两个配方」. Where the English has the
  digit, the digit stays: "3 coats" → 「3 层」.
- **Units**: half-width symbols as the English writes them, with its space: g, kg, mg, µg, mL, L, mm, cm, °C, °F, °Bé, %,
  mg/m³, µg/dL. Not ℃, not 克 for "g", not 毫米 for "mm". "per 100 g" → 「每 100 g」. Mesh: "80 mesh" → 「80 目」 (目 is the
  potters' and the industry's word; the figure stays). **Inches and feet** are **英寸** and **英尺** (README), with the metric
  figure in brackets as the English gives it.
- **Dates**: 2026年10月14日, 2026年10月, digits and 年月日 with no spaces (GB/T 15835 5.1.4). The check reads 10月 as the
  English's October. Never 二〇二六年.
- **Clock times**: the 24-hour clock, as 20:00 or 20时 (GB/T 15835 allows both; this page uses **20:00** in the app and
  **20时** only in running prose where the English writes "8pm"). "8 am to 5 pm" → 「8:00～17:00」 or 「8:00 至 17:00」. **Never 上午,
  下午, 晚上, 凌晨 or 中午 before an hour**: the check reports them (MS's own AM/PM table uses them; this page does not).
  "24 hours" → 「24 小时」.
- **Temperatures and rates**: 「1222 °C」「100 °C/h」 (or 「每小时 100 °C」 where the English writes it in words with the figure).

### Cones

Mainland potters mostly fire by temperature with a 温控仪; those who use cones buy **Orton** cones, which Orton's dealer
calls **测温锥** (with 测温三角锥 beside it, 麟文), as GB/T 13794-2017 calls its reference cones 标准测温锥. Shops and potters
also say 三角锥 and 温锥; the app writes **测温锥** everywhere (三角锥 is also the geometric triangular pyramid). The app also
has **SK cones** (Japan, JIS R 8101) and the German **Seger cones** of the firing guide.

- **Orton cones**: 「Orton 测温锥」, the name in Latin letters (not 奥顿, which some sellers write: the bag and box print Orton).
  The number is written as on the cone, **with 号**: "cone 6" → 「Orton 测温锥 6 号」 at the first mention in each section and in
  every table, label and setting, 「6 号锥」 after that within the same paragraph. "cone 06" → 「Orton 测温锥 06 号」: the zero
  stays (麟文: "号码前面加了'0'以区分"; Orton's own boxes print it 06, never O6). "cone 6 glazes" → 「6 号锥釉」 or 「Orton 6 号锥的釉」.
  The setting "To Orton cones" → 「按 Orton 测温锥」.
- **SK cones** are always written **SK**, a half-width space, and the number exactly as printed: 「SK 8」「SK 6a」「SK 015a」. The
  noun is 测温锥: 「SK 测温锥」; named 「SK 测温锥（JIS R 8101）」 only where the English names the standard.
- **Seger**: **塞格** appears only where the English says Seger: the older European 「塞格锥」 of the firing guide (Germany's
  series: say nothing that makes them SK or Orton cones), and the Seger formula (塞格式, The unity formula, below). Mainland
  texts also write 赛格 (于岩: 赛格式); 塞格 is Taiwan's and the commoner form in the papers read. **Owner decision**: 塞格
  (recommended) or 赛格, one of them for both.
- **Never** give an SK number for an Orton number or the reverse, never write ≈, 相当于, 对应 between them, and never add a
  temperature or conversion the English lacks.
- **One number hotter, one number cooler**: **「热一号的锥」「冷一号的锥」** (the English has no figure, so 一 is a word). Never
  「高一号」「低一号」「大一号」「小一号」 alone: in the 0-series (022 to 01) a larger number is cooler, and 麟文's own 「低号测温锥」 means
  the cooler one only by that convention. Where a longer form reads better: 「温度高一档的相邻锥号」. "two numbers hotter" → 「热两号」.
- Witness cone: 「证据锥」 (麟文); cone pack: 「三支一组的测温锥」; guide, firing and guard cones: 「导锥」「烧制锥」「后备锥」 (麟文) (2.4).
- Not 圆锥 (the geometric cone), not 锥体 alone, not 火锥.

### The unity formula

**釉式**, with 「（UMF）」 after it at the first mention on each page: 「釉式（UMF）」. Mainland textbooks write the Seger
formula as 釉式 (于岩: "实验公式(赛格式)表示法 … 这种表示法称为坯式或釉式"); the glaze's form of it, with the fluxes summed to 1, is
the 釉式 (the body's is the 坯式). Where the English says "also called the Seger formula", write 「也称塞格式」. The app's short
forms "unity formula", "Unity formula" and "Unity:" are all 「釉式」/「釉式：」. The three columns, as the textbooks name
them: 碱性氧化物 (RO and R₂O, the fluxes), 中性氧化物 (R₂O₃) and 酸性氧化物 (RO₂); the app's own column names are in 2.1. "in
unity" → 「在釉式中」; "scaled so the fluxes add up to 1" → 「以熔剂（碱性氧化物）总和为 1」. **Native check**, with the 塞格/赛格
question above.

### Gender-neutral wording

Chinese has no grammatical gender, but the written pronoun does: 他 and 她. MS 3.1: "Don't use gendered pronouns (她, 他,
etc.) in generic references. Instead: Rewrite to use the substitutive form (其). Omit the pronoun … Refer to a person's
role". So: no 他, 她, 他/她 or TA for a generic person; leave it out, write 其, or name the role: 「陶艺人」 (a potter, any
level), 「使用者」, 「工作室成员」. The reader is 您 or no one (Register). Avoid gendered job words (女工, 师傅 for any potter).
Children are 「儿童」 or 「孩子」.

### The reader in the guides

Plain, polite prose, short sentences, the English's order of ideas. Instructions are bare verbs (with 请 only where the
English says please); explanations are statements: 「釉层太厚会流釉。」「素烧过的坯体可以立即施釉。」 Where the English says
"you", leave it out or name the thing (「您的窑」 only when needed). No classical forms (之, 乃, 则 where 就 will do), no
nominal chains (「进行…的操作」). Quotations from sources are translated into “…” and keep their figures as the English

### Law in China

As the law and the standards write it, never paraphrased: the words below are fixed, and the region texts must use
them. Where the English names no standard, add none (no GB number, no GBZ number, no year).

- **以上, 以下, 不超过, 超过**: the Civil Code (民法典 第一千二百五十九条, from usage: not re-read) says 以上、以下、以内 include the
  number and 不满、超过、以外 do not. So: "at most", "no more than", "or less", "up to" → **不超过**, **以下** or **≤** as the
  standard writes it (the limit itself passes); "less than", "under", "below" → **低于** or **小于** or **＜** (it does not);
  "at least", "or more", "from" → **不低于**, **以上** or **≥**; "more than", "over", "above" → **超过** or **＞**. Never 低于 for "at
  most" and never 以下 for "less than": a slip moves a legal limit. Check each in the food-contact, silica and mask texts.
- **Silica (GBZ 2.1)**: "exposure limit" → **职业接触限值**; "8-hour average" → **时间加权平均容许浓度（PC-TWA）**; "respirable dust" →
  **呼尘** (呼吸性粉尘); "total dust" → **总尘** (总粉尘); "free silica" → **游离二氧化硅** (the standard writes 游离 SiO2); the bands
  as the standard writes them, "10%≤游离 SiO2 含量≤50%", "50%＜游离 SiO2 含量≤80%", "游离 SiO2 含量＞80%", only where the English
  gives them. Its limits are binding: "本标准正文表1～表3中的职业接触限值为强制性的" (`silica.kind.binding` → 「具有法律约束力的限值。」). The row's
  own name is 矽尘 (Script and characters).
- **Respirators (GB 2626-2019)**: the standard is 《呼吸防护 自吸过滤式防颗粒物呼吸器》; the classes **KN90, KN95, KN100** (non-oily
  particles) and **KP90, KP95, KP100** (oily too), kept as codes; facepieces 随弃式面罩 (disposable) and 可更换式半面罩 (half mask)
  (the class names are the standard's; the facepiece names from usage, the text was not read). "certified" → 「符合 GB 2626 的」
  only where the English names a standard; otherwise 「经认证的」.
- **Food contact (GB 4806.4)**: 《食品安全国家标准 陶瓷制品》; "lead and cadmium release" → **铅、镉迁移量** (the labs also write 溶出量);
  kinds of piece 扁平制品 (flatware), 空心制品 (hollowware), 小空心制品, 大空心制品, 口缘 (rim) (labs' summaries; the table was not
  read). Labs also name a GB 4806.4—2023 《食品接触用陶瓷材料及制品》: **owner**: confirm which edition is in force before the CN
  region data is written.
- **GHS (GB 30000 series, GB/T 16483)**: 象形图 (pictogram), 信号词 **危险** and **警告**, 危险说明 (hazard statement), 防范说明;
  **化学品安全技术说明书**（SDS）. Chinese H-statements as Chinese SDSs print them: H301 吞咽会中毒, H302 吞咽有害, H350 可能致癌,
  H360 可能对生育能力或胎儿造成伤害, H372 长期或反复接触会对器官造成损害 (Usage: Chinese SDSs, not re-read today). Codes kept.
- **Who to call**: **120** (急救), 119 (fire), 110 (police) (not verified on an official page; the numbers are not in doubt).
  The poison line: **中国疾控中心职业卫生与中毒控制所 中毒咨询热线**, as its page names it, and as the region data will give it.
  Bodies as named: 国家卫生健康委员会, 应急管理部, 国家市场监督管理总局, 中国疾病预防控制中心.
- **EU law**, where the English names it (Directive 84/500/EEC, Directive (EU) 2017/2398, Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008): no
  official Chinese text exists, so keep the number with a plain Chinese name: 「欧盟第 84/500/EEC 号指令（与食品接触的陶瓷制品）」,
  「欧盟指令 (EU) 2017/2398」, 「欧盟 CLP 法规 (EC) No 1272/2008」. The EU's food categories must still read so that the 3 L
  applies only to packaging and storage vessels: "Cooking ware of any size, and packaging and storage vessels over 3 L" →
  「各种容量的烹饪器皿，以及容量超过 3 L 的包装和储存容器」.

  （美国食品药品监督管理局）.

### Pitfalls

- **釉, 釉料, 釉浆, 搪瓷, 清漆**: the glaze on a pot is **釉** (釉面, 釉层, 施釉, 透明釉, 天目釉); the mixed material, dry or in a bag,
  is **釉料**; glaze mixed with water is **釉浆**. 搪瓷 is enamel on metal and 清漆 varnish: never for a glaze. Not 釉药 (Taiwan
  and Japan).
- **熔块 and 熔剂**: a frit is **熔块** (glass made in a factory); a flux is **熔剂** (the oxide's role and the category). 助熔剂 is
  what 钧瓷网 calls the secondary fluxes (calcite, talc, zinc) as against the main 熔剂 (feldspar); the app uses **熔剂** for every
  flux, and 助熔 only in "fluxing power" (助熔能力). Never 焊剂 (soldering flux).
- **窑, 炉, 烤箱**: a potter's kiln is **窑** (电窑, 气窑); 窑炉 is fine in compounds (窑炉看管器); 炉 alone is a furnace or stove;
  烤箱 is a kitchen oven, used only for "in an oven" in the cooking-ware note.
- **素烧 and 釉烧**: bisque firing is 素烧, the ware 素坯 (素烧坯); the glaze firing is 釉烧. Not 本烧 (Japanese).
- **陶器, 炻器, 瓷器**: earthenware **陶器**, stoneware **炻器**, porcelain **瓷器**. 维基 notes that China has no traditional 炻器 class
  (Ding ware counts as 瓷器), so many mainland potters call high-fired stoneware 陶 or 粗陶; the **show English** setting and
  the guides' explanation matter here. Where the English means a firing range ("low-fire earthenware"), say so: 「低温陶器」.
- **石英, 硅石, 二氧化硅**: the material is **石英** (石英粉); the oxide **二氧化硅** (SiO₂); "silica" in the app's categories and the
  unity formula is 二氧化硅. 硅石 (silica rock) is not used. Not 硅 (the element), not 硅胶 or 硅酮 (silicone).
- **碳酸钙, 方解石, 石灰石, 石灰**: whiting is calcium carbonate: **碳酸钙** (shops and 钧瓷网 sell it as 方解石 or 碳酸钙); 石灰 is lime
  (CaO or slaked lime), not whiting; 石灰石 is limestone. CaO is **钙 (CaO)** / **氧化钙**; "lime" in a frit's name → 石灰.
  "Jingdezhen lime glaze" is 石灰釉, made of **釉果** and **釉灰**: these two words appear only where the English names them.
- **高岭土 and 瓷土, 瓷石**: kaolin is **高岭土**; 瓷土 also means it in older texts (景德镇陶瓷在线), and "china clay" (UK) is 高岭土. **瓷石**
  is Chinese porcelain stone, a different material from "Cornish stone": never a translation of it.
- **溶出试验** for the leach test: a test of the metals a fired piece releases, **not narrowed to 铅、镉** unless the English names
  those two. The law's figure is a 迁移量; the potter's test is 溶出试验.
- **防尘口罩, 呼吸防护用品, 排风扇**: a respirator is **防尘口罩** (the certified kind: KN95 in China); the law's word 自吸过滤式防颗粒物呼吸器
  is used only where the English names the standard; 呼吸防护用品 is the general class. A hospital ventilator is 呼吸机, never meant
  here. Ventilation is 通风, a fan 排风扇. A "nuisance dust mask" → 「普通防尘面罩（非认证）」 or 「一次性普通口罩」: never 防尘口罩 alone.
- **指南 and 使用说明书**: the app's five guides are **指南**; a kiln's, controller's or kiln sitter's manual is **使用说明书**. Never
  the same word.
- **开片 and 釉裂**: crackle (wanted) is **开片**; crazing as a defect is **釉裂**. The same cracks, different intent: keep the two
  words apart.
- **should, must, may, some**: "must" → **必须**; "should" → **应** (应该); "should not" → **不应**. "must be tested" → 「必须经过检测」
  (the test is done, not passed); never 「必须通过检测」, which means passing it. "may" (a hazard) → **可能**. "some" (some fine
  quartz) → **一些** or **部分**, never 「少量」「一点」.
- **注销**: on mainland apps 注销账户 means deleting the account; so "sign out" is **退出登录** (not 注销, which Microsoft writes)
  and "Delete my account" is **删除我的账户**.
- **{region}** is a country name in Chinese from the browser (中国, 德国), so write each sentence to read with any country:
  「在{region}」「{region}的规定」, never 「{region}国」.
- **Trade names** take no grammar; put a generic noun beside them where the sentence needs one: 「Ferro 3134 熔块」「Custer 长石」.

in the Notes: the word Chinese potters and the industry use, not read on a page in this session; **No source**: a best
choice with no Chinese source.
