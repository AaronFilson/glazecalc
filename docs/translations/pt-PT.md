# Português europeu: folha de estilo e glossário (style sheet and glossary)

The rules and terms for translating Glazecalc into European Portuguese, for potters in Portugal (pt-PT, the 1990
spelling agreement, not Brazilian Portuguese). Every pt-PT message and guide follows this page: one Portuguese word for
one thing. The terms come from Portuguese shops for potters (their category and product names), a Portuguese kiln
dealer's guides, the Lisbon fine-arts faculty's ceramics programmes, the Centro Tecnológico da Cerâmica e do Vidro, the
EU term base IATE (its ceramics block and its CLP entries), the Priberam dictionary and Microsoft's pt-PT style guide.
Brazilian sources were read only to compare. Where sources disagree or none was found, the Notes say so.

## 1. Style sheet

**Register.** Formal, as Portuguese software is. Address the reader with the third-person verb and no pronoun:
"Escolha a matéria-prima.", "Peneire o vidrado duas vezes.", "Use uma máscara FFP3.", "Tem de iniciar sessão para
guardar." Never _tu_. Never _você_, which can read as curt in Portugal; where a pronoun seems needed, rephrase (MS
4.1.12: "Não pode fechar…", "Recomendamos que verifique…"), or use the possessive _o seu / a sua_ ("a sua conta", "a sua
pasta"). Use _por favor_ only where the English says "please" ("Please try again." → "Tente novamente."; MS drops it
too). "We" for the app's advice is fine where the English has it ("Recomendamos…", "Sugerimos…").

**Buttons, menu items, headings, field labels and tab names.** The pt-PT convention (MS 4.1.16, and every major
Portuguese UI: Guardar, Abrir, Imprimir, Cancelar):

- Buttons and menu commands, the user telling the app to act: the **infinitive**: "Guardar", "Guardar como cópia",
  "Imprimir", "Comparar", "Adicionar matérias-primas", "Descartar a experiência", "Sim, remover". Never the imperative
  ("Guarde") and never a noun ("Gravação") on a button. No full stop after an infinitive command.
- Instructions to the user, in sentences, hints and the guides: the **imperative** (third person): "Introduza uma
  quantidade (0 é válido).", "Abra a página de definições."
- Headings, page titles, tab and menu names: a noun phrase ("Receitas", "Definições", "Registos de cozedura", "Cones e
  trabalho térmico"), or the infinitive where the English heading is an action ("Read the unity formula" → "Ler a
  fórmula de Seger"). A question stays a question.
- Field labels: a noun, with the colon where the English has one: "Nome da matéria-prima:".
- Ongoing actions: _a_ + infinitive, never the gerund: "Removing..." → "A remover…", "Loading..." → "A carregar…"
  (MS: "A imprimir ficheiro…"). The gerund (_removendo_) is Brazilian.
- Error messages: "Could not discard the trial." → "Não foi possível descartar a experiência."

**Capitalisation.** Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and tabs: capital on the first word and on proper
nouns only ("Guardar como cópia", "Peso equivalente", "Ar primário", "Fórmula de Seger"). Where the English uses Title
Case ("Fired Weight", "Primary Air", "Unity Formula"), still write sentence case; this follows Portuguese usage and the
app's own English, which is mostly sentence case. Lower case after a colon (MS). Month and day names, _cone_, _grés_,
_faiança_ and the like are lower case. Trade names keep their form (Orton, Seger, Ferro, Kiln-Sitter, Glazecalc).

**Punctuation and spacing.**

- Quotation marks: «…» (CLDR pt-PT), with “…” inside them. Use « » for quoted sources, for words in the UI's own
  quotes ("boron blue" → «azul de boro») and for named UI items in running text that the English does not tag. Not
  "…" or “…” as the outer marks (Brazilian and MS-software habit).
- Apostrophe: rare in Portuguese (_d’água_, _Sant’Ana_); if one is needed use the typographic ’ (U+2019), and `''` only
  where the README asks (before `{`, `}` or `#`, and in `safety` and `records`).
- No space before ; : ! ? (unlike French). No space inside brackets.
- Ranges: the en dash, no spaces, unit once at the end: "1222–1240 °C", "cones 06–04", "1–3%". In a sentence "de 1222
  a 1240 °C" is also fine; do not mix ("de 1222–1240 °C").
- Hyphens in compounds follow the 1990 agreement: _matéria-prima_, _fórmula-limite_, _valor-limite_, _baixo-vidrado_,
  _alcalino-terroso_, _pré-aquecimento_, _autossuportado_ (s doubled), _semimáscara_, _infravermelho_.

**Numbers.**

- Decimal comma: "1,5%", "0,01 g", "1,45". (`{{SG 1.45}}` in the guides stays as written; the app renders it.)
- Thousands: four-digit numbers are **not** grouped ("1240 °C", "1000 g"), as CLDR pt-PT and the app print them; five
  digits and more take a no-break space ("12 500 g"). Never a period: "1.240" is Brazilian, and in pt-PT reads as one
  point two four.
- A space before °C, °F and every unit symbol: "1240 °C", "100 °C/h", "250 g", "80 mesh". **No space before %**:
  "15%", "0,5–2%", as CLDR pt-PT, the app's own numbers and the Portuguese press write it.
- Number words: keep the English's digits as digits ("2 coats" → "2 demãos"), and the English's words as words.
- Ordinals: "1.º", "2.ª" if ever needed. Times: "14h30" or "14:30".

**Cones.** The app's cones are Orton's (American); Seger cones (German, SK) and Staffordshire cones are different
series with different numbers, and are never converted or confused.

- "cone 6" → "cone 6"; "cone 06" → "cone 06" (said "cone zero seis"; the zero is never dropped); "cone 5–6" → "cones
  5–6"; "fire to cone 6" → "cozer ao cone 6"; "a cone 6 glaze" → "um vidrado para cone 6"; "mid fire, cone 5–6" →
  "média temperatura, cones 5–6".
- "cone 04 bisque" → "chacota ao cone 04"; "bisque to cone 04" → "cozer a chacota ao cone 04".
- "Orton cone" → "cone Orton" (IATE 1082863; CS sells "Cone pirométrico | Orton"); "Orton cone 6" → "cone Orton 6";
  "Orton's chart" → "a tabela de cones Orton". "Seger cones" → "cones Seger" (IATE 1434358).

**The unity formula.** Portuguese potters call it the **fórmula de Seger**. Write "fórmula de Seger (UMF)" at its first
use on a page or guide section, then "fórmula de Seger". "unity molecular formula (UMF)" → "fórmula de Seger (UMF)";
"unity formula" → "fórmula de Seger"; "New to unity formulas?" → "Nunca usou fórmulas de Seger?"; the inline label
"Unity:" → "Seger:". The scaling itself ("unity", "in unity") is _unitário_ ("Molecular / Unity" → "Molecular /
Unitária"; "fluxes add up to 1" → "os fundentes somam 1"). Keep UMF uninflected and feminine ("a UMF").

**Gender-neutral wording.** Portuguese adjectives and participles agree with the reader, whose gender is unknown.
Write so nothing agrees with the reader: "Bem-vindo!" → "Boas-vindas!"; "Está registado" → "A conta foi criada";
"Tem a certeza?" (neutral) is fine; "Are you sure you want to…?" → "Pretende mesmo…?". For people in general use
_ceramista_ (o/a, same form) or the plural ("quem começa", "os ceramistas"); avoid _oleiro/oleira_ for the reader and
never write "o(a)" or "o/a" (MS 3.1).

**The reader in the guides.** The same formal third person: "Peneire o vidrado", "Pese cada matéria-prima",
"Recomendamos que comece com duas cozeduras". Address a new potter plainly and kindly, as the English does; short
sentences, everyday words. Possessives "o seu forno", "a sua pasta" where the English says "your"; drop them where
Portuguese does not need them ("Limpe a bancada", not "Limpe a sua bancada").

**Feature names in running text.** Write the on-screen label exactly, with its capital, inside the tag or bold the
English uses; where the English has neither, put the label in « ». The labels:

| English feature               | pt-PT label                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Compare                       | Comparar                             |
| Suggest amounts (and compare) | Sugerir quantidades (e comparar)     |
| Match with what I have        | Fazer com o que tenho                |
| Replace lead                  | Substituir o chumbo                  |
| Settings                      | Definições                           |
| Notebook (studio notebook)    | Caderno (caderno do atelier)         |
| Library                       | Biblioteca                           |
| Standard                      | Padrão                               |
| My materials                  | As minhas matérias-primas            |
| Trial                         | Experiência (Período de experiência) |

"Under Settings" → "em <settings>Definições</settings>"; "click **Compare**" → "clique em **Comparar**" (MS uses
_clicar em_ and _selecionar_; avoid the colloquial _carregar em_).

**Pitfalls of pt-PT in this field.**

- **Brazilian words to avoid** (the potter is in Portugal): _esmalte_ → **vidrado**; _biscoito_ → **chacota**;
  _queima / queimar_ → **cozedura / cozer** (_queima_ only for burning off organic matter); _massa_ → **pasta**;
  _resfriar / resfriamento_ → **arrefecer / arrefecimento**; _peneira_ → **peneiro**; _serragem_ → **serradura**;
  _registro_ → **registo**; _porcentagem_ → **percentagem**; _contato_ → **contacto**; _fosco_ → **mate**; _rachadura_ →
  **fissura / racha**; _caulim_ (also Portuguese, but the bags say otherwise) → **caulino**; _cromo_ → **crómio**; _zircônio_ →
  **zircónio**; _oxigênio_ → **oxigénio**; _pirômetro_ → **pirómetro**; _crônico_ → **crónico**; _câncer_ → **cancro**;
  mineral names in _-ita_ (_dolomita_, _bentonita_, _colemanita_, _petalita_) → **-ite** (_dolomite_, _bentonite_,
  _colemanite_, _petalite_), as Portuguese shops label them.
- **Brazilian software words to avoid**: _arquivo_ → **ficheiro**; _tela_ → **ecrã**; _usuário_ → **utilizador**;
  _salvar_ → **guardar**; _configurações_ → **definições**; _senha_ → **palavra-passe**; _excluir / deletar_ →
  **eliminar**; _baixar_ → **transferir**; _desfazer_ → **anular**; _fazer login / entrar_ → **iniciar sessão**;
  _registrar_ → **registar**; the gerund (_salvando_) → **a guardar**; proclitic pronouns (_Se inscreva_) → enclisis
  (**Inscreva-se**); _tem que_ → **tem de**.
- **Spelling (1990 agreement)**: _ação, ótimo, atual, elétrico, direção, exato, seleção, refratário, ótico, receção,
  perspetiva, detetor_ (silent c and p go); but _facto_ and _contacto_ keep their c, which is pronounced in Portugal.
- **_cozer_ (fire) is not _coser_ (sew)**; both sound alike. _cozedura_, never _cosedura_.
- **False friends**: _esmalte_ in Portugal is an opaque tin glaze (_faiança esmaltada_), enamel on metal or nail
  varnish, not a glaze in general. _louça / loiça_ is tableware of any body, not earthenware. _soda_ is sodium
  carbonate or caustic soda and _potassa_ caustic potash: write the oxides as **sódio** and **potássio**. _sílica_
  (SiO₂) is not _silício_ (Si) or _silicone_. _respirador_ also means a hospital ventilator: say **máscara de proteção
  respiratória**. _grés_ also means sandstone and floor tile (_grés porcelânico_); context decides. _cristalino_ also
  means crystal-clear: a clear glaze is **transparente**. _tempo_ is both time and weather (firing-log fields "Time" →
  "Hora", "Weather" → "Estado do tempo"). _retração_ is shrinkage, so it cannot also name crawling.
- **Spanish interference** (many Portuguese shop sheets are Spanish): not _horno_, _bizcocho_, _esmalte_,
  _pérdida por calcinación_, _blanco de España_ as the main name; Portuguese has its own words for all of them.

Gender: (o) masculine, (a) feminine. **show English** marks the terms whose English a reader can ask to see:
`<en>…</en>` in messages, `{{en: …}}` in the guides.
