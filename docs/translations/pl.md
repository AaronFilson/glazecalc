# Polski: arkusz stylu i glosariusz (style sheet and glossary)

For translating Glazecalc into Polish (`pl`). Read [README.md](README.md) first; [terms.md](terms.md) gives the meaning
of every English term below. The terms come from Polish suppliers' shops and data sheets (AGANI, HEHO, Świat Gliny,
Pracownia Kamionka, Ceramikownia, Prodesco, Łukasiewicz-ICiMB), Polish vocational and university ceramics texts, Polish
potters' sites, Polish kiln manuals and sellers, and, for safety, Polish law and CIOP-PIB. Where no Polish source was
found, the note says so: those rows are for a Polish potter to check.

Key to sources cited by name in the notes (the full address is given once here):

- **AGANI**: [agani.pl, Surowce](https://www.agani.pl/category/surowce) (mostly Carl Jäger stock; its product texts
  are translated from German)

## 1. Style sheet

### Register

- **Formal, but without Pan/Pani and without ty.** Pan/Pani needs the reader's gender, and ty is too familiar for
  running text. Polish has gender-free forms that are both formal and natural:
  - **Impersonal verbs** for instructions and advice: _Należy przesiać szkliwo._ _Można dodać 1–2 % bentonitu._
    _Warto zacząć od płytki testowej._ _Nie należy wdychać pyłu._
  - **The -no/-to forms** for what has happened: _Zapisano recepturę._ _Nie znaleziono surowca._ _Usunięto._
  - **The passive or się** where a sentence describes a process: _Szkliwo nakłada się na biskwit._ _Receptura została
    zapisana._
  - **The infinitive** for numbered steps and for questions in dialogs: _1. Odważyć surowce._ _Usunąć tę recepturę?_
  - **Proszę + infinitive** for a polite request in an error message: _Proszę spróbować ponownie._ Use it sparingly.
- **Never write a past tense in the first or second person** (_zapisałeś_, _zapisałaś_). It shows gender. Use the
  -no/-to form, the passive or the present tense instead, as the MS guide (section 3.1) recommends: _Wprowadzone hasło
  jest nieprawidłowe._, not _Wprowadziłeś…_.
- **Drop "your"** where you can, or replace it with an impersonal adjective: "Your materials on hand" is _Posiadane
  surowce_, and "Your trial" is _Okres próbny_. Where the user speaks to the app, use the first person, as MS does
  ("Remember my password" is _Pamiętaj moje hasło_): "My materials" is _Moje surowce_, and "Match with what I have" is
  _Dopasuj do tego, co mam_.
- **The app speaks as "we"** where the English does ("we suggest"): _proponujemy_, _zalecamy_ (MS section 4.1.8).

### Buttons, menus, headings, labels and tabs

- **Buttons and commands use the imperative singular:** _Zapisz_, _Drukuj_, _Porównaj_, _Usuń_, _Anuluj_,
  _Wypróbuj teraz_. This is what Windows, Office, Google and Apple products do in Polish (MS writes _kliknij przycisk
  Anuluj_). Polish users read a button's imperative as the name of a command, not as ty-address. The infinitive
  (_Zapisać_) looks stiff on a button, and a verbal noun (_Zapisywanie_) reads as a heading. The imperative belongs on
  buttons and commands only, never in running text.
- **Menu items, page titles, tab names, headings and field labels are nouns or noun phrases:** _Receptury_,
  _Surowce_, _Ustawienia_, _Dzienniki wypałów_, _Nazwa surowca:_. A heading that is a question or an instruction in
  English becomes a noun phrase or an impersonal sentence: "What to watch for" is _Na co uważać_.
- **Checkbox and option labels** use the first person or the infinitive where the user tells the app what to do:
  _Pokazuj angielskie terminy_ is fine; avoid _Pokaż mi_.
- **In running text, name a UI element with a common noun before it** and keep the label exactly as the UI writes it,
  with its capital and uninflected: _przycisk Porównaj_, _zakładka Standardowe_, _strona Surowce_, _ustawienie Gęstość
  szkliwa_. The noun takes the case: _na stronie Surowce_, _w ustawieniach konta_. This follows MS section 4.1.6. Use no
  quotation marks around labels; bold them where the English bolds them.

### Capitalisation

- **Sentence case everywhere:** only the first word of a title, heading, button, tab or label has a capital, plus
  proper names. _Porównaj dwie receptury_, not _Porównaj Dwie Receptury_ (MS section 4.1.4: copying English title case
  "is an error").
- **Proper names keep their capital in every case:** _stożek Ortona_, _wzór Segera_, _stożki Segera_, _pierścienie
  Bullersa_. Adjectives made from them do not: _segerowski_ (rarely needed).
- Months, days, languages and nationalities are lower case: _angielski_, _w październiku_.

### Punctuation

- **Quotation marks:** „…” (low then high), with «…» inside: „Szkliwo «błękitu borowego» powstaje…”. A full stop or
  comma goes **outside** the closing mark, even when the whole sentence is quoted (MS). The UI's quoted terms, such as
  "boron blue", get „…”.
- **Apostrophe:** Polish rarely needs one. Use the typographic ’ (only in a foreign name whose last letter is not
  pronounced, before the ending: _Joyce’a_). Never write _Orton’s_ or _Seger’s_: use the genitive, _Ortona_, _Segera_.
  Before `{`, `}` or `#` a straight apostrophe must be doubled (README).
- **No space before ; : ! ?** Polish puts the mark straight after the word.
- **Dashes:** the en dash – with spaces as a sentence dash: _Szkliwo – cienka warstwa szkła – …_. The en dash
  **without spaces** in ranges (MS): _1222–1240 °C_, _stożek 5–6_, _1–3 %_.
- **Colon after a label**, as in English: _Nazwa surowca:_.

### Numbers and units

- **Decimal comma:** _1,5_; _0,01 g_; _SiO₂ : Al₂O₃ 8,5 : 1_.
- **Thousands:** four-digit numbers are **not** grouped: _1222 °C_, _2232 °F_, _1000 g_. From five digits, group with a
  no-break space: _12 000_. Never a full stop as a thousands separator. This follows CLDR and MS section 4.1.11.
- **A no-break space between a number and its unit, including °C and %:** _1240 °C_, _5 %_, _100 g_, _573 °C_. This is
  the rule of the Polish law on units of measure, as the Central Office of Measures restates it
  ([GUM](https://infokiosk.gum.gov.pl/inf/aktualnosci/komunikaty/2826,Liczbe-i-towarzyszacy-jej-symbol-zapisujemy-z-odstepem.pdf)).
  Press and some shops write _5%_; Glazecalc uses the space. Write the symbol _°C_, never _st. C_ (which
  `Intl` produces for Polish) or _stopni C_.
- **Numbers stay figures**, never words, and keep the English's unit (README).
- **Plurals:** Polish has `one` (1), `few` (2–4, 22–24…), `many` (0, 5–21, 25–31…) and `other` (fractions). Write all
  four: `{count, plural, one {# surowiec} few {# surowce} many {# surowców} other {# surowca}}`. Fractions take the
  genitive singular: _1,5 surowca_, _2,5 grama_.

### Cones

- **"cone 6" is _stożek 6_; "cone 06" is _stożek 06_**, read "zero sześć". Inflect the noun, never the number: _do
  stożka 6_, _na stożek 6_, _przy stożku 06_. Polish pages write cone numbers this way
  ([pl.wikipedia, Stożki Segera](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stożki_Segera); [CW](https://ceramicznawyspa.pl/o-stozkach-pirometrycznych/)).
- **"fire to cone 6"** is _wypalać do stożka 6_; **"cone 6 glazes"** are _szkliwa na stożek 6_; **"cone 04 bisque"** is
  _biskwit na stożek 04_ or _wypał biskwitowy do stożka 04_; **"cone 5–6"** is _stożek 5–6_.
- **"Orton cone" is _stożek Ortona_** (pl.wikipedia uses it). Glazecalc's cones are always Orton's American cones.
  Polish potters also know **Seger cones, _stożki Segera_**, whose numbers mean different temperatures (Seger 6a is not
  Orton 6). Never write "SK" or "Seger" for an Orton cone, and never turn an Orton number into a Seger one. Where the
  English says only "cone 6", write _stożek 6_; add _Ortona_ where the English names Orton.

### The unity formula

- Name it **_wzór Segera_** (m), with "UMF" after it the first time on a page: _wzór Segera (UMF)_. This is the name in
  the vocational textbook (PCEZ: "wzoru (formuły) Segera", then "wzór Segera" throughout) and on CW
  ([reguły Segera](https://ceramicznawyspa.pl/reguly-segera-paskudna-sprawa-cz-2-2/)). _Formuła Segera_ (f) also
  occurs, as a CW tag; do not mix the two. Avoid literal _jednostkowy wzór cząsteczkowy_. The UI's "Unity formula",
  "Unity" and "Unity:" are all _Wzór Segera_.

### Gender-neutral wording

- Besides avoiding gendered past tenses (above), refer to people by what they do, in the plural or impersonally:
  _ceramicy_, _osoby, które szkliwią_, or the impersonal verb. A generic masculine noun (_ceramik_, _użytkownik_) is
  understood as neutral in manuals (MS section 3.1), but a plural or impersonal sentence is usually smoother.
- For someone named in a source, follow how they are named there.

### The reader in the guides

- The guides teach a new potter. Write **instructions in impersonal forms or the infinitive**: _Należy odważyć
  surowce i przesiać szkliwo przez sito 80 mesh._ _Najpierw warto wypalić płytkę testową._ _Szkliwo nakłada się
  cienką warstwą._ A numbered list of steps uses the infinitive: _1. Odważyć wodę. 2. Wsypać proszek._
- **Never _ty_, _twój_ or a 2nd-person imperative in running text,** and never _Pan/Pani_. Where English says "you"
  in general ("you cannot see it"), use an impersonal form: _nie widać go_, _nie da się go wyczuć_.
- Warnings stay as firm as the English: _Nie należy…_, _Nigdy nie…_, _Trzeba…_.

### Pitfalls

- **_glazura_ is wall tile, not glaze.** A glaze is _szkliwo_ (n), "to glaze" is _szkliwić_, glazing is
  _szkliwienie_. [Wiktionary](https://pl.wiktionary.org/wiki/glazura) gives _glazura_ as a tile coating and,
  colloquially, the tiles. AGANI's product texts say _glazura_ (from German _Glasur_); its menus and every other shop
  say _szkliwo_. _Polewa_ is the traditional folk word: only for historical lead-glazed ware. _Emalia_ is enamel on
  metal, never a pottery glaze.
- **_krzem_ is silicon, _krzemień_ is flint, _krzemionka_ is silica (SiO₂), _silikon_ is silicone.**
- **_glin_ is aluminium, _glina_ clay, _glinka_ an earthy clay, _tlenek glinu_ alumina (Al₂O₃).** Do not use _glinka_
  for alumina.
- **_soda_ is sodium carbonate** (_soda kalcynowana_). The oxide Na₂O is _tlenek sodu_, or _sód_ for short. **_potaż_
  is potassium carbonate**; the oxide K₂O is _tlenek potasu_, or _potas_.
- **_wapno_ is building lime.** The oxide is _tlenek wapnia_, or _wapń_; whiting is _kreda_.
- **_respirator_ is a hospital ventilator in Polish.** A dust mask is _półmaska filtrująca_ or _półmaska z filtrami_;
  the formal class is _sprzęt ochrony układu oddechowego_.
- **_stabilizator do szkliw_ on a shop page is a suspender** (AGANI), and HEHO calls zircon a _stabilizator kolorów_.
  In the unity formula, _stabilizatory_ are the R₂O₃ oxides; keep the two apart.
- **_fajans_, _majolika_ and _terakota_ are particular kinds of earthenware**, not the general word.
- **_biskwit_ also means unglazed porcelain figurines.** In a firing context it is bisque ware.
- **_wypał na ostro_** means a glaze firing to studio potters, a high porcelain glost firing in industry, and a fast
  ramp on CW's [firing-curve page](https://ceramicznawyspa.pl/o-krzywych-wypalu-ceramiki-podstawy/). Use _wypał
  szkliwa_.
- **_czerep_** is the fired body (and also a potsherd). The unfired clay body is _masa_ (_masa ceramiczna_).
- **_pirometr_** in Polish usage leans toward optical instruments; for the kiln's thermocouple readout say _pirometr
  (wskaźnik temperatury)_ where it matters.
- **_przepisy_ also means regulations**, which the safety text cites often; hence _receptura_ for a recipe (2.8).
- **German calques from AGANI's texts** (_glazura_, _Kalkspat_, _Calz._, _WAK_ for expansion) are not Polish usage.
  Write the Polish word, and keep a product's name only where it is the name on the bag.
- No regional variants matter here; write standard Polish, not Silesian or other regional words.
