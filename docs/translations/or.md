# ଓଡ଼ିଆ: style sheet and glossary

For translating Glazecalc into Odia (`or`), in standard written Odia as Odisha's schools, press and the Odia Wikipedia write it, for potters in India (region `IN`: Odisha). Read [README.md](README.md) first; [terms.md](terms.md) gives the meaning of every English term below. [hi.md](hi.md) is the model for the shape of this page, [kn.md](kn.md) and [bn.md](bn.md) the models for an Indian language in its own script. Every Odia message and guide follows this page: one Odia word for one thing. Odia is not offered yet: `or` is known to `lib/regions` ahead of its files (`index.js`: native digits `orya`; `groups.js`: south-asia). India fires by Orton cones by default, with temperatures one tap away, and its shops sell Orton cones and label glazes by cone, so the Odia pages must read well with Orton cones and temperatures and say nothing about SK cones that the English does not say.

**The owner's choices** (as for Hindi, `scratch/round6/plan.md` and `scratch/round8/plan.md` in the main checkout), applied in four tiers; every row says its tier:

1. **Craft words** that Indian studio potters say in English (glaze, frit, kiln, bisque, cone, slip, slurry, crazing, pinholing): the loanword in Odia letters as potters say it (ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, ଫ୍ରିଟ୍, କିଲ୍ନ୍, ବିସ୍କ୍, କୋନ୍), with the Odia term in brackets at its first use in each guide section (କିଲ୍ନ୍ (ଭାଟି), କୋନ୍ (ଶଙ୍କୁ)).
2. **Materials**: the name on the bag. Indian shops sell in English (Bhoomi's "Soda Feldspar 1 Kg", Clay Station's "CS Whiting (G-200)"), so the material is that English name in Odia letters (ସୋଡା ଫେଲ୍ଡସ୍ପାର୍, ହ୍ୱାଇଟିଂ), trade names and grades in Latin letters (Ferro 3134, G-325).
3. **Chemistry and physics** (oxide, mole, formula, oxidation, solubility, density): the Odia of the textbooks as the Odia Wikipedia uses it (ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍, ମୋଲ୍, ସୂତ୍ର, ଜାରଣ, ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ, ଘନତ୍ୱ), with the potters' loanword first where they use one (ଅକ୍ସିଡେସନ୍ (ଜାରଣ) for an oxidation firing is tier 1).
4. **Health, safety and law**: plain Odia (ଧୂଳି, ବିଷାକ୍ତ, କ୍ଷତିକାରକ, ସିଲିକୋସିସ୍, ସର୍ବାଧିକ), so that every reader understands it; a loanword only where Odia has no plain word (ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର୍, HEPA).

Other decisions already taken (the owner, as for Hindi): a sentence ends with the Latin full stop "." (never the danda); the translations write the digits 0 to 9 only, never Odia digits; ଆପଣ is the only register. The page is drawn in the device's own font (Nirmala UI on Windows, Noto Sans Oriya on Android and Linux, Oriya Sangam MN on Apple).

**What the sources could and could not give.** Odia has almost nothing written about studio glazes, and the Odia web is thin: of 64 English Wikipedia articles on the subject (Kiln, Ceramic glaze, Porcelain, Kaolinite, Feldspar, Silicosis, Relative density, Redox, Viscosity, Respirator and the rest) only three have an Odia article (Lead ସୀସା, Hydrometer ହାଇଡ୍ରୋମିଟର୍, Pyrometer ପାଇରୋମିଟର). Google's suggestions in Odia complete to nothing for ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, କିଲ୍ନ୍, ସେରାମିକ୍, ଫ୍ରିଟ, ପୋର୍ସେଲିନ or ଟେରାକୋଟା; ଭାଟି completes to a brick kiln and a liquor still (ଇଟା ଭାଟି, ମଦ ଭାଟି). What Odia does have is the words of the **traditional potter** (the Odia Wikipedia's ମାଟିପାତ୍ର: କୁମ୍ଭାର, କୁମ୍ଭକାର, ଭାଟି, କୁମ୍ଭାରଶାଳ, ପୋଡ଼ିବା, କଞ୍ଚା ମାଟିପାତ୍ର, ଅଳ୍ପ ଓଦା, ଚିକିଟା ମାଟି; PARI's Odia report: ଚୂଲି, ନିଆଁରେ ପୋଡ଼ିବା, ମାଟିକୁ ଚକଟିବା), Odisha's **terracotta** (ଟେରାକୋଟା is the Odia Wikipedia's word and the first gallery of Kala Bhoomi, the Odisha Crafts Museum, ଓଡ଼ିଶା ହସ୍ତଶିଳ୍ପ ସଂଗ୍ରହାଳୟ), the art college's "କୁମ୍ଭାରକଳା ଏବଂ ସେରାମିକ କଳା" (B.K. College of Art and Crafts, Bhubaneswar), and the **chemistry and medicine** of the Odia Wikipedia (ଜାରଣ, ବିଜାରଣ, ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ, ଅଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ, ଦ୍ରବୀଭୂତ, ତରଳିବା, ଗଳନାଙ୍କ, ଘନତ୍ୱ, ସିଲିକା, କ୍ରିସ୍ଟାଲାଇନ ସିଲିକା, ସିଲିକୋସିସ, ବିଷାକ୍ତ, କ୍ଷତିକାରକ, ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର, ଭେଣ୍ଟିଲେଟର, ବାୟୁ ଚଳାଚଳ). **Not reached**: CSTT's English-Odia Glossary of Chemistry (sold in print only: CSTT's e-cart lists it, no copy online), the Board of Secondary Education, Odisha's Odia-medium science books, the Odisha Sahitya Akademi's dictionaries, any Odia text of the OSH Code, the Factories Act or the Orissa Factories Rules (the Odisha Labour Directorate's site refused the connection; PMIndia's Odia page on the Code answered 403), BIS's Odia titles of IS 9473 and IS 9806 (none found), any Odia GHS label text, AIIMS's pages in Odia (none found), Google's, Apple's and Microsoft's Odia help pages for signing in (Google's 2-Step Verification page is served in English with an Odia banner; no Apple or Microsoft Odia help page was found), the Kala Bhoomi site itself (connection refused), the art colleges of Bhubaneswar and Khallikote in Odia, and any Odia pottery shop, kiln catalogue or kiln manual. **So the glaze-chemistry, kiln-part, controller, firing-log and defect rows rest on the potters' English and on no Odia source, the law rows on plain Odia usage and on no Odia legal text; every such row is marked No source, and the rows a native potter should check first are marked Native check.**

## Choices for the owner and a native potter

These are the questions this sheet cannot settle from sources; each has a recommendation, used below until answered. A translator lists each sentence that uses a **Native check** row in the flags for the reviewer.

- **Kiln:** **କିଲ୍ନ୍**, with (ଭାଟି) at its first use in each guide section. ଭାଟି is the traditional potter's firing pit (Or Wikipedia, ମାଟିପାତ୍ର: "କୁମ୍ଭାରଶାଳ ବା ଭାଟି"), but in everyday Odia also a brick kiln and a liquor still (Google suggest: "ଇଟା ଭାଟି", "ମଦ ଭାଟି"); ଚୁଲି / ଚୂଲି is a cooking hearth (PARI writes it for a village kiln). A native potter may prefer ଭାଟି throughout. An electric kiln is **ଇଲେକ୍ଟ୍ରିକ୍ କିଲ୍ନ୍**, never ଇଲେକ୍ଟ୍ରିକ୍ ଚୁଲି (a cooking stove).
- **Firing:** the verb is **ପୋଡ଼ିବା** (Or Wikipedia: "ମାଟିପାତ୍ରକୁ ପୋଡ଼ାଯାଇଥାଏ"; PARI: "ନିଆଁରେ ପୋଡିବା"): "କୋନ୍ 6 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ପୋଡ଼ନ୍ତୁ". The noun "a firing" and the firing words in compounds are **ଫାୟାରିଂ** (tier 1: ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ଫାୟାରିଂ, ବିସ୍କ୍ ଫାୟାରିଂ, ଫାୟାରିଂ ଲଗ୍), with (ପୋଡ଼ା) at its first use per section. Bare ଫାୟାରିଂ is also gunfire, so a title or a heading never has it alone: "କିଲ୍ନ୍ ଫାୟାରିଂ", "ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ଫାୟାରିଂ". Native check.
- **Glaze:** **ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍**, with (କାଚର ପ୍ରଲେପ) at its first use per section. No Odia source has a technical word for a ceramic glaze; ପ୍ରଲେପ alone is any coating (Or Wikipedia: "ଚୂନ ପ୍ରଲେପ", "ଚନ୍ଦନ ପ୍ରଲେପ"), and ଚିକ୍କଣ is smooth or glossy (a leaf, a seed). Titles say **ସେରାମିକ୍ ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍**. Native check.
- **Cone:** **କୋନ୍**, with (ଶଙ୍କୁ) at its first use per section. ଶଙ୍କୁ alone is the geometric cone (Or Wikipedia: a sundial's gnomon, "ଛାୟା ଶଙ୍କୁ"); **କୋଣ** is an angle and is never written for a cone.
- **Specific gravity:** **ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ**, the relative density, a ratio without units, with the English's figure after it (`guides.density.sg`: "ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ {value}"). The Or Wikipedia writes ଘନତ୍ୱ for density (a planet's) and ଘନତା for population density; it has no page on relative density (0 hits for ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ and for ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱ), and its hydrometer page explains the idea ("ଜଳଠାରୁ କେତେଗୁଣ ଭାରୀ"). ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ is the school physics term as other Indian languages build it (Bengali আপেক্ষিক গুরুত্ব is another word); Native check against the BSE Odisha physics book. The setting "Glaze density" is **ଗ୍ଲେଜର ବହଳତା** (how thick the slurry is: ବହଳ, thick, as the Or Wikipedia's "ବହଳ ଚୂନ ପ୍ରଲେପ"), so ଘନତ୍ୱ in g/cm³ never stands for it. Native check.
- **Viscosity:** never **ସାନ୍ଦ୍ରତା**, which the Or Wikipedia uses for concentration and density ("ଅସ୍ଥି ସାନ୍ଦ୍ରତା ବା ବୋନ ଡେନସିଟି", "ଅମ୍ଳଜାନ ସାନ୍ଦ୍ରତା"), unlike Bengali সান্দ্রতা. A melt is **ବହଳ** (stiff), **ତରଳ** (fluid) or **ବହିଯାଉଥିବା** (runny); "viscosity" in a sentence is **ବହିବାର ଗୁଣ (viscosity)**. No source; Native check.
- **Soluble and melting**: soluble **ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ** (Or Wikipedia: "ଜଳରେ ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ"), insoluble **ଅଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ**, slightly soluble **ଅଳ୍ପ ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ**, solubility **ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟତା**, dissolve **ଦ୍ରବୀଭୂତ ହେବା** ("ପାଣିରେ ଦ୍ରବୀଭୂତ ହୁଏ"); melt **ତରଳିବା** (Or Wikipedia: "ବରଫ ତରଳିବା"), melting point **ଗଳନାଙ୍କ**. Everyday Odia may say ମିଳେଇଯିବା for both; the safety text keeps them apart, because a lead frit's "low solubility" (କମ୍ ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟତା) must never read as "low melting" (କମ୍ ତାପରେ ତରଳେ). Check this row first in every safety sentence.
- **Turn off:** **ବନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତୁ** (switch off: "କିଲ୍ନ୍ ବନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତୁ", "ସୀସା ବନ୍ଦ ରଖନ୍ତୁ"); choose is **ବାଛନ୍ତୁ** or **ଚୟନ କରନ୍ତୁ** (Google: "ଆପଣ ବାଛିପାରିବେ"; MS: ଚୟନ କରନ୍ତୁ). The two never meet in one verb. On is **ଚାଲୁ**, turn on **ଚାଲୁ କରନ୍ତୁ**.
- **Kaolin and ଚୀନାମାଟି:** kaolin is **କାଓଲିନ୍**, with (ଚାଇନା କ୍ଲେ) at its first use per section, since Indian shops sell it as China Clay. **ଚୀନାମାଟି** (Or Wikipedia: "ଚୀନାମାଟି ପାତ୍ରରେ", china crockery) means porcelain or crockery, so it is never written for kaolin or for porcelain; porcelain is **ପୋର୍ସେଲିନ୍**.
- **Guide and manual:** the app's guides are **ଗାଇଡ୍** (every link, page title and list); a kiln's, controller's or sitter's manual is **ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ ପୁସ୍ତିକା**. Never the same word. A manual kiln is **ହାତରେ ଚଳାଯାଉଥିବା କିଲ୍ନ୍**, never ମାନୁଆଲ୍ କିଲ୍ନ୍. A maker's, supplier's or school's own "guide" is a ପୁସ୍ତିକା (booklet), a ଲେଖା (article) or a ଦସ୍ତାବିଜ (document), never ଗାଇଡ୍. **ଗାଇଡ୍ କୋନ୍** is a cone's name and stays.
- **Lead:** **ସୀସା** (Or Wikipedia), the metal and the setting; its compounds by the bag name (ଲେଡ୍ ଫ୍ରିଟ୍, ଲେଡ୍ ବାଇସିଲିକେଟ୍). **ସିସି** is a bottle and **କାଚ** is glass: check every one.
- **Potter:** **କୁମ୍ଭକାର** for a potter in general (Or Wikipedia: "କୁମ୍ଭକାରଙ୍କର ପର୍ବ"; plural କୁମ୍ଭକାରମାନେ). **କୁମ୍ଭାର** is the traditional potter and also a community's name and a surname (Or Wikipedia: ଧନେଶ୍ୱର କୁମ୍ଭାର; ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଲୋକ lists it among surnames), so the guides write it only where the English means village potters. Native check.
- **Loanwords' final virama and endings** (see Script and spelling): ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ alone, ଗ୍ଲେଜରେ with an ending; never a virama with an ending joined, never a ZWNJ. Native check on how a loanword reads without its virama (ଗ୍ଲେଜର, ଫ୍ରିଟକୁ, କିଲ୍ନରେ).
- **Water:** **ପାଣି** (everyday, 760 hits) in the guides and messages; **ଜଳ** (formal, 694 hits) only in a fixed chemistry phrase where the English is formal. Native check.
- **Kiln dial labels** stay in Latin (Off, Low, Medium, High) with an Odia gloss at first use (Off ବନ୍ଦ, Low କମ୍, Medium ମଧ୍ୟମ, High ଅଧିକ), as Indian kilns print them. Native check.
- **Poison line:** AIIMS's National Poisons Information Centre (1800 116 117, 24 hours, toll free) is verified (IN research). No Odia page of AIIMS was found, so the name stays as published, in English, with an Odia description once: "ବିଷ ସୂଚନା କେନ୍ଦ୍ର"; a poison line in general is **ବିଷ ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ କେନ୍ଦ୍ର** (Or Wikipedia: "ବିଷ ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ କେନ୍ଦ୍ରରେ ରିପୋର୍ଟ").

## 1. Style sheet

### Register

**ଆପଣ**, with its honorific verb forms (-ନ୍ତୁ, -ବେ, -ନ୍ତି), in the interface, errors, notices, emails, the safety text and the guides alike; never **ତୁମେ** or **ତୁ** and their forms (କର, କରୁ). Google's Odia addresses the reader so ("ଆପଣ ବାଛିପାରିବେ", "ଯେତେବେଳେ ଆପଣ ଆମର ସେବାଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରନ୍ତି"), and so does MS 2.2.1 ("ଆପଣ ଜାରି ରଖିବାକୁ ଚାହିଁବେ କି?"). MS asks for everyday words over formal ones (2.1, 2.1.3): ଚେଷ୍ଟା କରନ୍ତୁ, ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ, ବଦଳାନ୍ତୁ. "ଦୟାକରି" (please) only where the English says please.

- **Button:** the -ନ୍ତୁ imperative, the verb last: "ସଞ୍ଚୟ କରନ୍ତୁ" (Save), "ତୁଳନା କରନ୍ତୁ" (Compare), "ହଟାନ୍ତୁ" (Remove).
- **Error:** a plain statement in the passive, never the computer in the first person (MS 5.4.2: "କିଛି ଭୁଲ ହୋଇଛି", "… ମିଳୁ ନାହିଁ"): "ରେସିପି ସଞ୍ଚୟ କରିହେଲା ନାହିଁ. ପୁଣି ଚେଷ୍ଟା କରନ୍ତୁ.", "ଏହି ଫାଇଲ୍ ପଢ଼ିହେଲା ନାହିଁ."
- **Notice:** a plain statement: "ଏହି ପୃଷ୍ଠା AI ଅନୁବାଦ କରିଛି. ଇଂରାଜୀ ପୃଷ୍ଠା ପଢ଼ିବା ପାଇଁ ଏଠାରେ ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ."
- **Guide sentence:** an impersonal statement or the -ନ୍ତୁ imperative: "ଗ୍ଲେଜକୁ 80 ମେସ୍ ଚାଲୁଣିରେ ଦୁଇଥର ଛାଣନ୍ତୁ.", "ଉପାଦାନ ଓଜନ କରିବା ବେଳେ ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର୍ ପିନ୍ଧନ୍ତୁ.", "ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ବହୁତ ବହଳ ହେଲେ ଫାୟାରିଂରେ ବହିଯାଏ."
- **Should, must, may** stay apart, as the English has them:
  - "must" → **-ବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ** or **-ବାକୁ ହେବ** ("ପରୀକ୍ଷା କରାଇବାକୁ ହେବ"); a legal duty ("shall", "is required") → **ବାଧ୍ୟତାମୂଳକ**. "must not" → **-ନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ** in an instruction ("ଶୁଖିଲା ଝାଡ଼ୁ ମାରନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ"), **ନିଷିଦ୍ଧ** only in a legal text.
  - "should" → **-ବା ଉଚିତ** (Or Wikipedia: "ଦେବା ଉଚିତ"; "ମାସ୍କ ପିନ୍ଧିବା ଉଚିତ"); "should not" → **-ବା ଉଚିତ ନୁହେଁ**. Never ଆବଶ୍ୟକ or ବାଧ୍ୟତାମୂଳକ for "should", never ଉଚିତ for "must".
  - "may" (a hazard, a hedge that must survive) → **-ପାରେ** ("କର୍କଟ ସୃଷ୍ଟି କରିପାରେ", "ଏଥିରେ କିଛି ସରୁ କ୍ୱାର୍ଟଜ୍ ଥାଇପାରେ"; Or Wikipedia: "ହୋଇପାରେ"); "may" (permission) → **ଆପଣ … ପାରିବେ** (Google: "ଆପଣ ବାଛିପାରିବେ"). "can" as a possibility is -ପାରେ too, never permission.
  - "must be tested" → **ପରୀକ୍ଷା କରାଇବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ**: the test is done, not passed; never "ପରୀକ୍ଷାରେ ଉତ୍ତୀର୍ଣ୍ଣ ହେବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ". "have it tested" (by a laboratory) → **ପରୀକ୍ଷା କରାନ୍ତୁ** (someone else does it); **ପରୀକ୍ଷା କରନ୍ତୁ** is a test you do yourself (a test tile).
  - "some" (some fine quartz) → **କିଛି**, never **ଟିକିଏ** or **ଅଳ୍ପ** (a little). "never" → **କେବେ ବି … ନାହିଁ** only where the English says never; no ସବୁବେଳେ, ନିଶ୍ଚିତ or ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ the English lacks.
  - The other hedges survive too: "about" **ପ୍ରାୟ**, "usually" **ସାଧାରଣତଃ**, "often" **ଅନେକ ସମୟରେ**, "mainly" **ମୁଖ୍ୟତଃ** (never କେବଳ, only), "tend to" **…ର ପ୍ରବଣତା ଥାଏ**. "only" is **କେବଳ** only where the English says only. Every unless, or, not and without in a safety sentence is checked against the English.

### Buttons, menus, headings, labels and tabs

- **Buttons and menu commands:** the -ନ୍ତୁ imperative, short, the verb last (MS 4.1.18: ଚୟନ କରନ୍ତୁ): ସଞ୍ଚୟ କରନ୍ତୁ, ପ୍ରିଣ୍ଟ କରନ୍ତୁ, ତୁଳନା କରନ୍ତୁ, ହଟାନ୍ତୁ, ବାତିଲ କରନ୍ତୁ, ଉପାଦାନ ଯୋଗ କରନ୍ତୁ. A loanword verb takes କରନ୍ତୁ: ସ୍କାନ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ, ସାଇନ୍ ଇନ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ. Not "ଦୟାକରି ସଞ୍ଚୟ କରନ୍ତୁ", not the bare stem (ସଞ୍ଚୟ କର).
- **Headings, tabs, page titles:** a noun phrase: "ରେସିପି", "ସେଟିଂସ୍", "ଫାୟାରିଂ ଲଗ୍", "କୋନ୍ ଓ ହିଟୱାର୍କ". A heading that names an activity takes the verbal noun in -ବା: "Mixing the glaze" → "ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ମିଶାଇବା", "Typing amounts" → "ପରିମାଣ ଟାଇପ୍ କରିବା". A question stays one: "ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ କାହିଁକି ବହିଯାଏ?"
- **Field labels:** a noun; the colon where the English has one, no space before it: "ଉପାଦାନର ନାମ:".
- **Confirmations:** the impersonal passive, which agrees with nothing: "Saved: {name}" → "ସଞ୍ଚିତ ହେଲା: {name}"; "Removed" → "ହଟାଗଲା".
- **Progress:** the -ଉଛି form with the ellipsis (MS 4.1.18: "ବିଲୋପ କରୁଛି"): "Saving…" → "ସଞ୍ଚୟ କରୁଛି…".
- **Settings** as a page or tab is "ସେଟିଂସ୍" (Google: "ଗୋପନୀୟତା ସେଟିଂସ"); in running text "ସେଟିଂସ୍" too.

### Capitalisation

Odia has no capitals; the English's Title Case and sentence case go with the English words, and a heading is capitalised like any sentence would be, that is not at all. Latin words inside Odia text keep the English's case where they are names, codes, units or formulas (Orton, SK, UMF, LOI, Ferro 3134, N95, mg/m³, SiO₂), with a space on each side ("Orton କୋନ୍ 6", "Ferro 3134 ପାଇଁ"). An English loanword is written in Odia letters (ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, ଫ୍ରିଟ୍, କିଲ୍ନ୍), never in Latin ones, unless it is a trade name, a trademark (Kiln-Sitter, Zircopax), a company or brand (Google, Apple, Skutt), a code or a unit. Google keeps its own name in Latin letters ("Google ଆକାଉଣ୍ଟ"), and so does this page. Emphasis by capitals in the English (CONTAINS LEAD, LEAD FRIT) has no equivalent: the warning starts with **ସାବଧାନ:** ("Caution:") instead, and the words follow in plain letters.

### Script and spelling

- **Odia letters as the Odisha standard writes them** (the normative references of MS 1.1: Purnachandra Odia Bhashakosha, Sarbasara Byakarana), with the virama (୍) and the conjuncts as Odia types them: କ୍ଷ, ଜ୍ଞ, ଷ୍ଟ, ଣ୍ଟ, ନ୍ତ, ସ୍ପ, ଲ୍ଟ, the ଯ-ଫଳା (୍ୟ) and the ର-ଫଳା (୍ର) and the reph (ର୍), as in ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ, ର୍ୟାମ୍ପ୍, ପ୍ରିଣ୍ଟ.
- **A loanword ending in a consonant** takes the virama when it stands alone (ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, ଫ୍ରିଟ୍, କିଲ୍ନ୍, ବିସ୍କ୍, ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍, ଡିଭାଇସ୍), as MS 2.1.3 writes ୱାଲେଟ୍, କ୍ଲାଉଡ୍, ଡିଭାଇସ୍ and the Or Wikipedia ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍, so that it is not read with Odia's final a. **With an ending or a suffix joined, the virama goes** and the ending follows the bare letter: ଗ୍ଲେଜରେ, ଗ୍ଲେଜର, ଫ୍ରିଟକୁ, କିଲ୍ନରେ, ଅକ୍ସାଇଡଗୁଡ଼ିକ (as Odia writes ଏକାଉଣ୍ଟକୁ, ପିସିକୁ, ମାଟିପାତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ). Never a virama followed by an ending (ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ର would read as a conjunct, gl-jra), and never a ZWNJ to stop it (as the Or Wikipedia types one after a final virama): the translations check refuses every ZWNJ and ZWJ in Odia. A word ending in -ing has the anusvara and no virama (ଫାୟାରିଂ, କ୍ରେଜିଂ). Google writes some software words without the final virama (ପାସୱାର୍ଡ, ଡିଭାଇସ, ଆକାଉଣ୍ଟ): this page keeps one rule and writes them with it (ପାସୱାର୍ଡ୍, ଡିଭାଇସ୍, ଆକାଉଣ୍ଟ୍); Native check.
- **ଡ଼ and ଢ଼** (as in ପୋଡ଼ିବା, ବଢ଼ିବା, ଗୁଡ଼ିକ, ପଢ଼ନ୍ତୁ) are written as the letter and the nukta (ଡ + ଼, U+0B21 U+0B3C; ଢ + ଼, U+0B22 U+0B3C), their NFC form, never the precomposed U+0B5C or U+0B5D, which NFC replaces. The vowel signs ୈ, ୋ, ୌ are typed as one character each (U+0B48, U+0B4B, U+0B4C), which NFC keeps. The check refuses text that NFC would change.
- **ଯ, ୟ and ୱ kept apart:** **ଯ** (U+0B2F) is the Odia ja-sound of Sanskrit words (ଯନ୍ତ୍ର, ଯୋଗ, ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ); **ୟ** (U+0B5F) is the ya-sound of loanwords and of Odia words that have it (ଫାୟାରିଂ, ୟୁନିଟି, ଷ୍ଟୋନୱେୟାର୍, ଲିଥିୟମ୍); **ୱ** (U+0B71) is the wa of loanwords (ୱାକ୍ସ, ହ୍ୱାଇଟିଂ, ଷ୍ଟୋନୱେୟାର୍, ପାସୱାର୍ଡ୍), never ଵ (U+0B35) or ଓ୍ବ. Mixing ଯ and ୟ changes the word.
- **No ZWJ or ZWNJ** (U+200D, U+200C) anywhere: Odia's spelling needs neither (the check's joiner rule allows none for `or`); no direction marks or word joiners (U+200E, U+200F, U+2060, U+2066 to U+2069) either. Typing an Odia word copied from the Or Wikipedia or from CLDR brings ZWNJs with it (the region name of Hong Kong, "ଫେଲ୍ଡସ୍ପର୍", "ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍"): retype it.
- **Never text pasted from an old non-Unicode Odia font** (Akruti, Sambad, legacy PDFs, MS's own extracted text): it comes out as Latin letters or as Odia letters in the wrong order (MS's "ରହାଇଛି" for ହୋଇଛି). Take only text that reads as Odia letters, and the scripts check refuses letters of another script.
- **Latin runs** (units, formulas, trade names, codes) stay exactly as the English writes them; a figure and its unit together: "1222 °C", "10 g".
- **Hyphens** in compounds where the English has one and Odia keeps it: ସୀସା-ମୁକ୍ତ is written **ସୀସାମୁକ୍ତ** (one word, as Odia writes ପ୍ଲାଷ୍ଟିକ ମୁକ୍ତ apart or joined; this page joins it), ଡ୍ରପ୍-ଆଣ୍ଡ-ହୋଲ୍ଡ, ଲେଦର-ହାର୍ଡ୍.

### Punctuation

- **The full stop "."** ends a sentence (the owner's choice, as for Hindi), with no space before it and one after: "ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ବହିଯାଏ." A heading, a button and a list item that is not a sentence end with nothing (MS 4.1.14: "Sentence fragments … should not have a full-stop"). **Never the danda "।"** (U+0964), though MS's Rule 1, Google's Odia and the Or Wikipedia use it, and never the double danda.
- **Comma, question mark, colon, semicolon, exclamation mark** as in English (MS 4.1.14: "the sign of colon is the same as in English"), no space before them. No "!" in the app's own voice.
- **Quotation marks:** “ ” for quotations and for a word quoted (“ବୋରନ୍ ବ୍ଲୁ”), ‘ ’ inside them (MS 4.1.14). **UI names in running text** (buttons, tabs, settings) in single curly quotes: "‘ତୁଳନା କରନ୍ତୁ’ ବାଛନ୍ତୁ". Never the straight ' around a name: before `{`, `}` or `#` ICU reads it as a quote (README).
- **Parentheses** as in English, no space inside them; the full stop inside only when the bracket holds a whole sentence (MS 4.1.14).
- **Dashes:** the hyphen as in English; the en dash for ranges with no spaces (10–60%, କୋନ୍ 06–04; MS: "1–10"); the em dash is not used (MS: "Em dash is not used in standard form of writings"): a comma, a colon or a new sentence instead.
- **Ellipsis** "…" only where the English has one.

### Numbers and units

- **The digits 0 to 9** wherever the English has digits (the international form of Indian numerals; CLDR's `or` uses them, numbering system `latn`; Google's Odia writes them). **Never Odia digits** (୦ ୧ ୨ ୩ ୪ ୫ ୬ ୭ ୮ ୯), which the Or Wikipedia uses and the numbers check does not read; never spelled-out figures where the English has digits.
- **The decimal point and the English's figures as the English writes them:** 1.5; 0.025; 12,500; 1222 °C and 1000 g ungrouped. The app groups numbers the Indian way (12,34,567.5) when it formats them; a translation writes only the English's figures, never **ଲକ୍ଷ** or **କୋଟି** for one ("12,500" stays 12,500, never "ବାର ହଜାର ପାଞ୍ଚ ଶହ").
- **Percent:** 5%, no space (CLDR); a range 10–60%. **ଶତକଡ଼ା** (Or Wikipedia) only where the English spells out "percent" ("percent of the base" → "ବେସର ଶତକଡ଼ା"), never "ଶତକଡ଼ା 5" for "5%".
- **Units:** the English's symbols in Latin letters, with the English's spacing: g, kg, mg, µg, mL, L, mm, cm, °C, °F, °Bé, %, mg/m³, µg/cm²; never ଗ୍ରାମ୍, କି.ଗ୍ରା., ସେ.ମି. or ସେ. for the symbol (MS 4.1.1 lists ଗ୍ରା. and କି.ମି. as abbreviations; the numbers check does not read them). The unit's name in running text only where the English spells it ("grams" → ଗ୍ରାମ୍). Inches and feet are the words **ଇଞ୍ଚ** and **ଫୁଟ** (Or Wikipedia: "… ଇଞ୍ଚ ବର୍ଷା", "…ଫୁଟ ଉଚ୍ଚର ମାଟିକାନ୍ଥ"), with the metric figure in brackets as the English gives it: "12 ଇଞ୍ଚ (30 cm)".
- **Endings after a figure** join it, as Odia writes them after any numeral (Google: "ଅକ୍ଟୋବର 16, 2026ରୁ"; Or Wikipedia: "…%ରୁ ଅଧିକ"): "1948ର", "6 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ", "10%ରୁ କମ୍", "9:00ରୁ 17:00 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ". After a figure with a unit symbol, the ending joins the symbol with no space: "2.5 cmରୁ କମ୍", "25 mmରୁ ଅଧିକ ନୁହେଁ", "1222 °Cରେ". The classifier **ଟି** joins a figure that counts things: "3ଟି ଟାଇଲ୍".
- **A number the English writes as a word** ("two coats", "three tiles", "once", "one number hotter") stays a word, since a digit would be a figure the English lacks and the check rejects it: "ଦୁଇଟି ସ୍ତର", "ତିନିଟି ଟାଇଲ୍", "ଥରେ", "ଏକ ନମ୍ବର ଅଧିକ ଗରମ". Where the English has the digit, the digit stays: "3 coats" → "3ଟି ସ୍ତର".
- **Dates:** "14 ଅକ୍ଟୋବର 2026", "ଅକ୍ଟୋବର 2026": the day, the month named beside the year, the Gregorian calendar, never an Odia-calendar (Anka) or Shaka year, and never "ମସିହା" added to a year the English writes bare. CLDR's own long form is "ଅକ୍ଟୋବର 14, 2026"; the app formats the dates it prints, so this rule is for dates written in a sentence. The month names are CLDR's (above).
- **Clock times:** the 24-hour clock, **20:00**, with an ending if the sentence needs it ("20:00 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ", "8:00ରୁ 20:00 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ"). Never **ସକାଳ**, **ଦିନ**, **ଅପରାହ୍ନ**, **ସନ୍ଧ୍ୟା** or **ରାତି** with a 12-hour figure and ଟା ("ରାତି 8ଟାରେ"), which the check reads as 8, and never AM or PM. "24 hours" → "24 ଘଣ୍ଟା"; "24/7" → "24/7" or "ଦିନକୁ 24 ଘଣ୍ଟା, ସପ୍ତାହକୁ 7 ଦିନ" (both figures kept).
- **No figures the English lacks:** no Odia digits, no ଲକ୍ଷ or କୋଟି, no Anka or Shaka years, no SK or Orton equivalents, no converted units, no IS numbers (IS 9473, IS 9806) where the English names none.

### Plural forms

CLDR gives Odia two cardinal categories (ICU 78.3: `[ 'one', 'other' ]`). **`one` is only 1** (`n = 1`): 0, 0.5, 1.5, 2 and 21 are `other`, unlike Hindi and Kannada, whose `one` covers 0. So the `one` form is only ever shown for exactly 1, and the `other` form must read right for 0 ("0ଟି ଉପାଦାନ" is good Odia).

| Category | Numbers                  | Example                                                                    |
| -------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `one`    | 1                        | 1ଟି ଉପାଦାନ, 1ଟି ସ୍ତର, 1ଟି ରେସିପି, 1ଟି ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, 1 ଘଣ୍ଟା                      |
| `other`  | 0, 0.5, 1.5, 2, 21, 100… | 0ଟି ଉପାଦାନ, 2ଟି ଉପାଦାନ, 3ଟି ସ୍ତର, 21ଟି ରେସିପି, 5ଟି ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, 2ଟି କୋନ୍, 0.5 g |

Every plural message has both forms, and `=0 {…}` stays where the English has it:
`{count, plural, one {# material} other {# materials}}` → `{count, plural, one {#ଟି ଉପାଦାନ} other {#ଟି ଉପାଦାନ}}`. Where the English's `one` has no `#` ("this passkey", "it"), the Odia `one` form says exactly one as the English does (ଏହି ପାସକୀ): Odia's `one` is never reached by 0, so no `=1` is needed for it.

How the noun takes the number: **after a figure an Odia noun stays singular**, with the classifier **ଟି** joined to the figure for things counted one by one (MS 5.4.2: "the numeral place holders typically precede the noun"; CLDR: "2 ଘଣ୍ଟା", "5 ଗ୍ରାମ୍"). A unit of time or measure takes no classifier. The plural suffix **-ଗୁଡ଼ିକ** (things; Or Wikipedia: "ମାଟିପାତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ") or **-ମାନେ** (people: କୁମ୍ଭକାରମାନେ) is written only without a number ("ଉପାଦାନଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଯୋଗ କରନ୍ତୁ"), never after one ("3ଟି ଉପାଦାନଗୁଡ଼ିକ" is wrong). A loanword takes them like any noun, without its final virama: ଗ୍ଲେଜଗୁଡ଼ିକ, ଫ୍ରିଟଗୁଡ଼ିକ, କୋନଗୁଡ଼ିକ. Odia verbs mark person, number and honour, not gender; a thing in the plural takes the singular verb ("3ଟି ଉପାଦାନ ଯୋଗ ହେଲା"), so both forms usually read the same.

### Placeholders and names

Odia joins its case endings to the word (-ରେ, -କୁ, -ର, -ରୁ, -ଠାରୁ, -ଙ୍କ), so a placeholder, a trade name in Latin letters or a name in a tag takes none: "{region}ରେ" and "{name}କୁ" are not written. Three ways, in this order:

1. **A separate postposition**, a word of its own that needs no ending on the word before it: **ପାଇଁ** (for), **ସହିତ** (with), **ମଧ୍ୟରେ** (in, within), **ବିଷୟରେ** (about), **ପରି** (like), **ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ** (up to, until). "{region} ମଧ୍ୟରେ କୌଣସି ସୀମା ନାହିଁ", "{name} ପାଇଁ ବିଶ୍ଳେଷଣ", "{library} ସହିତ".
2. **A generic noun beside it** that takes the ending: "ରେସିପି {name} ସଞ୍ଚିତ ହେଲା", "ଉପାଦାନ {name} ଆଉ ତିଆରି ହୁଏ ନାହିଁ", "{name} ନାମକ ରେସିପିରେ" (in the recipe called {name}), "Ferro 3134 ଫ୍ରିଟରେ".
3. **The label form**, a colon after a noun: "ସଞ୍ଚିତ ହେଲା: {name}", "ଅଞ୍ଚଳ: {region}".

`{region}` is a country's or region's name in Odia from the browser (ଭାରତ, ଯୁକ୍ତ ରାଷ୍ଟ୍ର, ଯୁକ୍ତରାଜ୍ୟ, ଜର୍ମାନୀ, ଜାପାନ; Hong Kong is a region): never "{region} ଦେଶ" or anything that calls it a country. `{count}` is a figure: the classifier ଟି and a figure's endings join it as they join any figure ("{count}ଟି ଉପାଦାନ", "{count}ରୁ ଅଧିକ"), since they never change with the number. `{library}`: "{library} ଲାଇବ୍ରେରୀ". **Tags** stay around the words that mean the same; an ending after a closing tag is avoided the same way.

### Cones

Orton cones are written **Orton** in Latin letters wherever the English says Orton, beside **କୋନ୍** (the potters' word: Bhoomi's "Cones Pyrometric", Clay Station's "Orton Cones"; the Odia ଶଙ୍କୁ in brackets at the first use in each guide section: "କୋନ୍ (ଶଙ୍କୁ)"): "Orton କୋନ୍ 6", "କୋନ୍ 06", "Orton କୋନଗୁଡ଼ିକ". **SK cones** are written SK with the number as printed (SK 6a) only where the English has them; India does not use them and its shops do not sell them. **Seger** is written **ସେଗର୍** only where the English says Seger: the firing guide's older European cones (ସେଗର୍ କୋନ୍) and the **ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର** (the unity molecular formula, UMF), which is another thing and never names a cone. Never give an SK number for an Orton number or the reverse, nor say they are equal, nor add a cone to "mid fire" or "high fire" where the English has none.

- **One number hotter, one number cooler:** **ଏକ ନମ୍ବର ଅଧିକ ଗରମ** and **ଏକ ନମ୍ବର କମ୍ ଗରମ** (the guard cone is "ଏକ ନମ୍ବର ଅଧିକ ଗରମ କୋନ୍"); never ଉଚ୍ଚ, ନିମ୍ନ, ବଡ଼ or ସାନ: cone 05 is cooler than 04 ("କୋନ୍ 05, କୋନ୍ 04ଠାରୁ ଏକ ନମ୍ବର କମ୍ ଗରମ").
- **cone 06** is written 06 with its zero (read "ଜିରୋ ଛଅ"); never 6.
- Not ଶଙ୍କୁ alone in running text (the geometric cone), and never କୋଣ (an angle).

### The unity formula

**ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର (UMF)**: so at its first use in each guide section and in the setting, "ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର" afterwards. The app's short "Unity formula" is "ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର", the label "Unity:" is "ସେଗର୍:". Where the English names both ("the unity molecular formula, also called the Seger formula"): "ୟୁନିଟି ଆଣବିକ ସୂତ୍ର, ଯାହାକୁ ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର ମଧ୍ୟ କୁହାଯାଏ". "unity" as the scaling is **ୟୁନିଟି**, explained once as "ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସଗୁଡ଼ିକର ମୋଟ ଏକ ଧରି" (ଏକ as a word: the English has no digit); never ଏକତା (togetherness). The three columns: **ଗ୍ଲାସ୍ ଫର୍ମର୍** (RO₂), **ଷ୍ଟେବିଲାଇଜର୍** (R₂O₃), **ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ** (R₂O ଓ RO). "formula" alone in a chemical sense is **ସୂତ୍ର** (ରାସାୟନିକ ସୂତ୍ର); a glaze recipe is **ରେସିପି**: never ସୂତ୍ର for a recipe. No Odia name for the UMF was found (No source; Native check).

### Gender-neutral wording

Odia verbs and pronouns mark person, number and honour, never gender (MS 3.1: "There are no gender-specific pronouns in Odia"; ସେ for he and she), so the text never guesses the reader's gender. Keep it so: people in general are plural or by role (MS 3.1: ଲୋକମାନେ, ବ୍ୟବହାରକାରୀଗଣ; here କୁମ୍ଭକାରମାନେ, ବ୍ୟବହାରକାରୀ), never a gendered compound (ମହିଳା କୁମ୍ଭକାର, ପୁରୁଷ …) where the English has none. A person named in a guide takes the honorific plural verb ("Derek Au … କରନ୍ତି"); a company, a maker, a website or an agency takes the singular ("Orton କହେ", "HSE ପରାମର୍ଶ ଦିଏ").

### The reader in the guides

ଆପଣ where the sentence needs a subject; steps are -ନ୍ତୁ imperatives; explanations are impersonal ("ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ବହୁତ ବହଳ ହେଲେ ବହିଯାଏ"). Short plain sentences, as the English, one Odia sentence for one English sentence where Odia allows. Quotations from sources are translated into “…” and keep their figures as the English writes them. **The bracketed Odia term** (tier 1 and 2 rows below) goes right after the loanword at its first use in each guide section, before the `{{en: …}}` mark where the term has one: "ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ (ଗଳନ ସହାୟକ){{en: flux}}". Never in a button, a label, a table heading or a message of the JSON files; in the glossary of glazing-basics (the definition list), each term line may give it once. Button and label names quoted in a guide are the app's real labels, from the Odia JSON files.

### Law in India

As the law writes it, never paraphrased. India's Acts are in Hindi and English, and no Odia text of the OSH Code, the Factories Act or the Orissa Factories Rules could be read (Not reached, above), so the law's words below are plain Odia usage, checked against the Or Wikipedia's uses; **Native check** on each against the Odia texts when they are read.

- **Limits:** "at most", "no more than", "up to" (a limit) → **ସର୍ବାଧିକ** or **…ରୁ ଅଧିକ ନୁହେଁ** (the limit itself passes): "up to 25% nepheline syenite" in a hazard line → "ସର୍ବାଧିକ 25%". **ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ** only where "up to" is no limit: a usage range, a temperature reached ("କୋନ୍ 6 ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ପୋଡ଼ନ୍ତୁ"; Or Wikipedia: "… ସେଲସିୟସ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ"), a time. "less than", "under", "below" → **…ରୁ କମ୍** (it does not pass; Or Wikipedia: "ପାଞ୍ଚ ବର୍ଷ ବୟସରୁ କମ"). "at least", "or more" → **ଅନ୍ତତଃ** (Or Wikipedia, in a legal sense: "ଅନ୍ତତଃ ସାତ ବର୍ଷ") or **ସର୍ବନିମ୍ନ**, or **…ରୁ କମ୍ ନୁହେଁ**. "more than", "over", "above" → **…ରୁ ଅଧିକ** (Or Wikipedia: "…%ରୁ ଅଧିକ"). Never …ରୁ କମ୍ for "at most", never …ରୁ ଅଧିକ for "at least": a slip moves a legal limit. Check every one in the food-contact, silica and mask texts.
- **The Code:** the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 → **ବୃତ୍ତିଗତ ସୁରକ୍ଷା, ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ଓ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ପରିସ୍ଥିତି ସଂହିତା, 2020** with the English name in brackets at its first use (no Odia text read: a description, not the Code's own title; Native check). Section 88 leaves the limits to the states: "each state sets its own limit" → "ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ରାଜ୍ୟ ନିଜ ସୀମା ସ୍ଥିର କରେ"; "We could not confirm a national limit" → "ଆମେ ଜାତୀୟ ସୀମା ନିଶ୍ଚିତ କରିପାରିଲୁ ନାହିଁ". The Factories Act, 1948 → **କାରଖାନା ଅଧିନିୟମ, 1948**.
- **Silica:** respirable crystalline silica → **ଶ୍ୱାସ ସହିତ ଫୁସଫୁସକୁ ଯାଉଥିବା କ୍ରିସ୍ଟାଲାଇନ୍ ସିଲିକା (RCS)** at its first use, then **ଶ୍ୱସନୀୟ କ୍ରିସ୍ଟାଲାଇନ୍ ସିଲିକା** (Or Wikipedia: "କ୍ରିସ୍ଟାଲାଇନ ସିଲିକା"; ଶ୍ୱସନୀୟ No source; Native check); silicosis **ସିଲିକୋସିସ୍** (Or Wikipedia: "ସିଲିକୋସିସ ରୋଗ"); the 8-hour average "8 ଘଣ୍ଟାର ହାରାହାରି". An exposure limit → **ସଂସ୍ପର୍ଶ ସୀମା**.
- **Masks:** IS 9473:2002 (filtering half masks against particles, classes FFP1, FFP2, FFP3), from the **ଭାରତୀୟ ମାନକ ବ୍ୟୁରୋ (BIS)** (usage; its Odia name not read on BIS's pages; Native check); its Odia title was not found: describe it as "କଣିକାରୁ ରକ୍ଷା କରୁଥିବା ଫିଲ୍ଟର୍ ହାଫ୍ ମାସ୍କ" and keep the number and the classes as the English gives them.
- **Food contact:** India has no mandatory rule for ceramic ware (IS 9806:2001 is voluntary; none is shown). The English's food-contact categories are written plainly (2.9).
- **GHS:** no Odia GHS label text was found; the words in 2.7 (ସଙ୍କେତ ଶବ୍ଦ, ବିପଦ, ସତର୍କତା, ଶ୍ରେଣୀଭୁକ୍ତ ନୁହେଁ) and the H-statement wordings there are this page's, from plain Odia; Native check.
- **Who to call:** **112** ("ଜରୁରୀକାଳୀନ ନମ୍ବର 112": police, fire and ambulance; ଜରୁରୀକାଳୀନ, 97 hits in the Or Wikipedia, over ଆପାତକାଳୀନ, 13); the **National Poisons Information Centre (NPIC)**, **ଅଖିଳ ଭାରତୀୟ ଆୟୁର୍ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ସଂସ୍ଥାନ (AIIMS), ନୂଆଦିଲ୍ଲୀ** (Or Wikipedia), **1800 116 117**, 24 ଘଣ୍ଟା, ଟୋଲ୍ ଫ୍ରି (the centre's name as AIIMS publishes it, in English, with "ବିଷ ସୂଚନା କେନ୍ଦ୍ର" once). Bodies: **ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ଓ ପରିବାର କଲ୍ୟାଣ ମନ୍ତ୍ରଣାଳୟ** (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; Or Wikipedia: "ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ଓ ପରିବାର କଲ୍ୟାଣ ମନ୍ତ୍ରୀ"), **ଶ୍ରମ ଓ ନିଯୁକ୍ତି ମନ୍ତ୍ରଣାଳୟ** (Ministry of Labour and Employment; usage), **DGFASLI** and **DGOSH** kept in Latin with the English name (no Odia name read).
- **EU law**, where the English names it (Directive 84/500/EEC, Directive (EU) 2017/2398, CLP): there is no official Odia text; write **ୟୁରୋପୀୟ ସଂଘର (EU) ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ 84/500/EEC**, **CLP ନିୟମାବଳୀ (EC) No 1272/2008**, with the number kept, so that the 3 L still applies only to packaging and storage vessels. US and UK bodies keep their English names with an Odia gloss once: OSHA (ଆମେରିକାର ବୃତ୍ତିଗତ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସଂସ୍ଥା), HSE (ଯୁକ୍ତରାଜ୍ୟର ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ଓ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସଂସ୍ଥା; named as the UK's at its first use in each guide section), FDA (ଆମେରିକାର ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଓ ଔଷଧ ସଂସ୍ଥା), CDC (ଆମେରିକାର ରୋଗ ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ ସଂସ୍ଥା), IARC (ବିଶ୍ୱ ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ସଂଗଠନର କର୍କଟ ଗବେଷଣା ସଂସ୍ଥା).

### Pitfalls

- **ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍, ଏନାମେଲ, ବାର୍ନିସ୍, ରଙ୍ଗ, ପ୍ରଲେପ:** a glaze is **ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍** (the material and the coat; "to glaze" ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ଲଗାଇବା, glazed ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍ ଲଗା); **ଏନାମେଲ** is enamel paint and tooth enamel (Or Wikipedia: "ରସାୟନିକ ଏନାମେଲ ରଙ୍ଗ", "ଦାନ୍ତର ଏନାମେଲ"), **ବାର୍ନିସ୍** varnish, **ରଙ୍ଗ** paint or colour (PARI: the village potter's "ରଂଗ"), **ପ୍ରଲେପ** any coating: none is a glaze. Titles say **ସେରାମିକ୍ ଗ୍ଲେଜ୍**.
- **ଫ୍ରିଟ୍ and ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ:** a frit is **ଫ୍ରିଟ୍** (a factory glass); a flux is **ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ** (the oxide's role; Or Wikipedia uses ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ for the metallurgist's flux and for magnetic leakage flux, "ଲିକେଜ ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ"): in running text "ଗ୍ଲେଜର ଫ୍ଲକ୍ସ" at first use where the sense could slip.
- **କିଲ୍ନ୍, ଭାଟି, ଚୁଲି, ଓଭେନ୍, ଚୁଲ୍ଲୀ:** a potter's kiln is **କିଲ୍ନ୍** (ଭାଟି at the first use per section); **ଭାଟି** alone is also a brick kiln and a liquor still; **ଚୁଲି** is a cooking hearth; **ଓଭେନ୍** a kitchen oven, written only where the English says oven; a furnace (metal) is **ଭାଟି** or **ଚୁଲ୍ଲୀ**: never written for the potter's kiln.
- **ବିସ୍କ୍ and କଞ୍ଚା:** **କଞ୍ଚା** is unfired (Or Wikipedia: "କଞ୍ଚା ମାଟିପାତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ"; କଞ୍ଚା ମାଟି, raw clay); **ବିସ୍କ୍** is the first firing and its ware (ବିସ୍କ୍ ଫାୟାରିଂ, ବିସ୍କ୍ ହୋଇଥିବା ପାତ୍ର). Never କଞ୍ଚା for bisque, never ବିସ୍କ୍ for unfired ware, never **ବିସ୍କୁଟ** (the biscuit; Or Wikipedia's 10 hits are all food). **କଞ୍ଚାମାଲ** is raw materials in the trade sense, not unfired.
- **ଆର୍ଥେନୱେୟାର୍, ଟେରାକୋଟା, ଷ୍ଟୋନୱେୟାର୍, ପୋର୍ସେଲିନ୍, ଚୀନାମାଟି, ମାଟିପାତ୍ର:** earthenware is **ଆର୍ଥେନୱେୟାର୍**, glossed once as "କମ୍ ତାପରେ ପୋଡ଼ା ଛିଦ୍ରଯୁକ୍ତ ମାଟିପାତ୍ର"; **ଟେରାକୋଟା** is the red, usually unglazed low-fired ware of Odisha's craft (Or Wikipedia; Kala Bhoomi's gallery), written only where the English says terracotta; **ମାଟିପାତ୍ର** (Or Wikipedia) is any clay pot and is not used for the class of ware; stoneware is **ଷ୍ଟୋନୱେୟାର୍**; porcelain is **ପୋର୍ସେଲିନ୍**; **ଚୀନାମାଟି** means china crockery and porcelain: never used for kaolin or for porcelain; kaolin is **କାଓଲିନ୍** (ଚାଇନା କ୍ଲେ). **ପୋଡ଼ାମାଟି** (fired clay, 2 hits) is terracotta's literal name and is not used.
- **ସିଲିକା, ସିଲିକନ୍, ସିଲିକୋନ୍, ବାଲି:** silica is the oxide **ସିଲିକା** (SiO₂; Or Wikipedia); the element is **ସିଲିକନ୍** (Or Wikipedia's title ସିଲିକନ); silicone rubber is **ସିଲିକୋନ୍**: never ସିଲିକନ୍ or ସିଲିକୋନ୍ for silica. Sand is **ବାଲି**; silica sand **ସିଲିକା ବାଲି**.
- **ହ୍ୱାଇଟିଂ, ଚୂନ, ଖଡ଼ି:** whiting is **ହ୍ୱାଇଟିଂ** (calcium carbonate, CaCO₃; Clay Station's "CS Whiting"); **ଚୂନ** is lime (CaO, slaked lime and the lime eaten with paan; Or Wikipedia: "ଚୂନ ପ୍ରଲେପ", "ଚୂନ ଭାଟି"); **ଖଡ଼ି** is chalk (and the ritual white clay of the Jagannath temple's "ଖଡ଼ି ଲାଗି"). Neither is used for whiting; "lime" in a frit's name is ଚୂନ only there ("ସୋଡା-ପଟାସ୍-ଚୂନ ଫ୍ରିଟ୍").
- **ବୋରନ୍, ବୋରାକ୍ସ, ବୋରିକ୍ ଏସିଡ୍, ସୋହାଗା:** B₂O₃ is **ବୋରନ୍ (B₂O₃)**, formally ବୋରନ୍ ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍; borax is **ବୋରାକ୍ସ** (Or Wikipedia, "ବୋରାକ୍ସ Na2B4O7"; the jeweller's ସୋହାଗା not used, 0 hits); boric acid is **ବୋରିକ୍ ଏସିଡ୍**. In the safety text, name each as the English does: borax and boric acid are reproductive toxins.
- **ପଟାସ୍ and ସୋଡା:** "potash" (K₂O) is **ପଟାସ୍ (K₂O)** (Or Wikipedia: "ପଟାସ୍ ଓ ସୋଡ଼ା" among the crust's oxides), formally ପୋଟାସିୟମ୍ ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍; in India ପଟାସ୍ is also the fertiliser, so the oxide always has K₂O at its first use in a section. The safety guide's potash (potassium carbonate) is **ପୋଟାସିୟମ୍ କାର୍ବୋନେଟ୍**. "soda" (Na₂O) is **ସୋଡା (Na₂O)**, formally ସୋଡିୟମ୍ ଅକ୍ସାଇଡ୍; soda ash is **ସୋଡା ଆଶ୍**; ସୋଡା alone is also soda water and washing or baking soda. The Or Wikipedia writes both ସୋଡା and ସୋଡ଼ା: this page writes **ସୋଡା**.
- **ଷ୍ଟେନ୍ and କଲରାଣ୍ଟ:** a stain (factory-made) is **ଷ୍ଟେନ୍** (Bhoomi's "Stains"), at its first use per section "ଷ୍ଟେନ୍ (ତିଆରି ରଙ୍ଗ)": ଷ୍ଟେନ୍ is also a mark or dirt. A colorant (any oxide that colours) is **କଲରାଣ୍ଟ (ରଞ୍ଜକ)**; ରଞ୍ଜକ alone is a dye (Or Wikipedia: "କୃତ୍ରିମ ରଞ୍ଜକ").
- **ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର୍, ଭେଣ୍ଟିଲେଟର୍, ବାୟୁ ଚଳାଚଳ, ଶ୍ୱାସଯନ୍ତ୍ର:** a respirator is **ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର୍ ମାସ୍କ** at its first use, then **ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର୍** (Or Wikipedia: "ଏନ୯୫ ରେସ୍ପିରେଟର"); its ଶ୍ୱାସଯନ୍ତ୍ର ("ଏନ୯୫ ଶ୍ୱାସଯନ୍ତ୍ର") is not used, since a hospital's breathing machine is called so too; a hospital ventilator is **ଭେଣ୍ଟିଲେଟର୍** (Or Wikipedia), never meant here; ventilation of a room is **ବାୟୁ ଚଳାଚଳ** (Or Wikipedia), ଭେଣ୍ଟିଲେସନ୍ in LEV only. A cloth or surgical mask is **କପଡ଼ା ମାସ୍କ / ସର୍ଜିକାଲ୍ ମାସ୍କ** (Or Wikipedia: "ସର୍ଜିକାଲ ମାସ୍କ"): say which.
- **ଗାଇଡ୍ and ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ ପୁସ୍ତିକା:** see "Guide and manual" above.
- **କ୍ରେଜିଂ, କ୍ରାକଲ୍, ଫାଟ:** crazing is **କ୍ରେଜିଂ** (a defect); crackle is **କ୍ରାକଲ୍** (wanted); a crack through the pot from heating or cooling is **ଡଣ୍ଟିଂ**; a crack in general is **ଫାଟ**.
- **ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ, ଘନତ୍ୱ, ସାନ୍ଦ୍ରତା, ବହଳତା:** specific gravity is **ଆପେକ୍ଷିକ ଘନତ୍ୱ**, a ratio with no unit (1.45); **ଘନତ୍ୱ** is density in g/cm³ and is never used for a glaze; **ସାନ୍ଦ୍ରତା** is concentration (never used here); a glaze's density (the setting) is **ବହଳତା**; never a litre weight, another measure.
- **ଦ୍ରବଣୀୟ and ତରଳିବା:** see "Soluble and melting" above.
- **ଏଲିମେଣ୍ଟ୍ and ମୌଳିକ:** a kiln's heating element is **ଏଲିମେଣ୍ଟ୍**; a chemical element (the Materials form's "Elements") is **ମୌଳିକ** (Or Wikipedia: "ରାସାୟନିକ ମୌଳିକ").
- **ଲିଚିଂ:** leaching is **ଲିଚିଂ** (ଧାତୁ ନିର୍ଗମନ); the leach test is a test of the metals a fired piece releases, not narrowed to lead and cadmium.
- **ସୂତ୍ର and ରେସିପି:** a recipe is **ରେସିପି**, a chemical formula **ସୂତ୍ର**, the unity formula **ସେଗର୍ ସୂତ୍ର**.
- **ସୀସା, ସିସି, କାଚ:** lead is **ସୀସା**; **ସିସି** is a bottle and **କାଚ** glass. Check every one.
- **ପ୍ଲାଷ୍ଟିକ୍, ଷ୍ଟେବିଲାଇଜର୍, ସସପେଣ୍ଡର୍, କୁକି, ଫୁଟ୍:** loanwords that mean something else in Odia homes (plastic, a voltage stabiliser, braces, a biscuit, the unit of length): see their rows for the wording that avoids them. A pot's foot is never ଫୁଟ୍ (feet), but **ପାତ୍ରର ତଳ**.
- **Placeholders:** see Placeholders and names.
